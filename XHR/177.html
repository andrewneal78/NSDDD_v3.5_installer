[1226] It is not easily recognized that there are other government bodies with enshrined law enforcement functions and powers that when not effectively discharged lead to disorder and can ultimately foster criminal activity

[1227] It requires greater public involvement and commitment in supporting all aspects of law enforcement activities

[1228] The society should therefore be less tolerant of deviant and antisocial behaviour

[1229] Public participation and partnership with the police in dealing with security issues needs a structured medium for this to work effectively

[1230] It requires that bodies-councils be established with set criteria for their composition, function and performance

[1232] An integrated approach through sets of codes of practice should guide partnership amongst these entities
