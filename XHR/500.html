[243] The existence and maintenance of an adequate defense industrial base are an important prerequisite for ensuring state security

[244] This is a collection of state and private capacities for research, development, production and testing of armaments and technology

[245] The industrial base of defence is significant from a strategic (e.g. reduction of dependence on foreign supplies, readiness of supply in crisis situations), political (e.g. increase of defence credibility) and economic (e.g. creation of job opportunities) perspective
