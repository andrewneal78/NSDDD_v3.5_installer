[244] CAPABILITIES OF THE ARMED FORCES

[245] The Armed Forces of the Czech Republic, whose main combat force is the Army of the Czech Republic, are built on the all-arms principle and with an emphasis on a combination of technological sophistication and robustness, resistance to hybrid action and cyber attacks, deployability, sustainability and the ability to operate in a digital environment and diverse climatic conditions, even at a strategic distance from the borders of the Czech Republic

[246] The Armed Forces of the Czech Republic must have the ability to quickly adapt to a changing situation

[247] Defense capabilities will also continue to be developed through multinational projects and initiatives of NATO and the EU

[248] The aim is to ensure access to capabilities that would be uneconomical for the Czech Republic on their own, to strengthen interoperability (standardization, unification), to reduce the number of platforms used, to simplify logistical support and to strengthen the Czech Republic's expertise in unique capabilities

[249] The aim is technologically advanced armed forces equipped with modern combat systems, including unmanned ones, and having the ability to operate with precision-guided munitions at long distances

[250] Command and control systems will enable effective operation in all operational domains within multi-domain operations, including the ability to process and evaluate large volumes of data

[251] The armed forces will be maintained at the necessary numbers and levels of readiness with equipment and supplies to conduct combat activities in a large-scale and long-lasting conflict of high intensity

[252] The development of the armed forces will not consist only in the acquisition of new types of equipment, but also in the mutual integration of individual elements across domains and their involvement in command and control systems

[253] This integration will be supported by the introduction of modern systems with the potential for further development

[254] The Czech Republic is allocating its forces and assets to all components of the New NATO Force Model

[255] To this end, it is increasing the readiness, interoperability and geographical focus of the allocated forces in accordance with the requirements and character of the individual components

[256] The Czech Republic is contributing to the elements and measures of alliance deterrence, particularly on NATO's eastern flank

[257] The priority operational directions for the armed forces of the Czech Republic are Slovakia (where the Czech Republic makes a significant contribution to the NATO multinational battle group), Poland and Lithuania

[258] The Czech Republic also contributes to the NATO Allied Response Force

[259] The readiness of all such allocable forces and assets is regularly verified, including through their participation in joint exercises with allies

[260] The focus of the Czech Republic's contribution to the NATO defense system will be the brigade task force (BÚU) based on a mechanized brigade supplemented by elements of combat support and combat support

[261] The BÚU will be equipped in advance with appropriate supplies (especially ammunition), in which it is necessary to invest continuously, and will be sustainable in combat within the framework of an alliance collective defense operation

[262] The Czech Republic is developing light, medium and heavy capabilities of ground forces, including combat support and combat support, enabling them to operate in the entire spectrum of the expected operational deployment

[263] Emphasis is placed on the readiness and deployability of units and their ability to operate within the framework of the NATO multi-domain command and control system

[264] The forces will be sufficiently robust to be able to conduct a high-intensity operation with technologically advanced enemy forces

[265] The Czech Republic will ensure the protection of its airspace and contribute to the protection of the airspace of allied countries through a new generation multi-role supersonic aircraft, which will be capable of deployment in the environment of the future multi-domain and digital battlefield, within the framework of the alliance system NATINAMDS and coalition air operations

[266] Emphasis will also be placed on significantly strengthening ground-based air defense capabilities against traditional and new threats, such as swarms of unmanned aerial vehicles or hypersonic missiles

[267] Attention will be paid to both the early detection of threats and the development of a wide range of means capable of eliminating them

[268] In addition to building its own tactical airlift capability, the availability of strategic air and sealift capabilities will be ensured through multinational cooperation between allied countries

[269] The Czech Republic will further develop its air support capability for ground, special and territorial forces

[270] The Czech Republic will also be able to contribute to alliance systems for aerial reconnaissance, warning, command and control

[271] It will acquire tactical unmanned aerial vehicle capabilities independently or as part of multinational solutions

[272] The Czech Republic is building capabilities for operations in cyberspace, including capabilities that can be used in multi-domain operations and deployed for the benefit of NATO and the EU

[273] The Czech Republic will be capable of independent defense in cyberspace and will also contribute to the joint cyber defense systems of NATO and the EU

[274] The Czech Republic strives to acquire the capability of active cyber defense

[275] The Czech Republic is building the capability of effective defense of its territory

[276] The main goal is to ensure defense needs on the territory of the Czech Republic with an emphasis on securing the HNS and on protecting and defending objects important for the defense of the state and objects of possible attack

[277] The defense of the territory is also closely related to the ability to develop the armed forces into a wartime organizational structure and to ensure mobilization

[278] The Czech Republic is developing the capabilities of special forces, which contribute to managing threats of a conventional and unconventional nature on the territory of the Czech Republic and beyond

[279] They include the capabilities of special reconnaissance, intelligence and offensive activities and contribute to the fulfillment of tasks in the area of support and projection of influence

[280] The Czech Republic is building the capability to conduct information and psychological operations

[281] The Czech Republic has military intelligence capabilities that contribute to the comprehensive intelligence support of the state's defense functions through multi-source verified intelligence production

[282] This includes the provision of strategic and situational intelligence and is implemented in all relevant operational domains

[283] In order to protect its armed forces and to secure the armed forces of allied countries present on its territory, the Czech Republic is developing comprehensive military police capabilities

[284] These capabilities are intended both for operations on the territory of the Czech Republic and for independent performance of tasks abroad

[285] They are also a necessary prerequisite for the development of the armed forces into a wartime organizational structure

[286] Within the space operational domain, the Czech Republic is actively involved in international initiatives of the EU and NATO with an emphasis on ensuring situational awareness and protecting jointly operated and used satellite assets

[287] The Czech Republic is also building its own capabilities for satellite reconnaissance, navigation, communication and imaging from low Earth orbit

[288] A reliable, secure and resilient connection using satellite communications will be ensured for the armed forces of the Czech Republic, including deployed forces

[289] Where appropriate, the strengthening of the Czech Republic’s defence capability will reflect the impacts of climate change on the security and operational environment, the nature of conflicts and the operational procedures of the Czech and allied armed forces (including the future limited availability of vehicles using fossil fuels and the emergence of alternative energy sources

[290] Sufficiently capacious and modern real estate infrastructure is one of the necessary prerequisites for a number of military capabilities and other functions of the Czech defence system

[291] The real estate infrastructure in the Ministry of Defence will therefore be adequately developed, especially with regard to the long-term neglect of storage capacities and training and accommodation facilities in particular

[292] It is also essential that the state has secured ownership rights to military facilities

[293] The Ministry of Defence will therefore strive to settle ownership relations in military facilities

[294] To ensure the medical security of the armed forces of the Czech Republic, including their wartime deployment, and to ensure support for allied forces on the territory of the Czech Republic, military healthcare, including military hospitals, is being developed

[295] In a crisis situation, non-military healthcare providers may also be included in the medical security system in order to strengthen it

[296] A sufficient number of motivated, competent and well-prepared soldiers and civilian employees will operate in the Ministry of Defence and in the defence system of the Czech Republic as a whole

[297] Success in the current and future security environment can only be achieved thanks to educated and motivated personnel

[298] The importance of satellite communications for the defence of the Czech Republic is growing across the entire public administration, which will develop these capabilities on the basis of government (and commercially) provided services

[299] The Government will ensure competitive working conditions and career management in order to attract and retain qualified people of all necessary specializations, able to deal with new technologies and cooperate with partners in the Czech Republic and abroad

[300] The Ministry of Defence will use a more flexible system of military recruitment, which will allow a wider range of interested parties to join the armed forces

[301] The Czech Republic will prepare and send soldiers and civilian employees to international structures important for joint defense

[302] These include, in particular, the NATO and EU command structures and the NATO International Secretariat

[303] WAR DEVELOPMENT OF THE ARMED FORCES

[304] The Czech Republic is systematically creating the capability for war development of its armed forces, including the mobilization system, building the necessary infrastructure and acquiring the necessary supplies of material, armament and equipment for the entire war-developed structure of the armed forces

[305] The obligations of citizens of the Czech Republic in the area of mobilization, including conscription, are defined in the relevant legal regulations

[306] The Czech Republic is creating the conditions for the involvement of the Czech defense industry in the economic mobilization system, including ensuring production capacities, primarily at the level of the Czech Republic or in cooperation with NATO and EU countries and their industrial entities in ensuring security of supply

[307] The development of the mobilization system will reflect the shortened warning period before the outbreak of a conflict

[308] The size of the war-ready armed forces of the Czech Republic is continuously assessed and approved by the government

[309] The reserve system is being developed and strengthened, with regard to their scope, readiness and flexibility of use

[310] Following the update of the Concept of Mobilization of the Armed Forces of the Czech Republic, mobilization implementation plans will be revised

[311] Those steps to prepare for mobilization will be identified for which, due to the need for a rapid response, the possibility of their implementation outside of crisis situations is important

[312] With the increase in defense spending and the ongoing modernization of the armed forces, including the development of an active reserve, there is a need to gradually increase the allocation of funds to finance the needs related to the preparation and implementation of war deployment

[313] INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT

[314] The state must defend itself not only against current but also against future threats

[315] Most technical innovations are carried out in the private sector and in research institutions, often without a direct relationship to defense

[316] Therefore, as part of a societal effort, the Czech Republic is strengthening its ability to monitor and evaluate development trends and respond to the possibility of their military use and misuse (for example, in the case of artificial intelligence), introduce emerging and breakthrough technologies, and operate in new operational domains (cyber and space

[317] The Czech Republic will create conditions enabling the direct involvement of relevant state organizations and institutions in research and development projects led by private entities that are usable in the field of defense

[318] For this purpose, it is necessary to create an innovation infrastructure and eliminate the fragmentation of research and development activities within the Ministry of Defence

[319] The Czech Republic cooperates with the private and academic sectors and establishes appropriate partnership and contractual relationships with them

[320] It also directly supports research and development contributing to the achievement of the set goals

[321] It supports academic institutions and industrial entities, including small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups, in their efforts to participate in international initiatives

[322] In the field of innovation, the Ministry of Defence effectively uses the opportunities of international programmes

[323] Within NATO, these include in particular the defence innovation accelerator DIANA and the NATO Innovation Fund

[324] In the EU, these are mainly programmes of the European Defence Fund and relevant initiatives of the European Commission and the European Defence Agency

[325] The Czech Republic actively participates in the creation of international legal and ethical standards for the military use of new domains and technologies

[326] ROLE OF INDUSTRY

[327] The defence and security industry is one of the pillars of the defence system of the Czech Republic

[328] A developed and resilient national defence-industrial base is a prerequisite for promoting the security interests of the state and contributes to ensuring the operational needs of the armed forces

[329] It is therefore essential that the domestic defence and security industry is economically strong, technologically advanced, competitive and has the necessary capacities

[330] The Czech Republic will support the supply of material from the Czech defence and security industry to cover the needs of the armed forces

[331] Ensuring the security of supplies of military material is essential for the defence capability of the Czech Republic

[332] The complexity of supply chains can affect their fluidity and limit the deployability and sustainability of the armed forces

[333] The Czech Republic therefore supports increasing the competitiveness of the domestic defence and security industry with the aim of developing its ability to ensure both peacetime and crisis supplies of military material

[334] The Czech Republic supports the integration of domestic entities into international supply chains with an emphasis on eliminating technological and raw material dependencies on unreliable or hostile states

[335] In order to ensure the security of supplies, the Czech Republic also concludes strategic partnerships with necessary foreign suppliers

[336] The strategic and specific objectives of supporting the development of the defence industry are elaborated in the Strategy for Armament and Support for the Development of the Defence Industry of the Czech Republic until

[337] In areas where the domestic defence and security industry does not have the full range of necessary production and technological capacities, the Ministry of Defence will require the maximum possible involvement of domestic entities in research, development, production and in ensuring support for the life cycle of the procured material from foreign suppliers when procuring military material

[338] The Czech Republic strives to increase the industry's ability to respond flexibly to a rapid deterioration in the security situation

[339] Crisis production requires sufficient experienced personnel, access to raw materials including their stocks and energy supplies

[340] The Ministry of Defence maintains an overview of the production and technological capabilities of the defence and security industry and, in coordination with the State Material Reserves Administration, supports the creation of stocks of raw materials, components and technologies

[341] The Czech Republic will strive to link the requirements for supplies of military material outside of crisis situations with mobilization supplies

[342] The Czech Republic, in cooperation with other EU countries, strives to ensure that the defence and security industry is recognised as socially beneficial and has adequate access to public and private financial resources (loans, investments

[343] A necessary condition for maintaining the capabilities and further development of defence and security industry enterprises is their success on foreign markets

[344] The Czech Republic therefore supports the export activities of the defence and security industry, including through strengthened state support instruments

[345] The foreign policy interests and international obligations of the Czech Republic will be taken into account

[346] The Ministry of Defence develops state-owned enterprises under its jurisdiction to ensure security of supply, support the life cycle of military equipment and maintain and develop technical expertise

[347] They also play a significant role in ensuring support for the training of the armed forces

[348] The Ministry of Defence strives for the economic stability of its state-owned enterprises, but profit-making is not their main purpose

[349] Creating partnerships between state-owned enterprises and private entities can also be useful

[350] IMPLEMENTATION OF THE DEFENCE STRATEGY OF THE CR

[351] Effective implementation of the Strategy requires a whole-of-government and whole-of-society approach

[352] Only timely and continuous efforts will ensure adequate preparedness and the ability to respond to threats and crisis situations

[353] Ensuring defence is a long-term, never-ending process

[354] Defence planning must therefore be conducted with a long-term perspective

[355] Sufficient financial security is absolutely essential for the defence capability of the CR

[356] The Czech Republic will therefore spend at least 2% of GDP on its defense and, if necessary, will proceed to further increase defense spending above this level

[357] The Government will create the prerequisites for sufficient personnel, technical and organizational capacities that will enable optimal use of these resources

[358] Acquisition processes need to be simplified, also given the speed of technological development

[359] Individual elements of the Strategy will be elaborated in subsequent conceptual, implementation and planning documents of the Ministry of Defense

[360] These include in particular the Central State Defense Plan and other state defense plans, the Long-Term Defense Outlook, the Concept of Building the Army of the Czech Republic, the Concept of Operational Preparation of the State Territory, the Concept of Preparing Citizens for State Defense, the Concept of Mobilizing the Armed Forces of the Czech Republic, and the Strategy for Armament and Supporting the Development of the Defense Industry of the Czech Republic

[361] These documents will be updated as necessary in connection with this Strategy

[362] Specific tasks and measures for the Ministry of Defence department resulting from the Strategy and related conceptual and planning documents are implemented primarily through the regularly updated Medium-Term Plan of Activities and Development of the Ministry of Defence department

[363] Other relevant public administration bodies will implement the Strategy through their strategic, conceptual and implementation documents

[364] Within the framework of the interdepartmental working group for resolving legislative and legal issues in the field of defence of the Defence Planning Committee, an analysis of all relevant statutory and sub-statutory regulations related to ensuring defence, including the legal regulation of crisis situations, will be carried out

[365] In cases where their current wording is not in accordance with the needs of implementing this Strategy, their amendment will be proposed

[366] Implementation of the Strategy at the government-wide level will be regularly evaluated primarily through the Report on Ensuring the State's Defence and verified through national and alliance exercises

[367] Its implementation and evaluation will also be part of the implementation and evaluation of the Security Strategy of the Czech Republic

[368] In the event of a fundamental change in the basis, needs and objectives of the defence policy of the Czech Republic, the Strategy will be revised

[369] This Defence Strategy replaces the Defence Strategy of the Czech Republic, which was approved by the Government by Resolution No. 194 of 13 March
