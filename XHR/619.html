[1772] The Finnish Defence Forces is responsible for Finland’s military cyber defence and for its direction and implementation together with other authorities and sectors

[1773] Military mobility The capabilities and ability to move military troops, equipment and materiel quickly and flexibly to where they are needed

[1774] Military mobility requires, for example, the international harmonisation of the rules and procedures concerning cross-border military activities and the development of logistic infrastructure and capabilities to match the requirements for the deployment of troops and their support

[1775] Military strategic communications Military strategic communications (MILSTRATCOM) are part of the state leadership’s strategic communications

[1776] MILSTRATCOM are the coordinated influence activities by the Finnish Defence Forces against the situational awareness of intended target groups to attain military strategic objectives

[1777] Military strategic communications are coordinated with NATO’s strategic communications

[1778] The framework, services and related functions of the defence system and the critical infrastructure as well as the vital functions of society that are essential enablers of homeland defence at all levels of readiness

[1779] Multi-domain operations (MDO

[1780] The orchestration of activities across all services and operational domains

[1781] The five operational domains of warfare – land, sea, air, cyber and space – are increasingly interrelated

[1782] In MDO, military activities are synchronised with non-military activities across all operational domains and environments to create converging effects at the speed of relevance in the physical, cognitive and virtual dimensions

[1783] Information defence is implemented in all domains and dimensions

[1784] National cyber defence

[1785] The national and international military and civilian measures that safeguard the sovereignty of Finland and the living conditions of its population against external cyber threats and incidents by state actors and that execute necessary countermeasures at all levels of readiness

[1786] A key prerequisite for the preparedness and efficient execution of national cyber defence is close cooperation between public authorities and different actors in society

[1787] The Ministry of Defence is responsible for coordinating national cyber defence

[1788] Operations planning The planning of military operations at the strategic, operational or tactical levels

[1789] In NATO, the term ‘operational planning’ is not to be used so as to prevent confusion with operational-level planning

[1790] Prepositioning Storage of defence materiel, equipment or supplies under normal conditions in the territory of another NATO Ally

[1791] It speeds up access to materiel, equipment and supplies, enables quick force projection and ensures timely sustainment of forces when the level of readiness is being raised or under emergency conditions

[1792] Strategic communications Strategic communications mean open, systematic and proactive communications that aim to attain jointly agreed goals

[1793] Strategic communications are always a combination of verbal and visual messages and concrete acts and activities

[1794] Strategic communications are above all daily activities, but their importance is highlighted under emergency conditions or in situations with significant and far-reaching effects spanning across administrative boundaries

[1795] Total defence Total defence is a combination of all the national and international military and civilian measures to enable homeland defence in all security situations

[1796] Finland’s membership in NATO means that total defence support must be extended beyond the Finnish Defence Forces’ troops to Allied forces operating in the territory of Finland

[1797] The Ministry of Defence is responsible for coordinating total defence
