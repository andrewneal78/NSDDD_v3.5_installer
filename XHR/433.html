[7] President of the Republic of Belarus A. Lukashenko *** APPROVED Law of the Republic of Belarus dated 20.07.2016 No. 412-Z **MILITARY DOCTRINE OF THE REPUBLIC OF BELARUS** **SECTION I GENERAL PROVISIONS** **CHAPTER 1 FUNDAMENTALS OF THE MILITARY DOCTRINE

[8] The Military Doctrine of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter referred to as the Military Doctrine) is a system of views on ensuring its military security officially adopted in the Republic of Belarus

[9] The Military Doctrine defines the military-political, military-strategic and military-economic foundations of the military security of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter, unless otherwise specified, military security) at the present stage, including the attitude of the Republic of Belarus to military conflicts and their prevention, measures to ensure military security, the main directions of military development, the procedure for the use of military force for the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus

[10] The provisions of the Military Doctrine are based on the results of the assessment of the military-political situation in the world, including the European region, the forecast of its development in the medium term, scientific research in the field of armed protection of the Republic of Belarus, as well as the existing experience of military development and ensuring military security

[11] They are specified in the defense plan of the Republic of Belarus and other documents of strategic defense planning

[12] The legal basis of the Military Doctrine consists of the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, the Concept of National Security of the Republic of Belarus, other legislative acts, as well as generally recognized principles and norms of international law, international treaties of the Republic of Belarus

[13] CHAPTER 2 MAIN TERMS AND THEIR DEFINITIONS

[14] The Military Doctrine uses the following main terms and their definitions: * military security — the state of protection of the national interests of the Republic of Belarus from military threats; * military danger — the state of the military-political situation characterized by the interests, intentions, capabilities, actions of states (coalitions of states), as well as non-state entities, including terrorist and extremist organizations, which under certain geopolitical, military-strategic, socio-political and economic conditions may lead to a military conflict; * military organization of the state — a set of military-political governing bodies, the Armed Forces of the Republic of Belarus, other troops and military formations, paramilitary organizations of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter, unless otherwise indicated, the Armed Forces and other troops), the defense sector of the economy with its governing bodies, other state bodies and organizations, the joint activities of which are aimed at solving the problems of ensuring military security; * military policy of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter referred to as military policy) – activities of the state aimed at preparing the forces and means of its military organization, determining the procedure and methods of their use to ensure military security, including the prevention of military conflicts and the defense of the Republic of Belarus; * military threat – the highest level of military danger, at which the state of interstate or intrastate relations is characterized by the actions of another state (coalition of states), as well as non-state entities, including terrorist and extremist organizations, indicating a real possibility of a military conflict; * military development (development of the military organization of the state) – a system of legal, political, economic, scientific, technical, military, ideological, social, organizational and other measures carried out in the interests of developing the military organization of the state, increasing its military potential to ensure military security; * military-political situation – a set of conditions and factors resulting from the actions of states (coalitions of states), as well as non-state entities, including terrorist and extremist organizations, and characterizing the probability, intensity and scale of their use of military force (means of armed combat) in their own interests or to resolve emerging contradictions; * military conflict – a form of resolving contradictions in interstate or intrastate relations with the use of military force (means of armed combat) by the opposing parties: wars of various scales, international and internal armed conflicts, as well as other forms of use of military force; * war – a socio-political phenomenon, which is an extreme form of resolving political, economic, ideological, national, religious, territorial and other contradictions between states, peoples, nations and social groups

[15] To achieve the set goals in modern warfare, political, economic, ideological, military and other violent and non-violent means and corresponding forms of struggle are used; * armed conflict is one of the forms of resolution of interstate and intrastate contradictions through the limited use of military force by states (international armed conflict) or by opposing parties within the territory of one state (internal armed conflict), in which the state (state) participating (participating) in the conflict does not enter (does not enter) a state of war; * source of military threat is a factor or set of factors that can, under certain conditions, lead to an increase in military danger to the level of a military threat; * large-scale war is a global armed clash between coalitions (alliances, blocs) of states, covering a large part of the world's territory and affecting the interests of the entire world community; * local war is a war limited in political goals and scale, which is waged primarily between two neighboring states, covers a limited geographic area and affects mainly the interests of only the opposing parties (territorial, political, economic and others); * illegal armed formation — a controlled stable association of persons, at least one of whom has a weapon (with the knowledge of other persons included in this association), created for the purpose of committing actions aimed at the violent change of the constitutional order and (or) territorial integrity of the state, the seizure or retention of power by unconstitutional means, the organization of or participation in mass riots; * defense infrastructure — part of the infrastructure of the Republic of Belarus, which is a set of military infrastructure facilities and dual-use civilian infrastructure facilities, intended to ensure the preparation and implementation of the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus; * defense sector of the economy — an integral part of the economy of the Republic of Belarus, including organizations that have special permits (licenses) to carry out activities related to specific goods (works, services), and carry out scientific and (or) production activities to create products (perform work, provide services) for military purposes; * period of increasing military threat – the part of peacetime preceding wartime and characterized by a high intensity of contradictions in interstate or intrastate relations, intensified direct preparations of the opposing parties for war or armed conflict; * regional war – a war involving primarily three or more states, which is waged within a certain region (territory), usually by coalition armed forces (groups of troops (forces)) with decisive military-political goals; * strategic deterrence – a set of coordinated political, economic, scientific, technical, military, ideological, social and other measures taken by the state, aimed at deterring a potential enemy, demonstrating determination to defend the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus, and preventing military threats

[16] SECTION II MILITARY-POLITICAL FOUNDATIONS OF MILITARY SECURITY** **CHAPTER 3 MILITARY-POLITICAL SITUATION

[17] The current state of the military-political situation around the Republic of Belarus is characterized by a constant increase in the intensity and dynamism of ongoing processes

[18] The struggle of states (coalitions of states) for leading positions in the world, control over markets and management of the movement of raw materials has intensified

[19] The desire of geopolitical centers of power to expand their zones of strategic influence has led to the infringement and blocking of the interests of other states, provoking tensions and contributed to the intensification of interstate contradictions

[20] The construction and development of the architecture of a multipolar world order, along with the intensification of integration processes and the formation of new geopolitical centers of power, are accompanied by the strengthening of national, ethnic and religious extremism, separatism, leading to the emergence of non-state actors, including terrorist and extremist organizations that create and use armed formations

[21] Attempts by individual states (coalitions of states) to secure leadership without regard for the interests of all actors in international relations lead to the illegitimate appropriation by these states (coalitions of states) of the rights and functions of international organizations on matters of international peace and security through the use of military means

[22] The use of military force in violation of current norms of international law destabilizes the international situation

[23] At the same time, the use of diplomatic methods for conflict resolution and adherence to generally recognized principles and norms of international law do not become the dominant rules in resolving issues related to maintaining international peace and security

[24] The development of the military-political situation, caused by the desire of states (coalitions of states), as well as non-state actors, including terrorist and extremist organizations, to gain economic and resource advantages to advance their interests, is characterized by a tendency to provoke intrastate contradictions

[25] This is facilitated by the development by individual states (coalitions of states), as well as non-state actors, including terrorist and extremist organizations, of concepts and mechanisms for replacing the current government in other states using military force or violating their territorial integrity

[26] Attempts to interfere in the internal affairs of individual states, including European ones, have provoked internal armed conflicts involving the large-scale, comprehensive use of military force, both in traditional forms and by traditional means, and through sabotage (guerrilla) and terrorist methods of warfare

[27] These conflicts have a significant negative impact on global and regional security

[28] Scientific and technological progress determines the main trends in the development of the military-political situation

[29] The development of new types of weapons, including space and hypersonic, remotely controlled and robotic, provides previously unavailable opportunities for the comprehensive operational use of military force within a global reach

[30] The technological advantage of economically developed states in creating new types of weapons is a catalyst for the activities of individual countries to create "weapons of an adequate response" and leads to an increased possibility of proliferation of nuclear and other types of weapons of mass destruction (hereinafter referred to as WMD), their components and production technologies not only among states but also among non-state actors in international relations

[31] The use of information and communication and information and psychological methods and technologies for aggressive purposes is acquiring threatening proportions

[32] CHAPTER 4 MAIN MILITARY DANGERS

[33] The trends in the development of the military-political situation in their entirety expand the range of sources of military threats to the Republic of Belarus

[34] Objectively existing sources of military threats within the framework of the current military-political situation in the European region determine the presence of a military danger at the level of risks and challenges to military security

[35] The main external military dangers at the level of risks and challenges to the Republic of Belarus are recognized as

[36] the desire of individual states (coalitions of states) to resolve existing contradictions with the use of military force

[38] strengthening the offensive strike capabilities of states (coalitions of states), including the unilateral creation of strategic missile defense systems, high-precision weapons in non-nuclear warheads to strike troops (forces) and infrastructure of the Republic of Belarus in conditions of global reach, leading to a violation of the existing balance of power, as well as a build-up of military infrastructure in the territories of states adjacent to the Republic of Belarus; 11.5. reduction in the potential and capabilities of military-political alliances with the participation of the Republic of Belarus to ensure collective security

[39] the emergence of hotbeds of armed conflict and their escalation with the involvement of special operations forces, private military companies and illegal armed groups in the territories of states adjacent to the Republic of Belarus, as a result of practicing mechanisms for an unconstitutional method of changing the current state power; 11.7. reduction in the periods of readiness for the use of military formations (including coalition ones) located in the territories of states adjacent to the Republic of Belarus, allowing states (coalitions of states) to quickly create strike groups of troops (forces) for actions against the Republic of Belarus; 11.8. build-up of the scale of operational and combat training measures of the armed forces of other states (coalitions of states) of an offensive nature near the State Border of the Republic of Belarus, carried out without prior notification to the Republic of Belarus, with the practicing of issues of planning and conducting large-scale military operations in the European region

[40] The creation and functioning of special paramilitary formations in states (coalitions of states) to conduct activities in the information space to exert destructive information influence on the population, state and military command and control bodies, and infrastructure of the Republic of Belarus

[41] The activities of states (coalitions of states), as well as non-state entities, including terrorist and extremist organizations, to prepare illegal armed formations to destabilize the situation in the Republic of Belarus

[42] Discrimination against the rights and legitimate interests of citizens of the Republic of Belarus in other states

[43] The introduction by other states (coalitions of states) of political and economic sanctions, an embargo on the supply of weapons (systems), military and special equipment (hereinafter referred to as WMSE) and their components to the Republic of Belarus in order to exert pressure on the defense sector of the economy

[44] The main internal military dangers at the level of risks and challenges to the Republic of Belarus are recognized as

[45] weakening of the sense of patriotism in society, readiness of citizens of the Republic of Belarus for armed defense of the Republic of Belarus

[46] significant increase in the crime rate on the territory of the Republic of Belarus, growth of illegal migration into the country and transit of illegal migrants through its territory, facilitating the illegal trafficking of weapons and ammunition that can be used to commit acts of terrorism and other illegal actions

[47] creation of terrorist and extremist organizations on the territory of the Republic of Belarus, organization of their training of their members for action as part of illegal armed formations to commit acts of terrorism and other illegal actions to destabilize the situation in the Republic of Belarus, including for the purpose of unleashing an internal armed conflict

[48] provoking interethnic and social tension, extremism, inciting ethnic and religious hatred or enmity

[49] reduction of the capabilities of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter referred to as the Armed Forces) to strategically deter aggression and solve other peacetime tasks, as well as to provide armed defense of the Republic of Belarus in the event of military actions being unleashed against it

[50] Under certain conditions, the escalation of negative trends in the military-political situation may lead to an increase in military danger to the level of a military threat to the Republic of Belarus, while military threats to the Republic of Belarus are

[51] concentration of armed forces of another state (coalition of states) along the State border of the Republic of Belarus, indicating a real intent to use military force against the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus

[52] emergence of hotbeds of armed conflicts directed against the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus

[55] CHAPTER 5 FUNDAMENTALS OF MILITARY POLICY

[56] In proclaiming the Military Doctrine, which is purely defensive in nature, the Republic of Belarus proceeds from the fact that no state is its adversary

[57] The Republic of Belarus condemns any military conflict as a means of implementing its policy and adheres to the principle of peaceful settlement of disputes

[58] At the same time, the Republic of Belarus will defend its national interests using all available means, including through the use of military force, and reserves the right to adopt a set of preventive measures of strategic deterrence in order to prevent an attack or neutralize an internal armed conflict

[59] The use of military force is considered an extreme measure after exhausting all possibilities of taking political, diplomatic, legal, economic, informational, ideological and other measures to ensure military security

[60] At the same time, the Republic of Belarus reserves the right, in case of an attack, to seek assistance, including military, from the member states of the Collective Security Treaty Organization (hereinafter referred to as the CSTO) and other states in accordance with international treaties of the Republic of Belarus

[61] The Republic of Belarus will consider as its adversary a state (coalition of states), as well as any non-state entity, including terrorist and extremist organizations, whose activities pose a military threat to the Republic of Belarus, lead to interference in the internal affairs, an encroachment on the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus

[62] The objectives of the military policy are

[63] protection of the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus; 17.2. ensuring military security

[64] Strengthening global and regional security, internal political stability in the state in order to prevent military conflicts

[65] Development of the military organization of the state, maintaining its readiness for armed defense of the Republic of Belarus in any conditions, improvement of the forms and methods of using the military organization of the state

[66] The Republic of Belarus in its relations with other states proceeds from the principles of sovereign equality of states, non-interference in internal affairs, and other generally recognized principles and norms of international law

[67] Implementing an independent military policy in accordance with its national interests, the Republic of Belarus: 18.1. pursues a peaceful foreign policy and advocates the resolution of all interstate disputes exclusively through negotiations and the use of other methods of their peaceful settlement

[68] respects the inviolability of borders and territorial integrity, does not put forward any territorial claims against other states and does not recognize such claims on the part of other states

[69] Respects the political independence and state sovereignty of other states, recognizes their right to resolve issues of ensuring national security in accordance with their interests without prejudice to the security of other states

[70] Complies with the established levels of armament and the number of servicemen of the Armed Forces, facilitates the implementation of control over military activities in the European region within the framework of the international obligations of the Republic of Belarus

[71] excludes unilateral reduction of military potential, allowing it only taking into account objective political and economic feasibility to ensure the necessary level of its defense capability

[72] The Republic of Belarus considers its participation in the activities of the United Nations (hereinafter referred to as the UN) as the basis for improving mechanisms for ensuring global security

[73] As a member of the Organization for Security and Cooperation in Europe, the Republic of Belarus advocates the development of a regional security system taking into account the interests of all states based on the principles of trust and mutual understanding

[74] While maintaining good-neighborly and mutually beneficial relations with the European Union and partnership with the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, the Republic of Belarus strives to build an equal dialogue, increase openness and develop mutual understanding in the context of strengthening regional security

[75] The Republic of Belarus considers the strengthening of the collective security system and further development of relations to be the main priority areas in its coalition military policy

[76] with the Russian Federation within the framework of the Treaty on the Establishment of the Union State of 8 December 1999 — on issues of maintaining the necessary military potential, taking joint measures to prevent military threats to the Union State and repel aggression in the common defense space, ensuring the functioning of the regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation; 20.2. with the CSTO member states within the framework of the Collective Security Treaty of 15 May 1992 — on issues of development and use of collective security forces, creation of joint military systems, development of the CSTO peacekeeping potential and strengthening the CSTO status in the international arena as a whole

[77] with the member states of the Commonwealth of Independent States — on issues of ensuring regional security, military cooperation, and development of joint (combined) systems

[78] with other states with which the Republic of Belarus has concluded bilateral international treaties on the development of strategic partnership

[79] CHAPTER 6 MEASURES TO ENSURING MILITARY SECURITY

[80] In order to ensure military security, the Republic of Belarus shall determine and implement a set of measures aimed at preventing military threats, strengthening and effectively functioning the military organization of the state

[84] formation and implementation of a consistent peaceful military policy of the state, putting forward and supporting initiatives aimed at creating effective systems for ensuring global and regional security, taking into account the interests of all subjects of international relations, development of a belt of good-neighborliness in the military and political dimensions along the perimeter of the State Border of the Republic of Belarus

[88] Implementing state policy to strengthen in society a sense of patriotism and readiness to protect the national interests of the Republic of Belarus, fostering a conscious attitude among citizens of the Republic of Belarus to its armed defense, social protection of military personnel (employees, staff, workers) of the structural components of the military organization of the state, persons dismissed from military service (from service in paramilitary organizations), and their family members

[110] SECTION III MILITARY-STRATEGIC PRINCIPLES OF MILITARY SECURITY** **CHAPTER 7 THE NATURE OF MODERN MILITARY CONFLICTS

[111] Modern military conflicts that may be unleashed against the Republic of Belarus or in which the Republic of Belarus may be involved are classified as follows: * large-scale war; * regional war; * local war; * international armed conflict; * internal armed conflict

[112] Before the start of a military conflict in which the Republic of Belarus may be involved, one should expect: * the adoption by the enemy of measures to shape international public opinion in support of external intervention; * increasing political, diplomatic, economic and information pressure, intensifying the actions of destructive forces in order to provoke social tensions among the population and destabilize the situation in the Republic of Belarus

[113] The common features of modern military conflicts are

[114] non-obligation to declare war for unleashing and waging military conflicts

[115] reducing the time required to prepare for waging war, the rapidity and increasing intensity of military operations; 28.3. active use by the warring parties of non-military measures, primarily political, diplomatic, economic, informational and ideological ones

[116] waging information warfare, which is an integral part of military conflicts

[117] increasing the role of special operations forces and other special military formations in the integrated use of regular troops (forces) with irregular armed formations, including illegal armed formations, private military companies, terrorist and extremist organizations

[118] The widespread use of sabotage (guerrilla) and terrorist methods of warfare

[119] Conducting military operations primarily in urbanized areas in order to establish control over populated areas

[120] The desire of the warring parties to disrupt the systems of state and military command and control

[121] The use of highly effective precision weapons systems with non-nuclear warheads, including those based on the new use of physical principles, providing for the delivery of rapid strikes within global reach and ensuring the destruction of troops (forces), rear facilities, the economy, and communications throughout the enemy's territory

[122] Catastrophic consequences of the damage to (disruption of functioning) of critically important infrastructure facilities of the Republic of Belarus, including energy facilities, chemical and other hazardous industries, and life support systems

[123] A large-scale war may result from the escalation of a local or regional war due to the involvement of the majority of states in various regions of the world

[124] In a large-scale war, the states (coalitions of states) participating in it will pursue radical military-political goals

[125] In a regional war, in which the Republic of Belarus may be involved as a result of the escalation of an internal or international armed conflict, a local war, military operations will be carried out, as a rule, by coalition groupings of troops (forces

[126] A local war, in which the Republic of Belarus may be involved, may result from the gradual escalation of both an internal and international armed conflict or begin as a result of a surprise attack by another state

[127] An international armed conflict, in which the Republic of Belarus may be involved, is possible as a result of interference by another state (other states) in its internal affairs, attempts to resolve territorial claims to part of the territory of the Republic of Belarus by military force

[128] An international armed conflict may result from provocative actions of armed formations of another state (coalition of states) in the border territory of the Republic of Belarus

[129] An internal armed conflict in the Republic of Belarus may result from an escalation of internal military threats by terrorist and extremist organizations, a sharp destabilization of the situation in the Republic of Belarus due to the aggravation of internal political, national-ethnic and religious contradictions, including those inspired from outside

[130] In all types of military conflicts that may be unleashed against the Republic of Belarus or in which it may be involved, the Republic of Belarus will pursue the goal of preserving the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty, constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus and the termination of military actions on terms that do not contradict the national interests of the Republic of Belarus

[131] Taking into account the nature of each type of military conflict, the goal will be achieved: 34.1. in a regional (large-scale) war - by defeating the enemy; repelling his attack as part of a regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation, coalition groupings of troops (forces) of the CSTO member states; 34.2. in a local war - localizing the hotbed of tension; inflicting maximum damage on the enemy; forcing him to abandon the continuation of the armed invasion of the territory of the Republic of Belarus

[132] in an international armed conflict - by demonstrating determination to defend the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus, the inviolability of the State Border of the Republic of Belarus; neutralizing the military threat; blocking the area of possible military operations (actions of a provocative nature); preventing the capture by the enemy of critically important infrastructure facilities of the Republic of Belarus (delivering strikes on them); defeating the armed formations of the enemy participating in the armed clash, as well as in the attack; preventing the armed conflict from escalating into a local or regional war; 34.4. in an internal armed conflict - by ensuring special legal regimes introduced in the state; localizing hotbeds of tension and blocking areas of military operations; defeating the enemy, including armed formations from illegal armed formations, private military companies, terrorist and extremist organizations; preventing the capture by the enemy of critically important infrastructure facilities of the Republic of Belarus, including for the purpose of disrupting the work of the state and military command and control systems, seizing weapons and ammunition; stabilizing the situation in the Republic of Belarus; preventing the escalation of an armed conflict into a local or regional war

[133] CHAPTER 8 MILITARY ORGANIZATION OF THE STATE AND THE PRINCIPLES OF ITS APPLICATION

[134] The achievement by the Republic of Belarus of its goals in military conflicts should they occur, as well as the implementation of measures to ensure military security in peacetime, including during periods of increasing military threat, are ensured by an effectively functioning military organization of the state, which forms the basis of its defense potential

[135] The military organization of the state is intended: * in peacetime, including during periods of increasing military threat, to solve the problems of ensuring military security, minimizing the risks and challenges to military security, preventing a military threat, preventing destabilization of the situation or resolving an internal armed conflict in the Republic of Belarus; * in wartime - to ensure the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus and achieve the goal defined in paragraph 34 of the Military Doctrine

[136] The President of the Republic of Belarus - Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces exercises general leadership over the preparation and use of the military organization of the state in order to ensure military security

[137] The Security Council of the Republic of Belarus is the highest collegial coordinating and political body created for the purpose of implementing the powers of the President of the Republic of Belarus as the Head of State and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces in the field of national security

[138] The jurisdiction of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus includes consideration of domestic and foreign policy issues affecting the interests of national security and defense of the Republic of Belarus, as well as adoption of fundamental (most important) decisions on ensuring military security

[139] The working body of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus is the State Secretariat of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus, which provides information and analytical, legal, organizational and technical support for its activities

[140] With the imposition of martial law, the General Staff of the Armed Forces performs the functions of the executive body of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus in the system of strategic command and control of the Armed Forces and other troops under the direct leadership of the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces

[141] The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus resolves issues and is responsible for comprehensively meeting the needs of the Armed Forces and other troops for the necessary financial, material and other resources for the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus, organizes the equipping of the Armed Forces and other troops with weapons and special equipment and other material assets and resources upon their orders, manages civil defense, directs republican government bodies, local executive and administrative bodies and other state organizations in the preparation of the economy of the Republic of Belarus for operation in a wartime mode and conditions, as well as their mobilization deployment, organizes the development and formation of the state defense order, mobilization plans for the economy and its transition to operation in wartime conditions

[142] Government bodies determine the values of indicators of the state of national security of the Republic of Belarus in the military sphere and, in accordance with the possible levels of military danger, take measures to ensure military security

[143] Local executive and administrative bodies, within the limits of their authority, shall implement a set of measures to prevent destabilization of the situation in the region, carry out direct preparation and management of the territorial defense of the zone (district) and civil defense in the corresponding territory, determine the volumes of budgetary appropriations for the needs of territorial and civil defense, create the necessary reserves of material resources, ensure their accumulation, storage and timely renewal, carry out measures, when martial law is introduced, to transfer subordinate and other organizations, the corresponding territories to work in wartime conditions, ensure the implementation of mobilization plans, assist military commissariats in mobilization work in peacetime and when mobilization is declared

[144] The goal of military development is the creation of an effective military organization of the state, which in peacetime is guaranteed to solve the problems of ensuring military security, and in wartime ensures the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus and the achievement of political goals in a military conflict

[145] The principles of functioning of the military organization of the state, its composition and structure shall be determined in the process of military development by the relevant regulatory legal acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus and specified in the tasks of the structural components of the military organization of the state established by legislative acts

[146] The main areas of development of the military organization of the state at the present stage are

[147] improvement of the regulatory legal framework for the development of the military organization of the state and its application

[148] increasing the efficiency of functioning of the system of state and military administration in various conditions of the military-political situation

[149] optimization of the structure, composition and number of structural components of the military organization of the state in accordance with the tasks of ensuring military security and the economic capabilities of the state

[150] improvement of the system of economic support for the functioning of the military organization of the state

[151] improving the quality of training of military personnel (employees, employees, workers) of the structural components of the military organization of the state to perform tasks for their intended purpose; 39.6. improvement of the system of manning the Armed Forces and other troops; 39.7. improving ideological work in order to achieve and maintain high morale among military and civilian personnel, instilling in citizens of the Republic of Belarus a sense of patriotism, strengthening the rule of law, law and order and military (labor) discipline among military personnel (employees, staff, workers) of the structural components of the military organization of the state

[152] improving the systems for planning and applying the military organization of the state

[153] strengthening the material and technical base, developing weapons and special equipment, increasing the efficiency of the systems for producing, purchasing, operating, repairing and servicing weapons and special equipment, and modernizing them; 39.10. improving the system of information and scientific support for decisions made in the sphere of military security; 39.11. improving territorial and civil defense

[154] enhancing the prestige of military service and preparing citizens of the Republic of Belarus for it

[155] The Armed Forces and other troops, which are the security component of the military organization of the state, as part of the implementation of a set of measures for the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus, must be ready

[156] solve problems as part of the state system of response to acts of terrorism, the activities of terrorist organizations, illegal armed groups and mass riots

[157] carry out a set of deterrent, preventive and preparatory measures to increase their readiness for armed defense of the Republic of Belarus in any conditions of the military-political situation in accordance with the level of military danger

[158] in the event of an internal or international armed conflict or a local war - ensure stabilization of the situation in the Republic of Belarus, conduct active military operations in order to preserve the independence, territorial integrity, sovereignty and constitutional order of the Republic of Belarus

[159] in the event of an outbreak of a regional or large-scale war - conduct joint military operations as part of coalition groupings of troops (forces) and ensure the creation of conditions for ending the war (concluding peace) on terms that do not contradict the national interests of the Republic of Belarus

[160] In order to strengthen the protection of the State Border of the Republic of Belarus, the defense of objects of strategic importance, participation in maintaining martial law, conducting joint combat operations with the Armed Forces and other troops, as well as to carry out other measures for the armed defense of the Republic of Belarus, territorial defense shall be organized

[161] The objectives of territorial defense are: * increasing the level of the state's defense capability; * giving the fight against the enemy a universal (nationwide) character; * participation in ensuring the stable functioning of state bodies and organizations, industries and facilities of the economy, and the infrastructure of the Republic of Belarus in wartime conditions; * increasing the effectiveness of the Armed Forces and other troops

[162] The composition, tasks of the territorial defense forces, the procedure for their use and interaction shall be determined by the legislation of the Republic of Belarus

[163] The armed defense of the Republic of Belarus in the event of military conflicts unleashed against it shall be organized and carried out in accordance with the documents of strategic planning for the defense of the Republic of Belarus, as well as on the basis of directives of the President of the Republic of Belarus - Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces and other regulatory legal acts

[164] The issues of organizing the use of military force by the Republic of Belarus for the purpose of ensuring the military security of the Union State and its armed defense shall be determined by international treaties of the Republic of Belarus concluded with the Russian Federation and shall be resolved within the framework of the regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation

[165] In order to command the regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation, a collegial military command body shall be created by decision of the Supreme State Council of the Union State during a period of increasing military threat – the Joint Command of the Regional Grouping of Troops (Forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation

[166] The issues of organizing the military security of the Republic of Belarus and the Union State during a period of increasing military threat, including the creation of a collegial military command body, shall be regulated by the legislation of the Republic of Belarus and the regulatory legal acts of the Union State

[167] State bodies and other organizations that are not part of the military organization of the state, as well as citizens of the Republic of Belarus, participate in ensuring military security in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Belarus

[176] improvement of the system of assessment, planning and financing of the military needs of the state within the framework of a comprehensive system of strategic planning for national security based on the parameters of macroeconomic forecasting of the country's socio-economic development

[177] efficient use of the capabilities of the state defense procurement system, taking into account market mechanisms

[178] optimization of the costs of financial, material and other resources directed at ensuring military security

[179] preparation of the economic management system for sustainable functioning both in peacetime and in wartime

[184] mobilization preparation of the economy of the Republic of Belarus is consistent with the systems of mobilization deployment of troops (forces), territorial and civil defense in order to increase the sustainability of the functioning of industries and facilities of the economy, timely deployment of production of military and civilian products in accordance with mobilization plans, while ensuring

[185] regulation of financial and economic relations, including the functioning of the financial and credit and budget and tax systems, entrepreneurial and economic activities

[186] Maintaining the economic potential of the Republic of Belarus, which allows it to sufficiently meet the needs of the military organization of the state and the needs of the population in wartime

[187] Redistribution of financial, material and other resources necessary to solve the problems of the military organization of the state and the stable functioning of the economy in wartime conditions

[188] Creation of special formations intended for use in wartime in the interests of the economy and defense of the Republic of Belarus

[189] Preparation and organization of the rationed supply of citizens of the Republic of Belarus with basic food and other essential goods

[190] In order to create, maintain and develop defense infrastructure, timely strategic deployment of the Armed Forces, bringing other troops and military formations, paramilitary organizations of the Republic of Belarus to readiness to perform tasks for their intended purpose, ensuring the use of the Armed Forces and other troops, operational equipment of the territory of the state is carried out under the leadership of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus

[191] The Armed Forces and other troops, state bodies and organizations, in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Belarus, shall create and maintain reserves of material resources in peacetime for the mobilization deployment of troops (forces) and the conduct of combat operations by them

[193] Planning for the training of military-trained reserves, their military registration and registration of vehicles transferred to the Armed Forces and other troops during their mobilization deployment shall be carried out and supervised by the General Staff of the Armed Forces in close cooperation with state bodies and organizations
