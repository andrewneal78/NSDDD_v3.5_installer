[130] The concept of Total Defence

[131] The current situation has made it necessary to adapt the Total Defence concept to meet the challenges posed by the new threat picture

[132] The security of society has become a more prominent consideration, and the traditional Throughout the post-war period, Norway’s defence was based on the concept of «Total Defence»

[133] Fundamentally, this means being able, if necessary, to mobilise the full range of resources available to Norwegian society in order to defend the homeland and for the management of acute security situations that might arise in war or under the threat of war

[134] Total Defence concept has had to be revised in the light of this development

[135] The Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Justice and the Police have consequently reviewed and refined the concept

[136] The new Total Defence concept is essentially based on mutual support and cooperation between the defence organisation, with its military arm, and civil society

[137] This applies across the whole spectrum of crisis, from peacetime incidents, through security crises of whatever magnitude, to a war situation

[138] It is no longer necessary for the emergency powers legislation to be invoked

[139] The Total Defence concept includes mutual support and cooperation between military and civil communities in relation to preventive measures, emergency planning and operational matters

[140] The principle of civil assistance for the Armed Forces in situations of crisis or war remains unchanged

[141] The new Total Defence concept increases the emphasis on military aid to the civil authorities

[142] Service units can provide support for the civil community in the context of serious incidents

[143] A guiding principle is that the armed services will contribute all available capabilities, skills and resources with which they have been provided for the performance of their primary tasks

[144] protection Today, at a time when there is no evident military threat, work being done in the area of civil protection is focused on the protection of the civil community, including vital public functions and material assets

[145] The aim is to make Norway a safe and secure country to live in

[146] Current civil protection work is therefore based on coordinated and prioritised use of the full range of resources available

[147] This work on civil protection affects every sector of society and every form of business

[148] It has its basis in the principles of responsibility, locality and normality

[149] The principle of responsibility means that whoever has responsibility in a normal situation also has responsibility in the event of an extraordinary incident or situation

[150] The principle of locality means that any crisis should be handled at the lowest possible level

[151] And the principle of normality means that society must be able to function as normally as possible even in extraordinary situations, and with normal lines of responsibility unchanged
