[1] MINISTRY OF DEFENCE STRATEGY 2025 Securely into the future

[2] SECURELY INTO THE FUTURE The Ministry of Defence Strategy 2025 “Securely into the future” has three objectives

[3] It makes a concise assessment of the long-term development in our security environment

[4] On the basis of the assessment, the Strategy describes the future challenges facing Finnish defence policy and military defence

[5] Thirdly, it explores the means by which these challenges can be met

[6] By this publication we hope to increase awareness of the key points in our military defence

[7] The Strategy is based on the strategic planning process of the Ministry of Defence, with representatives of the Defence Forces also participating

[8] Long-term strategic planning is done simultaneously with mid- and short-term planning

[9] Government Reports on Finnish Security and Defence Policy (White Papers) are submitted every four years

[10] Choices described in these reports influence long-term planning

[11] The Strategic Plan, for its part, serves the preparatory work for the White Paper by clarifying the picture vis-à-vis future development options

[12] The cornerstones of Strategy 2025 are the common values of the Ministry of Defence and the Defence Forces: Security, reliability, credibility and patriotism

[13] It is on these values that we will proceed into the future

[14] Security** means safeguarding the functions vital to our society

[15] We defend the entire territory of our nation and participate in preventing and repelling internal security threats

[16] Furthermore, we participate in international cooperation and military crisis management

[17] Reliability**, for us, implies that in all situations we act as part of a democratic society and carry out the decisions of the state leadership

[18] Citizens, the state leadership and cooperation partners, domestic and foreign, as well as our own personnel must be able to rely on the Ministry of Defence and the Defence Forces

[19] Credibility** stands for the credibility of our defence capability

[20] This is the most important factor with regard to preventing external security threats to Finland as well as maintaining our defence will

[21] Patriotism** means that we work for our nation’s future while respecting the sacrifices of previous generations

[22] We want to ensure that future generations will also be prosperous, safe and secure, and able to make independent decisions in the constantly changing international security environment

[23] Regardless of how our security environment develops, Finland will have to respond to several challenges

[24] Our international dependence will increase

[25] This expanding interdependence will result in the increased significance of cooperation

[26] In the future, Finland will engage in cooperation ever more broadly, both at home and abroad

[27] The age structure of the Finnish population is experiencing great change

[28] People age and this, too, influences the resources available for our defence

[29] The ever-intensifying technological development has a similar effect as well

[30] New technologies provide many opportunities, but the flipside is that the price of defence materiel is rapidly increasing

[31] In spite of growing collaboration and increasing interdependence, the foundation of Finnish defence will remain national

[32] We Finns will remain responsible for the defence of our own country, regardless of future defence solutions

[33] The solid core of Finland’s defence will always be Finnish

[34] By publishing this paper the Finnish Ministry of Defence aims to spur the debate regarding the future of our military defence

[35] An active debate on the justifications of defence solutions is part of a genuine democratic process

[109] International conflicts multiply Recent history testifies to the fact that single dramatic events and chains of events, such as the fall of the Berlin wall, terrorist strikes in the United States and in Europe or disasters, such as a tsunami, can amount to seminal events that influence the security environment

[496] Total defence** The use of all military and civilian measures to safeguard Finland’s independence and the livelihood and security of her citizens against external threats, posed by states or by others

[498] Wide-ranging security threats** Threats which cause severe danger to the population or serious or significant interruptions to society’s vital functions

[501] They usually represent a component of military threats or are linked to them
