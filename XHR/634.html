[104] Corporal Nicole Dorrett Our Key Activities In 2022, Defence commenced a major transformation of performance measurement and reporting

[105] Our new approach improves the clarity of Defence performance information and creates a clear line of sight between our mission, outcomes, strategic objectives, the key activities we undertake to deliver on them and the performance measures and targets that help us determine whether we are successful

[106] In the 2022–26 Defence Corporate Plan, we are introducing seven new key activities and ten performance measures

[107] This streamlined approach will improve the transparency and focus of our performance reporting to the Australian Government and the Australian people, replacing a system that included 76 activities, 21 performance criteria and 63 measures of success

[108] Our new key activities are: **Key Activity 1: Conduct operations and deployments to defend and advance Australia’s national interests** The Australian Government deploys Defence personnel to operations overseas and within Australia to defend and promote Australia and its national interests

[109] National and international security are whole-of-nation commitments

[110] To deliver on Key Activity 1, Defence also works closely with international and whole-of-government partners and state and territory agencies to respond to disasters and emergencies at home and abroad

[111] Key Activity 2: Be a strategy-led organisation** Defence is a strategy-led organisation

[112] Australian Government endorsed direction sets the strategic objectives

[113] The Defence Strategy Framework, endorsed by the Secretary of Defence and the Chief of the Defence Force, informs how strategy cascades through the organisation

[114] The classified Defence Planning Guidance, released annually, is the primary strategic guidance document within Defence

[115] It helps the organisation translate Government direction into action

[116] Key Activity 3: Enable intelligence-informed strategic policy and international operations** Classified intelligence assessments and products inform Defence strategic policy and international operations

[117] This ensures that Defence’s activities are grounded in an intelligence-based understanding of drivers, capabilities and likelihood

[118] Key Activity 4: Invest in Defence people** Defence’s integrated workforce brings together Australian Defence Force members, Australian Public Servants and contractors

[119] Defence invests in building and maintaining an environment that attracts and retains a highly skilled, agile and resilient workforce with the necessary skills and capabilities to meet Australia’s future defence and national security needs

[120] Key Activity 5: Promote regional and global security and stability** Defence contributes to the promotion of regional and global security and stability

[121] Defence does this through steady and consistent international engagement anchored in our resolve to safeguard our national interests and support a rules-based order

[122] Defence diplomacy and cooperation focuses on enhancing Australia's ability to work with partners in response to common security challenges, particularly in the Indo-Pacific region

[123] Key Activity 6: Deliver future capability The Australian Government is providing Defence $270 billion over the decade to 2029–30 to deliver capabilities across all domains, including Information, Cyber, Maritime, Air, Space and Land, supported by critical enabling capabilities

[124] This capability will provide the Government with a flexible range of options to deliver on Defence's strategic objectives

[125] Key Activity 7: Advance Australia’s prosperity Defence will grow and sustain its military capability and, through this, contribute to the Australian economy

[126] The significant investment the Australian Government is making in Defence has the potential to have positive impacts on Australia’s prosperity, supporting new and existing industries

[127] Our new performance measures and targets are detailed in the Our Performance section and will be included in the October 2022 Portfolio Budget Statements
