[176] Defense constitutes the external facet of Security, and therefore the Ministries of Foreign Affairs and National Defense operate in this area

[177] However, in those situations where the competent national bodies so decide, they will support the internal security forces

[178] Security is a concept that encompasses Defense

[214] The end of the Cold War and the Soviet bloc, and the progressive resolution of differences in our country's immediate surroundings, seem to indicate a disappearance of threats to Uruguay's peace and security

[234] Defense Policy and Foreign Policy The Defense Policy of a country must necessarily be related to its Foreign Policy, since both complement each other

[235] Uruguay has a clear commitment to peace, and through its defense policy it seeks only to protect national interests, without posing a threat to other countries

[236] The fundamental principles of national foreign policy are respect for treaties, non-interference in the internal affairs of other states, respect for international law, the peaceful settlement of disputes, the use of force only as a defense against aggression, and the self-determination of peoples

[237] Our Defense Policy is based on an Armed Forces structured and sized to ensure a reasonably deterrent effect, and on a foreign service that acts preemptively to achieve a peaceful resolution to any disputes that may arise, within the framework of an appropriate and timely preemptive strategy

[238] Within these guidelines, it will be necessary to ensure perfectly defined and internationally recognized boundaries, and to maintain close relations with neighboring countries and the international community, based on mutual trust and respect

[243] DEFENSE POLICY The National Defense Policy establishes the general guidelines to achieve the National Defense Objectives

[244] It coordinates political, economic, military, social, and scientific-technological factors in order to address threats to the country's external security

[248] Ultimately, it is about establishing how external agents will be prevented, through threats or force, from affecting the primary interests of the Nation
