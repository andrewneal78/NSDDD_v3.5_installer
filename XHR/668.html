FUNDAMENTALS OF THE NATIONAL SECURITY AND DEFENCE STRATEGY In a globalised world, the quality of the national strategy is crucial for the survival of a modern state and an open society

[48] National values and interests are defined by the National Constitution and by the National Defence Law

[49] They produce international commitments for the state, namely the United Nations Charter, the North Atlantic Treaty and the EU treaties
