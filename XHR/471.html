[87] INTERNATIONAL SCENARIO

[88] Security Environment

[89] With the end of the Cold War, the challenges arising from globalization, the resurgence of terrorism, the development of ICTs, the global economic situation, and other factors, dramatically reconfigured global geopolitics, allowing the emergence of new dynamics and consequently the emergence of new concerns for the various actors in the international political arena

[90] The new context resulting from these changes has altered the geopolitical landscape globally, not allowing for the reduction, as desired, of the possibility of a military confrontation between the great powers, despite the continued opening of international trade and the world's main financial centers to new actors, and has also provided a relative increase in interdependence and dialogue between countries

[91] Africa does not escape these global dynamics, despite its specificities

[92] The low level of socioeconomic development and the shortcomings of the internal policies pursued by some African states have contributed to the deterioration of the living conditions of the populations of a significant number of countries on the continent

[93] Simultaneously with this worrying scenario, a wave of popular uprisings has emerged across the planet, particularly in some countries in North Africa and the Middle East, whose objectives have been aimed at changing the established order there

[94] These revolts, contrary to the aims promoted by their mentors, further degraded the already difficult political, military, and socioeconomic situation of the targeted countries, which demonstrates that it is not the best path to achieving power

[95] In Sub-Saharan Africa, the arbitrary delimitation of borders imposed by colonial powers, ethno-religious fundamentalism and the lack of viable policies aimed at the populations have been identified as the main causes of the almost exponential growth of inter- and intrastate conflicts, leaving many of them in a situation of "near bankruptcy", a factor that has been exploited by criminal organizations and religious fundamentalism, as a base of support to destabilize them

[96] In this context, new external and internal dynamics determine a new model for Angola's insertion in the international arena and force the Angolan Armed Forces to assume new responsibilities, compatible with the level of political ambition of our country

[97] Angola has defined political strategies aimed at the geopolitical spaces of its interest, which aim to strengthen its position and role in the search for Peace and Development in the geopolitical regions where it is located or participates, with emphasis on the Southern African Development Community (SADC), Economic Community of Central African States (ECCAS), International Commission for the Great Lakes Region (ICGLR), Gulf of Guinea Commission (CGG), Community of Portuguese-Speaking Countries (CPLP), South Atlantic Peace and Cooperation Zone (ZOPACAS), and Portuguese-Speaking African Countries (PALOP

[98] In this context, Angola continues to prioritize cooperation with various countries and international organizations to combat terrorism, organized crime, and illegal immigration, while also investing in environmental protection, humanitarian assistance, and national reconstruction

[99] International Strategic Scenario

[100] The end of the Cold War ended the balance of power between the old political-military blocs and ushered in a new era, marked by the proliferation of internal conflicts and the growing prominence of non-state actors in the international political system

[101] The profound change that has occurred in the political and strategic situation has created new opportunities and challenges for the development of cooperation and dialogue between States, and has also strengthened the role of international institutions

[102] On the other hand, the dizzying development of science, technology and technology has been exploited by criminals and groups to destabilize the international environment that is supposed to be peaceful

[103] In this sense, transnational organized crime, terrorism and other evils have seen ongoing globalization as a favorable space for the expansion of their criminal activities

[104] In this context, the resurgence of terrorist actions in their various forms, and the expansion of other crimes committed by non-state actors, constitute one of the main threats to the security and well-being of the population today

[105] In this changing scenario, traditional models for assessing the problems of National Defense, the very concept of national sovereignty, the institutional nature of the Armed Forces, their missions, their organizational structure and their functioning, tend to undergo significant changes with the aim of adapting them to the new strategies

[106] The New Conflict

[107] As mentioned, the change in the geostrategic balance created conditions for the outbreak of accumulated tensions, essentially in the form of radicalism of a nationalist, ethnic and religious nature

[108] The instability resulting from these imbalances has led to the emergence of diverse crises that have more frequently required the intervention of the international community

[109] With the shift in conflict factors from political ideology to multiple causes associated with the resurgence of nationalism and fundamentalist radicalism of an ethnic and religious nature, internal conflicts have become more frequent in the post-Cold War world, with Africa being the continent where this scenario is recurrent

[110] In this situation, the prevention and management of these new types of conflict requires the creation of adequate structures and capabilities to deal with them

[111] Angola cannot remain aloof from these new geopolitical dynamics and for this reason has initiated a Process of Rebuilding and Modernizing the Angolan Armed Forces, which aims to provide them with adequate resources to respond effectively to any challenges that may arise within the framework of their constitutional responsibilities

[230] With the proclamation of the Independence of the Republic of Angola on November 11, 1975, the People's Armed Forces for the Liberation of Angola were institutionalized as the single National Army for the entire national territory

[231] During the first year of its existence, the General Staff, in its territorial implementation, was part of the Army

[232] The following year, on January 21, 1976, the National Air Force Branch was created and on July 10 of the same year, the Angolan People's Navy

[233] From this point onwards, the National Armed Institution was made up of two branches: the FAPA/DAA and the MGA, with the Army Branch reporting directly to the then General Staff

[234] With the signing of the Bicesse Agreement on May 31, 1991, between the Government of the People's Republic of Angola and UNITA, the Angolan Armed Forces were institutionalized, which are the unification of the People's Armed Forces for the Liberation of Angola, with the military forces of UNITA

[235] With the approval of the Constitutional Law of 1992, within the scope of National Defence, structural laws were drawn up, namely Law No. 1/93, of March 26, General Law on Military Service and Law No. 2/93, of March 26, Law on National Defense and the Armed Forces (LDNFA

[236] Regarding matters of National Defense, due to its content, form and scope, the 1992 Constitutional Law evolved substantially in terms of its systematization and constitutional enshrinement

[237] With the approval of the 2010 Constitution and in compliance with the Directive of the President of the Republic and Commander-in-Chief on the Reconstruction of the Angolan Armed Forces, they are undergoing an adaptation process in many areas

[238] In this context, the Organic Statute of MINDEN, the Law on National Defense and the Armed Forces (LDNFA) and the General Law on Military Service and other norms that make up the Legislative Package for National Defense and the Angolan Armed Forces are being amended

[239] The Process of Reconstruction and Development of the Angolan Armed Forces

[240] The changes that have occurred in the domestic, regional and international political landscape over the last two decades require a change in the structural concepts of the Angolan Armed Forces due to the change in the objectives of the National Defense Policy

[241] At the international level, with September 11, 2001, a new concept of war emerged that appeared exclusively in the realm of hypotheses studied in military establishments: Asymmetric Warfare, whose objectives can be ideological, political or religious (fundamentalist), which means the imbalance of various orders, being nothing more than a war that is not restricted to a national space, it has a global character whose objective is to achieve the imbalance of economic and financial power, imbalance of resources, the imbalance of war capacity and organizational structure, creating conditions for the fight to develop in any space and at any time, with firepower being less relevant and mobility a fundamental factor, since what is immediately sought is not the occupation or maintenance of the space, but rather its contamination (enemy presence

[242] The objective of rebuilding and developing the Angolan Armed Forces is to create Armed Forces that meet the demands of political-military power, whose modern technological evolution allows them to be capable of effectively fulfilling the missions of National Defense, guaranteeing the independence, territorial integrity of the Country, freedom and security of the people against external aggression or the threat thereof, acting within the framework of International Law and the established Constitutional Order

[243] Tasks of the Reconstruction and Development of the Angolan Armed Forces

[244] The tasks of rebuilding and developing the Angolan Armed Forces stem from the objectives of the National Defense Policy and are defined by the need to guarantee the readiness and capacity of the Angolan Armed Forces, to protect the vital interests of the Republic of Angola and necessarily involve reflecting on its current state

[245] The achievement of the above objective is ensured by fulfilling the following tasks

[246] The review and updating of the Legislative Package, namely: Basic Law of National Defense and Armed Forces, General Law of Military Service, Basic Law of the Organization and Functioning of the Angolan Armed Forces, Law of Military Careers, Law of Military Ranks and Badges, Law of Military Decorations, Law of Military Heraldry, Law of Military Programming, Remuneration Statute of Military Personnel and the Organic Statutes of the Ministry of National Defense (MINDEN), of the General Staff of the Angolan Armed Forces, of the Army, of the National Air Force and of the Angolan Navy, constitute the legal basis of the activity of National Defense

[247] The redefinition of the system of forces and the device of the Angolan Armed Forces will continue to be based on the 3 (three

[248] Branches (Army, National Air Force and Angolan Navy), adopting the territorial component and the operational component, the latter equipped with regular unit structures and special units intended for use against subversive groups, providing them with great firepower, high maneuverability and projection capacity

[249] Analysis of the current military administrative division and adoption of new territorial concepts that enable the fulfillment of the missions of the military component of National Defense

[250] Improvement of the organic structures of the Command and Management Bodies of the Angolan Armed Forces, Branches, Large Units, Military Units, Bodies and Establishments drawn up based on new concepts

[251] Improvement of the command, communications, control and information technology system of the Angolan Armed Forces, capable of guaranteeing the operational direction of the troops, fulfilling the missions of the military component of National Defense based on the centralization of the operational command of forces and resources, as well as improving their cooperation both in times of peace and in times of war

[252] The Current Situation of the Angolan Armed Forces and the Strategic Military Concept (CEM) In order to materialize the Restructuring Directive of the Commander-in-Chief of the Angolan Armed Forces, the Strategic Military Concept has as its fundamental objective its implementation, and to transform the military apparatus of the Angolan Armed Forces into one of the means of authority and capacity for effective military response, through which the achievement and maintenance of national objectives is ensured, with emphasis on the military defense of the Country

[253] Peace and national stability reinforce the need for the Angolan Armed Forces to have a structure capable of guaranteeing National Independence, Territorial Integrity and the freedom and security of the population against any aggression or external threat, in light of the Constitution of the Republic of Angola and International Law

[254] The Military Strategy for the Reconstruction of the Angolan Armed Forces aims to strategically adapt the Angolan Armed Forces in order to, on the one hand, continue conducting the country's military defense with a view to ensuring peace, security and stability and, on the other hand, continue preparation and development aimed at increasing its capacity and operational readiness

[255] The organization and implementation of the reconstruction strategy of the Angolan Armed Forces is embodied in a set of measures and actions of a conceptual, organizational functional, and operational nature that aim to: - Prevent and overcome external and internal threats and risks, with an emphasis on multifaceted aggression, subversion, and local terrorism, among others; - Prepare and develop the Armed Forces, paying attention to strategic planning, restructuring, and re-equipment; - Coordinate the system of forces, the military apparatus, and educational-patriotic work; - Military education and training; - Military empowerment; - Logistics and infrastructure provision; - Technical maintenance and repair; - Military administration; - Administer justice and strengthen military discipline and the social protection of the Angolan Armed Forces

[256] The principles of military strategy guide

[257] In accordance with constitutional principles, through a defensive posture, create the capacity to prevent or react by the most appropriate means to any aggression or external threats with the development of offensive military actions necessary to defend sovereignty and territorial integrity

[258] The military policy for the rebuilding of the Angolan Armed Forces aims to configure the Armed Forces as the core expression of the defense of national power

[259] Internal Defense Capacity

[260] The operational capabilities of the Armed Forces must enable the achievement of the following objectives and actions: - Flexibility in organization based on the separation between operational and administrative command structures; - Obtaining strategic-operational and tactical information; - Reaction (rapid intervention and mobility) and effectiveness of the Forces; - Surveillance and intervention in maritime and river areas; - Speed and operational efficiency of Command, Communications, Control and Computing and Intelligence; - Military doctrine, unified procedures, and the ability to sustain the Force System; - Maintaining the forces and resources necessary to enable, depending on the likely enemy, a suitable defensive capability, safeguarding national interests

[261] Joint Action Development Capabilities

[262] Military Doctrine, organization, planning, procedures, training, preparation of resources, the structure of the Angolan Armed Forces, logistical support and operational conduct of the Forces must be geared towards creating the capacity to carry out joint and/or combined operations

[263] The coordination of actions with the Security and Civil Protection Forces is carried out at the level of the Operational Commands under a single command that guides the direction of the forces in the interest of defending the security of the populations, preventing sabotage actions in urban centers, communication routes and vital strategic objectives

[264] Concept of Action

[265] The Angolan Armed Forces perform different strategic missions in times of peace, crisis or conflict

[266] In times of peace** - Improve the organization of the force system and the permanent device with an adequate level of combat readiness and support to prevent, discourage, neutralize, or annihilate potential threats, providing a device capable of enlisting reserve forces whenever necessary; - Develop a secure Command, Communication, Control, IT, and Information System supported by a concrete and timely information system to obtain adequate combat power associated with troop mobility; - Improve the social conditions of military personnel, with a particular emphasis on their installation in suitable barracks and the implementation of an adequate logistical system; - Create a national military education system capable of developing human resources in the Armed Forces, providing them with a high and specific technical and military preparation; - Improve the recruitment, mobilization, and requisition system to ensure the preparation, employment, and support of the force system; - Develop Social and Psychological Action work in the Angolan Armed Forces; - Develop a military health system, implementing measures to promote and protect the health of military personnel and their families; - Support the Government in National Reconstruction tasks, building infrastructure, rebuilding communication routes, rural reorganization infrastructure, and reestablishing State Administration, preserving the environment and national heritage; - Prepare defense plans, train on their implementation, and assess their suitability, feasibility, and acceptability; - Carry out missions of public interest, namely the Civil Protection Service, supporting the satisfaction of basic needs and improving the quality of life of the population, and permanently participating in the National Emergency Plan in the event of a crisis or conflict in order to anticipate the necessary logistical functions to be undertaken; - Plan and prepare appropriate measures in the event of the Nation having to transition to a State of Siege or Emergency; - Lead joint training with Security and Territorial Defense Agencies on the country's preparedness in the event of a State of Siege or Emergency

[267] In times of crisis** - Place the Military Force on "Alert" and be ready to respond in a timely manner to political decisions; - Receive, prepare and train reservists in accordance with current legislation and existing plans; - Enhance the Surveillance and Intelligence System at all levels; - Direct deployment plans appropriate to the situation; - Enhance the readiness of existing forces and assets and be ready to occupy previously determined areas; - Create the conditions for the operation of the Joint Headquarters in cooperation with the Security and Protection Agencies; - Direct the necessary civil requisition measures as the situation evolves

[268] In the event of an armed conflict** - Defend the national territory by repelling, destroying, or neutralizing any military aggression; - Ensure the coordinated use of Land, Air, and Naval Forces in the enemy's most sensitive areas; - Ensure land, air, and naval coverage of the country's main economic and strategic objectives, as well as of its main military units; - Ensure the readiness of reserve units to be formed; - Directly direct all Security, Internal Order, and Civil Protection Forces in the National Resistance; - Direct the appropriate mechanisms for the growth and improvement of the Forces System; - Direct the General Mobilization Plan of the Nation's Forces and Resources for the defense of the territory; - Plan the measures to be taken after the end of the conflict; In the Operational Theater, the Armed Forces must derail enemy military programs, thus ensuring the political conditions necessary for the achievement of peace and development of the country

[269] Thus, the Armed Forces direct their missions in two fundamental domains

[270] In the Military Domain** - Ensure national sovereignty, territorial integrity, and the well-being of the population; - Strengthen and maintain the defense of land, sea, and air borders, increasing security cordons and ensuring the socioeconomic stability of the population; - Strengthen surveillance of air, sea, and land spaces, create the capacity for intervention in maritime and river spaces, protect port infrastructure and offshore and onshore operations, and ensure free movement on communication routes; - Support territorial administration throughout the national territory; - Transfer its main combat units to a situation of reorganization and combat preparation, continuing to ensure their cohesion and operational readiness

[271] In the Organization Domain

[272] The organization of the Angolan Armed Forces is based on the general military policy measures envisaged, namely: - The configuration of an Armed Forces, the core expression of the defense of national power, with its own capabilities and which acts in accordance with the national will and under the direction of the State, with the capacity to ensure the autonomy of political and military decision-making, the power of deterrence and the realization of military defense and the preparation and development of the Armed Forces, as well as the strengthening of their prestige as a national institution at the service of the Nation; - Adaptation of the structure, organization and functioning through their reorganization and modernization towards the adoption of a capable Armed Forces with a high degree of readiness and effectiveness, as the sole military institution of the State of the Republic of Angola and the main instrument of defense of the community in its broadest sense, organized and prepared based on the concept of national sufficiency adjusted to the political and strategic stature of the Country

[293] Force System

[294] The Angolan Armed Forces' Force System is structured and developed jointly and must ensure the implementation of a Command, Communications, Control, Computing, Information, and Reconnaissance System, with the capacity to: - Surveillance, detection, and timely warning of emerging threats; - Planning and conducting joint operations of the Armed Forces in areas where they are expected to operate under Operational Command; - Integrating, supporting, and controlling any efforts within the Force System; - Ensuring coordination and leadership of appropriate actions to be implemented by the Force System; - Ensuring a structure that ensures the convening, mobilization, and requisitioning that enables efficient coordination between the mobilization pace, existing equipment, and available personnel phased over time

[295] The Forces System is composed of: - An operational component encompassing the set of forces and resources of the Armed Forces related to each other from a perspective of integrated operational employment; - A territorial deployment component encompassing the set of bodies and services essential to the organization and general support of the Armed Forces

[296] The space of military servitude of the Angolan Armed Forces comprises the administrative-military territorial organization and the operational-military territorial organization

[297] The administrative-military territorial organization of the Angolan Armed Forces comprises the space where commands, general staffs, bodies, units and military establishments are located and operate within the national territory, taking into account the possibilities of employment, and is divided into Military, Air and Naval Regions

[298] The operational-military territorial organization comprises the space for deployment and operational conduct of commands, general staffs, bodies, units and military establishments within the national territory (and when necessary in external spaces of strategic interest), for the purposes of operational employment aimed at fulfilling missions, divided into military operational theaters and theaters of war coinciding in general with the Military, Air and Naval Regions

[299] The military device of the Armed Forces has as its essential objective the initial strategic deployment of military units within the framework of the system of forces in the directions and regions, with a view to obtaining the best operational employment of the Armed Forces, in accordance with the war plans, and must ensure the strategic escalation of the Armed Forces in the operational theater in order to allow the intervention of the military units of the Armed Forces in the threatened directions and regions, before or during the aggression, and integrates the military units and bodies of the three Branches in harmony with the specific Missions and the Force Systems arising therefrom

[300] The device must ensure effective presence in all parts of the national territory and have the capacity to generate forces, support the Forces System in a flexible territorial component that ensures the normal development of the training, logistical and administrative systems that make it possible to generate additional forces

[301] Preparation, Training and Instruction of the Angolan Armed Forces

[302] The training of Angolan Armed Forces personnel has three components: - A generic component, which is part of the National Education System; - A second component, specific to the institution, in continuity with the first component, which incorporates the training necessary to meet the needs of the Armed Forces, aiming at the efficient and effective performance of the tasks assigned to the military; - A third, equally specific component, which reflects the ability to maintain the efficiency and effectiveness of the military's performance in their duties

[303] Purpose of the Preparation, Training and Instruction of the Angolan Armed Forces - The first component aims to achieve long-term results, resulting in the general development of the individual, stimulating their reasoning and reflection capacity, memory, critical and analytical thinking, creativity and innovation, called Teaching; - The second component aims to acquire knowledge, skills and personal qualities required for a particular activity or function, called Training; - The third component allows, through systematic and planned practice, individually or in groups, to perfect and maintain the skills, knowledge and abilities obtained through the first two components, called Training

[304] Implementation Objectives of the Military Education, Training and Education Policy in the Angolan Armed Forces** - Conduct the military education, training and education policy of the Angolan Armed Forces, considering: - Military Education, Training and Education in the Angolan Armed Forces as priority activities capable of keeping human resources updated according to the evolution and progress in all fields of knowledge; - The use of new technologies, particularly modern combat means associated with the Information Technology Area, as a tool for transmitting knowledge, serving as support in the individualization of learning

[305] As part of the rebuilding process, the Angolan Armed Forces defined a Military Education System with a view to raising the level of training of military personnel, which is summarized in the following organization: - Education Subsystem of the General Staff; - Education Subsystem of the Army; - Education Subsystem of the National Air Force; - Education Subsystem of the Angolan Navy

[315] Maximizing Available Resources

[316] The current economic, financial and exchange rate climate imposes significant restrictions on all areas of the country's life

[317] In this sense, spending reduction has become a reality in all sectors of national life, including National Defense

[318] In light of this unfavorable situation, the watchword in Angola at this time is rationalization, a decision by the Executive that aims not only to save money, but also to better channel state revenues towards various sectors and needs

[319] In this context, the Executive adopted measures aimed at compensating for the necessary cuts to be made in the Defense Sector, maximizing available resources in order to maintain high levels of Defense and Security in the Country

[320] With regard to the logistical support of the Armed Forces, among others the Executive has been implementing an ambitious program whose objective is to produce some industrial and food goods for the National Armed Forces using local products

[321] To this end, the political authorities instructed the Military Chiefs to create the conditions for their troops to dedicate part of their time to producing food and consumer goods for themselves

[322] This effort must be organized not only by the different units, but also and above all by the Ministry of National Defense, the Command of the General Staff of the Angolan Armed Forces and the Commands of the Branches that have great responsibilities in the effective pursuit of this goal

[323] This work philosophy guarantees a drastic reduction in our dependence on the external market, with a view to strengthening our Defense and Security System, an indispensable condition for dealing with any challenges that jeopardize our readiness and capacity for the climate of stability and peace that prevails in the country

[324] Military and Defense Support Industry

[325] The existence of the Angolan Armed Forces is one of the intrinsic elements of the Concept of Sovereignty

[326] Linked to the existence of the Armed Forces came the need to ensure the supply of weapons systems and other material necessary to wage war to maintain Peace

[327] This fact imposes the need to create in-house manufacturing capabilities for a large part of the means and equipment used by the Angolan Armed Forces

[328] Unfortunately, in our country we cannot speak of a Defense Industry as such, due to the destruction that the long Civil War imposed on the national industrial park

[329] Aware of this need, the Executive of the Republic of Angola, within the scope of the strategy concerning the development of the Industry in general and the Defense Industry in particular, has developed activities aimed at optimizing the existing industry, providing it with resources that allow its participation in the supply process of the Angolan Armed Forces

[330] Strategic Interest of a Defense Industry

[331] With the end of the armed conflict in Angola, the socioeconomic stabilization of the country and the consequent gradual development of the National Industry, the need arises to develop a Defense and National Defense Support Industry, as the current situation of national companies linked to the production of materials necessary for the National Defense and war effort is characterized by low productivity, mismatch between the demand and supply profile, outdated products and obsolete production lines, with great difficulty in competing in international markets

[332] The Strategy for Boosting the Defense and Defense Support Industry involves: - Stimulating investment in the Defense and Security Industry Sector by national and foreign businesses, to generate new jobs and economic and social development; - Reducing imports in the supply of logistical resources to the Angolan Armed Forces, thus reducing the strong dependence on other countries; - Increasing the capacity to react in the event of war or other disasters; - Promoting cooperation, partnerships and the internationalization of companies, providing contacts for the import and export of various products; - Encouraging research work in areas of interest to National Defense

[333] The steps taken by the Executive regarding the diversification of the economy currently underway in our country are more than clear proof that in the medium term we can promote the progressive replacement of many consumer goods and equipment in use by the country's Defense and Security Forces
