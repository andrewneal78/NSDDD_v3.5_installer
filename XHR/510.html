[1818] Some of the civilian forms are very close to the service carried out in the armies: by the duration, by the type of employment, by the regime of life of the conscripts

[1824] This is the case with those called for technical assistance and conscientious objection, which are sometimes used for purposes that are not in line with the initial objectives

[1826] The three countries that host the most aid workers are the United States of America, Great Britain and Germany

[1827] Civilian conscripts also benefit from advantages that are often much higher than those granted for military service

[1828] This is particularly true for protocols and cooperative service

[1829] Some forms may also mask the beginning of a professional career

[1830] Overall, several civil forms are seen as offering a possibility of avoiding compulsory service and obtaining material advantages, whereas military service would, from these points of view, be penalizing

[1831] This lack of rigor and transparency in the conditions of recruitment and execution of the service contributes to the existence of a feeling of inequality between civilian and military conscripts

[1832] Directions for the future If civil forms correspond to a real need and are a source of consolidation of national service, various directions must strengthen the bases of a harmonious and sustainable development. CIVIL DEFENSE

[1841] International developments and the emergence of new threats require the development of a global concept of defense within which civil defense would find the essential role it deserves. The current organization of civil defense and military participation

[1842] Civil defence missions are normally assigned to the Minister of the Interior, who has his own resources for this purpose: civil security and the national police

[1843] The Civil Security Directorate's priority mission is to protect the population

[1844] It uses specialized resources - planes, helicopters, bomb disposal teams - and relies on some 230,000 increasingly professional firefighters

[1845] The national police, with 130,000 men, ensures the maintenance of order and surveillance of the territory and interventions on behalf of sensitive installations

[1846] The Air and Border Police are responsible for border control

[1847] The Minister of the Interior benefits from the permanent assistance of the national gendarmerie for the maintenance of order and the protection of sensitive points

[1848] The armed forces are permanently involved in the general security system by providing specialist units, the Paris fire brigade, Marseille marine fire brigade, and civil security training and intervention units

[1849] The Minister of the Interior can obtain the support of the armed forces, and if necessary, in various circumstances. For the mission of protecting the population, it is a question of calling on immediately available intervention capacities, significant numbers of personnel, specialized means not available to the civilian services. This competition becomes more frequent over the years. For the mission of maintaining public order, which is primarily the responsibility of the police and the national gendarmerie, the participation of the armed forces must remain exceptional and limited to serious crisis situations

[1850] Furthermore, the armed forces can provide occasional assistance which is decisive in the implementation of minimum operating plans

[1851] If necessary, they provide personnel and means of transport. For the mission of maintaining the continuity of government action, the armed forces can participate in the protection of sensitive installations (priority defense installations, first and second category sensitive points

[1852] Finally, the need for the government to maintain continuity of action, to protect the population and to safeguard the organs essential to national defence, could lead it to decide to implement operational defence of the territory (DOT

[1853] This would only be fully implemented in the event of external aggression or a specific threat

[1854] Then, and as needed, it would bring together all the vital forces of the nation to oppose any attack on the territory anywhere

[1855] The maritime defense of the territory, the air defense of the territory, the entire civil defense and the DOT form the Defense of the Territory

[1856] Its organization is global

[1857] Its implementation is adapted to the circumstances

[1858] A new development for civil defense

[1859] Faced with new threats of whatever nature, which may affect the security of the territory, everyone must become aware of their responsibilities

[1860] Without necessarily imagining new structures, we must reduce our vulnerability to attacks on the security of sensitive installations, communications and access to vital centres

[1861] These different areas require specific responses

[1862] Likewise, civil security units must be able to effectively carry out their mission of protecting the population, by providing them with the necessary means of intervention and relief

[1863] This effort in favor of civil defense is primarily the responsibility of the relevant administrations: Interior, Transport, Communication, Energy, Industry, Economy

[1864] In their respective fields, these must take into account this dimension of the defense effort

[1865] Bringing out this priority requires, as is emphasized elsewhere, that an interministerial grouping of the various budgets makes it possible to report on it in a specific document

[1866] Otherwise, civil defense would continue to remain secondary, despite the reaffirmed objective of a comprehensive defense concept. At the central level, Senior Defense Officials, placed directly with ministers to assist them in the exercise of their defense responsibilities, will intensify their action by focusing, on the one hand, on identifying the main vulnerabilities in the sectors of activity falling within the area of competence of their ministry in order to prepare possible countermeasures and responses, and on the other hand, on verifying from the outset that the provisions taken in their sector take into account the constraints of defense

[1867] To this end, they will benefit from increased power

[1868] This increased role of civil defense does not imply entrusting the armed forces with tasks that fall to others

[1869] The effort made must allow for measured support to be requested

[1870] The armed forces must not replace civil security resources in action on the ground, nor in the minds of officials and citizens

[1871] We must avoid trivializing their interventions: they should only intervene when the civil resources available to the administrations - or those they may have requested - are insufficient, unsuitable, non-existent, or when the emergency justifies it

[1872] This exceptional nature of the intervention of the armies must be particularly emphasized in the case of maintaining public order

[1873] This is a mission for which the armies are not trained, for which recourse to them, when decided, must be carried out within the framework of legal provisions

[1874] It normally remains circumscribed and limited to support and assistance missions

[1875] To maintain the continuity of public service, the establishment of a "reduced service" provided by the civilian companies concerned is preferable to the replacement of these services by the armed forces. However, the effectiveness of military interventions for the benefit of civil defense must be maintained

[1876] The optimal use of armies is all the more essential

[1877] that the reduction in their size, and in particular that of the land army, significantly lightens the traditional “national military fabric”

[1878] Some departments do not welcome

[1879] no military unit other than those of the gendarmerie

[1880] This discontinuity in the territorial system must in no way diminish the effectiveness of the response of the armed forces to defence requirements

[1881] The objective is to identify the units of the armed forces which will be able to intervene quickly, at all points in the country, with the means in terms of specialized personnel and equipment, as soon as those available at the territorial level concerned prove insufficient or unsuitable

[1882] This is a new concept, that of internal defense. Finally, the relevance of the concept of operational defense of the territory (DOT) is no longer imposed today with the same force

[1883] Even if it cannot be ruled out because of the mortal risks it presents, the hypothesis of a major conflict in Europe, the integrity of the territory and the survival of the nation, is unlikely on the horizon of the White Paper

[1884] To take this new situation into account, the notion of “land defense”, which must be explored further, corresponds to taking into account, from normal times, the possibility of serious and high-intensity crises where the assistance of the Armed Forces is essential to the functioning of institutions

[1885] Coordination of civil and military powers The coordination of civil and military powers must enable civil defence interventions to be carried out under conditions of maximum efficiency

[1886] The most appropriate level is that of the defense zone, the place where civil and military skills are best combined

[1887] This common and interministerial level of defence law must be strengthened, both in budgetary terms and through the support of personnel from the various ministries

[1888] The quality of relations between the prefectural authority and the military authority is essential to the smooth running of operations which are often of an emergency nature

[1889] Therefore, these relationships must pre-exist and be maintained both at the level of the defense zone and on the ground

[1890] Within the defence zone, the general commanding the military defence district is best placed to provide the zone prefects with an advisory role in the use of army resources

[1891] It coordinates the action of land defense, which includes all actions that can be carried out by the armed forces for the benefit of civil defense. As an exception to the general vocation of the defense zone, the use of certain highly specialized military resources must be the direct responsibility of the central administration, or even of the national political authority

[1892] Similarly, the national level must ensure coordination between the different defense zones and the transfer of the required resources when these are not stationed in the area concerned. Finally, the prominent role of local authorities and elected officials must be emphasized

[1893] First and foremost, the mayor is the guarantor of the safety of his constituents; he has a deep and pragmatic knowledge of local realities and the needs of a population for which he assumes responsibility at the primary level

[1894] This knowledge of the environment is an irreplaceable and indispensable contribution to civil defense, both in the implementation of protective measures and in the dissemination of information on risk management

[1895] The protection of populations therefore requires a partnership between elected officials, even if unity in the direction of operations constitutes a major requirement in the management of all situations of tension or instability

[1896] Mayors play a significant role in providing assistance to prefects, particularly through the exercise of their police powers

[1897] This role must be developed widely in the preparation and implementation of civil defense efforts within each defense zone

[1898] ECONOMIC DEFENSE

[1899] Like civil defense, economic defense is seeing its importance grow, in light of new threats and vulnerabilities

[1900] Its necessary reinforcement

[1901] insert into the framework of European construction

[1902] Economic defense and European construction

[1903] France's economic policy decisions are now largely transferred to the European level, both for the development of decisions and for the definition of scope for national action

[1904] As a result, economic defence is only partially a national matter

[1905] This is the case with the protection of basic economic activities and the control of technology transfers, to cite just two examples. Community construction is the source of new interdependencies, which, by their very nature, go beyond the borders of a single State

[1906] Furthermore, the principles of free movement of people, goods and services may conflict with the ability of States to mobilise all available and necessary resources in the event of a crisis

[1907] Security imperatives and economic rules are in conflict

[1908] The problems posed by the oil regime or the control of airspace are clear proof of this

[1909] The effectiveness of national defence systems is affected by these developments

[1910] The growing inadequacy of the national level to define an economic defence policy is combined with the equally growing need for such a policy

[1911] The solution lies in the eventual creation of a European economic defense. With the Common Defense and Security Policy, the Treaty on European Union paves the way for such a process, through the prospect of adjusting the legal frameworks of the Member States

[1912] A diversified economic defense

[1913] Reducing our economic vulnerabilities arises in different terms, depending on the more or less strategic nature of the activities

[1914] It is important to seek and implement, in conjunction with our European partners, responses adapted to each sector

[1915] The legal instruments and coordination structures that will enable them to be achieved will have to be gradually developed

[1916] Four sectors should be prioritized

[1917] The first is the arms industry

[1918] Pursuant to Article 223 of the Treaty of Rome, the decision-making autonomy of States remains intact. All of the guidelines mentioned above regarding developments in defence-related industries should help contain and reduce our vulnerabilities in this area

[1919] The second sector is that of basic services without which no defense can be organized

[1920] These include transport and its infrastructure, civil engineering, energy production and storage, telecommunications and distribution networks (water, gas, electricity, oil, etc

[1921] The deregulations envisaged will require these services to be taken into account, at national and European level, with the aim of allowing States the possibility of making requisitions

[1922] Military force projection actions may in fact require the requisition or chartering under legal constraint of air or sea transport

[1923] The third sector is technology and industry, which includes space, electronics, IT, aeronautics and nuclear power

[1924] A European policy is necessary, also in the space sector, and must therefore take into account the imperatives of economic defence

[1925] In other cases, in the absence of any common policy, the solution lies in the coordination of the policies of the different States concerned

[1926] The fourth sector includes all the capabilities that do not directly concern defense

[1927] but which represent the keys to independence: fundamental research, nuclear engineering, databases, and certain specific productions

[1928] The link with defense is sometimes difficult to establish for some of these activities

[1929] It is, however, obvious and cannot be neglected or ignored. THE ORGANIZATION

[1943] Information is now at the heart of how our democracies function

[1944] Both abundant and partial, it continuously influences populations naturally disposed to be moved, to be indignant or to worry in the face of the image of events likely to impress them

[1945] This phenomenon, which affects all Western countries, tends to provoke a uniform reaction from public opinion

[1946] Its excesses require reflection on defense matters and lead us to question the processing and prioritization of information by the media

[1947] The government and the media may have differing opinions on the seriousness of a situation, with the latter dramatizing facts that are otherwise considered trivial, or, on the contrary, considering as secondary what the government considers important

[1948] Moreover, there is always the possibility of overbidding between media subject to economic imperatives of competition

[1949] The reality of a situation can be completely distorted: recent examples of this are frequent

[1950] Finally, the media, for technical reasons, may be led to summarize, to present the spectacular, like a shocking image taken out of its context, emphasizing the sensational while only very imperfectly describing the situation

[1951] The capacity to manipulate public opinion using such instruments is therefore real in times of crisis; it can disrupt the functioning of public authorities

[1952] it can, in the extreme, harm the national interest and defense. In the particular case of terrorist actions, the means of information are of capital importance

[1953] Without them, terrorism has only limited effect

[1954] With the media coverage that any action of this type can give, it becomes a scourge capable of undermining the reaction capacities of even the strongest democracies

[1955] It seems difficult to find a happy medium between total freedom and restriction of information

[1956] In the first case, the preservation of secrecy, the safety of people and the smooth running of an operation could be compromised

[1957] In the second, the action of the public authorities would undoubtedly be facilitated, but what confidence could public opinion place in censored information

[1958] The Armed Forces Information and Public Relations Services (SIRPA) are responsible, on the one hand, for processing defense information and, on the other hand, for promoting the armed forces

[1959] This last aspect of their action is permanent and independent, most of the time, from direct news. The national interest and respect for democratic rules must make it possible to define the field of action where representatives of defense and the media can work together, clearly and effectively

[1960] The basic principle in this regard is transparency

[1961] To avoid disinformation actions

[1962] false rumors, rumours, suspicions, interpretations-, everyone must first strive to disseminate precise and clear factual information, to react immediately by following the course of events as closely as possible, and finally, when it is proven and necessary, to announce and justify any retention or control

[1963] This orientation presupposes mutual trust between defense officials and media representatives

[1964] Both sides must understand and accept the constraints inherent in each type of situation

[1965] This trust, the result of regular attendance, will be reinforced by the establishment of a consultation and advisory body, where both parties will be able to better assess each other's needs, requirements and constraints

[1966] The debate would then focus on procedures to follow in times of crisis and conflict, or on the processing of information that can lead to confusion or interpretation in certain normal situations

[1967] A certain number of rules, supported by experience and commonly accepted, will thus gradually emerge

[1968] A de facto relationship must be established between the information services of the armed forces and accredited journalists who are knowledgeable about defence issues

[1969] Armed forces officers responsible for media relations must have training in information technology

[1970] to be true media specialists

[1971] The same knowledge of professional techniques, the same ethics of information, the same concern to serve the national interest and democracy, these will be the combined qualities of each party, for a successful partnership
