Sembremos Seguridad Strategy (ESS) arises as an initiative that seeks to work on crime prevention by identifying and prioritizing types of crimes and social risks in the different cantons of the country, as well as the identification of criminal organizations

[100] To this end, it is making an effort to coordinate institutional and inter-institutional capacities, in order to deploy public policy inputs and joint strategies aimed at achieving results in improving citizen security

[101] This strategy works hand in hand with various cantonal institutions, and especially with municipalities

[102] The ESS was planned and implemented during the Solís Rivera administration, as a pilot plan for the Comprehensive Analysis of Citizen Security and Coexistence (AISECC) tool, which was being developed in Colombia

[103] Its implementation in Costa Rica is achieved thanks to the cooperation of the United States Embassy, which established a partnership between the Costa Rican Ministry of Security and the Colombian National Police. This partnership is designed to analyze and adapt the project to our environment, especially considering the key players in the process, the country's legal implications, as well as its geographic distribution and social and institutional contexts

[104] The tool was implemented in Costa Rica by staff from the Vice Ministry of Public Security, who received two months of training to perform their duties as implementers of the Strategy

[105] The results of this pilot exercise were materialized in various documents called Strategic and Operational Prioritization Analysis for Citizen Security, developed for each of the cantons that participated in the pilot. These documents are intended to serve as a guide for decision-making regarding territorial citizen security management

[106] In 2018, with the change of government to the new Alvarado Quesada administration, the Vice Ministry of Security decided to continue the intervention and include it in the 2019-2022 National Development and Public Investment Plan for the Bicentennial (PNDIP) after improvements detected in the pilot exercise

[107] Following these improvements, the “Comprehensive Prevention Strategy for Public Safety” was formalized under the slogan “LET'S PLANT SAFETY.”

[109] The implementation of the ESS is a joint effort by several stakeholders, including MSP departments and municipalities, which play a key role since they are responsible for adapting and implementing the lines of work

[110] These bodies receive the report and are responsible for carrying out the necessary coordination with other public bodies and the public to fulfill the commitments outlined in the report

[115] Question: What is the degree of coherence in the design of the Sembramos Seguridad strategy? Based on the analysis of the problem, objectives, target population, stakeholders, monitoring, and key planning aspects, it is possible to conclude an intermediate level of coherence with respect to the basic elements of the program design

[116] In general terms, the main conclusions that support the level of coherence identified are the following: • The fact that there is no Design document, with an identification of the problem, an appropriate characterization of the target population, a homologation of general and specific objectives and a coherence between these, has generated confusion among the actors participating in the Strategy regarding the end or the expected medium and long term changes as a consequence of its implementation

[117] • The lack of coherence between the general objectives and the specific objectives found in the different documents (and their variability) does not allow for identifying a clear direction of intervention, given that the general objectives refer to management issues and the specific objectives are not limited and do not reflect the sequence in which the general objective will be achieved

[118] Regarding the Theory of intervention or the logic of the Strategy's operation, this is one of the aspects that needs to be improved the most: it is necessary to make it explicit, work on it in a participatory manner and ensure coherence between its elements

[119] The visibility of the results chain is based on a reconstruction carried out within the framework of this evaluation with respect to what was identified in the Strategy in terms of management and results
