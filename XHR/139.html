[423] This function also includes the official reception and dispatch of foreign-flagged vessels arriving at national ports, in coordination with other institutions

[424] Another important function delegated to the Ministry of National Defense is that of Flag State, which requires the effective exercise of jurisdiction and control over administrative and technical issues related to vessels flying the National Flag, executed through the Commands and Port Captaincies of the Republic of Guatemala in coordination with the General Directorate of Maritime Affairs of the MDN

[517] Its probability of occurrence is variable

[518] On the one hand, it depends on the quality of the prospective analysis; on the other, on the ability of the respective political leaders to manage and control the situation, preventing temporary situations from contributing to a prospective scenario of real collision, whether a crisis or a war

[519] Normally, conflict hypotheses are deduced from the Political Strategic Appreciation
