[2] Fulfilling this task requires increasing the state's readiness to face security challenges, threats and crises, contributing to maintaining international peace and security, preventing situations threatening the security of the state and its citizens and having adequate resources, tools and policies, as well as the trust of the population

[3] The Slovak Republic is still a safe country, but in recent years global security has deteriorated in several respects, also with direct impacts on our security and resilience

[4] The threats and challenges we face are increasingly complex, interconnected, immediate and have greater consequences for our security

[5] The global pandemic has not only made some of our vulnerabilities visible, but has also accelerated and deepened existing trends of weakening multilateralism, renewed geopolitical rivalry, assertive pursuit of individual interests and continued instability in Europe's neighbourhood and regional crises in the world

[7] This situation is an opportunity for a strategic assessment and adjustment of our security policy

[160] V. SECURITY POLICY OF THE SLOVAK REPUBLIC

[161] The security policy of the Slovak Republic will be focused on promoting its security interests and responding to security threats and challenges in order to maintain and strengthen the security of the state

[162] The Slovak Republic promotes its security interests through security policy instruments, with the contribution and support of citizens and in cooperation with allies and partners

[163] The basic instrument of the security policy of the Slovak Republic is a security system, which must be ready to respond to security threats and the changing security environment

[164] Its main priorities are:  to ensure an effective and efficient management system in all crisis situations;  to identify an emerging crisis situation and provide early warning;  to analyze the impact of the crisis situation on the security interests of the Slovak Republic and propose a way to resolve it;  to act preventively against the emergence of crisis situations and increase the resilience of the state and society and, in the event of their emergence, to nip these situations in the bud;  to respond to confirmed threats and adapt to changes in the security situation, including responding to unexpected threats;  to ensure the rapid elimination of the consequences of the crisis situation and the restoration of the original state;  to guarantee the continuity of its functioning;  to improve the sharing of information between individual elements of the security system;  to systematically practice and evaluate its functionality

[165] The Slovak Republic will ensure adequate resources for the functioning of the security system and their effective and transparent use

[166] Defense and security of the state and its inhabitants

[167] Ensuring the defense of the Slovak Republic is a necessary condition for preserving its sovereignty, territorial integrity and inviolability of borders

[168] The defense of the state is also ensured in cyberspace

[169] The Slovak Republic fulfills its primary responsibility for its own defense by using NATO collective defense as the best option for ensuring the defense of the state

[170] The development of the Slovak Republic's defence capabilities strengthens its defence capability and is a necessary prerequisite for the full functioning of collective defence within NATO and the resilience of the state

[171] Membership in NATO and the EU contractually ensures international assistance and support to the Slovak Republic in the event of an armed attack

[172] The Slovak Republic is also obliged to provide such assistance to another NATO ally or an EU member state in the event of an attack

[173] The Slovak Republic takes a responsible approach to increasing its readiness for national defence, including the collective defence of allies and the growth of military capabilities in NATO and the EU

[174] To this end, it is developing the national defence system and defence capacities and ensuring the necessary resources and their effective and transparent use

[175] The Slovak Republic will improve the quality of national defence management, increase the readiness of the armed forces to defend the Slovak Republic and fulfil international obligations, as well as the readiness of other elements and capacities of the national defence system to fulfil tasks in times of war or a state of war

[176] It will regularly practice the fulfilment of these tasks, including the tasks of constitutional bodies

[177] It will also develop the capacity to provide support to foreign armed forces in accordance with international obligations and the Constitution of the Slovak Republic

[178] It will increase its readiness to defend the state in cyberspace

[179] The armed forces will also be prepared to perform tasks of supporting peace, security and stability outside the territory of the Slovak Republic and to perform tasks in the event of an emergency and non-military crisis situation on its territory

[180] The Slovak Republic will support the engagement of its defence industry in ensuring the defence of the state and its involvement in international cooperation and will also strengthen the preparation of the population for the defence of the state

[181] The fight against terrorism will focus on preventing the causes that give rise to it and strengthening capabilities in this area, including responding to new threats such as radicalised individuals, foreign terrorist fighters and the abuse of social networks

[182] The Slovak Republic will prevent the planning, organisation, financing and execution of terrorist activities on its territory and at the same time strengthen the legislative and institutional framework for the fight against terrorism

[183] It will focus mainly on the preparedness and coordination of all components involved in preventing, suppressing, detecting and documenting acts of terrorism, further on reducing the risk of endangering the population and critical infrastructure in connection with the threat of the use of explosives, chemical, biological, radioactive and nuclear means by terrorists, and also on the effective implementation of anti-terrorist sanctions regimes

[184] The Slovak Republic will increase the preparedness of entities fighting terrorism, mainly by increasing analytical capacities, conducting exercises and international cooperation

[185] It will also increase the awareness of the population about radicalization, extremism and terrorism, about the need to report suspicious activities and about behavior in the event of a terrorist attack or threat

[186] The Slovak Republic will support international efforts aimed at preventing terrorism

[187] The development of information technologies requires a constant increase in the level of cybersecurity

[188] The Slovak Republic will take measures to ensure a high level of security in the national cyberspace, which will be based on the rule of law and respect for fundamental human rights and freedoms

[189] It will be based on the application of a comprehensive approach to cybersecurity and the principles of ongoing risk management, prioritizing support, cooperation and prevention over repression and unilateral decisions

[190] The Slovak Republic will build an effective detection system, improve and accelerate the ability to respond to cybersecurity incidents, unify various existing regulations in this area into a single regulatory framework and create modern and effective forms of cooperation between the state and the security and private sectors

[191] It will also cooperate at the international level, develop forms of bilateral cooperation and gain experience from partner countries

[192] The Slovak Republic will ensure that when building communication, telecommunication and data networks that are under the administration of the state, used by the state or that form part of critical infrastructure, technologies that do not pose a security threat and come from our partners and allies are used

[193] A functioning critical infrastructure is the basis for the operation of a modern, economically vital and secure society, the functioning of the economy and public administration

[194] The Slovak Republic will increase its ability to withstand and quickly recover from accidents, deliberate attacks, incidents or natural disasters

[196] With new emerging technologies, it is essential to ensure the security and reliability of critical infrastructure and systems and networks owned, used or managed by the state, as well as other communication networks and systems used by the state or a large part of the population

[197] The Slovak Republic will strive to create links between operators and central government bodies, achieve compatibility of crisis management with other NATO and EU countries, improve prevention and increase the ability to respond to security threats and thus reduce the damage caused by possible incidents in critical infrastructure
