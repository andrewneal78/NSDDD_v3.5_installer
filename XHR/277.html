[210] Migration policy Coordinating migration policy with the economic, social and security policies: Develop and carry out a comprehensive migration policy, coordinated with security, economic and social policies, taking into account both current and projected needs of the labour market, integration of migrants with the Polish society ensuring social cohesion as well as counteracting possible threats to public order and security related to migration processes

[211] Economic security Strengthening economic security, including financial security

[212] Support measures to increase resilience to international financial crises in particular by strengthening the stability of the system of public finances while ensuring conditions for stable and balanced economic growth

[213] Continue working towards a change in the structure of public expenditure in order to increase resources for development-oriented activities

[214] Continue efforts to close the development gap with respect to better developed economies, while maintaining capacity to respond flexibly to external and internal challenges

[215] Ensure efficient cash flows in times of disruption in the functioning of the banking system

[216] Strengthen the supervisory capacity and competence to counter threats related to destabilisation of financial markets, speculative attack on the Polish currency or capital drain

[217] Strengthen instruments of supervision over financial markets, including the expansion and intensification of cooperation between supervisory institutions and law enforcement agencies

[218] Energy security** Ensuring energy security of the state, based on traditional energy sources, by creating conditions for the development of alternatives

[219] Expand and modernise generation capacities and electricity transmission and distribution networks to ensure the continuity of supplies, including the prevention of unexpected power cuts

[220] Develop dispersed electric energy sources in a sustainable manner, ensuring the adaptation of the National Power System to the operation characteristics of these sources

[221] Increase diversification of oil and gas supply sources

[222] Expand the existing natural gas import capacities (inter alia increasing reception capacity of the LNG terminal in Swinoujscie) and build new entry points to the Polish transmission system (construction of the Baltic Pipe, construction of the LNG terminal in the Gdańsk Bay

[223] Continue to work on projects diversifying gas supplies to the countries in the region, including the Three Seas Initiative states

[224] Continue to work on the extension of natural gas transmission and storage systems, including the completion of the North-South route which will lay foundations for the operation of a gas hub in Poland

[225] Increase the capacity, work safety and the range of oil and fuel pipelines as well as the capacity of fuel and crude oil storage facilities

[226] Continue diplomatic, legal and administrative efforts to halt the construction of the transmission infrastructure increasing the dependence of Central Europe on gas supplies from the Russian Federation, strengthen the region’s resilience to the risk of using gas supplies as an instrument of political pressure

[227] Protection of natural environment Ensuring ecological security of the state

[228] Create conditions for effective enforcement of environment-related legislation

[229] Establish a coherent policy for the protection, restoring and management of water resources taking into account food security of the state

[230] Intensify efforts to combat smog, develop electromobility and the use of alternative fuels, support the development of energy industry based on the use of zero-emission energy sources and improve waste management

[231] Adjust national policies and actions to the climate objectives agreed on in the fora of international organisations related to energy transition and achieving climate neutrality, in a way that takes into account the specificity of the country and maximizes their positive impact on the standard of living of the citizens, the economic development of the country and the competitiveness of the economy, taking advantage of the opportunities arising from the implementation of new energy production technologies

[232] Aim to preserve all functions of the natural environment, including forests as one of the key elements of the country’s ecological safety

[233] Scientific and technological potential** Management of human capital, scientific and technological potential for the economic development of the country

[234] Provide conditions conducive to the development of innovation, inter alia, through state aid in financing projects, their commercialisation and internationalisation, as well as counteracting the outflow of human capital and projects abroad

[235] Promote exact sciences in order to increase technological competence, especially in the field of security, including defence, and the directions of education particularly desirable for the development of the country

[236] Promote the development and implementation of modern technologies and use of their effects for national security

[237] Increase investment in research and development to reach the European average and enhance the effectiveness of the use and commercialisation of its results

[238] Conclusion** The mechanisms for the implementation of the provisions of the present Strategy shall be defined in the Act on National Security Management

[239] Pending its adoption, the Strategy shall be implemented within the framework of the currently binding law

[240] The ministers in charge of the departments of government administration, heads of central offices, province governors, local government bodies and other entities competent in the field of national security shall be responsible for implementing the provisions of this Strategy

[241] They are obliged to take into account national interests and strategic objectives in the area of national security, included in this Strategy, in all projects planned for implementation and in day-to-day operations

[242] Verification of the implementation of the tasks set out in this Strategy and development of proposals to update it may take place as part of national security strategic reviews
