[375] As instructed by the President to “effectively exploit offset resources to stimulate industrial development,” the MND employs the offset mechanism to strive for transfer of key technologies, promotes cooperation between domestic and foreign suppliers, and assists them to join the OEM (original equipment manufacturers) supply chain, so as to establish maintenance for depot level and production capacities, upgrade our domestic industries, and boost the strength of self-reliant defense

[378] Gray zone exists in an ambiguous realm between peace and war, and is characterized by having a mix of indirect force application and paramilitary or low-intensity military means to harass, coerce, and undermine stability

[379] In recent years, the PRC has been frequently using gray zone tactics, such as cognitive warfare, information warfare, and incursion by aircraft and vessels, aiming at weakening morale, depleting the resources of the ROC Armed Forces, and eroding national security, which urgently require precautions and responses

[380] Consolidating Security: The mentality and will of the opponents

[381] Over the years, the PRC has been using the “three warfare” tactics: psychological warfare, public opinion warfare, and legal warfare, to disseminate confusing disinformation and hurl verbal attacks and saber-rattling threats across the board to create divisions among the people

[382] In order to counter PRC’s cognitive warfare tactics, the ROC Armed Forces has established a prompt reaction mechanism by engaging with society and solidifying mental defense of the people and service members

[383] Meanwhile, strategic communication is also conducted to make timely clarification and win the understanding and support from the international community

[384] The purposes of PRC’s cognitive warfare Politically, it is used to suppress Taiwan’s international space, and compel it to accept PRC’s political demands

[385] Economically, it is used to make the most of commerce and trade advantages of China to solicit the approval of the people and business circles in Taiwan

[386] Militarily, it is used to expand the effects of military coercion by media and internet propaganda through a show of force and ever-growing numbers of intrusions and provocations along our surrounding waters and airspace

[387] The means of PRC’s cognitive warfare The cognitive warfare is an extension of the PRC’s “three warfare” and united front tactics, stressing that the propaganda has to permeate “into the island (Taiwan), every household, everyone’s head, and ultimately individual’s mind.”

[388] It is combined with modern media and internet hacking, trying to sway public opinions, manipulate public perceptions, create fear and suspicion among the people, and eventually destroy their trust in the Taiwan government

[389] Currently, it primarily uses social media to spread disinformation that is massively and quickly produced, carefully packaged, and disseminated through diverse circulation channels

[390] Making the best of technological toolkits The MND makes use of new technological toolkits such as big data systems to identify and sift out controversial messages, verify and report the results, trace the sources, provide clarifications and evaluations, and make the best of diversified means to amplify countermeasure effects

[391] In addition, the MND maintains awareness of domestic public opinions and draws up publicity strategies by timely utilizing video clips or printed media to harden the psychological defense of the people

[392] Following people’s preferences and habits to absorb information, the MND takes actions actively by expanding its broader use of the internet and social media for publicity purposes, so as to multiply its effects, improve people’s comprehension of public information and recognition of national defense, and support the force buildup efforts of the ROC Armed Forces

[393] Appealing to the international community for a concerted effort Taiwan continues to communicate with regional countries to improve their understanding, broaden the friendly sphere in the international community, and win over international support and cooperation, so as to collectively counter the threat of PRC’s cognitive warfare

[394] In parallel with information technology development and prevalence of internet applications, hackers and malicious programs are quickly evolving and posing ever greater threats that can sabotage major networking systems and influence daily routines and social orders

[395] The ROC Armed Forces operates all cybersecurity management mechanisms in place, and strengthens protection capacities and raises a secure barrier against IW threats

[396] Over the years, the PRC has completed military reorganization and has been aggressively improving its capability for cyber operations

[397] At this stage, the PRC has placed its IW focus on gathering intelligence through its cyber force, grabbing information from the military, industrial, and commercial sectors, aiming to control Taiwan’s critical nodes, and compiling a target list for cyberattacks in its future operations

[398] In a possible wartime situation, the PRC may initiate attacks on Taiwan’s critical information infrastructure and military-related information systems to disrupt Taiwan governmental operations, cause disruption to military and police forces, and severely impact national security and social stability

[399] The ROC Armed Forces Cybersecurity and Information Protection Center is in charge of monitoring military network activities full-time, and continues to enhance system efficiency

[400] The Center, with redundant backups, has been performing rigorous cybersecurity management work, such as network snooping, endpoint detection and response (EDR), and cyber auditing, so as to ward off hacking activities by PRC cyber force

[401] As instructed by the policy of “cyber security represents national security,” the MND has established partnerships with governmental agencies and cybersecurity institutions

[402] Through joint cybersecurity and communication mechanism, the MND routinely holds emergency reaction drills and shares cybersecurity information, so as to construct a joint civil-mil arrangement for cybersecurity

[403] Furthermore, as requested by the AI, the MND executes cybersecurity checks and infiltration tests on our critical infrastructures and collects all hacking patterns to improve overall capacities of contingency response

[404] The MND continues to nurture cybersecurity talents by enhancing cybersecurity courses in the military educational institutions, providing cybersecurity training for individual units, and introducing technological capacities from academic, research, and civilian sectors, so as to increase cyber operational capabilities of the ROC Armed Forces

[409] PRC’s Harassment Means The PRC imposes gray zone harassments and coercive effects across the Taiwan Strait via various activities such as repeatedly assigning aircraft and vessels, conducting drills and exercises

[410] These activities are categorized as follows: The PRC has been assigning various kinds of aircraft and UAVs to intrude the southwest area of our ADIZ on a regular basis

[411] It attempts to intensify PLA's surveillance and reconnaissance activities, probe and disturb ROC’s air defense measures, constrain reaction ability in terms of time and airspace, and threaten the aerial security of the Taiwan Strait

[412] Air and maritime long-distance training over the sea A long-distance training task force of the PLAN and the PLA Air Force (PLAAF) has been conducting exercises for joint operations in our surrounding waters and airspace as well as the Western Pacific, in order to strengthen their maritime maneuvering and replenishment, air refueling, chain of command, and the performance of joint operations

[413] Carrier cross-regional cruise training

[414] In order to establish an area denial capability and impose threats towards Taiwan, the PLAN has assigned a carrier group transiting through the Taiwan Strait to the Western Pacific

[415] The cross-regional cruise training aims to sharpen the carrier’s abilities of prolonged maritime deployment and the carrierbased fighters’ capabilities for all-weather operations

[416] Maritime activities around our waters The PRC has been intensifying its maritime patrol activities on the waters confronting Taiwan

[417] It has been assigning spy boats and survey vessels to collect intelligence and hydrological information, conduct seabed terrain survey, and enhance its maritime surveillance capabilities

[418] Military operations with coercive effects By building tactical training bases with facilities mocking Taiwan targets and conducting drills of invasion against Taiwan, the PLA has been increasing the intensity of practical operational training and coercion against Taiwan

[419] ROC Armed Forces' Countermeasures Facing various intrusions and provocations initiated by the PRC, the ROC Armed Forces follows the guidance of “Absolutely No Concession on Sovereignty; No Backing Away from Democracy and Freedom” to steadfastly demonstrate our determination and competency to defend the homeland and vigorously strengthen all necessary responses

[421] Cautiously reacting to contingencies The ROC Armed Forces regularly deploys air and maritime forces to patrol airspace and waters around Taiwan, and maintains ground forces on offshore islands and Taiwan for proper operational preparedness and timely reaction to contingencies, securing our territorial waters and airspace by following the principle: “The tougher the enemy is, the tougher the response from ROC would be.”

[422] Working with the CGA to deal with emergencies The ROC Armed Forces continues to coordinate with the CGA closely, shares information, and conducts joint ISR missions to strengthen mutual reactive capabilities to handle maritime contingencies
