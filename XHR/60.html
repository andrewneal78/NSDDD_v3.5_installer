[103] To respond effectively to any threat and for national security as a whole, Cambodia needs to change its defense priorities to adapt to the current security environment

[104] Those priorities include: Enhance border defense capabilities against transnational crimes and infiltration of terrorist groups

[111] Consideration for the RCAF’s Roles The RCAF’s four primary roles defined in Chapter 5 of the White Paper 2000 have not been changed

[112] However, there will be a reduced emphasis on the RCAF’s role of ensuring peace, stability and social orders

[114] The RCAF will increase its engagement in the restoration and development of national infrastructure by using as much as possible of its existing resources

[115] On the other hand, the military must be prepared and ready to help the people suffering from various natural disasters

[116] Maintaining Military Strategy Military Strategy of “Flexible and Controlled Response” introduced in the White Paper 2000 remains fundamental to implementing the Royal Government’s defense policy

[117] The three layers of the national defense system (the High Command, Military Regions and Deployed Units) are core actors in implementing this strategy effectively

[118] Nevertheless, the adoption of the following measures could ensure success in the future

[119] Those measures are: - Improve the effectiveness of command, control and communication system among the three layers of the national defense system, enabling timely exchange of intelligence and appropriate actions for response

[120] Extend the means of patrolling for Border Defense Units where necessary and possible

[122] This is aimed mainly at prevention and crackdown of cross-border illegal activities, combating terrorism and rescue operations

[123] Be prepared to respond in a timely and effectively manner to any contingencies occurring in the maritime areas
