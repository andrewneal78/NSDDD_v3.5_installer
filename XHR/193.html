[68] STRATEGIC GOALS OF NATIONAL DEFENSE

[69] The goal of national defense The people of Latvia and the state of Latvia have been continuously influenced by European geopolitical events

[70] The Latvian people have experienced existential threats more than once in their history, therefore national defense is the cornerstone of the existence of the Latvian state and people

[71] The goal of national defense is to prevent and overcome potential threats to the state, guarantee statehood, the ability and continuity of state power and the system, as well as to promote a responsible attitude of all Latvian residents towards the state and its security

[72] National Defense Strategy

[73] The goal of the National Defense Strategy is to make Latvia's defense strong enough so that any attack would cause significantly greater losses to the aggressor than the potential gains, thus deterring it from carrying out an attack

[74] Regardless of the balance of power, the National Armed Forces are ready to protect Latvia in the event of a military attack, and no potential aggressor should live under the illusion that Latvia's territory can be taken away without resistance or significant losses

[75] In addition to guarantees of collective defense, Latvian society must have a clear understanding that no one else will protect Latvia if Latvian society itself is not ready to do so

[76] Taking into account the development of modern technologies and methods of influencing society using the Internet and big data, deterrence can no longer be ensured only by military means

[77] It is also formed by the will of society to protect the country and timely preparation for shocks of various kinds

[78] The psychological resilience of society in peacetime is becoming an increasingly important factor in reducing external influence and ensuring deterrence

[79] Comprehensive defense is the best tool for building a resilient society

[80] Comprehensive defense will not only increase readiness to overcome the upheavals of peace and war, but will also reduce the distrust gap between the population and the state administration, as well as unite society as a whole

[81] National defense is based on four main areas of activity: • NATIONAL ARMED FORCES

[82] The goal of the National Armed Forces is to ensure the continuity of state power, the ability to make decisions and quickly respond to an unexpected attack, quickly localize hidden aggression, identify the threat, assess and eliminate it

[83] In the event of massive military aggression, the National Armed Forces must weaken and delay the enemy and cause him maximum losses

[84] In the event of the occupation of the state's territory, the National Armed Forces organize a public resistance movement

[85] • COMPREHENSIVE DEFENSE

[86] The goal of comprehensive national defense is to ensure that state institutions, public organizations and citizens are ready to provide support to the National Armed Forces and implement vital functions for the existence of society and the economy, as well as civil defense activities in times of war

[87] The resilience of society is based on psychological 9 resilience to external influences and timely preparation, so that during a crisis and war all members of society are informed, know their duties and the desired actions

[88] • NATO COLLECTIVE DEFENSE

[89] NATO unity, the presence of allied forces in Latvia and the reliability of the guarantees of Article 5 of the North Atlantic Treaty are the main elements of NATO collective defense, which ensure the deterrence of aggression against Latvia

[90] • INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION

[91] In order to ensure full-fledged deterrence and the state's readiness for external aggression, it is also essential to strengthen bilateral military cooperation, primarily with the USA and the countries of the Baltic Sea region

[94] STRATEGIC BASIC PRINCIPLES OF NATIONAL DEFENSE

[95] National Armed Forces The National Armed Forces (hereinafter – NAF) are the cornerstone of national defence

[96] The development of the NAF must be designed in such a way that it ensures the most effective defence against potential attack scenarios

[97] The defence system must be able to detect signs of threat in a timely manner

[98] In order to successfully implement national defence tasks, it is necessary to use the advantages of Latvia’s territory, hindering the enemy and inflicting maximum personnel losses on him, disrupting and destroying the enemy’s command and control and supply system, as well as ensuring the reception of allied reinforcements

[99] The operational core of the National Armed Forces consists of the Land Forces, including the National Guard and the Special Task Force

[100] The development of the NAF is aimed at ensuring that the units are: • mobile; • with high decentralization of command and control and supply; • with high survivability; • mutually integrated; • with high firepower; • resistant to electronic warfare attacks; • capable of maintaining the equipment at their disposal; • technologically advanced

[101] Within the framework of the existing defense funding, it is possible to develop only the most necessary capabilities

[102] Therefore, the development of capabilities such as medium-range air defense, active maritime defense and long-range indirect fire support will only be possible in the long term

[103] Therefore, the support of our allied countries is essential in reducing these shortcomings, including financial support for the development of capabilities

[261] The following are the basic elements for readiness to react sufficiently quickly: • preventive presence of Allied forces in threatened Member States; • available, sufficiently powerful, secured and rapidly deployable units; • operational defense plans prepared for the deployment of reinforcements; • effective political and military leadership; • rapid military mobility
