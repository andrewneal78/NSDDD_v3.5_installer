[543] The Honduran Army originated with the first Political Constitution, passed on December 11, 1825, which, in one of its articles, provided for the provision of an armed force for the defense of the state

[544] The wars during the Central American Federation, led by General Francisco Morazán, were an important element in the formation of the Honduran Army. [545] After the era of General Morazán, the Army was fragmented and led by the Departmental Arms Commanders, which made further organizational development impossible

[546] The threat posed in Central America by the filibuster William Walker motivated the participation of the Central American Armies to confront his claims, with the Honduran generals Florencio Xatruch and José Santos Guardiola standing out in these campaigns

[547] A new period of significant transformation occurred beginning in 1876, during the reformist government of Dr. Marco Aurelio Soto, with the creation of a second Military School under the direction of the French officer Guillermo Gallinier and the enactment of a modern set of laws. These efforts were thwarted by the internal wars that dominated the Honduran scene in the last quarter of the 19th century

[548] In the first decade of the 20th century, important changes were introduced in the Army, such as the acquisition of modern weapons, a new codification and organization, and the creation of a Military School that produced a generation of officers who brought greater dynamism to the professionalization of the Army, such as Generals Abel Villacorta and Vicente Tosta Carrasco
