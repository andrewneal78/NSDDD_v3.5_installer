[47] Basis of the Czech Republic's security policy

[48] The Czech Republic's security strategy understands the concept of security as a desirable state in which the risks to the Czech Republic arising from threats to: the population, sovereignty and territorial integrity, democratic system and principles of the rule of law, internal order, property, environment, fulfilment of international security obligations and other defined interests are reduced to the lowest level

[49] Security policy is a set of measures and steps for the purpose of preventing and eliminating threats and the risks arising from them with the aim of ensuring internal and external security, defence and protection of citizens and the state

[50] Security policy is implemented through foreign, defence and economic policy and policies in the area of internal security and public information, which are equivalent, coherent and interconnected

[51] In ensuring security, active and preventive action across the entire spectrum of security policy is important

[53] Terrorist attacks and counter-terrorist efforts confirm that in today's open and interconnected world, internal and external security must be ensured on the basis of a comprehensive approach

[54] The performance of the Czech economy and the level of international cooperation significantly determine the possibilities of the Czech Republic in ensuring its security

[55] Ensuring the security of citizens, a democratic system and the principles of the rule of law and the principles of a market economy in the Czech Republic are key prerequisites for effective economic development

[56] No less important factors for the development of the Czech Republic's economy (long-term and balanced GDP growth) are economic security and global economic stability

[57] The task of the Government of the Czech Republic (the government) and local government bodies at the regional and municipal levels, to the appropriate extent, is to ensure the security of citizens, the sovereignty of the country, the democratic system and the principles of the rule of law

[58] The security of the Czech Republic is based on the principle of ensuring the security of the individual, the protection of his life, health and property, and of state institutions, including their functionality

[59] Although the government is primarily responsible for ensuring security, active cooperation between the citizens of the Czech Republic and public administration bodies is desirable to reduce the risks of threats

[62] The security policy of the Czech Republic is based on the principle of the indivisibility of security

[63] The security of the Czech Republic cannot be separated from security in the Euro-Atlantic area, or from the global security situation

[64] The Czech Republic therefore has a permanent interest in maintaining the effective role of the UN and strengthening unity in the Euro-Atlantic security area

[65] The Czech Republic cannot be indifferent to the fate of other nations, countries and regions, and is therefore prepared to participate, within its capabilities, together with the international community, in solving security problems and mitigating their consequences

[66] The Czech Republic gives priority to active prevention of armed conflicts and preventive diplomacy

[85] The fulfilment of strategic interests serves to protect vital interests

[89] Other significant interests

[90] The purpose of fulfilling other significant interests is to contribute to ensuring vital and strategic interests, increasing the quality of life of citizens of the Czech Republic and the efficiency of public administration

[141] Trends in the security environment

[142] The Security Strategy uses two basic concepts in connection with the characterization of the security environment: threat and risk

[143] In the Security Strategy, the term threat is understood to mean any phenomenon that has the potential to harm the interests of the Czech Republic

[144] A threat can be natural, i.e. a phenomenon directly independent of human activity, or it can be caused by an actor endowed with will and intent - an individual, group, organization, state

[145] The Security Strategy takes into account threats resulting from intentional actions that can harm the interests and values of the Czech Republic

[146] In the Security Strategy, the term risk is understood as the possibility that an event will occur with a certain probability that we consider undesirable from a security perspective

[147] Risk is always derivable and derived from a specific threat

[148] The level of risk, i.e. the probability of harmful consequences resulting from the threat and the vulnerability of an interest, can be assessed on the basis of the so-called risk analysis, which is also based on an assessment of our readiness to face threats

[149] The threat assessment shows that a massive military attack against the Czech Republic is unlikely in the medium term

[150] However, as a result of the simultaneous and mutually supporting effects of a number of negative trends, the security situation at the global level has deteriorated in recent times, which also has consequences for security in the Euro-Atlantic area

[151] Trends result in threats that are difficult to predict

[152] Their emergence and rapid spread are facilitated by globalization

[153] The perpetrators are increasingly non-state actors (traditional and new terrorist organizations, radical religious, sectarian and extremist movements and groups) who create purposeful alliances with each other or with totalitarian, dictatorial and ideologically intolerant regimes

[250] As part of the fight against terrorism, the armed forces will focus on increasing the specific capabilities and effectiveness of military intelligence

[251] In accordance with the legal order of the Czech Republic, they will also be prepared to provide effective assistance to the Police of the Czech Republic and other elements of the security system in the event of a non-military threat

[252] An integral part of ensuring the defense of the Czech Republic is the preparation of citizens, the scope of which will be part of the defense plans prepared by individual bodies of the security system

[253] The armed forces are permanently involved in the alliance air defense system (NATINEADS) and are prepared to receive possible alliance reinforcements on the territory of the Czech Republic

[254] The Czech Army also allocates and prepares a part of its forces for NATO and EU operations responding to crisis situations (enforcement, support, or peacekeeping outside Article 5 of the Washington Treaty

[255] The limits for the formation of a contingent are set by the Military Strategy of the Czech Republic

[256] The composition, size and duration of the contingent will be adjusted according to current operational requirements and the economic capabilities of the state

[257] The ongoing reform of the armed forces is aimed at achieving the necessary capabilities to fulfill the set defense and security tasks and obligations

[258] An integral part of the reform is the transition to full professionalization of the armed forces

[259] The obligations of state administration bodies, territorial self-government and other elements of the security system in ensuring defense are set out in the legal order of the Czech Republic, which also enshrines the principle of democratic control and civilian management of the armed forces

[260] Civilian and democratic control of the armed forces includes a regular and transparent assessment of their readiness and the effectiveness of defense expenditures

[261] The government will submit the assessment to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for information

[262] Internal security policy

[263] The main objective of the internal security policy is to formulate and implement principles aimed at protecting the democratic foundations of the state, ensuring internal security and public order in the Czech Republic

[264] Through institutions authorized by law, the government continuously analyzes the situation in the area of internal security and public order in the Czech Republic, identifies threats, assesses risks and initiates proposals for their effective elimination, and strives to promote strategies and effective measures of both non-legislative and legislative nature

[265] All elements of the security system actively participate in ensuring internal security

[266] The government places great emphasis on the professionalism and high quality of the relevant security forces and bodies involved in criminal proceedings

[267] Responsibility and support of citizens play a very important role

[268] The Czech Republic actively fights against all forms of terrorism

[269] In response to the events of September 11, 2001 in the USA, a National Action Plan to Combat Terrorism was developed, the aim of which is to maximally reduce the vulnerability of the Czech Republic to terrorist attacks directed against its territory or its interests abroad and to the entire spectrum of terrorist acts

[270] The Czech Republic is prepared to adopt systemic preventive measures in the fight against terrorism

[271] An important task of the country's security forces is to combat all forms of organized crime

[272] The Czech Republic focuses on creating legislative and organizational conditions that will make it more difficult and, if possible, eliminate the possibility of legalizing the proceeds of this type of crime and enable their subsequent confiscation

[273] The Czech Republic strives to significantly limit all forms of illegal migration and related illegal activities, both through measures in the field of international cooperation and internal measures

[274] The Czech Republic consistently harmonizes and applies the EU's migration, visa and asylum policy

[275] The full implementation of the Schengen Agreements remains a priority

[276] The Czech Republic's priorities in the area of internal security include suppressing the distribution, sale and abuse of narcotics and psychotropic substances as one of the main activities of international organised crime

[277] The Czech Republic intends to do so through balanced preventive and repressive activities and effective international cooperation

[278] The government is also intensively focusing on the fight against corruption and serious economic crime, which are one of the tools for organised crime to penetrate public administration and which threaten economic competition and the basic principles of a democratic system

[279] The Czech Republic considers the creation of legislative prerequisites for preventing corruption in public administration and the formulation of an anti-corruption programme aimed at assessing corruption risks associated with the activities of elected representatives of the state to be one of its priority tasks

[280] The successful implementation of anti-corruption measures is unthinkable without the support of citizens

[281] The Czech Republic strives to consistently detect and punish manifestations of xenophobia, racism and political extremism

[282] Using preventive and repressive measures, it strives for the maximum reduction of visible and latent forms of extremism

[283] It attributes a significant role to preventive measures for the protection of members of national and ethnic minorities with the aim of supporting conflict-free and tolerant multicultural coexistence

[284] Educational activities in the field of human rights and multicultural education and the systematic approach of state authorities to the integration of foreigners make a significant contribution to the prevention of xenophobic attitudes in the public

[285] The Czech Republic suppresses illegal or undesirable trade in weapons, military equipment and dual-use materials

[286] It pays increased attention to measures against the proliferation of WMD and transfers of technologies and services that can be used in their development, production, storage and other related activities

[287] The Czech Republic adopts and improves measures for the protection and sharing of classified information

[288] In the technically and financially demanding areas of security of communication and information systems, it intends to continue to strive for the protection of important information systems and critical infrastructure and to consistently combat information crime

[289] Great emphasis is currently placed on the very sensitive areas of intellectual property protection and personnel security

[290] The Government will continue to continuously monitor and analyze the situation in the area of public order, identify risk factors and initiate proposals for solutions not only in the area of repression, but also in the area of crime prevention

[291] Of key importance in the area of public order is primarily strengthening the safety of citizens, including by reducing crime and road traffic accidents and their negative impacts on citizens

[292] The Government will continue to promote prevention and create capacities to protect public health

[293] These measures are intended to prevent the emergence and spread of, and limit the occurrence of, infectious, especially fatal diseases

[294] Individual components of the integrated rescue system and other components involved in ensuring the internal security of the state and the protection of the population must be able to respond professionally and, in cooperation with other entities, intervene effectively in the event of an emergency or crisis situation caused by terrorist attacks, natural and ecological disasters, industrial accidents, accidents and other dangers that threaten lives, health, property, the environment, internal security or public order in the Czech Republic

[295] The Czech Republic is constantly developing the concept of crisis management and the methodology of crisis planning

[296] It provides expert support for planning, preparation, coordination and unification of procedures of state and local government bodies, legal entities and natural persons doing business in their preparation for crisis situations

[297] Crisis situations in which the risk of disruption of internal security and public order increases significantly increasingly require coordinated international cooperation

[298] In accordance with its international obligations and in the spirit of international solidarity, the Czech Republic is determined to effectively assist in resolving crisis situations outside the territory of the state

[299] The Czech Republic will continue to adopt measures aimed at increasing the quality of activities and results of bodies operating in the field of internal security and public order and their mutual coordination

[300] The government is ready to use the potential of other security actors, such as security, health and social services, the private sector or volunteers

[301] It will also consistently promote the integration of mechanisms for combating corruption, racism or extremism into all executive components in the field of internal security and public order

[302] In the following period, conditions will be systematically created for effective coordination of individual bodies, such as conditions for better coordination of the security needs of higher territorial self-governing units with the activities of the Police of the Czech Republic or strengthening cooperation between the intelligence services of the Czech Republic (intelligence services) and the Police of the Czech Republic

[303] Public support, cooperation with non-governmental organizations and the security community are important for the successful implementation of plans in the field of internal security

[304] In order to ensure the necessary professional standard in solving conceptual and strategic tasks, the government supports science, research and education in the field of internal security

[305] Economic policy and ensuring security

[306] Economic policy creates conditions for ensuring the security of the state, eliminates existing and potential security risks in the country's economy and in external economic relations, and fundamentally influences the production of resources to ensure the country's security

[307] The economy of the Czech Republic is open, market-based, macroeconomically stabilized and involved in international trade and financial relations, which creates a prerequisite for the country's sustainable economic growth

[308] Economic policy will continue to strengthen macroeconomic stability by creating an acceptable economic climate: • by reforming public finances with the aim of reducing external and internal debt, thereby stabilizing public budgets in the long term; • by the state's efforts to maintain a stable price level through appropriately designed and implemented macroeconomic policies; • by continuing the transformation process in the economy with regard to the security interests of the state; • by ensuring the financial and foreign exchange economy of the state

[309] An important task of economic policy is to minimize potential sources for the emergence of threats and the actions of their carriers

[310] This concerns in particular: • maximum transparency of financial flows in the country's economy; • prevention of monopolization of key segments of the Czech economy; • permanent provision of human, material and financial resources to maintain the security of the state, with regard to its economic capabilities; • implementation of consistent financial control by financial offices and financial directorates and, in the area of import, export and transit, by financial control in cooperation with the Customs Administration; • finalization of legal norms enabling the blocking of bank accounts of entities suspected of supporting terrorism; • creation of conditions for increasing economic performance, i.e. conditions for entrepreneurship, protection of investments and exports

[311] Economic policy also aims to minimize the dependence of the Czech economy on supplies from economically or politically unstable areas

[312] To this end, it is necessary to: • ensure diversification of sources and transport routes of strategic raw materials; • through a suitably designed industrial, energy and raw materials policy of the state, create conditions for reducing the energy and raw materials intensity of the Czech economy and ensure the effective and economically advantageous use of domestic primary energy resources; • through timely preventive measures, ensure food security and food supply in crisis situations; • minimize the impacts on the economy in the event of fluctuations in the supply of strategic raw materials; • support the development of the defense industrial base and the participation of industry in covering the needs of the armed forces, armed security forces, rescue services and emergency services with an emphasis on flexibility and the ability to respond quickly to the changing security environment

[313] The Czech Republic is constantly developing a system of economic measures for crisis situations, creating and maintaining an optimal range of necessary commodities and production capacities

[314] The Czech Republic intends to continue to maintain the capabilities that make it possible, if necessary, to mobilize sufficient material and financial resources to resolve crisis situations

[315] Public information policy

[316] The Government is aware of the need to actively inform the public about security and developments in the security environment and about the measures that the Government and other bodies and institutions in this area are taking to ensure the security of citizens and the entire Czech Republic

[317] This need is based on the belief that increasing public awareness and mutual communication will lead to a better understanding of security issues by the public and to improved feedback, as well as more active participation of citizens in preventing and solving individual security problems

[318] The elements of the legislative, executive and judicial branches of government and local government, which are responsible for ensuring the security of the Czech Republic, must inform the public in a targeted and systematic manner and cooperate with it

[319] Every citizen must have enough information to form their own responsible attitude

[320] It is desirable that the public be continuously informed about the opinions and motivations that lead legislative bodies and representatives of the executive branch and local government to take the measures necessary to ensure the security of the Czech Republic

[321] This requires that the public be systematically and in advance informed about threats and risks arising from them, as well as about the measures taken and the consequences resulting from them

[322] In the area of public information, it is necessary for state and local government bodies to take the following steps in particular: • strengthen cooperation with the media and, through them, provide the public with more up-to-date information on security; • support the creation and operation of the public administration portal as a single point for providing important information needed to ensure state security, both for the public and for individual elements of the security system; • support the system for providing information in the area of security, important for decision-making by individual elements of the security system, through the public administration portal; • support the development of a communication infrastructure to ensure citizens' access to information intended for the public; • use various forms of communication and create conditions for individual communication with individual citizens, especially at the local level; • periodically publish assessments of threats and risks; • presented periodic reports on the state of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic at all levels; • published other non-periodic documents and publications; • supported studies in the field of security at universities (lectures, financial contributions, etc.); • supported educational programs on security topics; • presented and drew attention to issues related to security within their scope of competence; • continued to present the results of the activities of intelligence services; • created conditions for communication with the security community, especially at the local level, with the aim of actively involving this level; • placed special emphasis on educating youth and used state targeted subsidies, grant policy, preventive systems and activities for this purpose

[323] These and other forms of public information will create conditions for the active participation of the public in ensuring the security of the Czech Republic and at the same time for its awareness that its cooperation is necessary to ensure security

[324] Security system of the Czech Republic

[325] The current security system is functional and reflects several years of development

[326] In its creation, it was based on Czech traditions and experience, and at the same time, the knowledge of comparable European democracies was used

[327] The main principles of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic are defined in the constitutional order and related legal regulations

[328] The security system is an institutional tool for the creation and implementation of the security policy of the Czech Republic

[329] It operates within the Czech Republic, but at the same time it is closely connected with NATO and the EU and other international institutions (UN, OSCE, World Health Organization), which ensures its compatibility and interoperability with alliance and other, primarily European, security systems

[330] The aim of the security system is to ensure the security of the Czech Republic, protect and promote the vital, strategic and other important interests of the Czech Republic

[331] Its basic function is to manage and coordinate the activities of individual elements in ensuring the security interests of the Czech Republic and in times of direct threat or in the event of a crisis situation

[332] The security system creates qualified documents for updating the state's security policy and for decision-making by its individual elements

[333] To ensure the functioning of the security system, uniform and coordinated procedures have been established at all levels, which are based on valid legislation and respect international treaties, political principles and obligations of the Czech Republic

[334] An integral part of the functioning of the security system is the systematic preparation and preventive action of its individual elements

[335] The security system is made up of elements with the relevant competences set out in valid laws

[336] These elements are centrally managed and coordinated, or they may also operate independently at individual levels within the scope of their competence

[337] They must adequately and promptly respond to an emerging crisis situation and be able to respond conceptually and in the long term to the changing security environment

[338] The readiness of individual elements of the security system is verified primarily through exercises

[339] Individual elements of the security system create a hierarchical structure in which mutual functional relationships and competences in ensuring the security of the Czech Republic at all levels are defined by general rules, principles and specific procedures

[340] The entire structure enables a rapid transition from a normal to a crisis state, which at the same time creates the prerequisites for successful and organized management of the initial stage of resolving a crisis situation

[341] The security system is made up of the relevant elements of the legislative, executive and judicial branches, territorial self-government, as well as legal and natural persons who are responsible for ensuring the security of the Czech Republic

[342] The structure of the security system includes in particular the President of the Republic, the Parliament of the Czech Republic, the Government, the State Security Council (SSC) and its working bodies, central administrative authorities, regional and municipal authorities and their executive crisis management bodies, as well as the armed forces, armed security forces, intelligence services, rescue services, rescue services and emergency services

[343] The President of the Republic has his powers in terms of state security given by the Constitution of the Czech Republic and other applicable legislation

[344] He is the commander-in-chief of the armed forces

[345] He has a special position in relation to the Government and the SSC

[346] In addition to approving laws relating to state security, the Parliament of the Czech Republic is responsible for co-decision-making on the orientation of the state's security policy, deciding on the declaration of a state of threat to the state, a state of war and the participation of the Czech Republic in the defense systems of international organizations of which we are members, and giving consent to the deployment of armed forces outside the territory of the republic and the presence of foreign troops on the territory of the republic

[347] The Government is the supreme executive body of state power, which implements the state's security policy

[348] The Government is responsible for the management and functionality of the entire security system

[349] It is authorized to declare a state of emergency in the event of serious crisis situations that significantly threaten lives, health, property or internal order and security

[350] The Government, under specified conditions, decides on the deployment of armed forces outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the presence of armed forces of other states on the territory of the Czech Republic, for a maximum period of 60 days

[351] The BRS is a permanent working body of the government in the area of security, coordinates and evaluates this issue and prepares proposals for measures to ensure it for the government

[352] In the area of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic, the BRS may request information and analyses from all ministers and heads of other administrative offices and assign them tasks related to ensuring the security of the Czech Republic

[353] The BRS assesses and subsequently submits to the Government of the Czech Republic for discussion a regular report on the state of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic with proposals for measures

[354] It cooperates with the regional security councils

[355] It is designed as a body for preparing measures in this area, but also for resolving the crisis situation that has arisen

[356] The BRS system includes its permanent interdepartmental working bodies: • The Committee for the Coordination of Foreign Security Policy, which coordinates the foreign security policy of the Czech Republic nationally

[357] It is under the responsibility of the Minister of Foreign Affairs; • The Committee for Defence Planning, which coordinates the planning of measures to ensure the defence of the Czech Republic

[358] It is under the responsibility of the Minister of Defence; • Committee for Civil Emergency Planning, coordinating the planning of measures to ensure the protection of the internal security of the state, the population and the economy and the requirements for civilian resources necessary to ensure the security of the Czech Republic

[359] It is under the responsibility of the Minister of the Interior; • Committee for Intelligence, coordinating the activities of the intelligence services of the Czech Republic and planning measures to ensure intelligence activities and cooperation between state authorities

[360] The committee does not independently develop intelligence activities

[361] It is under the responsibility of the Prime Minister of the Czech Republic

[362] The Central Crisis Staff (ÚKŠ) is also included in the BRS system, which is the working body of the Government of the Czech Republic for resolving crisis situations

[363] The ÚKŠ is activated in times of direct threat or when a crisis situation arises and submits proposals for its resolution to the BRS or, in the event of danger of delay, directly to the Government of the Czech Republic

[364] Depending on the nature of the crisis situation, the Prime Minister appoints the Minister of the Interior or the Minister of Defence as the Chairman of the ÚKŠ

[365] The tasks of ministries and other central administrative authorities in the area of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic are based on applicable laws and other legal norms

[366] Ministries and other administrative authorities shall establish crisis staffs as their working bodies for resolving crisis situations

[367] At the level of regions and designated municipalities, regional security councils and municipal security councils shall act as coordinating bodies for preparing for crisis situations

[368] The working body of the governor or mayor for resolving crisis situations shall be the regional crisis staffs in the regions and the municipal crisis staffs in the municipalities

[369] A structure of flood authorities of municipalities and river basins shall be created for preparing for and resolving floods, which shall be under the umbrella of the Central Flood Commission, under the authority of the Minister of the Environment

[370] These bodies shall manage, control, coordinate and, if necessary, assign tasks in the entire scope of flood protection management

[371] In order to prevent and combat dangerous diseases, a Central Disease Commission shall be created under the authority of the Minister of Agriculture

[372] In the event of a state of emergency, flood and disease commissions are integrated into crisis staffs at the relevant levels of management and become part of them

[373] Public health protection in the event of impending or existing epidemics is ensured by public health authorities, which are primarily the Ministry of Health of the Czech Republic and regional hygiene stations

[374] In the event of a pandemic, a special working group is established under the Ministry of Health of the Czech Republic

[375] The main executive elements of the security system are the armed forces, armed security forces, rescue services, rescue services and emergency services, while these elements are also components of the integrated rescue system when ensuring rescue and liquidation work

[376] Specific tasks in ensuring security at the municipal level are performed by the municipal police, which is a municipal body established by the municipal council

[377] Intelligence services play an irreplaceable role in obtaining, collecting and evaluating information important for the security of the Czech Republic, for whose activities and coordination the government is responsible

[378] Early identification of potential security threats and quantification of security risks is ensured in particular by regular tasking and coordination of intelligence services, which also ensures a systematic exchange of information and analyses between intelligence services, the government and executive elements ensuring the security of the Czech Republic

[379] The diplomatic service of the Czech Republic plays a significant role in ensuring the security interests of the Czech Republic

[380] Private security services of the Czech Republic and volunteer activities carried out by non-governmental organizations or individual volunteers also play an important role

[381] The security system must constantly respond to developments and changes in the field of security, and therefore it is necessary to continuously update and improve it

[382] For this purpose, the Government of the Czech Republic is preparing a concept of the security system, in which it emphasizes its effectiveness and simplification and the improvement of cooperation and coordination between individual elements and areas of the security system

[383] In this context, the Government is promoting further improvement of mutual relations at individual levels and between central, regional and municipal elements and strengthening cooperation between security councils and crisis staffs of all levels

[384] At the same time, it is promoting improved coordination in the area of internal security and clarification of the executive powers of the BRS and the ÚKŠ in times of crisis situations, strengthening coordination of intelligence services, including cooperation with our allies and friends

[385] Based on the experience from the 2002 floods, the Government is preparing a reassessment of the system of flood commissions, particularly at the regional level

[386] The Security Strategy of the Czech Republic is a document that reflects the security interests and needs of the Czech Republic in the context of the developing security environment and must respond flexibly to its significant changes

[387] The Government will regularly evaluate the state of ensuring the security of the Czech Republic in two-year cycles, or based on current needs, through the Report on Ensuring the Security of the Czech Republic

[388] In this context, it will assess the development of risks in the security area

[389] In the event of fundamental changes, the Security Strategy of the Czech Republic will be updated

[390] The first Report on the State of Security in the Czech Republic will be prepared within two years of the approval of this Security Strategy and submitted to the government for approval via the BRS
