[12] MILITARY STRATEGY BASELINES

[14] The international environment is characterized by the overcoming of global bloc confrontation

[15] The consequence of its demise is a significant reduction, if not complete elimination, of the possibility of a general war breaking out

[18] The emphasis on preventing potential destabilizing factors and the simultaneous interconnection of internal and external, military and non-military threats to the security of the state requires that the armed forces be prepared to cope with a wide range of possible risks and threats, including those that are still difficult to predict and unknown in both their scope and consequences

[19] Security risks and the resulting threats to the security of the state differ in the degree of probability of their emergence and activation, the scope and nature of the threat, and the time that the armed forces have available for preparation for use

[41] DESTINATION AND BASIC TASKS OF THE ARMED FORCES OF THE CZECH REPUBLIC

[42] The Armed Forces of the Czech Republic are divided into the Army of the Czech Republic, the Military Office of the President of the Republic and the Castle Guard

[43] They consist of soldiers on active duty and soldiers off active duty

[44] They are intended to defend the Czech Republic against external attack and to fulfill tasks arising from the international contractual obligations of the Czech Republic on joint defense against attack

[45] They cooperate with foreign armed forces on the basis of international treaties, participate in the activities of international organizations by participating in peacekeeping operations, rescue and humanitarian actions and take part in military exercises together with foreign armed forces on the territory of the Czech Republic or abroad

[46] They can be used to guard objects important for the defense of the state, to fulfill tasks in ensuring the protection of state borders, to ensure internal order and security, to perform rescue work and humanitarian tasks in the event of disasters or other serious situations threatening lives, health or significant property values

[47] They participate in supporting security in Europe through partnership programs of understanding and trust between the armed forces of European democratic states with an emphasis on Central and Eastern Europe
