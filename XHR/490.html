[15] Terminology Terms and definitions of terms used in the context of this Policy are given in Annex 1 of the Policy. INTRODUCTION The defense system of BiH The defense system of BiH is part of the security system of BiH

[16] This system represents a unique form of organization, resources and activities of the institutions of BiH and the Armed Forces of BiH aimed at achieving defined state defense goals, preparations for defense, state defense and assistance to the civilian population, and collective security activities within the framework of international security and defense organizations

[17] The defense system of BiH is the conceptual and regulatory basis from which all other subsystems, resources, functions and defense plans are developed, aligned with the Law on Defense of BiH and the Defense Policy of BiH

[18] Within the framework of the defense system, Bosnia and Herzegovina organizes, maintains and develops the capacity and readiness of the Armed Forces of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the defense institutions that are responsible for the management, leadership and command of the Armed Forces of Bosnia and Herzegovina in the chain of command and control

[19] The main subsystems of the defense system are the Defense Planning System and the Defense Resource Management System

[20] These systems are mutually interactive, supported by functional and operational defense systems and linked by a unique System of Planning, Programming, Budgeting and Execution (SPPBI

[21] Defense planning system The defense planning system consists of and is implemented by all defense structures, and it includes resources, documents, procedures, procedures and activities related to defense military visions, policies, strategies, doctrines, guidelines, plans and priorities

[22] As a form of organization, resources and activities of the defense institutions of Bosnia and Herzegovina, this System represents a continuous process of assessing defense capabilities and creating documents of interest to the security and defense of the state

[23] The basic role of the Defense Planning System is the transformation of the strategic instructions of the state leadership into defense plans and other documents that optimally direct and control the use of the available resources of the BiH defense institutions, and define the development of forces

[24] This process, starting from recognized security and defense goals, identifies BiH's interests, challenges and risks it faces, defense goals and strategy, and results in assessments of defense capabilities and priorities for funding in the field of defense

[30] Principles of defense planning As a process that ensures the creation and implementation of documents of importance for the defense of the state, defense planning is based on the following basic principles: - legality (based on laws and by-laws), - efficiency (timeliness, speed, accuracy and positive results), - affordability (compliance with state capabilities and defense needs), - economy (efficiency with the least costs), - applicability (reality and possibility of implementation), - rationality (efficiency and economy of use of available resources), - transparency (availability of relevant data to the authorities and the public), - continuity (uninterruption of processes), - NATO interoperability (compliance with NATO norms and common practices

[31] The aforementioned principles are the basis on which defense planning and the structure of the Armed Forces of Bosnia and Herzegovina are based

[32] This means that the forces created as a result of the defense planning process are affordable and capable of effectively confronting the defense challenges that BiH is facing

[33] The requirement to fulfill the stated principles establishes a necessary connection and interaction between the Defense Planning System and the Defense Resource Management System

[34] Objectives of defense planning The end result of defense planning is a comprehensive and consistent set of system documents on the basis of which defense institutions plan and execute legal obligations and assigned tasks

[35] The priority goal of the defense planning process is the definition, creation and implementation of the main elements of the Defense Planning System

Defense policy of BiH The defense policy of BiH provides an assessment of the security environment and an assessment of security challenges and risks in the field of defense, defines the goals of BiH in the field of defense and the goal and principles of the defense policy, considers collective security integrations, international cooperation in the field of defense and the defense system of BiH, and provides a vision of the development of the defense system of BiH

[106] Concept of development The system of defense planning in the defense institutions of BiH will generally develop according to the following concept: * defense planning is carried out as: - strategic, - operational, - executive defense planning

[107] Strategic defense planning defines the security and defense goals of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the means of achieving them

[108] The documents of strategic defense planning are adopted by the competent state institutions of the legislative and executive authorities

[109] The Ministry of Defense of BiH participates in or is the lead in the development of proposals for these documents

[110] In accordance with their legal competences, strategic defense planning documents can also be adopted by the defense institutions of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Operational defense planning defines priority and priority tasks or activities of defense institutions in order to achieve strategic goals in the field of security and defense

[111] Executive defense planning directs the execution of priority and priority tasks or activities of defense institutions, and ensures the management of available defense resources in order to achieve as complete as possible strategic goals in the field of security and defense

[112] Operational and executive defense planning, in accordance with their competences, is carried out by defense structures subordinated to the Minister of Defense in the chain of command and control

[113] defense planning is realized through the following phases: - preparatory phase, - phase of making the plan-document, - phase of approval of the plan-document, - phase of execution of the plan-document, - phase of analysis of planning and execution of the plan-document

[114] Within the framework of the mentioned phases, the identification of defense challenges, risks and threats, the definition of defense goals and tasks, the definition of defense resources and the distribution of resources for the execution of tasks are carried out

[115] Application of the mentioned phases by levels of defense planning is carried out in accordance with needs and possibilities

[116] Their application should ensure efficient development and management of defense resources in response to identified challenges and risks

[117] according to the continuity of implementation, defense planning is divided into: - regular planning, which is carried out in order to carry out ongoing tasks and tasks from the competence and scope of work of the defense institutions of BiH, - special planning, which is carried out in order to carry out other significant activities of the defense institutions of BiH, limited in scope and duration, and which directly contribute to the realization of legal obligations, assigned tasks and set goals

[118] according to the time frame for which it is carried out, defense planning is divided into: - short-term planning (1-2 years), which includes programming and planning of jobs and tasks, the annual defense budget, executive and other guidelines, operational concepts, projects and activities of importance for the defense system, and all other individual short-term activities of the defense institutions of BiH, - medium-term planning (3-5 years), which includes the framework defense budget, policies, strategies and doctrines in the defense system, and medium-term plans and programs, - long-term planning (6 and more years), which includes a defense review, and plans and other documents that define long-term capabilities, activities, the vision of the development of defense structures and goals in the field of defense in peace, war and emergency. * documents in the Defense Planning System are divided into: - conceptual (defining the basic defense vision, goals, principles and priorities of action), - normative (regulating activity in a certain area of the defense system or in the system as a whole, including tasks, competences, and the rights and obligations of the bearer and participant of the activity), - doctrinal (defining the way of using all elements of defense structures in achieving the set visions and goals, and in the execution of assigned tasks), and - planning (specify the bearers, participants and deadlines for the realization of the activities of fulfilling the defined vision, goals and assigned tasks according to the established priorities of action
