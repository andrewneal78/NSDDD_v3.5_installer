[37] GENERAL PROVISIONS II

[38] Principles of national security policy

[39] The national security strategy is based on the principles of: • rule of law; • mutual commitment and consistency in the relations between security and fundamental rights and freedoms of citizens; • dialogue and expanded cooperation between citizens, society and the state; • trust between state institutions and cooperation with the private sector, non-governmental organizations and citizens, with NATO and EU allies and with partner countries; • national consensus on national security policy; • inseparability of national security from the security of NATO and the EU; • proactivity and coordination in the activities of state institutions and organizations in accordance with their competence; • implementation of the complex inter-institutional approach in building the national security system; • openness, transparency and responsibility in the formation and implementation of security policies; • effectiveness and efficiency of management and executive activities; • democratic control over the national security system

[40] National interests

[41] Vital interests are: • guaranteeing the rights, freedoms, security, health and well-being of the citizen, society and the state

[42] • preserving the sovereignty, territorial integrity of the country and the unity of the nation; • protecting the constitutionally established order and democratic values; • protecting the population and critical infrastructure in the event of crises, disasters, accidents, catastrophes and other risks and threats; • preserving and developing the national identity, built on the basis of Bulgarian citizenship; • guaranteeing the integrity of Bulgarian civil society; • overcoming negative demographic processes, significant disparities in the development of the regions and building a socio-economic environment, providing conditions for the development of generations of Bulgarian citizens, capable of guaranteeing the Republic of Bulgaria a worthy place in the EU and in global political, economic, financial and social processes; • development of education, upbringing, science and scientific-applied activities in the spirit of national and pan-European values

[43] Other important interests are: • a favorable and predictable security environment; • effective functioning of Bulgarian institutions together with those of the EU and NATO to achieve our collective security and improve its condition through the development of interoperability; • maintaining good neighborly relations and ensuring regional security and stability; • strengthening the participation of NATO and the EU in security policies and mechanisms in the Black Sea region and continuing the integration processes of the countries of the region, which have set themselves the relevant goals, into European and Euro-Atlantic structures; • further stabilization and full inclusion in the Euro-Atlantic and European political and economic space of the countries of the Western Balkans; • development of regional cooperation in the Danube region and strengthening the active role of the Republic of Bulgaria in a bilateral and multilateral plan in support of peace and security in the region and for the implementation of economic programs and infrastructure projects of regional and pan-European importance in the context of the EU Strategy for the Danube Region; • ensuring energy security through diversification of energy types, sources and routes of supply of energy and other strategic raw materials; • ensuring economic, financial and social stability and economic prosperity; • protecting public health; • preventing and combating corruption and organized crime; • limiting attacks on the person and property; • developing and ensuring a secure cyberspace; • protecting the environment and rational use of natural resources

[392] Such an analysis is also carried out when conducting a strategic review of national security

[393] A priority of the defense policy is the development of a modern defense institution, based on an integrated system for effective defense management under civilian and parliamentary control

[394] This requires the implementation of: • modern defense management, based on the effective use of resources, ensuring the connection between long-term planning and the achievement of set results, in accordance with the principles of accountability and transparency; • planning based on capabilities and probable scenarios for their use, taking into account the specific risks and threats identified by the intelligence community; • technological rearmament of the defense system and the armed forces, applying the experience of participation in missions and operations, achievements and results of defense research; • training and education of people in defense in accordance with modern alliance standards; • increasing our participation in structures, programs and projects together with our allies and partners within the framework of NATO agencies, the European Defense Agency and the implementation of the EU program for security research and innovation; creating maximum opportunities for the inclusion of the national defense technological and industrial base and research/innovation capabilities; • a conceptual approach to filling the gap in defense capabilities, maintaining the system of doctrinal documents of the armed forces up to date and harmonizing them with alliance publications; • a project approach to implementing investment projects for the modernization and rearmament of the armed forces, defined and managed in a single Portfolio, by applying the principles of good management of defense resources, comprehensive planning, delegation of rights and responsibilities, coordination, monitoring and control of all aspects of the project

[395] Cybersecurity Policy

[396] The Republic of Bulgaria implements a single national cybersecurity policy by protecting critical communication and information infrastructure and enabling citizens, businesses and public institutions to process and exchange digital information freely and reliably

[397] Our country actively participates in the formation and implementation of policies for cybersecurity within the EU and NATO and strives to achieve cyber resilience of the entire society and state through the creation of a sustainable, comprehensive and flexible National Cybersecurity and Cyber Crisis Management System

[398] The cybersecurity policy should provide: • a mechanism for coordinated actions at the political and strategic level to develop the necessary capacity and capabilities for cybersecurity and resilience; • an up-to-date comprehensive cyber picture and understanding of the situation in cyberspace at the national level; • interaction for effective and coordinated prevention, response and recovery

[399] Crisis management

[400] Crisis management is an integral part of national security and an essential element of its protection activities

[401] The strategic goal of the crisis management policy is to prevent their occurrence, to limit the negative consequences as much as possible and to quickly restore the functioning of state and public life in the event of a crisis

[402] Crises are inherently political, military-political or humanitarian

[403] The main reasons for their occurrence are political contradictions, armed conflicts, industrial accidents or natural disasters

[404] The transition from a reactive approach to crisis management, focusing on responding to an already existing crisis, to a proactive approach to crisis management, covering the entire process – before, during and after the crisis, is essential for building sustainable institutions and civil society

[405] Effective management of crises, disasters, accidents and catastrophes to protect citizens, property, critical infrastructure and the environment and documents from the National Archives Fund is achieved through: • building capacity to assess risks and impact them through planning and implementing measures for prevention, preparedness, response and recovery in ministries, departments, municipalities, commercial companies and sole proprietors; • building and developing early warning systems for crises, disasters, accidents and catastrophes throughout the country

[406] The implementation of the strategic objective of the crisis management policy is based on building a sustainable, comprehensive and flexible National Crisis Management System

[407] The system provides the necessary institutional framework and tools for coordination and interdepartmental cooperation in the implementation of crisis management responsibilities

[408] The Council of Ministers forms the policy and carries out the general management of crisis management activities

[409] The national crisis management policy is based on: • preparation of the country for defense and defense mobilization preparation of the population; • effective preliminary preparation of society for action in crises; • use of a modern and sustainable early warning system, including the national notification system; • effective interdepartmental cooperation and coordination; • implementation of continuous monitoring of potential and developing crises; • constant improvement and harmonization of the regulatory framework, based on the comprehensive approach with the use of all available resources of the country at all levels - union, national, regional and local; • building a flexible mechanism for accelerated deployment of military capabilities and expertise for crisis response, in cases of lack of a civilian alternative; • maintaining a sufficient stock of resources to ensure the autonomy of society during crises; • interaction and cooperation in the field of crisis management at the regional and international level; • availability of a communication strategy in the form of coordinated, timely, clear, transparent and widely accessible information about what is happening, about the actions taken, expected results and subsequent development; • promotion of the use of insurance as a tool for providing additional financing for the purpose of faster and more effective recovery after crises, disasters and accidents, which will ensure the necessary adaptability of the national security system

[410] The National Crisis Management System includes: National Situational Center, departmental and regional situational centers and others, attracted in crisis conditions

[411] The National Crisis Management System guarantees activities to prevent and manage crises on the territory of the country or outside it - in fulfillment of obligations arising from international treaties to which the Republic of Bulgaria is a party

[412] V. NATIONAL SECURITY SYSTEM V

[413] Main guidelines for the operation of the national security system

[414] The system aims to maintain national security in an optimal state, to increase the manageability of state bodies with the active involvement of citizens and their organizations and to function effectively to implement this strategy

[415] Citizen security is a basic criterion for its effectiveness

[416] The goals of the strategy can be achieved only in the presence of a normatively regulated and institutionally organized process of dialogue, consultation and interaction between the legislative, executive and judicial branches, the President of the Republic, citizens and their organizations

[417] The high quality of dialogue, consultations and interaction between the three branches of government and the elements for the protection of the national security system can be achieved by developing and implementing a unified system of standards describing the basic concepts and principles of work

[418] The presence of these standards will increase the interoperability and efficiency of work

[419] Laws and by-laws in the field of national security are improved in accordance with the dynamics of public relations and in accordance with the principles and guidelines specified in the strategy

[420] The regulatory framework is established and is being developed with the aim of achieving a unified legal space in the field of national security, in which contradictions between legal norms, institutional and functional structures and mechanisms are not allowed to arise

[421] Citizens and their organizations are users of the national security system and at the same time actively participate according to their capabilities in its functioning

[422] The state ensures the possible transparency of the system towards citizens and assists in the development of the capacity of civil society to carry out activities in the interest of national security

[423] Of particular importance is the balance between the fundamental rights and freedoms of citizens and the restrictions caused by the requirements for guaranteeing national security

[424] The formation of the state national security policy is the responsibility of the National Assembly, the Council of Ministers and the President of the Republic

[425] In the field of national security, the National Assembly adopts laws, the National Security Strategy, doctrines and other documents within its competence, with which it sets the parameters of the national security policy

[426] It assesses its status and the fulfillment of obligations by state institutions

[427] It makes recommendations to the bodies of state power and government, to the bodies of local self-government and to citizens and their organizations

[428] The powers of the President of the Republic in implementing the national security policy are defined in the Constitution and the laws of the country

[429] The President is the Supreme Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces and heads the National Security Advisory Council

[430] The National Security Advisory Council forms the political and advisory level in the national security system

[431] Its role is to achieve broad public consensus on national security issues

[432] The implementation of the national security policy is carried out by the bodies of the central and territorial executive power

[433] The bodies of local self-government and local government and the bodies of the judiciary contribute in accordance with their competences to the implementation of the security policy

[434] The effectiveness of the system depends on mutual trust and cooperation between state institutions, the private sector, non-governmental organizations and citizens, as well as with partner countries for the exchange of information and joint actions

[435] The Council of Ministers reports on the implementation of the National Security Strategy through an annual report to the National Assembly

[436] The preparation of this report is carried out by the executive branch administration in cooperation with the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences, higher education institutions and experts from the non-governmental sector

[437] The intermediate priorities of the national security policy and the resources for it are determined by the Council of Ministers in consultation with the President of the Republic and the relevant standing committees of the National Assembly

[438] Ministers, heads of departments and regional governors are responsible for the state of national security in the sphere of their competence

[439] For this purpose, they create the necessary functional and structural organization

[440] Institutional capacity is built on the basis of the principles of permanent functional interaction and operational autonomy

[441] The simultaneous existence of duplicative structures, as well as over-concentration of powers, competencies and resources in one institution, is not allowed

[442] The guiding principle is the separation of competencies and achieving a high degree of coordination

[443] When determining the ratio between the management, administrative and executive staff, the principles of a minimum number of units for making management decisions and optimality of structures and positions are observed

[444] The tools for managing and functioning of the national security system include: • analysis and assessment of the state and trends in the security environment and taking preventive actions; • timely and adequate exchange of information; • planning, preparation and management of implementation; • application of a broad and systematic inter-institutional approach; • coordination in the work of the competent institutions and organizations; • methodology and techniques for assessing the security system and its components in relation to their resource provision and the achieved result; • mechanisms for assessing the actions taken and the achieved results in order to increase their effectiveness and efficiency; • building a system for internal control and correction of the national security system

[445] An essential element of the national security system are the consultative and coordinating bodies under the Council of Ministers, carrying out activities in the field of security, public order, prevention and counteraction to corruption and for the development of the national defense industrial base

[446] They coordinate the operational implementation of state policy and provide the necessary consultation to the government and the relevant state authorities in the preparation and implementation of decisions within their competence

[447] The activities of the Security Council under the Council of Ministers, its secretariat and the regional security councils are supported by the bodies and structures of the central and territorial executive authorities and by attracting experts

[448] Financing is carried out under a separate program within the budget of the Council of Ministers

[449] The security services, and if necessary, the public order services, regardless of their subordination, carry out their activities to protect national security independently and/or in a coordinated manner within the framework of the intelligence community

[450] The intelligence and security services carry out activities for: • revealing, preventing and counteracting secret attacks against national interests and against actions threatening or damaging political, economic and defense capabilities; • protecting national security and countering international terrorism and its financing, as well as extremism, organized crime and drug trafficking, the illegal proliferation of products and technologies under international control, as well as weapons of mass destruction, money laundering and other risks and threats directed against the interests of the Republic of Bulgaria and its allies and partners; • protecting and promoting national historical and spiritual values and cultural heritage, part of which are the documents from the National Archives Fund; • acquiring information necessary for making strategic decisions to preserve and increase the well-being of citizens, society and the state and for their development; • protecting the functions and security of Bulgarian military contingents participating in operations and missions outside the territory of the country; • countering hybrid threats and other new forms of threat to national and allied security, implemented including in cyberspace; • ensuring the safety of the highest bodies of state power and management

[451] The implementation of foreign and domestic security policy, defense policy and other sectoral policies is carried out with the active use of the potential of the intelligence and security services

[452] The Council of Ministers increases the effectiveness of the intelligence and security services by improving the structures, personnel potential, financing mechanism, implementation of modern technical and special means, improvement of the social status of employees

[453] When carrying out their activities to protect national security, the security and public order services may interact in accordance with the laws of the country with the security structures of private business entities

[454] The preparation and preservation of human resources is of particular crucial importance for the functioning of the national security system

[455] The state shall build a legally and resource-secured subsystem of programs, institutions and flexible organizational forms for training and improving the qualifications of employees in state institutions, as well as of citizens and their organizations

[456] State institutions shall apply flexible forms for using the professional capacity and accumulated experience of Bulgarian citizens after their release from civil service in the field of national security

[457] The state shall create and/or ensure the effective functioning of specialized units in state higher education institutions and scientific organizations for training and improving the qualifications of employees in the field of national security and defense

[458] The training and qualification system shall provide general and specialized training for the maintenance and development of the national security and defense sector

[459] The Bulgarian Academy of Sciences, higher education institutions and other state educational and scientific structures, as well as civil society organizations shall conduct scientific research and activities to accumulate knowledge and experience and to develop innovative solutions related to security and defense

[460] The Security Council of the Council of Ministers, together with representatives of the academic community, develops and proposes to the Council of Ministers a National Program for Scientific Research and Innovation in the Field of Security and Defense

[461] Control and Accountability of Bodies with Direct Responsibilities for the National Security System

[462] Democratic control over the security system is exercised by the National Assembly and by civil society organizations

[463] The institutions of the executive branch are accountable to the National Assembly and provide information to civil society in accordance with the requirements of the law

[464] The National Assembly exercises parliamentary control over the activities of the national security system

[465] The Prime Minister, ministers, heads of departments, regional governors, local government bodies and local self-government are responsible for creating mechanisms for timely informing the public about their actions in implementing the national security policy

[466] The updated National Security Strategy of the Republic of Bulgaria uses a comprehensive approach to security and defines national interests, as well as the instruments and policies for their implementation and protection in the conditions of the dynamically changing security environment, of new risks and threats

[467] The Council of Ministers, upon proposal of the Security Council, decides to conduct a strategic review of national security and, if necessary, proposes to the National Assembly the updating of this strategy
