[39] Technical Support Secretariat INTRODUCTION

[40] The Central African Republic is embarking on a new process of Security Sector Reform (SSR) as part of a dynamic of national recovery, following the effects of recurring military-political conflicts: coups d'état, armed rebellions, crime

[41] These conflicts have destroyed the economic fabric, weakened the Defense and Security Forces (FDS), disorganized the judicial system, destroyed basic infrastructure, and caused insecurity throughout the national territory

[42] Restoring order to the security sector in the CAR requires the active participation of all actors who can contribute to consolidating peace, security, social cohesion, justice and stability, the foundations of the country's sustainable development

[43] It will have to serve as a basis for restoring the confidence of the population in the country's institutions and in the State in the various areas where it exercises its authority, namely public administration, parliament, justice, security and defence

[44] The reestablishment of the security sector in the CAR draws its source from the imperatives of national security, as described in the National Security Policy (PNS) document, validated on November 4, 2016 by the Strategic Disarmament Committee

[45] Repatriation, Security Sector Reform and National Reconciliation (DDR-RSS-RN

[46] and creates the conditions to ensure the security of Central African citizens

[47] It is also rooted in the international commitments entered into by the Central African State, mainly through the principles laid down by the United Nations within the framework of SSR as well as the African Union's Orientation Framework on SSR

[48] It also draws lessons from various studies and analyses carried out, highlighting the concerns of the population regarding the protection of people and their property

[49] This strategy document defines in detail the orientations of the CAR's commitment to SSR, determining the actions to be undertaken to achieve the objectives of capacity building and good governance of the security sector according to the principles of "national ownership

[50] It should be noted that the SSR process in the CAR takes into account the various programs aimed at consolidating peace, namely the Disarmament, Demobilization, Reintegration, Repatriation (DDRR) program, the National Reconciliation (NR) program, the program to combat the proliferation of Small Arms and Light Weapons (SALW), the promotion of Gender, as well as the various initiatives to consolidate the justice sector

[51] It contributes to the efforts made to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the achievement of which depends on (i) securing the integrity of the territory and restoring the authority of the State, (ii) mobilizing the resources necessary for the implementation of sectoral programs and projects that condition the achievement of the SDGs, (iii) strengthening the capacities of security institutions and the deployment of services throughout the national territory, and (iv) strengthening the dynamics of reconstruction and broadening its scope of action. Finally, this strategy document, the development of which was facilitated by the various workshops and meetings with national and international partners, serves as a basis for the work of the sectoral groups with partners in the context of assistance for capacity building and governance of the security sector

[245] VISION, OBJECTIVES AND PRINCIPLES

[246] The vision supporting CAR's commitment to SSR stems first and foremost from the people's current perceptions of their security, and their burning desire to see the country return to normal life

[247] The population fears residual acts of banditry and the activities of political-military movements that are resistant to the peace process

[248] They express the need to clean up the environment by putting an end to the situation of proliferation of armed groups

[249] They identify the Central African Armed Forces (FACA) and the Internal Security Forces as the legitimate tools for regaining control of the territory and restoring national sovereignty

[250] These aspirations were transcribed in the National Security Policy, whose vision is reflected in the desire to "build a strong, united and prosperous nation, favoring dialogue, justice and the spirit of national reconciliation and responding to the deep aspirations for peace, security and good governance expressed by the populations". 3.2 Objectives The main objective of the CAR's commitment to an SSR process is to satisfy and respond to the deep aspirations for peace, security and good governance expressed by the populations, as defined by the PNS

[251] This Strategy sets three specific objectives

[252] Securing the territory and restoring the administration

[253] Protection of people and property

[254] Democratic governance of the security sector. Achieving these objectives requires a strategic commitment to streamlining and harmonizing the implementation of reforms

[255] SSR must serve to give the Central African State the tools essential to its sovereignty, by regaining control and mastery of the national territory

[256] The actions of the SSR should make it possible to seriously tackle the problem of political instability fuelled by the proliferation of armed groups or movements

[257] Achieving this objective requires a return to a stable social and economic life, supported by the deployment and commitment of professional, well-trained, well-equipped forces and services with the means to carry out their missions, in compliance with the laws and international commitments of the CAR. **Protection of people and property

[258] RSS must respond to the aspirations of populations affected by successive crises which have led to general impoverishment

[259] Actions to standardize the provision of security services through the RSS process should contribute to development and the fight against poverty, by improving the freedom of movement of goods and people, and by guaranteeing the security of producers and their products

[260] Protection must necessarily be accompanied by strict respect for the rights of individuals, and measures must be taken to ensure that populations feel close to security actors. Democratic governance of the security sector

[261] The implementation of the RSS process in the CAR should lead to a change of context, with a new framework in which the various executive, legislative, consultative and coordination actors, security providers are governed and work in accordance with the rules of the rule of law, including through measures to promote the gender approach in recruitment and employment

[262] In this situation of primacy of law, the management of allocated resources as well as the behavior of the armed forces and security services are subject to strict control

[263] This control, both internal and external, will be effective and exercised by elected officials, the population, but also by the different hierarchical levels of management and command.
