[1] PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC Government of Fernando Henrique Cardoso NATIONAL DEFENSE POLICY BRASILIA PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC Government of Fernando Henrique Cardoso NATIONAL DEFENSE POLICY BRASILIA

[2] In the face of the new global landscape of challenges and opportunities, it is necessary to promote in Brazil the development of its own flexible and creative modes of strategic thinking, capable of meeting the country&#39;s defense needs

[3] The transformations that have occurred domestically, as well as those observed in the regional and global context, make it necessary and opportune to explicitly define a national defense policy, emanating from the highest level of the State, that reflects the aspirations of society and serves as a benchmark for the resulting strategies

[4] The National Defense Policy, focused on external threats, aims to establish objectives for the nation&#39;s defense, as well as guide the preparation and use of national capabilities at all levels and spheres of power, with the involvement of both the civilian and military sectors

[5] The National Defense Policy, a topic of interest to society as a whole, is based on the foundations, objectives, and principles stipulated in the Federal Constitution and is in line with the country&#39;s external attitude, which is based on the pursuit of peaceful solutions to controversies and the strengthening of international peace and security

[6] The Brazilian profile – simultaneously continental and maritime; equatorial, tropical, and subtropical; with a long land border with almost all South American countries and extensive coastline, exclusive economic zone, and continental shelf – gives the country geo-strategic depth and makes the task of general defense planning complex

[7] The broad demographic and territorial bases, as well as the availability of natural resources, based on the diverse national physiography, shape differentiated scenarios that demand, at the same time, a general and integrated policy and a specific approach for each case

[8] The implementation of a sustainable defense policy, aimed at the gradual modernization of self-protection capabilities, depends on the construction of a development model that strengthens democracy, reduces social inequalities and regional imbalances, and reconciles priorities in the political, social, economic, and military fields with defense needs and diplomatic action

[9] In this process, it is necessary to reconcile defense needs with the availability of resources, through the approval of resources by the National Congress and, whenever possible, with the involvement of the academic, scientific-technological, and industrial sectors of the country

[10] THE INTERNATIONAL CONTEXT

[11] With the end of the East-West confrontation, the current process of realignment of forces is resulting in the emergence of an undefined and unstable multipolar international environment, generated by the lack of correspondence between the centers of strategic-military, political, and economic power, whose evolution is still difficult to predict

[12] The end of the Cold War rendered obsolete the simplistic generalizations resulting from the ideological and military bipolarity that prevailed until then

[13] Currently, although the risks of a nuclear confrontation on a planetary scale have been reduced, the relative strategic predictability has disappeared

[14] The localized conflicts currently occurring on almost every continent, the resurgence of ethnic, nationalist, and religious extremism, as well as the phenomenon of fragmentation observed in several countries, make it evident that traditional concepts such as sovereignty, self-determination, and national identity continue to be relevant

[15] In this transitional phase, in which new political and economic rules of coexistence between nations are being established, characterized by the absence of clear paradigms and the growing participation of non-governmental actors, the country seeks to determine, free from ideological constraints, its strategic insertion and priorities in the field of defense

[16] The climate of uncertainty that marks the current global context demands that defense continue to deserve the attention of national governments and that military expression remain of paramount importance for the survival of states as independent units

[17] It is unrealistic to conceive of a state of reasonable size and international influence that can abdicate a reliable defense force

[18] The Armed Forces are an instrument for the exercise of the right to self-defense, a right, moreover, enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations

[19] South America, distant from the world&#39;s centers of tension, is considered the most demilitarized region in the world

[20] The redemocratization that has occurred on the continent tends to reduce the probability of conflicts

[21] Regional disputes have been managed at tolerable levels

[22] For Brazil, a country with different internal regions and a diversified profile—simultaneously Amazonian, Atlantic, Platine, and part of the Southern Cone—the concept of regional space extends beyond the South American continental landmass and also includes the South Atlantic

[23] Thus, when examining the strategic perspectives of the region, it is important to remember that the mosaic of countries and sub-regions that compose it is extremely complex, requiring a multifaceted regional insertion based on a policy of harmonizing interests

[24] Having not been involved in conflict with its neighbors for over a century, Brazil has worked to promote greater integration and rapprochement among the countries of the continent, and, consequently, has acquired international credibility

[25] The strengthening of the integration process provided by Mercosur, the closer relations with Amazonian neighbors – developed within the framework of the Amazon Cooperation Treaty – the intensification of cooperation with Portuguese-speaking African countries, and the consolidation of the Zone of Peace and Cooperation in the South Atlantic – the result of positive and concrete diplomatic action – form a true ring of peace around the country, enabling the concentration of efforts towards the achievement of a national development project and the fight against social inequalities

[26] The country is not, however, entirely free from risks

[27] Despite coexisting peacefully within the international community, it may be compelled to become involved in externally generated conflicts as a consequence of threats to its patrimony and vital interests

[28] At the regional level, zones of instability persist that may contradict Brazilian interests

[29] The actions of armed gangs operating in neighboring countries, on the borders of the Brazilian Amazon, and crime International organization is one of the points that causes concern

[30] Military capacity and diplomacy are expressions of national sovereignty and dignity, and Brazil has consistently and clearly demonstrated its determination to live in peace and harmony, according to the principles and norms of international law and in respect of the commitments undertaken

[31] OBJECTIVES

[32] From the examination of the situations above, the need emerges to specify specific strategic priorities, arising from the national interest and the basic objectives of national defense

[33] Despite the favorable situation in the immediate environment, an adequate defense system must be maintained to preserve national sovereignty and the democratic rule of law

[34] This is the meaning of formulating a national defense policy, with resources to implement it, that integrates strategic visions of a social, economic, military and diplomatic nature, and that has the support of the Nation

[35] The objectives of National Defense are: a. a a. guarantee of sovereignty, with the preservation of territorial integrity, national assets and interests; b. guarantee of the rule of law and democratic institutions; c. preservation of national cohesion and unity; d. safeguarding of Brazilian persons, assets and resources or those under Brazilian jurisdiction; e. achievement and maintenance of Brazilian interests abroad; f. projection of Brazil in the concert of nations and its greater insertion in the international decision-making process; and g. contribution to the maintenance of international peace and security

[36] STRATEGIC GUIDANCE

[37] The scope of action of the Brazilian State in the area of defense is based on the obligation to provide security to the Nation, both in times of peace and in situations of conflict

[38] To this end, this policy is centered on active diplomacy geared towards peace and a deterrent strategic posture of a defensive nature, based on the following premises: - perfectly defined and internationally recognized borders and limits; - close relations with countries neighbors and the international community in general, based on mutual trust and respect; — rejection of wars of conquest; and — pursuit of the peaceful resolution of disputes, with the use of force only as a means of self-defense

[39] The preventive aspect of Brazilian defense lies in valuing diplomatic action as the primary instrument for conflict resolution and in the existence of a credible military structure capable of generating an effective deterrent effect

[40] The defensive character does not imply that, in the event of conflict, the Armed Forces must strictly limit themselves to carrying out defensive operations

[41] In the context of a broader defense plan, and in order to repel any armed aggression, the country will employ all necessary military power, with a view to resolving the conflict in the shortest possible time and with minimal damage to national integrity and interests, imposing conditions favorable to the restoration of peace

[42] Balanced strengthening of national capacity in the field of defense is essential, with the involvement of the industrial, university, and technical-scientific sectors

[43] Scientific development and Technological development is fundamental to achieving greater strategic autonomy and improved operational capabilities of the Armed Forces

[44] Military power must be based on the capacity of the Armed Forces, the potential of national resources and mobilizable reserves, with the purpose of inhibiting possible intentions of disrespect for the rules of peaceful coexistence among Nations

[45] The Armed Forces must be adjusted to the political-strategic stature of the Nation and structured, in a flexible and versatile way, to act promptly and effectively in different areas and scenarios

[46] The Armed Forces, whose mission is assigned by the Constitution, are responsible for defending the Nation whenever necessary, ensuring the maintenance of its integrity and sovereignty

[47] To this end, it is essential to maintain the continuous improvement of the integration of the Armed Forces, in their preparation and employment, as well as in the rationalization of related activities

[48] GUIDELINES

[49] To achieve the objectives of the National Defense Policy, the following guidelines should be followed

[50] The following objectives were observed: a. to actively contribute to the construction of an international order, based on the rule of law, that fosters universal and regional peace and the sustainable development of humanity; b. to increasingly participate in relevant international decision-making processes; c. to improve and increase Brazil&#39;s negotiating capacity on the international stage; d. to promote Brazil&#39;s position in favor of global disarmament, conditioned on the dismantling of nuclear arsenals and other weapons of mass destruction, in a multilaterally agreed process; e. to participate in international peacekeeping operations, in accordance with national interests; f. to actively contribute to the strengthening, expansion, and solidification of regional integration; g. to act to maintain a climate of peace and cooperation along national borders, and for solidarity in Latin America and the South Atlantic region; h. to intensify exchanges with the Armed Forces of friendly nations; i. to maintain the participation of the Armed Forces in subsidiary actions aimed at national integration, civil defense, and the socio-economic development of the country, in harmony with its constitutional purpose; j. to protect the Brazilian Amazon, with the support of the entire society and with the enhancement of the military presence; l. to prioritize actions to develop and revitalize the border area, especially in the northern and central-western regions; m. to improve the organization, equipment, training, and coordination of the Armed Forces, ensuring them the conditions, organic means, and qualified human resources for the fulfillment of their constitutional purpose; n. to improve the command, control, and intelligence capacity of all bodies involved in national defense, providing them with conditions that facilitate the decision-making process, in peace and in conflict situations; o. to improve the surveillance, control, and defense system of the borders, jurisdictional waters, continental shelf, and Brazilian airspace, as well as maritime and air traffic; p. to guarantee sufficient and continuous resources that provide effective conditions for the preparation of the Armed Forces and other bodies involved in national defense; q. to strengthen the national transportation, energy, and cooperation systems

[51] communications; r. to seek a level of scientific research, technological development and production capacity, in order to minimize the country&#39;s external dependence on strategic resources of interest for its defense; s. to promote scientific knowledge of the Antarctic region and active participation in the decision-making process regarding its destiny; t. to improve the Mobilization System to meet the country&#39;s needs when compelled to engage in armed conflict; and u. to sensitize and enlighten public opinion, with a view to creating and maintaining a national defense mentality, through the encouragement of civic duty and dedication to the Fatherland
