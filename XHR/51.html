[768] AND THE MILITARY INSTRUMENT BRAZILIAN AIR FORCE

[769] The Brazilian Air Force (FAB) had its origins in the aviation of the Brazilian Navy and the Brazilian Army

[770] In 1916, the Navy founded the Naval Aviation School on Enxadas Island, in the city of Rio de Janeiro

[771] In 1919, the Army created the Military Aviation School, based at Campo dos Afonsos, in the same city

[772] In 1932, from Campo dos Afonsos, the first Military Air Mail (CAM) flight departed, still under the auspices of Military Aviation, a service that gave rise to the current National Air Mail (CAN), of enormous importance for national integration

[773] In 1941, during the Second World War, the Ministry of Aeronautics was created, born from the union of the air assets and human resources of the Army, the Navy and the Department of Civil Aviation

[774] Its armed wing was initially called the National Air Forces and, in June of the same year, it was renamed the Brazilian Air Force

[775] The Air Force had its baptism of fire during World War II

[776] He participated in the South Atlantic campaign, combating the Axis submarine threat, and fought in the skies over Italy, alongside the Allied Forces with the 1st Fighter Aviation Group and the 1st Liaison and Observation Squadron

[777] With the creation of the Ministry of Defense in 1999, the Ministry of Aeronautics was transformed into the Aeronautics Command (COMAER

[778] The challenges to be faced by the Brazilian Air Force are proportional to the million km² of its area of operation

[779] With the continental extent of airspace to be controlled, defended and monitored, combined with the complexity of the current international scenario, multiple challenges are created for the Force, which must be able to respond to demands with modern and efficient means

[780] AIR FORCE MISSION Maintain sovereignty in the airspace and integrate the national territory, with a view to defending the homeland

[781] The Air Force acts to prevent the use of Brazilian airspace and outerspace for hostile acts or acts contrary to national interests

[782] To this end, it must have effective surveillance, control and airspace defense capabilities over sensitive points and areas of the national territory, with detection, interception and destruction capabilities

[783] Likewise, contributing to the development of the Nation, it participates in the integration of its territory

[784] To this end, it makes its operational and logistical resources available, whenever possible, to bring the State's presence to all parts of the country, in support of federal, state or municipal public bodies, as well as in compliance with public and social policies

[785] The following are also specific subsidiary responsibilities of the Air Force: - to provide air navigation safety; - to establish, equip, and operate the aerospace, aeronautical, and airport infrastructure under its responsibility; - to operate the National Air Mail; - to cooperate with federal agencies in the repression of crimes involving the use of airspace and airport areas, in the form of logistical support, intelligence, communications, and instruction; and - preserving the exclusive powers of the judicial police, to act continuously and permanently, through actions to control Brazilian airspace, against all types of illicit air traffic, with emphasis on those related to the trafficking of drugs, weapons, ammunition, and illegal passengers

[1144] THE TRANSFORMATION OF DEFENSE

[1145] Brazil, due to its importance on the world stage and the need to face the most diverse challenges in the field of National Defense, needs to have Armed Forces compatible with its political-strategic stature

[1146] As a result of the need to improve the preparation of the Armed Forces and provide them with capabilities appropriate to fulfill their constitutional purpose, it was found appropriate to initiate a transformation process, which will facilitate the implementation of innovations consistent with operational needs and the rationalization of the Forces

[1147] In the Defense Sector, transformation means obtaining new capabilities, and not just improving existing ones, which would only fall within the scope of the modernization concept

[1148] Considering the dynamics of the current and future world, the realization that military capabilities have a life cycle and that there will be a permanent need for the Armed Forces to be equipped with new capabilities, it can be seen that a transformation process has a characteristic of continuity, that is, it will always be developing, in order to keep the Armed Forces updated and in a position to be employed effectively

[1149] The changes require, as a priority, the development of new employment doctrines (combat, equipment and organization), with the objective of keeping the Armed Forces capable of operating in a highly complex operational environment

[1150] A transformation process, however, must be developed on a large scale and involve the Armed Forces as a whole

[1151] Although operational missions are carried out by the “armed wings” of each Force, the transformation must also take place in other sectors

[1152] Thus, in addition to the introduction of innovation and the implementation of rationalization occurring in operational structures, there is a need for non-operational aspects to also be included in this process

[1153] On the other hand, it should be highlighted that the effectiveness of a transformation process is also linked to the capacity to acquire and apply cutting-edge technology in the research and development phases of new weapons systems and platforms

[1154] In Brazil's case, the transformation of its defense system, in addition to enabling greater capacity building for its Armed Forces, will create a range of opportunities for economic and social development
