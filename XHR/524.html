[1] NATIONAL DEFENSE POLICY 1999 – 2020 Let it be known that this National Defense Policy was approved at a meeting of the National Defense Council held on the seventh day of October of the year one thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine, in accordance with Act number

[2] Vice Admiral ANTONIO C. CABALLERO PATIÑO Permanent Secretary National Defense Council 1 PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC NATIONAL DEFENSE COUNCIL PERMANENT SECRETARY NATIONAL DEFENSE POLICY I

[3] INTRODUCTION This White Paper presents the new Defense Policy of the Republic of Paraguay with a vision for the future

[4] It is based on a recently conducted Defense assessment, which examined Paraguay's strategic situation, regional and international trends over the coming decades, and the likely implications these trends will have for our country

[5] It takes into account fiscal constraints, as well as the need for professional, appropriately trained and equipped Armed Forces

[6] This document shows our allies and friends that Paraguay is modernizing its structures

[7] In short, it establishes a convenient and appropriate path to ensure our security now and in the future

[8] POLICY AS A FRAMEWORK FOR DEFENSE This Defense Policy is the framework that guides our national efforts in response to future national and international security problems, and its purpose is to provide the Republic of Paraguay with an effective prevention and response instrument designed to guarantee the integral and permanent security of the following national interests: The existence of the Paraguayan State, its freedom, independence and sovereignty

[9] The integrity of the national population and the Republic's heritage, both tangible and intangible, within and outside the country

[10] The full validity of the rule of law and democratic institutions

[11] The preservation of the identity and unity of the Nation

[12] The conditions conducive to achieving and/or maintaining national objectives

[13] The permanence of the conditions of stability and predictability essential for the normal development of national life

[14] In addition to the traditional roles of defense, this policy contemplates the control of low-intensity threats such as drug trafficking, arms trafficking, terrorism, illegal migration, attacks against national identity and unity, and environmental degradation, among others; and the effective contribution of Paraguay to the preservation of international peace and security, in compliance with the commitments assumed by the Republic

[15] A fundamental aspect of this policy is the systemic approach to National Defense, within which military defense is assigned to the Armed Forces, while the defense of the political, economic, psychosocial, and scientific-technological interests of the State is assigned to other public bodies, within the constitutional and legal framework, under the direction of the President of the Republic

[16] Self-management in cooperation is the strategy adopted for the implementation of this policy

[17] Self-management is essential for the national tasks that every independent nation must undertake, such as defending its integrity, sovereignty, independence, national identity, among others

[18] Cooperation with other states involves protecting national interests that coincide with those of other countries, such as environmental protection and conservation and other transnational threats such as drug trafficking, terrorism, and arms trafficking

[19] This can only be achieved through concerted action between States that share similar interests

[20] DIRECTIONS FOR INVESTING IN DEFENSE The Government's first priority will be to rectify the most critical structural deficiencies in the National Defense System

[21] To this end, in the short term, it will propose to the National Congress a new institutional and legal framework for Defense, so that it can adapt to both national and international needs, enabling Paraguay's active participation in peacekeeping operations and the improvement of national capabilities to control river, land, and air traffic, as well as to effectively combat drug trafficking, terrorism, arms trafficking, money laundering, illegal migration, attacks on national identity and unity, and environmental degradation

[22] At the same time, the Armed Forces of the Nation will be subject to a series of improvements in their efficiency, through the improvement of the educational plans of the military personnel (including new and demanding study programs), transfer of resources from logistical functions to operational areas on the borders, so that Paraguayans can count on a better defense with their fiscal contributions

[23] Investment in staff will be the key

[24] This will involve radical changes in operations to ensure that professional standards are met and maintained, allowing human resources to be recruited, trained, and retained to serve Paraguay in a new and demanding career

[26] territorial integrity and legitimately constituted authorities (Art

[27] cultural heritage (Art

[28] and the environment (Art

[29] among others

[30] Due to the breadth of interests that must be defended, it is impossible for a single institution to be responsible for protecting all of them

[31] Consequently, National Defense must be implemented through a SYSTEM capable of forecasting and immediate response

[32] Every time an aggression or threat affects a national interest defined as a “NATIONAL OBJECTIVE” (to be achieved or maintained), there must be an IMMEDIATE reaction from the NATIONAL DEFENSE SYSTEM, made up not only of the Armed Forces but also other State bodies

[33] Depending on the nature of the aggression or threat, the institution with constitutional jurisdiction in the case intervenes in the first instance

[34] If this is exceeded, it is supported by the other organs of the system

[36] PROPOSED REFORMS IN THE DEFENSE SECTOR In order to comply with the provisions of the National Constitution and to ensure the control of the civil constitutional authority over the Armed Forces, a complete reform of the Defense is proposed, and must be initiated from several fronts, such as the reformulation of the concepts of security and defense, covering all national interests that must be protected by the State, the restructuring of the Ministry of Defense, the Organization of the Armed Forces and military personnel

[37] the rethinking of its regulations and the updating of its doctrine to respond to the challenges of the new sub-regional and global context

[38] The proposed amendments to the legal framework for defense are aimed at providing an effective prevention and response instrument designed to guarantee the overall security of the Republic, and, in military terms, to strengthen civilian constitutional oversight of the Armed Forces and optimize the use of resources by improving management in the sector

[39] In these aspects, it is worth highlighting the inclusion of the Minister of National Defense in the chain of command of the Armed Forces, and the operational reorganization of the Army into Divisional Commands and Brigades, reducing the cumbersome structure based on Army Corps, Divisions, and Brigades, thus adopting a more flexible, operational organization that is more suited to our needs and resources

[40] RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN DEFENSE POLICY AND FOREIGN POLICY Complexity and uncertainty are the dominant characteristics of the international security environment; however, it is clear that any challenge to Paraguay's security and defense will be limited and localized

[41] Defense Policy must be consistent with Foreign Policy, based on the constitutional provisions that govern our actions within the concert of nations and which are aimed at contributing to the construction of an international order based on respect for international law and the principles of independence and self-determination of peoples; legal equality among states; international solidarity and cooperation; international protection of human rights; free navigation of international rivers; non-intervention; and the condemnation of all forms of dictatorship, colonialism, and imperialism, in accordance with the provisions of Article 143 of the National Constitution

[42] Serious challenges will continue to exist in the fight against drug trafficking, terrorism, international trafficking of arms and prohibited substances, international organized crime and other transnational threats that can only be effectively combated through concerted action with other countries

[43] Globally, opportunities for Paraguay to participate in peacekeeping operations, specialized operations such as demining, collective security activities, and so on will increase

[44] International demand for peacekeeping has increased in recent years and will almost certainly continue to do so, and the nation's armed forces must be prepared to face these challenges

[45] Paraguay has signed a memorandum of understanding with the United Nations, under which it must have operational forces available to actively participate in the international collective security system

[46] Although the possibility of a serious threat to the security of Paraguay's territorial integrity is low, it cannot be ruled out

[47] Therefore, our defense needs will be taken into account through the availability of an adequate deterrent capability

[48] REQUIREMENTS OF NATIONAL DEFENSE For all the above reasons, the requirements of our defense can be summarized as follows

[49] AT THE NATIONAL LEVEL: Capacity to effectively prevent and control threats to the overall security of the Republic, including low-intensity threats such as acts of sabotage, terrorism, and illegal trafficking; to assist Paraguayans in danger; and to comply with international obligations for air and river traffic safety

[50] This capacity must include, in the event of natural disasters, the provision of essential services for the life and health of the population; and, where legally required, support for maintaining public order and defending legitimately constituted authorities

[51] It must also provide a capacity that responds to Paraguay's long-term strategic challenges and demonstrates Paraguay's commitment to international security, sufficient to secure the support of other nations

[52] AT THE SUBREGIONAL LEVEL Capacity to effectively contribute to the maintenance of peace and security in the Mercosur subregion, based on the availability of conditions of complementarity and defensive interoperability

[53] AT THE GLOBAL LEVEL Conditions to effectively contribute to the preservation of international security and peace, fulfilling the obligations and responsibilities assumed by Paraguay before international organizations, maintaining a military capacity that allows the country to participate in the full range of peace and humanitarian operations, thus strengthening Paraguay's image before the international community

[54] PURPOSE OF THE NATIONAL DEFENSE POLICY The purpose of the Defense Policy is to provide the Republic of Paraguay with an effective prevention and response instrument designed to guarantee the integral and permanent security of the following interests

[55] The existence of the Paraguayan State, its freedom, independence and sovereignty

[56] The integrity of the national population and the Republic's heritage, both tangible and intangible, within and outside the country

[57] The full validity of the rule of law and democratic institutions

[58] The preservation of the identity and unity of the Nation

[59] The conditions conducive to achieving and/or maintaining national objectives

[60] F. The permanence of the conditions of stability and predictability essential for the normal development of national life

[61] These interests are permanent and their protection is the State's obligation

[62] However, considering the need to provide defense policy with the flexibility necessary to adapt to changes arising from the dynamics of strategic reality, each government is empowered to establish defense objectives aimed at protecting these interests, based on changes in the global, continental, regional, subregional, and national strategic situation

[63] PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC NATIONAL DEFENSE COUNCIL PERMANENT SECRETARY In this sense, the National Defense Objective is defined as everything that the Government must achieve or maintain within the different fields of National Power in each constitutional period, to guarantee the full and permanent security of the national interests stated above

[64] The defense objectives, arising from the conclusions of each government's analysis of the strategic situation, must be established in the National Defense Directive, which in turn constitutes the basis for National Defense and Military Defense Planning

[65] STRATEGIC ORIENTATION The National Defense of the Republic of Paraguay is oriented toward achieving a security environment within which the fundamental interests of the Nation stated above are free and exempt from all danger, damage or risk

[66] In this context, the Comprehensive Security of the Republic appears as an END, and National Defense as a set of activities that aim to PROTECT, Shelter, and FREE the Nation as a whole from all danger, harm, or risk, whether from outside or inside the Republic

[67] NATIONAL DEFENSE then assumes a scope that goes far beyond the sphere of purely military interests, and encompasses the Political, Economic, Military, Psychosocial and Scientific-technological fields, consequently involving everything that constitutes a National Interest stated as an object of defense

[68] On the other hand, by encompassing fields other than the purely military, NATIONAL DEFENSE ceases to be the exclusive responsibility of the Armed Forces and becomes a right and obligation of ALL PARAGUAYANS

[69] It must be implemented within the framework of constitutional and legal provisions, under the direction of the Government

[70] The implementation of National Defense is closely linked to the country's political, economic, psychosocial, and scientific and technological priorities and must be consistent and compatible with the national policies established in each of these areas

[71] Paraguay's approach to its defense is based on a non-aggressive, dissuasive stance, prone to respect and support for the international order and its values; framed within the provisions of the National Constitution and the commitments assumed by the country as a Member of the United Nations, the Organization of American States, and as a party to Integration Agreements and other bilateral and multilateral conventions signed by the Republic

[72] Paraguay's national defense must be based on a strong negotiating capacity and the timely detection of threats to its national interests; on the immediate response capacity of the National Defense System; and on the maintenance of a permanent and sufficient military force to guarantee an adequate and timely response in the event that the inadequacy and ineffectiveness of peaceful means forces the country to resort to military force as a last resort in the exercise of its legitimate defense

[73] Since all national interests must be protected by the State, encompassing all five spheres of national power, it is impossible for a single institution to be responsible for protecting all of them

[74] Consequently, NATIONAL DEFENSE is implemented through a SYSTEM with the capacity for foresight and immediate response, responsible for carrying out the functions of planning, organization, coordination, execution and control aimed at preserving the integral security of the Republic and reestablishing it in all cases in which it is breached

[75] Every time an aggression or threat affects a national interest defined as a NATIONAL OBJECTIVE (to be achieved or maintained), an appropriate reaction must IMMEDIATELY occur from the NATIONAL DEFENSE SYSTEM, the paths of which are the following: A. A peaceful path: which is used to defend National Interests by diplomatic or other means, without resorting to force

[76] B. A violent route: which is used when the peaceful route is insufficient or ineffective, and which is the ONLY CASE in which the use of force is resorted to

[77] The National Defense System operates under the direction of the President of the Republic, who makes decisions and issues relevant orders to the System's bodies, with the advice of the National Defense Council

[78] PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC NATIONAL DEFENSE COUNCIL PERMANENT SECRETARY Each Institution that makes up the National Defense System acts within the specific scope of its constitutional and legal powers

[79] If any of these institutions is overwhelmed in its capacity to adequately confront the aggression or threat in question, the President of the Republic, by virtue of the power granted to him by the Constitution in Art

[80] may order other institutions to assist the institution that has primary responsibility for handling the case until normality is restored

[81] The Minister of National Defense assumes the roles of formulator, executor, and coordinator of the implementation of military policy and defense plans approved by the Executive Branch; maintains the balance and integration of forces within the Military Structure; and acts as its liaison with the political, economic, psychosocial, and scientific-technological fields; and directs the Armed Forces' relationship with civil society

[82] GUIDELINES FOR ACTION To ensure the achievement of its National Defense objectives, the Republic of Paraguay implements the provisions and actions necessary to: A. Contribute to the construction of an international order based on respect for international law and the principles of independence and self-determination of peoples; legal equality among States; international solidarity and cooperation; international protection of human rights; free navigation of international rivers; non-intervention and condemnation of all forms of dictatorship, colonialism and imperialism, in accordance with the provisions of Article 143 of the National Constitution

[83] B. Increase and improve the country's human resources negotiation capacity oriented toward the defense of national interests

[84] C. Train human resources to participate in international peacekeeping operations, in accordance with national interests and possibilities

[85] D. Implement the participation of the Armed Forces in subsidiary actions that tend towards national integration in a manner consistent with their constitutional mission

[86] E. Protect territorial integrity on the basis of national presence, including military presence, and the support of all of society

[87] F. Improve the command, control, and intelligence capabilities of all bodies involved in National Defense, providing them with conditions that facilitate the decision-making process, whether in peace or conflict situations. G. Improve and perfect the system for monitoring and defending the inviolability of the national territory

[88] H. Modernize, improve, and permanently update the organization, structure, training, and coordination of the Armed Forces and other bodies involved in national defense, providing them with the appropriate conditions and means to fulfill their constitutional and legal missions

[89] I. Regulate and control land, air and river traffic within the Republic

[90] J. Promote the scientific, technological, and productive development of resources of strategic interest to the country, thereby minimizing foreign dependence. K. Strengthen national transportation, energy, and communications systems

[91] L. Maintain and improve the National Mobilization System to meet the country's needs when it is forced to engage in armed conflict; M. Improve civil defense in the event of a disaster; N. Improve environmental protection

[92] N. Promote the interest and participation of all sectors of Paraguayan society in national defense activities

[94] REFERENCE FRAMEWORK FOR THE ISSUANCE OF THE NATIONAL DEFENSE DIRECTIVE The specific guidelines derived from the defense policy are established in the National Defense Directive

[95] This is the document in which the Government, at the proposal of the National Defense Council, defines the objectives of National Defense aimed at protecting the permanent national interests established in the National Defense Policy and sets forth the general lines of action it considers most appropriate to achieve them within a constitutional term of government, in accordance with the current strategic situation

[96] It constitutes the basis for National Defense planning, Military Policy, and Military Defense planning, establishing the geopolitical framework within which they should be developed and the essential goals for maintaining the Comprehensive Security of the Republic

[97] ORIENTATION OF MILITARY POLICY Military Policy must derive from National Defense Policy

[98] It is directed exclusively toward the Armed Forces and consists of a set of laws, regulations, and decrees that exclusively direct and guide military activities, including aspects of modernizing the organization and doctrine of the Armed Forces; defining operational and logistical aspects; and the measures necessary to achieve a balanced military structure suitable for responding to potential threats
