[695] Ethiopia's policy towards Somalia

[707] It is now over ten years since Somalia has become stateless

[714] Somalia has become a haven and conduit for terrorists and extremists

[715] Antipeace elements are using the country as a base and place of transit in order to threaten Ethiopia's peace
