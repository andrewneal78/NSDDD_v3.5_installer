[210] Command and control system of the armed forces There are three levels of leadership, command and control of the armed forces: strategic, operational and tactical

[211] The strategic level of leadership, command and control includes the National Assembly, the President of the Republic, the Council of Ministers, the Minister of Defense and the Chief of Defense

[212] The operational level of command and control includes the commander of the Joint Forces Command, the commanders of the Land Forces, the Air Force and the Navy

[213] The tactical level of command and control includes the commanders (chiefs) who exercise tactical command and control of their subordinate military formations

[214] The analysis of the security environment, the existing risks and threats and the requirements for the armed forces to perform the tasks under the three missions showed that they can be implemented with the following Command Structure and Chain of Command: The command structure of the armed forces will include the Ministry of Defense, the Joint Forces Command, the commands of: the Land Forces, the Air Force, the Navy and the military formations

[215] A chain of command is a sequence or order of subordination from the most senior to the most junior commander/commander through which command is exercised

[216] The chain of command and structure of the Armed Forces shall remain unchanged, in accordance with the current regulatory framework set out in the Law on Defence and the Armed Forces of the Republic of Bulgaria

[217] System for communication and information support of the command and control of the Armed Forces The system for communication and information support of the command and control of the Armed Forces of the Republic of Bulgaria is provided through the established stationary and mobile units, structured in two formations – Stationary Communication and Information System (CIS) and Mobile Communication and Information System

[218] The stationary communication and information system ensures the information exchange necessary for the interaction of the Ministry of Defence with other ministries and departments, for the interaction with NATO and the EU, as well as carries out management of the structures that make up the Integrated Communication and Information System for the management of the country in crises

[219] The mobile communication and information system carries out the communication and information support of the Joint Command of the Forces and performs tasks in its interest

[220] The capabilities of the system for communication and information support of the command and control of the armed forces of the Republic of Bulgaria are developing in the following directions: improvement of information systems and expansion of the scope of the network infrastructure, based on the construction and maintenance of a network of data centers based on cloud technologies; improvement of the capabilities for monitoring, analysis, and response to communication and information incidents by building a center for monitoring, analysis, response, and restoration of CIS; technological updating of navigation and sensor systems in accordance with NATO and EU trends; unification of the CIS services of the armed forces and their integration with the services of the unified electronic, communications network for state administration; achieving the necessary interoperability of the country's CIS networks with those of NATO and the EU; expansion of the possibilities for using NATO classified information exchange networks to an operational level; increasing the capabilities of the networks for the exchange of national classified information by expanding and protecting them with nationally approved cryptographic means; accelerated introduction into operation of modern digital technologies and replacement of the existing analog CIS equipment manufactured in countries that are not members of NATO and/or the EU, etc

[222] Military intelligence and capabilities for intelligence support of the forces Military intelligence unites the activities of all intelligence structures and formations in the Armed Forces (AF) of the Republic of Bulgaria

[223] It is conducted with the aim of obtaining, processing and disseminating intelligence information in the interests of the country's defense, national and collective security, early warning of risks and threats to them and providing intelligence support to national, allied and coalition operations and missions

[224] Military intelligence provides intelligence information to the state and military leadership necessary for defense planning and for the implementation of the intelligence contribution to NATO and the EU

[225] The main goal of intelligence support is to fill the deficit of intelligence information necessary for commanders/commanders for the successful planning and conduct of operations

[226] The construction of the Armed Forces and the development of defense capabilities in conditions of severely limited and insufficient financial resources, a dynamic military-strategic environment with the dominance of asymmetric and hybrid threats, with an accelerated rhythm of the use of formations in operations far from the territory of the country, necessitates an increase in the role of intelligence, surveillance and early warning forces and means

[227] As a basic element of the early warning system, military intelligence must have the necessary capabilities for surveillance and reconnaissance in the full spectrum of operations and for obtaining information from air, land, sea, electromagnetic and cyberspace in real time

[228] In response to changes in the security environment and newly emerging risks and threats, the acquisition of components or entirely new specific capabilities for obtaining, analyzing and exchanging intelligence information is of key importance

[229] Intelligence in the Armed Forces of the Republic of Bulgaria is a centralized process conducted at the strategic, operational and tactical levels, according to a unified plan and common goals

[230] At the strategic level, intelligence is organized and conducted by the intelligence forces of the Ministry of Defense, separated into the Military Information Service (MIS

[231] It is a structure directly subordinate to the Minister of Defense, which maintains and develops capabilities for conducting strategic intelligence using human resources and technical means

[232] The service collects and processes all information obtained by the intelligence bodies of military intelligence or received from the intelligence structures of NATO and the EU, from the intelligence bodies of NATO and the EU member states and from partner services

[233] The SIE carries out strategic management of the intelligence process in the armed forces and assists the Chief of Defense in exercising his powers of leadership of intelligence in the Bulgarian Army

[234] Intelligence at the operational level is led by the Commander of the Joint Forces Command and the commanders of the armed forces

[235] The commands of the armed forces plan, organize and lead the intelligence and training of their subordinate intelligence bodies and formations

[236] At the tactical level, military intelligence is organized by the commanders (chiefs) of military formations and is conducted by their subordinate intelligence bodies, in the interest of the combat missions they perform

[237] To achieve these capabilities, formations for various types and methods of intelligence and electronic warfare in the battle space are maintained in the troops

[238] In connection with the implementation of the NATO Initiative for Joint Multi-Component Intelligence and Surveillance in the Armed Forces, military intelligence will build capabilities for working in a network environment and will develop access to BICES at the operational and tactical levels
