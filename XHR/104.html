Each member state exercised sovereignty in specific aspects and actively participated in the Federal Republic through its representatives in Congress

[110] This Treaty is the instrument that establishes the guidelines for consolidating the security model, a fundamental requirement for regional integration and defense in Central America, and which El Salvador considers of primary importance for national and regional stability

[112] This model establishes that democratic security is comprehensive, indivisible, and inseparable from the human dimension, based on the supremacy and strengthening of political power, a reasonable balance of power, the security of people and their property, environmental protection, and the eradication of violence, corruption, terrorism, drug trafficking, and arms trafficking, among others

[113] Likewise, its implementation is governed by principles relating to the rule of law; the strengthening and improvement of democratic institutions in each state; solidarity and humanitarian aid in the face of emergencies, threats, and natural disasters; sovereign equality among states and legal certainty in their relations; the peaceful settlement of disputes; the solidarity and security of the Central American peoples and governments in the prevention and joint resolution of common regional security problems; collective and solidarity-based defense in the event of armed aggression by a state outside the region against the territorial integrity, sovereignty, and independence of a Central American state; national unity and the territorial integrity of states within the framework of regional integration; and respect for the purposes and principles of the Charters of the United Nations and the Organization of American States, among others

[127] The purpose of Salvadoran state action in the field of National Defense is based on the obligation to provide security to the Nation, both in times of peace and war

[137] Therefore, as a function of the State, the defense of the Nation is the most relevant and therefore merits the establishment of a specific policy, represented, among others, by the National Defense Law

[138] Purposes of National Defense

[139] The National Defense Law establishes the systematic framework for institutions and all residents of the country to actively participate in the defense of national heritage

[140] Hence, the Law establishes the legal, organic and functional bases essential for the preparation and execution of National Defense, also determining its purposes in two major axes of action: a) Maintain the inviolability of the sovereignty and independence of El Salvador and the exercise of its freedom of action, as well as the integrity of its material heritage and national identity, both internally and externally

[141] Overcome obstacles to achieving the National Objectives and prevent the achievement of objectives that are vulnerable to the Salvadoran State

[142] In keeping with these objectives, it is emphasized that the defensive nature of the country is based on three fundamental principles: respect for Salvadoran international political boundaries; the practice of harmonious relations with neighboring countries based on mutual respect and trust; and the peaceful resolution of disputes

[143] National Defense Objectives

[144] National Defense, based on the principle of the inherent right to self-defense, is characterized by preventing threats to national security and maintaining a state of readiness to confront and eventually neutralize any threats that arise. Its actions are based on active diplomacy, based on full respect for the sovereignty, territorial integrity, and independence of other states, as a fundamental pillar for peaceful coexistence and security

[145] Within this scope, the main objectives of National Defense are derived, relating to maintaining the sovereignty of the State and the integrity of the territory, as defined in Article 84 of the Constitution of the Republic of El Salvador; developing and maintaining a modern National Defense System appropriate to the country's reality; and contributing to the maintenance of international peace and security

[146] From these objectives, the following are also derived: a) Protecting the life and physical integrity of the Salvadoran population, as well as the country's assets and resources

[147] Guarantee the legal order of the State through the preservation of the rule of law and democratic institutions, within the framework of the constitutional mandate

[148] Promote and strengthen the commitment, unity, and cohesion of Salvadorans to National Defense

[149] To project El Salvador towards the achievement of its national interests in the international system

[150] e) Strengthen the elements and organizations of the National Defense System

[151] f) Modernize the Armed Forces for the effective fulfillment of the constitutional mission

[152] g) Contribute to preserving international peace and security

[302] In general terms, this vision implies that El Salvador should have an Armed Force led by highly trained officers and non-commissioned officers; versatile and capable of confronting diverse and unforeseen threats, of a scale appropriate to the country's needs; more closely aligned with the general interests of society; and solidly grounded in civic and democratic values, without losing its characteristics of discipline, courage, obedience, subordination, and other virtues that characterize the military establishment

[304] The Arce Plan 2010, in addition to representing the instrument of modernization, describes the Military Policy to be achieved until the year 2010, deriving from it the following institutional strategic objectives: a) Regarding education: Promote education and the culture of National Defense in Salvadoran society and strengthen the Educational System of the Armed Forces through the development of an educational strategy in accordance with the evolving demands of the Nation

[305] b) Regarding doctrine: Develop the systematization of institutional doctrine, harmonizing the organization, education, training and planning system of the Armed Forces

[306] Regarding institutional projection: Consolidate civil-military relations and support for various government programs through participation and contribution to national development

[307] d) Regarding support for the population in cases of disaster: Strengthen capacities aimed at supporting the population in cases of catastrophe

[308] e) Regarding legislation: Propose, update and harmonize the legal regulations that govern the National Defense System

[309] “National Defense is everyone's task” f) Regarding financing: Establish a budget structure that allows strengthening institutional capacities

[310] g) Regarding the organization: Design and implement an organization that allows for a high degree of versatility to meet the needs of the State within the constitutional framework

[311] Regarding the modernization of the branches of the Armed Forces: Develop the capabilities of the Army, Air Force, and Navy, with the goal of achieving a level of operational readiness that allows them to fulfill the constitutional mission and, additionally, the tasks generated in compliance with government programs

[312] i) Regarding the Armed Forces Logistics System: Optimize the System so that it is sustainable and allows support to military units in fulfilling their missions

[314] The budget management of the National Defense Branch is carried out based on the regulations established by the Ministry of Finance, specifically the Integrated Financial Administration Law and its regulations

[315] Resource allocation

[316] The purpose of the budget policy is to make rational use of the assigned human, material, technological, and financial resources to keep the Armed Forces capable of fulfilling their constitutional mission of defending the sovereignty and integrity of the territory; to participate in missions established by the President of the Republic, within the framework of exceptional circumstances and in compliance with the constitutional mission; and to collaborate on public works and assistance to the population in cases of national disasters

[317] In this context, budget policy is geared toward promoting the ongoing modernization of education, communications, weapons, logistics, and administrative systems. Likewise, priorities are determined for the allocation of financial resources, which are directed toward, among other things: a) Improving operational readiness, maintaining a reasonable number of highly trained and equipped personnel under the command and control of strong leadership; and strengthening and modernizing organizational branches to effectively monitor the international political boundary, territorial waters, and national airspace

[318] Strengthen the training and development of military reserves as a means of improving mobilization capacity in the event of a national emergency

[319] Continue strengthening the educational system and training of members of the institution; improve facilities and logistical services in military units; and provide healthcare to their members and their families

[320] Modernize activities related to registration and document issuance; control and regulation of firearms, ammunition, explosives, and similar items; Continue supporting the National Civil Police in the fight against crime to maintain internal peace

[321] Strengthen institutional representation abroad, contributing to efforts to maintain international peace and security

[322] g) Continue improving communications systems that allow for efficient command and control at all levels of military leadership

[323] The institutional budget structure has been designed according to the general parameters established by the policies of the Integrated Financial Administration System (SAFI), which includes activities of the Armed Forces through the establishment of the following budgetary units: a) The Institutional Management and Administration Unit, whose main purpose is to provide efficient and timely support to military agencies, units and offices to ensure the normal functioning of institutional activities

[324] The Institutional Operational Management Unit, which aims to ensure the effective fulfillment of the constitutional mission through the various land, air, naval, and institutional support units, in order to carry out the operational activities of the Armed Forces

[325] The Institutional Investment Unit, which is responsible for implementing projects related to the improvement of physical infrastructure, communications systems, and the acquisition of equipment to replace obsolete or deteriorated equipment

[326] Within this structure, each budget unit is subdivided into work streams, which represent related activities that combine to achieve more specific objectives and contribute to the achievement of the overall goals of each unit

[327] Thus, the budgetary unit of the Institutional Directorate and Administration, for the purpose of directing and controlling management, includes the line of work known as Senior Administration, as well as that of institutional financial management, to administer the financial resources of the National Defense Branch

[328] Regarding the Institutional Operational Management budget unit, the lines of work relating to Army and institutional support operations, the Air Force, and the Navy are distinguished, established to maintain the operational readiness of units in the land, air, and naval spheres, respectively

[329] Similarly, the Institutional Investment budget unit includes the following lines of work: physical infrastructure, established to build, remodel, improve, and maintain the institution's facilities; strategic communications, to develop radio communication systems at the various levels of military leadership; and institutional equipment, to acquire equipment to replace obsolete or deteriorated equipment in the areas of information technology, military industry and health, ground, air, and naval transportation, and administrative and logistical support

[330] The budget management process is based on the institutional budget cycle, which is nothing more than a series of integrated and interrelated phases and processes designed to achieve the objectives set by the Armed Forces for the effective fulfillment of its constitutional mission

[331] This cycle extends from the formulation phase to the implementation phase, concluding with the annual budget closing. These phases are implemented through budgetary procedures that facilitate management

[332] Regarding the formulation of the institutional budget project, the draft is first formulated eighteen months in advance, before the start of the fiscal year to be budgeted. To this end, the Budget Formulation Directive is drawn up, which establishes the general policies, and is distributed to all relevant units of the National Defense Branch, so that they may submit their draft budgets based on objectives and goals

[333] “National Defense is everyone's task.” The Institutional Financial Directorate then reviews and consolidates the draft budgets in order to present the draft budget for the National Defense Branch to the Institutional Budget Committee for approval and subsequently submit it to the High Command for authorization and then submit it to the Ministry of Finance in July of the year prior to the start of the budgeted fiscal year

[334] On another level, the project is integrated with those of other State institutions to form the General Budget, which is presented to the Honorable Legislative Assembly before September 30, for review by the Finance and Special Budget Committee

[335] This general budget, once approved by the Legislative Assembly, becomes the General Budget of the Nation for the given fiscal year, which runs from January 1 to December 31 of each year

[336] Based on the approved budget, the fiscal year begins and the budget execution phase begins, which includes activities related to budget programming, the institutional cash budget, the recording of budget commitments, budget modifications, accrual of obligations, the request and collection of transfers of funds, payment of obligations and their respective bank reconciliations, accounting records, and monthly and annual accounting closings

[337] Institutional Procurement System

[338] The system in the National Defense Branch is regulated by the Law on Public Administration Acquisitions and Contracting (LACAP) and is administered by the Institutional Acquisitions and Contracting Directorate (DACI), which is responsible for developing the Procurement Plan in the most effective, efficient, and transparent manner. This plan establishes the execution of all Armed Forces acquisitions for the fiscal year, through procedures such as free management, public bidding, and direct purchase, among others
