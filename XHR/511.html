[326] In the exercise of democratic oversight, especially oversight by the Legislative Branch and citizen oversight, evaluations of the progress of the National Security System constitute an accountability tool that allows for reporting on the results of investments in security and the benefits to the population

[327] Participation will be promoted to ensure citizen oversight of public action, fostering access to information and discussion on security issues as matters of public interest

[328] In this sense, society will have political spaces for convergence of security interests, so that these can be realized in joint actions that reflect their expectations, contributing to the democratic system

[329] Likewise, the influence of various sectors in the design, implementation, and evaluation of public policies in the area of National Security will be promoted
