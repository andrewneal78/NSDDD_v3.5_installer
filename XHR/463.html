[1] LAW OF MONGOLIA September 1, 2016 Ulaanbaatar ON THE DEFENSE OF MONGOLIA (Revised) CHAPTER

[2] ONE GENERAL PRINCIPLES Article

[3] Objectives of the Law

[4] The objective of this Law is to determine the defense system and activities of Mongolia and to regulate relations related to the rights and obligations of state bodies, administrations, territorial units, business entities, organizations, and citizens

[5] Legislation on defense

[6] Legislation on defense consists of the Constitution of Mongolia, this Law, and other legislative acts issued in accordance with these laws

[7] If an international treaty of Mongolia provides otherwise than in this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply

[8] Definition of Legal Terms

[9] The following terms used in this Law shall be understood as follows

[10] Defense" means a set of political, economic, social, legal, informational and military measures aimed at ensuring the state's readiness to protect the independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of Mongolia from external armed attacks and aggression; 3.1.2. "Defense capability" means a set of political, economic, social, legal and military measures aimed at implementing defense activities, and the management and organizational structure for their implementation; 3.1.4. "Defense policy" means the goals and objectives determined by the state regarding the defense of the state; 3.1.5. "Defense interests" means ensuring the independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity, and inviolability of the borders of Mongolia; 3.1.6. "Local defense" means what is specified in Article 4.1.1 of the Law on Local Defense; /This provision was amended by the Law of June 27, 2018./ 3.1.7. "State military organization" means the system of state military organizations; 3.1.8. "Military organization" means a state organization whose functions are to ensure the independence, territorial integrity, and security of the people of Mongolia by military force, whose activities are regulated by common legal norms and which uses military ranks and uniforms; 3.1.9. "Defense material resources" means resources intended to meet the needs of military material and equipment in peacetime and in times of war

[11] Principles of Defense Operations

[12] The following principles shall be adhered to in defense operations

[13] Prioritizing national interests

[14] Upholding the rule of law

[15] Having unified and centralized leadership

[16] Being based on local defense

[17] The armed forces shall be based on professional soldiers

[18] Being based on a unified command and planning mobilization system

[19] Being unified and coordinated

[20] Being based on scientific and technological achievements

[21] Mutual responsibility of state institutions and citizens

[22] Preserving national unity

[23] Defense activities

[24] Defense is a component of the activities to ensure the national security of Mongolia

[25] The purpose of defense activities is to protect the independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity, inviolability of the state borders of Mongolia, and ensure the security of citizens

[26] Defense capabilities are provided by the state economy, health of the population, education, the capabilities of the state military institutions, information and political-diplomatic forces

[27] Political, economic, social, legal and military measures aimed at defending the state are unified

[28] Protecting the population from disasters, unexpected other dangers, and the harm of any means of destruction is a component of defense activities and has a unified planning and unified system

[29] Relations related to defense activities during a state of war or the declaration of a state of war shall be regulated by law

[30] The State's Duty to Defend the Country

[31] It is the duty of the State to defend the independence of the Motherland

[32] The State shall establish and strengthen a unified system of national defense

[33] Mongolia shall repel external armed attacks and aggression with its own armed forces and defend itself with the participation of the people, and may, if necessary, obtain assistance from international organizations and other countries

[34] Defense System

[35] The basis of the defense system of Mongolia shall be local defense based on the participation of the state, administrative organizations, and all citizens, an armed force based on professional soldiers, and mobilization with unified command and planning

[36] Mongolia shall establish a unified defense system based on local defense

[37] Local defense is the self-defense strategy of Mongolia and shall be based on the cooperation of all levels of state organizations and the participation of all the people

[38] Local defense relations shall be regulated by law

[39] The armed forces are a system of military organizations with the function of ensuring national security and defense, and Mongolia shall establish and strengthen a professional and capable armed force

[40] Mongolia shall establish a mobilization system with unified management and planning

[41] The relationship between ensuring military preparation and conducting mobilization shall be regulated by law

[42] CHAPTER TWO STATE MILITARY ORGANIZATION OF MONGOLIA Article

[43] State military organization of Mongolia

[44] The state military organization system of Mongolia shall consist of the armed forces, border and internal troops, judicial decision-making bodies, and emergency organizations in peacetime

[45] section was supplemented by the law of June

[46] State military organizations shall be unified in terms of military legal norms, common military regulations, military ranks, physical standards of military personnel, and participation in peacekeeping operations

[47] The structure, rights and functions of the armed forces, border and internal troops, court decision enforcement and emergency bodies shall be regulated by law

[48] section was supplemented by the law of June

[49] It is prohibited to use the state military organization in any way against the foundations of the state and social institutions, civil rights and legitimate interests established by the Constitution of Mongolia

[50] Management of the state military organization of Mongolia

[51] The management of the state military organization of Mongolia shall consist of the highest political, state administrative and military professional management, and these managements shall be implemented by the following organizations and officials within the framework of the Constitution of Mongolia, this law and other laws, rules and regulations

[52] The highest political management of the state military organization shall be the Great Khural of Mongolia

[53] The state administrative leadership of the state military organization is the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Mongolia, the Government of Mongolia, the member of the Government in charge of defense, border protection, judicial decision enforcement and emergency situations; /This provision was supplemented by the Law of June

[54] The highest military professional leadership of the state military organization is the General Staff of the Armed Forces

[55] The highest military professional leadership is understood as a comprehensive management activity aimed at implementing common military rules and planning joint operations between the armed forces, border and internal troops, judicial decision enforcement, and emergency situations organizations, approving a unified plan for joint training and preparation to ensure readiness, monitoring its implementation, ensuring the mutual coordination of military organizations' weapons and technology, and creating a unified communication and information system

[56] section was supplemented by the Law of June

[57] The military professional management of the state military organization shall be jointly implemented by the Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces, the Commander of the Border and Internal Troops, the Head of the Court Enforcement Agency, the Head of the Emergency Situations Agency and the commander of the military unit, and the Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces shall be responsible for ensuring its coordination

[59] Defense Management
