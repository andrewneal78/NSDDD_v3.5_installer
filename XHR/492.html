[484] Brazilian outer space exploration capabilities

[485] Access to images, communication media and global positioning – updated and from any part of the globe – will be increased, as well as the processing of information from them, constantly and progressively facilitating the planning, execution and control of human activities, both for civilian and military purposes

[486] Given the history and current stage of development of its space activities, Brazil will remain dependent on contracting foreign services, and it is likely that the acceleration of investments and government initiatives, such as the company created to work on the integration of the Geostationary Defense and Strategic Communications Satellite (SGDC) system, among others, will gradually reduce external dependence

[487] However, due to the increase in the number of countries launching satellites and the demand for communications services, certain types of orbits and frequency bands will likely soon be saturated

[488] As a result of the increase in scientific research and the greater availability of data produced for this purpose by space means, there will be an increase in agricultural productivity

[489] For the same reasons, knowledge about the environment, as well as the possibilities for preventing and predicting natural disasters, will be expanded

[566] Location of Brazilian centers of gravity

[614] Opportunities Economic Growth, Cooperation and Multilateralism

[616] Diplomatic and economic instruments, therefore, could increase national power and, consequently, Brazil's deterrent capacity

[617] ID 09 – Increased global demand for natural resources

[627] ID 14 – Exchanges and exercises with NATO/European Union countries

[629] The doctrinal standards, organizational structures, and command and control processes adopted by UN forces in peacekeeping missions, like those of the EU itself, are analogous to those of NATO

[649] The high circulation of people and goods around the world, or even deliberate actions, could cause pandemics, affecting humans and animal and agricultural production
