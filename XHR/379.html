[10] I. General Provisions

[11] The Military Doctrine of Ukraine (hereinafter referred to as the Military Doctrine) is a system of views on the causes, essence and nature of modern military conflicts, the principles and ways of preventing their occurrence, the preparation of the state for a possible military conflict, as well as on the use of military force to protect state sovereignty, territorial integrity, and other vital national interests

[12] The legal basis of the Military Doctrine is the Constitution of Ukraine, the laws of Ukraine, the National Security Strategy of Ukraine, approved by Decree of the President of Ukraine dated May 26, 2015 No. 287, as well as international treaties of Ukraine, the consent to which was given by the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine

[13] The Military Doctrine is based on the results of the analysis and forecasting of the military-political situation, the principles of defense adequacy, non-nuclear status, high readiness for defense, systematic defense planning, as well as the principles of domestic and foreign policy defined by the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine

[14] The main provisions of the Military Doctrine are derived from the National Security Strategy of Ukraine, develop its provisions in the areas of ensuring military security and are aimed at countering aggression from the Russian Federation, achieving by Ukraine the criteria necessary for membership in the European Union and the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, ensuring equal mutually beneficial cooperation in the military, military-economic and military-technical spheres with all interested partner states

[15] The terms used in the Military Doctrine have the following meanings: **military policy of Ukraine** - the activities of subjects of ensuring the national security of the state, related to the prevention of military conflicts, the organization and implementation of military construction and the training of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, and special-purpose law enforcement agencies for the armed protection of national interests; **military-political situation** - the state of military-political relations at a certain moment (in a certain period); **military-political relations** - the totality of intentions and actions of the parties (states, coalitions of states, international corporations, political parties, social movements) aimed at achieving their own interests with the use of military instruments together with all other available instruments in the political, military, economic and other spheres of life; **military conflict** - a form of resolving interstate or intrastate contradictions with the bilateral use of military force; the main types of military conflicts are war and armed conflict; **armed conflict** - an armed clash between states (international armed conflict, armed conflict on the state border) or between warring parties within the territory of one state, as a rule, with external support (internal armed conflict); **local war** - a war between two or more states, in which military actions are limited to the territories of the warring states and pursue military-political goals that mainly affect the interests of only these states (territorial, political, economic, etc.); **regional war** - a war that covers a certain region (part of a continent) with the participation of all or most of the states of the region and with the aim of achieving military-political goals that affect the interests of these states; **military-political challenge** - intentions or actions of one of the parties to military-political relations, aimed at achieving their own goals without taking into account the interests of the other party, with awareness of the possibility of harming such interests; **military-political risk** - intentions or actions of one of the parties to military-political relations, which under certain conditions may harm the national interests of the other party; **threat of the use of military force** - intentions or actions of one of the parties to military-political relations, which indicate readiness to use military force against the other party in order to achieve their own goals; **defense-industrial complex** - a set of enterprises, institutions and organizations of industry and science that develop, produce, modernize and utilize military products, perform services in the interests of defense to equip and materially support the security and defense forces, and also supply military and dual-use goods, provide military services during the implementation of military-technical cooperation measures of Ukraine with foreign states; **security and defense sector** - a set of state authorities, the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, and special-purpose law enforcement agencies, whose activities, according to their functional purpose in accordance with the Constitution and laws of Ukraine, are aimed at protecting national interests from external and internal threats to the national security of Ukraine, covered by a single leadership; **security forces** - state law enforcement and intelligence agencies, civil defense forces and bodies of general competence, which are entrusted by the Constitution and laws of Ukraine with the functions of ensuring the national security of Ukraine; **defense forces** - the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, as well as law enforcement and intelligence agencies, in terms of involving them in the performance of tasks related to the defense of the state; **defense forces capabilities** - the ability to achieve the required result when performing defense tasks in certain conditions in accordance with defined action scenarios and using available resources; **strategic communications** - coordinated and proper use of the state's communication capabilities - public diplomacy, public relations, military relations, information and psychological operations, measures aimed at promoting the state's goals

[16] Security environment (global, regional and national aspects) in the context of military security

[17] The security environment around Ukraine is complex and dynamic

[18] Due to the armed conflict in Eastern Ukraine, military-political instability in the Middle East, and the struggle for influence on global financial and energy flows, global military-political instability is intensifying

[19] Leading states are increasing military spending, intensifying the development of new types of weapons, and increasing the intensity of military exercises

[20] The formation and development of the security environment in the world is taking place under the influence of the following main trends: increasing contradictions regarding the division of spheres of influence between world centers of power, increasing their aggressiveness, intransigence, the desire to upset the military-strategic balance in their favor, in particular, the aggravation of the confrontation between the USA and the Russian Federation; aggravation of the security situation in the countries of the Middle East and North Africa, the intensification of religious extremism and the spread of ideas of radical Islam in the countries of Central Asia, contradictions between the Asian-Pacific states regarding the ownership of island zones; the current crisis and uncertainty of the foundations of the new system of international security, the weakening of the role of international security institutions, attempts to strengthen the role of military force outside the existing mechanisms of international security; shifting the emphasis in military conflicts to the asymmetric use of military force by armed formations not provided for by law, shifting the emphasis in conducting military conflicts to the complex use of military and non-military instruments (economic, political, informational and psychological, etc.), which fundamentally changes the nature of armed struggle; violation of the norms and principles of international law enshrined in the UN Charter, the Final Act of the Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe of 1975 and other international treaties; weakening of legislative restrictions on the use of military force by states outside their own territory; global climate change, reduction in natural resource reserves, shortage of drinking water and food, increased migration processes, as well as increased risks of large-scale emergencies of a natural and man-made nature; expansion of the scale of terrorism, piracy, and other phenomena associated with the use of armed violence

[21] The main trends affecting the military-political situation in the region around Ukraine are: the spread of the practice of conducting special operations and provocative actions to create conflict situations; increased internal instability in neighboring states caused by intervention by other states, a decrease in the standard of living of the population, ineffectiveness of leadership actions, attempts by ethnic groups to separately solve pressing problems; intensive modernization of the armed forces by neighboring states, intensification of the development of new-generation weapons and military equipment with fundamentally new firepower and control capabilities; increased militarization in the region around Ukraine, an increase in foreign military presence in the territories of neighboring states; active destabilizing foreign and security policy of the Russian Federation towards neighboring states, as well as towards international organizations, including NATO and the EU; refusal or evasion of the Russian Federation from fulfilling its obligations under international treaties in the field of arms control, strengthening trust and security in military activities; modernization and improvement of technical intelligence systems and complexes by special services of foreign states, increasing their capabilities, attempts at unauthorized access to the objects of the information infrastructure of Ukraine; slowing down the process of contractual and legal registration of state borders and delimitation of exclusive (maritime) economic zones and continental shelf between states; information war of the Russian Federation against Ukraine

[22] Foreign policy conditions do not contribute to the settlement of the armed conflict in Eastern Ukraine

[23] Under such circumstances, Ukraine can rely primarily on its own forces and the support of the USA, EU and NATO member states, which believe that preserving the independence and territorial integrity of Ukraine is one of the determining factors in ensuring global and regional stability

[24] Current military threats to Ukraine are: armed aggression and violation of the territorial integrity of Ukraine (temporary occupation by the Russian Federation of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, the city of Sevastopol and military aggression by the Russian Federation in certain areas of the Donetsk and Luhansk regions), the buildup of the military power of the Russian Federation in the immediate vicinity of the state border of Ukraine, including the potential possibility of deploying tactical nuclear weapons on the territory of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea; militarization by the Russian Federation of the temporarily occupied territory by forming new military formations and units, as well as supplying militants, military equipment and logistical support; the presence of a military contingent of the Russian Federation in the Transnistrian region of the Republic of Moldova, which can be used to destabilize the situation in the southern regions of Ukraine; the buildup by the Russian Federation of a group of troops with a powerful offensive potential near the state border of Ukraine, the creation of new ones, expansion and modernization of existing bases and military infrastructure facilities; intensification of intelligence and subversive activities in Ukraine by the special services of the Russian Federation with the aim of destabilizing the internal socio-political situation in Ukraine, as well as supporting illegal armed formations in the eastern regions of Ukraine and creating conditions for expanding the scale of armed aggression; activities of illegal armed formations on the territory of Ukraine aimed at destabilizing the internal socio-political situation in Ukraine, intimidating the population, depriving it of its freedom to resist, disrupting the functioning of state authorities, local self-government, important industrial and infrastructure facilities; territorial claims of the Russian Federation to Ukraine and encroachment on its sovereignty and territorial integrity

[25] Military-political challenges that may develop into a threat of the use of military force against Ukraine are: interference in the internal affairs of Ukraine by the Russian Federation, aimed at violating the constitutional order, territorial integrity and sovereignty of Ukraine, internal socio-political stability and law and order; opposition to the implementation of the European choice of the Ukrainian people, the formation of collective security systems with the participation of Ukraine; unresolved issues regarding the delimitation of the state border of Ukraine in the waters of the Black and Azov Seas, incomplete contractual and legal registration of the state border of Ukraine with the Russian Federation, the Republic of Belarus and the Republic of Moldova; attempts by the Russian Federation to destabilize the socio-political and economic situation in Ukraine, as well as provoking separatist sentiments in areas of compact residence of national minorities on the territory of Ukraine; targeted information (information and psychological) influence using modern information technologies aimed at forming a negative international image of Ukraine, as well as at destabilizing the internal socio-political situation, aggravation of interethnic and interconfessional relations in Ukraine or its individual regions and places of compact residence of national minorities; actions of the Russian Federation to complicate and slow down the economic development of Ukraine; proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, terrorism, organized crime, illegal trade in weapons and ammunition, illegal migration

[26] Taking into account the development trends and the nature of the military-political situation, in the medium term, armed conflicts with the use by the enemy of both its own armed forces and armed formations not provided for by law, terrorist groups, mercenaries as a tool to achieve its own interests are most likely

[27] The likelihood of such conflicts escalating into war will increase significantly

[28] Threats to the military security of Ukraine, if implemented, may lead to a change in the constitutional order of Ukraine, further occupation of Ukraine or its individual territories, establishment of direct or indirect control over Ukraine and loss of its state sovereignty and territorial integrity

[29] Threats to the military security of Ukraine may be implemented in the following scenarios: full-scale armed aggression of the Russian Federation against Ukraine with the conduct of land, air, space, and sea operations with decisive military-political goals; a separate special operation of the Russian Federation against Ukraine with the use of military units and/or units, fire strikes, information, information-psychological operations (actions) in combination with the use of non-military measures, including peacekeeping forces in the absence of a corresponding decision of the UN Security Council; blockade of seaports, coast or airspace of Ukraine with the use of military force, disruption of Ukraine's communications by the Russian Federation; an armed conflict within the state, inspired by the Russian Federation with an attempt to separate administrative-territorial units in the eastern and southern regions of Ukraine from Ukraine, with the participation of armed formations not provided for by law, terrorist groups in cooperation with political, non-governmental, ethnic, religious or other organizations; an armed conflict on the state border of Ukraine, in particular border armed incidents (provocations, clashes) with regular or irregular forces of the Russian Federation, armed formations not provided for by law; terrorist acts on the territory of Ukraine or against citizens of Ukraine, attacks on the lives of state or public figures, representatives of foreign states (committed with the aim of provoking war or international complications), sabotage (including at critical infrastructure facilities), as well as explosions, arson in the premises of state authorities and their seizure, kidnapping of citizens or taking hostages

[30] Ukraine's ability to adequately respond to challenges and risks to military security is negatively affected by internal economic and socio-political factors: economic crisis, violation of the integrity of the national economy as a result of the temporary occupation by the Russian Federation of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea and the city of Sevastopol and the activities of illegal armed formations supported by it in certain areas of the Donetsk and Luhansk regions, the resulting limitation of the state's financial capabilities; imbalance and incompleteness of systemic reforms in the field of national security and defense, insufficient resource provision for the defense forces and inefficient use of available resources; low efficiency of the system of state management of subjects of ensuring Ukraine's national security in the military sphere, imperfection of mechanisms for planning the development of such subjects; insufficient level of readiness of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, as well as special-purpose law enforcement agencies to conduct modern armed struggle; low efficiency of state bodies conducting intelligence and counterintelligence activities; insufficient level of coordination and coherence of actions of state authorities, local self-government bodies, low level of training of their specialists on security and defense issues; inconsistency with modern challenges of civil defense forces and their technical equipment; insufficient and unprofessional efforts of state authorities of Ukraine in the field of countering propaganda and information and psychological operations of the Russian Federation

[31] Military-economic and military-technical support of the military security of the state directly depends on the country's defense-industrial complex, the main problems of its functioning are: low efficiency of implementation of military-technical and military-industrial policy, policy of military-technical cooperation, unregulated issues of determining state customers for the development and implementation of state target programs of reform and development; lack of state regulation and insufficient support for strategically important scientific institutions and industrial enterprises for the defense, security and economy of the state in order to transition from a raw material model of building the state economy to a model of innovative development, meeting the needs of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service and other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, as well as special-purpose law enforcement agencies, in weapons, military and special equipment; lack of technologically closed production cycles for most types of weapons and military equipment, destruction of traditional scientific, technical and production cooperation, low rates of diversification of purchases of military and dual-use goods; critical physical and moral wear and tear of fixed assets, low efficiency of the scientific and production base, significant energy intensity of production, technological lag behind the leading countries of the world, critical financial and economic condition of most enterprises, low profitability of production, lack of working capital and lack of investment resources, insufficient capacity of the domestic market for defense products, which significantly complicates its sale on the foreign market; critical condition of providing enterprises of the defense-industrial complex with highly qualified workers, technical and engineering personnel

[32] Goals and main tasks of military policy

[33] Ukraine seeks to maintain friendly relations with all states of the world on the basis of international treaties concluded on the principles of equality, non-interference in internal affairs, mutual respect for independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity

[34] The main goal of Ukraine's military policy is to create conditions for the restoration of the territorial integrity of the state, its sovereignty and inviolability within the state border of Ukraine

[35] The main goals in the field of Ukraine's military policy are: repelling armed aggression of the Russian Federation against Ukraine; ensuring Ukraine's defense capability at a level sufficient to prevent the outbreak of an armed conflict, and in the event of an armed conflict - to localize and neutralize it; Ukraine's participation in the implementation of the common security and defense policy of the European Union; improving the system of ensuring military security, which would guarantee reliable protection of the state from external and internal threats, a worthy perception of Ukraine at the international level and meet the criteria for Ukraine's membership in the EU and NATO

[36] Based on the principles of domestic and foreign policy, taking into account the nature of current threats to national security, the main tasks of Ukraine's military policy in the near future and in the medium term are: localization and neutralization in the shortest possible time of the military-political crisis in the eastern regions of Ukraine, preventing its development into large-scale armed aggression; elimination of armed formations not provided for by law and restoration of full control of the state border of Ukraine; restoration of the prestige of military service; integration of volunteer formations that were formed or self-organized to protect the independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of Ukraine, took direct part in the anti-terrorist operation in Donetsk and Luhansk regions, ensuring its conduct, into the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine, the National Guard of Ukraine, other military formations and law enforcement agencies formed in accordance with the laws of Ukraine; reforming the Armed Forces of Ukraine in order to achieve operational and technical compatibility with the armed forces of NATO member states; improving the system of mobilization training and mobilization, including ensuring the creation of the necessary number of trained military reserves; maintaining, in conditions of armed confrontation and a real threat of aggression, the mixed principle of manning the Armed Forces of Ukraine and other military formations formed in accordance with the laws of Ukraine with a gradual increase in the number of contract servicemen in combat military units

[37] The manning of non-combat military units and positions that do not determine combat readiness in combat military units will be carried out mainly by conscripts and those called up for military service during mobilization (in the event of a decision on mobilization); adhering to the principle of not involving conscripts in combat operations; creating a unified system of specialized intelligence with the appropriate infrastructure for receiving and processing information in a time regime close to real; creation of an integrated security and defense sector of the state as the main element of the military security system, integration of the capabilities of its components for timely and effective response to existing and potential threats; ensuring the enhancement of the capabilities of the defense forces necessary to achieve the goals of military policy; increasing the capabilities of the domestic defense-industrial complex through the introduction of the latest military technologies, creating the most closed cycles possible for the development and production of the most important types of weapons, special and military equipment, using the opportunities for military-technical cooperation with states - strategic partners of Ukraine; improving the state information policy in the military sphere; preventing and effectively countering the information and psychological influences of foreign states aimed at undermining the defense capability, violating the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Ukraine, destabilizing the internal socio-political situation, provoking interethnic and interfaith conflicts in Ukraine; ensuring social guarantees for military personnel, law enforcement officers, participants in the anti-terrorist operation in Donetsk and Luhansk regions and their family members; improving the system of democratic civilian control over the security and defense sector of the state in accordance with EU and NATO standards; increasing the level of coordination of the components of the security and defense sector and improving the mechanisms of their consolidated development and strengthening the relevant operational capabilities to ensure military security

[38] The defined goals and objectives of the military policy correspond to the current state and medium-term forecast of the military-political situation and may be specified taking into account changes in the security environment, conditions of socio-economic development of Ukraine, and capabilities of the defense forces

[39] After the restoration of the territorial integrity of Ukraine, the main goal of the military policy will be to prevent the emergence of new armed conflicts, systematically strengthen the defense capabilities of the state, and increase the role and authority of Ukraine in the international arena

[40] Ukraine will maintain a level of defense capability that, while fully utilizing the opportunities for peaceful settlement of interstate disputes, will correspond to the level of military threats and at the same time ensure military-strategic parity in the region

[41] The training of Ukraine's defense in terms of content, direction and scale is carried out in accordance with the Law of Ukraine "On the Defense of Ukraine

[42] The training of the defense forces of Ukraine is focused on conducting both defensive and counter-offensive and offensive actions

[43] In accordance with this, programs and plans for combat and operational training, combat statutes and instructions of the Armed Forces of Ukraine are developed

[44] The main principle of using defense forces in a military conflict is active defense with the aim of defeating the enemy and forcing him to cease military (combat) actions

[45] Special attention is paid to the defense of the most important operationally and strategically important borders and areas, zones, administrative-political and economic centers, areas of the Black Sea and Azov coasts that are dangerous for landing, and communications

[46] The defense of Ukraine by conducting joint operations of the defense forces is combined with territorial defense, and resistance is organized in territories temporarily occupied by the enemy

[47] The basis of the territorial defense forces are units of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, other military formations formed in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, as well as special-purpose law enforcement agencies

[48] During the implementation of defense measures, the management bodies and forces of the unified state civil defense system ensure the elimination of the consequences of emergency situations caused by the use of weapons, and the protection of the population and territories from the consequences of military operations

[49] Given the presence of environmentally hazardous facilities on the territory of Ukraine and neighboring states, as well as a certain probability of the enemy using weapons of mass destruction in war, the security and defense forces and the population of Ukraine are preparing for action in conditions of radiation, chemical pollution and bacteriological contamination, large fires and significant areas of flooding

[50] Ukraine does not exclude the possibility of using military force also for the localization and liquidation of an internal armed conflict

[67] Ukraine is conducting a strategic review of the defense concept, taking into account the experience of overcoming the current crisis, introducing new methods of defense management, which are based on Euro-Atlantic experience and meet the single criterion - high efficiency at acceptable costs

[83] Compared with the Russian Federation, Ukraine's economic, military, human, information and other resources are significantly smaller

[84] Taking into account the presence of strategic nuclear weapons in the Russian Federation and its abuse of its status as a permanent member of the UN Security Council, the reaction of the world community to Russian aggression against Ukraine also becomes limited

[85] The armed conflict in the eastern regions of Ukraine has revealed serious shortcomings in the military-economic policy of our state, in particular, the long-term underfunding of the needs of the defense forces and the irrational use of allocated resources, the lack of state support for the reform and development of the defense-industrial complex

[86] Problems are becoming more acute in the defense-industrial complex of Ukraine, caused, in particular, by the breakdown of cooperative relations with the Russian Federation, the loss of fixed assets of enterprises as a result of the temporary occupation of the territory of Ukraine

[87] In the military-technical sphere, the issues of the lack of modern means of armed struggle, the unavailability of the repair base, and the malfunctioning of weapons and military equipment remain problematic

[88] Economic support for military security will be carried out through the formation and implementation of a fundamentally new unified military-economic, military-industrial and military-technical policy, the main directions of which are: determining at the state level long-term scientific and material and technical needs of defense, ensuring the creation, production, repair and modernization of weapons, military and special equipment and ammunition to meet security and defense needs in accordance with the nature and scale of military threats, goals, priorities and objectives of the state's military policy; implementing a system of strategic planning for the development of the defense-industrial complex, interconnected with the goals and objectives of state policy in the field of national security and defense, socio-economic and scientific and technical development of Ukraine; forming a balanced structure of the defense-industrial complex, determining priority areas of its reform and development, technical rearmament, ensuring maximum utilization and building up of the scientific and production potential of the defense sector of the economy; ensuring the interaction of science and production, preservation and development of basic and critical technologies, creation of a state fund for the development of basic and critical technologies and support of innovations in the defense-industrial complex; implementation of a set of organizational, technical, economic, legal and other measures aimed at reducing Ukraine's dependence on critical imports of products (goods, works, services), increasing the efficiency of international scientific and technical cooperation, primarily with EU and NATO member states; ensuring the financial recovery of scientific institutions and production enterprises and their sustainable functioning by implementing a set of measures and economic mechanisms of targeted state support and state protectionism regarding direct procurement from enterprises of the defense-industrial complex of products to meet priority state needs within the framework of the state defense order; improvement of the system of creation and preservation of mobilization stocks, development and maintenance of production capacities for mobilization purposes, formation and maintenance in proper condition of the state material reserve; creation of a system for continuous provision of scientific institutions and production enterprises of the defense-industrial complex with information, analytical and other materials on world achievements in the field of science, engineering and technology, development of weapons, military and special equipment; development and implementation of interrelated regulatory legal acts on the revival of the strategic role of the defense-industrial complex in the system of national security and defense of the state; creation of a system of state orders for the training of workers, technical and engineering personnel to meet the needs of the defense-industrial complex, favorable conditions for the effective functioning and development of research, technological and design institutions, design bureaus and production enterprises of the defense industry; provision of modern models of weapons and military equipment, in particular their development and production by the forces of the domestic defense-industrial complex, including under foreign licenses, development and production together with foreign partners, import of weapons and military equipment, the development and production of which in Ukraine is impractical or technologically impossible; improving the mechanisms for forming and controlling defense spending, optimizing budget expenditures and ensuring their rational distribution; combating corruption in all its forms

[89] The development of economic and military potential in the context of ensuring Ukraine's defense capability will require some time and the implementation of radical reforms envisaged by the Sustainable Development Strategy "Ukraine - 2020", approved by Decree of the President of Ukraine dated January 12, 2015 No

[90] To achieve its interests, Ukraine will develop the national economy, increase military power, participate in maintaining international security, use all possible peaceful ways to resolve conflicts and crisis situations, and if necessary, use military force

[91] V. Ways to achieve the goals of Ukraine's military policy

[92] The decisive factor in strengthening military security is the reform of the defense forces

[93] The reform of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, the State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine, the State Special Transport Service, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, and special-purpose law enforcement agencies is carried out with the aim of creating effective, mobile, equipped with modern weapons, military and special equipment, defense forces capable of ensuring the defense of the state

[94] The formation of national defense capabilities will be carried out by: improving the legislation on defense of Ukraine, proper regulation of activities in the military sphere and adaptation of basic legislative, conceptual and program documents on defense to modern realities; improving the interaction and coordination of actions of state authorities and components of the security and defense sector, taking into account the peculiarities of modern armed struggle, during which not only traditional military operations (actions) are widely used, but also various non-military forces and means; creating and implementing a unified strategy for communications of security and defense sector entities, defining a single body for coordination and control of its implementation; improvement of the crisis planning and management system in the defense sector, implementation of troop management standards adopted in NATO member states, ensuring constant readiness of management bodies to perform tasks, their regular exercises and training, as well as a decentralized management style; improvement of analytical capabilities and interconnections at the state level, modernization of the military intelligence system at the strategic and operational-tactical levels, bringing military intelligence and military counterintelligence systems into line with NATO standards; clarification of functions and tasks, review of the structure and number of defense forces, in particular the Armed Forces of Ukraine and their functional structures - immediate response forces, surge forces, reserve forces, combat duty forces and means; improvement of the principles of application and preparation of defense forces for actions in conditions of modern war; restoration of military infrastructure, review of the locations of deployment of military units and formations of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and other components of the defense forces, taking into account the strengthening of the permanent military presence in the eastern and southern regions of Ukraine; development within the framework of creating a promising security and defense sector management system of the management system of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, other military formations established in accordance with the laws of Ukraine, as well as special purpose law enforcement agencies to ensure reliable management of troops (forces) in a special period without restructuring and conducting large-scale organizational measures, ensuring a higher level of readiness of the management system compared to the level of readiness of subordinate troops (forces); proactively ensuring a high level of combat training of personnel and combat coordination of military units with the subsequent performance of real combat missions by them; priority development of the Special Operations Forces of the Armed Forces of Ukraine in accordance with NATO standards; implementation of budget planning standards adopted by NATO in order to increase the efficiency of the use of budget resources, fully ensuring the key capabilities of the defense forces that determine their ability to win an armed conflict; ensuring the readiness of the defense forces financing system in a crisis period, formation and adjustment of financing procedures taking into account additional sources, effective management of reserves, and combating corruption; creation of a single effective system of logistics and supply of defense forces both in peacetime and wartime (special period) for the most prompt and proper satisfaction of their needs with centralized procurement of material and technical means; creation of a single unified system of personnel training for defense forces taking into account the experience of NATO member states, the civilian sector and business; reforming the system of military education and personnel training, increasing the prestige of military service, improving the financial and social security of servicemen and their families; reforming the state system of mobilization and mobilization training based on basic European approaches and taking into account the experience gained in forming a first-tier operational mobilization reserve, which will allow for rapid increase in forces during a crisis situation, with early prediction of the costs necessary for this; increasing combat potential, restoring serviceability, extending the resource, carrying out modernization, creating new systems and unifying models of weapons and military equipment of the Ground Forces and Air Forces of the Armed Forces of Ukraine; revival of the state's naval potential, development of the Naval Forces of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, which should be able to defend the coastline of the Black and Azov Seas, the exclusive (maritime) economic zone, as well as be involved in international operations of NATO and the EU; effective use of bilateral and multilateral cooperation with partners and allies in the military sphere, including by receiving military assistance from them; modernization of the military health care system in the direction of concentrating efforts on measures for medical support of troops (forces) in possible conflicts with its maximum integration with the civilian health care system; strengthening the capabilities of territorial defense, ensuring its effective functioning; improvement of the unified state civil protection system, bringing it into line with EU standards and ensuring its effective functioning, as well as equipping the civil protection forces with modern types of equipment, means and equipment; development of a comprehensive regulatory document on conducting special information operations, providing for the coordination of the conceptual apparatus, the definition of specialized structural units of state bodies and their tasks and powers in peacetime and wartime

[95] Standards of ethics for military personnel will be introduced, in particular regarding the value of human life and health, primarily regarding the personnel of combat units

[96] The number and structure of the defense forces and their components will be determined taking into account the state of the security environment and the needs of Ukraine's defense, the need to repel armed aggression of the Russian Federation and prevent expected conflicts, as well as the financial and economic capabilities of the state

[97] The defense forces will try to provide capabilities that primarily determine their ability to armed defense of Ukraine and repel armed aggression in the eastern regions of Ukraine

[98] The Armed Forces of Ukraine, in interaction with other components of the security and defense sector, will adhere to the standards adopted in the EU and NATO member states regarding the activities and distribution of functions and main tasks

[99] The main role in ensuring the military security of Ukraine belongs to the Armed Forces of Ukraine

[100] In solving the tasks of ensuring the military security of Ukraine, preparing it for armed defense, other components of the security and defense sector, taking into account the competence defined by law, will play the following role: **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine** - providing diplomatic means of protection and strengthening the independence, state sovereignty, security, territorial integrity and inviolability of the state border of Ukraine, its national interests; diplomatic support for the process of solving tasks related to ensuring the military security of Ukraine, preparing for armed defense of national interests; **National Guard of Ukraine** - participation in territorial defense, protection of the state border and the fight against terrorism, as well as with paramilitary or armed formations not provided for by law; maintaining public order and ensuring public safety in the conflict zone; protecting and defending important state facilities determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine; **State Border Service of Ukraine** - participation in the cessation of armed conflict on the state border, the fight against terrorism; cessation of armed and other provocations on the state border; protection of the state border and sovereign rights of Ukraine in its exclusive (maritime) economic zone; **Security Service of Ukraine** - the fight against terrorism, counterintelligence, countering the intelligence and subversive activities of foreign special services, combating corruption and organized crime; **Foreign Intelligence Service of Ukraine** - obtaining intelligence information, implementing special measures to influence and counteract external threats to the national security of Ukraine in the political, economic, military-technical, scientific-technical, informational and environmental spheres; participation in the fight against terrorism, international organized crime, illegal trade in weapons and technologies for their manufacture; **Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine** - countering criminal and unlawful encroachments, protecting and ensuring public order and public security in the conflict zone, protecting important state and regime facilities determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine; **State Emergency Service of Ukraine** - direct management of the activities of the unified state civil protection system in eliminating the consequences of emergencies caused by the use of weapons, and protecting the population and territories from the consequences of military operations; **State Service for Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine** - ensuring the functioning of government communications of the Supreme Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine with officials of the Armed Forces of Ukraine, other military formations, and special-purpose law enforcement agencies during their stay at control points, ensuring cyber protection of critical infrastructure facilities; **State Special Transport Service** - ensuring the stable functioning of transport; **State Security Department of Ukraine** - participation in the implementation of tasks of territorial defense, combating terrorism, ensuring the security of designated officials and protecting facilities

[101] The defense-industrial complex is entrusted with the tasks of ensuring the creation, production, repair and modernization of weapons, military and special equipment and ammunition to meet the needs of security and defense in accordance with the goals, priorities and objectives of the state's military policy

[102] The Armed Forces of Ukraine will interact with other components of the security and defense sector in the implementation of the tasks assigned to them and will avoid duplication of the functions and tasks of their structural units with the functions and tasks of units of other components of the security and defense forces

[103] The Armed Forces of Ukraine will be involved in implementing measures of the legal regime of martial law and a state of emergency, combating terrorism and piracy, strengthening the protection of the state border of Ukraine, the sovereign rights of Ukraine in its exclusive (maritime) economic zone and the continental shelf of Ukraine and their legal registration, countering the illegal transportation of weapons and narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues or precursors on the high seas, eliminating emergencies of a natural and man-made nature, providing military assistance to other countries, and will also participate in international military cooperation and joint operations with NATO

[104] To solve the tasks of ensuring the military security of Ukraine, the defense forces and their components will interact and coordinate their activities with each other and with other components of the security and defense sector, state bodies, local self-government bodies, non-governmental organizations and associations, in particular volunteer ones, institutions and enterprises, including the defense-industrial complex, citizens, and relevant entities of other states

[105] The level of interaction between the components of the defense forces and state authorities will be increased on the basis of the formation of permanent and temporary interdepartmental bodies

[106] Ukraine's rejection of one of the most powerful nuclear arsenals in the world gives it the right to count on support from the international community in the development of conventional defense capabilities that will guarantee Ukraine's military security, including through obtaining modern technologies and joint development of the latest weapons, forming military alliances and receiving foreign military assistance, and participating in the construction of regional missile defense systems

[107] Ukraine's external security guarantees will be created by forming a network of alliances with both individual states and regional organizations (by concluding agreements on joint defense or military assistance) and with international security organizations (by participating in the application of collective security mechanisms

[108] At the same time, in the medium term, Ukraine will primarily use its own capabilities and reserves the right to choose the method of guaranteeing state sovereignty and territorial integrity

[109] Ukraine believes that every state has the right to guarantee its own security by integrating into such security structures that best meet its interests

[110] Rejecting the policy of non-alignment, Ukraine is building new approaches to ensuring national security, and will prioritize participation in the improvement and development of Euro-Atlantic and European collective security systems

[111] To this end, Ukraine will integrate into the European political, economic, and legal space with the aim of gaining membership in the EU, as well as deepening cooperation with NATO to achieve the criteria necessary for gaining membership in this organization

[112] The priority task of deepening cooperation with NATO is to achieve full compatibility of the Armed Forces of Ukraine with the corresponding forces of NATO member states by

[113] Deepening cooperation with NATO involves: developing multilateral relations within the framework of modern NATO mechanisms, in particular within the framework of the Common Security and Defense Policy with the EU, the Charter on a Distinctive Partnership between Ukraine and the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, the Partnership for Peace program, the NATO Operational Capabilities Concept (COM/OSS), the NATO Force Planning and Assessment Process (PARP/PARP) and the Mediterranean Dialogue; developing Ukraine's bilateral relations with NATO member states; reliable fulfillment of the assumed partnership commitments, assuming a proportional share of responsibility in joint operations with NATO; impartial and transparent analysis of reforms in the Armed Forces of Ukraine with the aim of implementing NATO standards, ensuring the mobility of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and the efficiency of their deployment; ensuring the readiness of personnel, technical compatibility of weapons, military and special equipment, as well as operational interoperability of units of the Armed Forces of Ukraine and NATO member states within the framework of the NATO Operational Capabilities Concept (OCC) Review and Feedback Program

[114] Ukraine will adhere to the following principles of warfare adopted by NATO member states: **humanity** - compliance (non-exceeding) of the level of armed violence with universal human values and norms of international humanitarian law, as well as the set goal of the combat operations; **readiness** - early ensuring the level of readiness of forces and means to perform assigned tasks in accordance with the conditions of the combat situation; **purposefulness (selection and support for achieving goals)** - unity and unambiguousness of the goal of the combat operations, which is the key to their success; **security** - constant support of the operational environment in order to ensure the necessary freedom of action to achieve goals; **surprise** - the consequence of unpredictable actions, which ensures the enemy's confusion, loss of operational, tactical balance and unity of control; **concentration of efforts** - decisive synchronized use of combined forces and means to achieve the main goal of combat operations

[115] economy of efforts** - reasonable use of available capabilities to achieve the set goal while avoiding unnecessary excess; **flexibility** - the ability to quickly change the composition of forces and means, forms and methods of their combat use in accordance with changes in the situation without losing combat capabilities

[116] cooperation** - the application of combined coordinated efforts of forces and means in order to make the most complete use of their capabilities

[117] constancy** - the creation and use of forces and means capable of ensuring continuous impact on the enemy, depriving him of freedom of maneuver and the ability to restore forces

[118] Ukraine will use the principles of administrative leadership adopted in NATO member states: one hundred percent readiness of organizational structures and transparent distribution of powers between organizational structures and officials; a clear procedure for distributing and delegating responsibility during the formation and implementation of decisions in the entire management system - from the top military-political leadership to a separate unit; compliance of the functions, tasks and powers of structural units with their operational and tactical capabilities; efficiency of management, its ability to perform its functions in the event of non-standard tasks; implementation of innovative management methods, elimination of bureaucracy and combating corruption

[119] In the short and medium term, Ukraine will transform and adapt documents on defense planning, operational and combat management, statutes and guidelines to NATO standards, taking into account the experience of the anti-terrorist operation in the Donetsk and Luhansk regions

[120] Deepening cooperation and collaboration with NATO and the EU in the field of intelligence to counter the aggressive policy of the Russian Federation, international terrorist, religious extremist and criminal organizations, and the fight against cybercrime involves attracting the assistance of NATO and EU intelligence structures, as well as NATO and EU member states in reforming Ukraine's intelligence agencies, attracting NATO trust funds to provide resources for such reforms, and gaining access to information networks that are replenished with intelligence information from various sources, including from NATO and EU member states

[121] Deepening cooperation with NATO, reliable implementation of the assumed partnership commitments, transformation and adaptation of defense planning, operational and combat management documents, and achieving full compatibility of Ukraine's defense forces with the corresponding forces of NATO member states will contribute to achieving the necessary criteria for Ukraine to gain full membership in NATO

[122] Financing of defense needs

[123] Financing of the state's defense needs is carried out at the expense of and within the limits of the funds specified in the Law on the State Budget of Ukraine for the relevant year

[124] To achieve the goals of the Military Doctrine, the total amount of defense expenditures must be at least three percent of the planned amount of gross domestic product for the relevant year

[125] Priority areas for financing defense needs are: conducting intensive combat training of military units and divisions of the defense forces; implementing the state defense order taking into account the priority of procurement and development of new models of weapons and military equipment; implementing state target programs for reforming and developing the defense-industrial complex, developing, mastering and introducing new technologies, creating, expanding the range and volumes of production of science-intensive competitive products in the defense sector of the economy; ensuring the effective functioning of the intelligence agencies of Ukraine; implementing social and legal guarantees for servicemen and members of their families; financing strategic communications activities

[126] Risk Management

[127] In the interests of reducing risks in the field of military security of the state, an integrated risk management system is being created as an element of the defense planning system, the basis of whose activities is to be based on systematic monitoring and assessment of the implementation of plans (programs) for the development of the defense forces and their components

[128] The main risk management measures are: systematic analysis of the development of the defense forces and their components, adjustment of development tasks taking into account the possibilities of resource provision; directing the efforts of defense planning entities to timely finance the state defense order and control the implementation of relevant programs; implementation of modern management technologies in the interests of effective targeted use of defense resources, search for reliable sources of financing for measures to form the necessary capabilities of the troops (forces

[129] In the short term, the main efforts will be aimed at ensuring comprehensive systemic changes in the organization and functioning of the Ukrainian Defense Forces, primarily on: introducing, taking into account the experience of the armed conflict in the eastern regions of Ukraine, new methods of defense management that are based on NATO standards and meet the criterion of high efficiency at acceptable costs; improving the legislative framework on military security and defense, developing an effective mechanism for responding to crisis situations, developing a management system in operations and combat operations, decentralizing decision-making; clarifying the role and tasks of the components of the defense forces at the strategic, operational and tactical levels, gradually improving the organizational structures of the Ukrainian Defense Forces, optimizing the number of personnel, the amount of weapons and military equipment; achieving operational interoperability of the components of the Ukrainian Defense Forces, a systematic transition to NATO standards in the organization, armament and training of troops (forces), as well as in the operational decision-making system; organization of joint training of the defense forces to perform the tasks assigned to them, review of approaches to training and education of personnel; restoration of serviceability and extension of the resource, modernization, creation of new systems and unification of models of weapons, military and special equipment; review of the concept of budget planning and the system of providing resources, radical improvement of ensuring the conduct of combat operations

[130] With the resolution of priority problems, the repulsion of armed aggression and the completion of the anti-terrorist operation in the Donetsk and Luhansk regions, under favorable conditions of the international situation, military-political situation and the availability of appropriate resource support, additional measures will be introduced to ensure the proper defense capability of the state

[131] Final provisions

[132] The military doctrine is the basis for the preparation and adoption of military-political, military-strategic, military-economic and military-technical decisions, the development of relevant concepts and programs

[133] The implementation of the provisions of the Military Doctrine is ensured by the President of Ukraine, the National Security and Defense Council of Ukraine, the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine, other state authorities in accordance with the powers defined by the Constitution and laws of Ukraine

[134] The activities of military security entities in terms of repelling armed aggression against Ukraine, implementing measures to prevent the emergence of military conflicts, preparing the state for armed defense and protecting national interests are managed by the President of Ukraine in accordance with the Constitution and laws of Ukraine

[135] The provisions of the Military Doctrine will be adjusted in accordance with the established procedure, taking into account changes in the military-political situation in the world, the nature of the threat of the use of military force, and the conditions of socio-economic development of Ukraine
