[125] Iraq is experiencing a historically dangerous period which is a transition between the quick collapse of a dictatorial, abusive, totalitarian regime; and the transformation of the Iraqi people into a civilized community

[126] This period is characterized by a number of challenges and opportunities that form the strategic environment for the next few years

[127] The greatest challenge in this period is that the transformation itself provides a fertile environment for religious, sectarian, and ethnic groupings in a diverse community

[128] This environment permits interference and short-range policies, by regional countries, that are built on special interests and exaggerated fears toward the new Iraq

[129] The followers of the previous regime represent another big challenge

[130] The previous dictatorial regime relied on a small group of the citizenry that possessed weapons, money, and experience but lacked the simplest of values, ethics, and knowledge
