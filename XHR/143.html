[11] National Security Strategy of Hungary “A Secure Hungary in a Changing World” I. Introduction

[12] Since the publication of Hungary’s National Security Strategy in 2012, the global security environment has undergone fundamental changes

[13] These processes particularly enhance the need for security-related thinking and the identification of responses to new challenges

[14] The new challenges are based on the emerging, multipolar world order, the effort to transform the rules affecting the relations of international actors, the changing face of security challenges, as well as global challenges such as the acceleration of climate and demographic changes, the closely related illegal and mass migration, the depletion of natural resources, and finally the societal shaping effects of the technological revolution

[15] One of the most defining characteristics of the processes affecting our security is that their formation, development and impact are difficult to predict, and this results in increasing uncertainty

[16] Therefore, at the heart of our responses to changing challenges must be a solid system of Hungary's values, capabilities and priorities, one of the defining elements of which is the national economy, including the development of the defense industry

[17] Thus, the starting point of our responses to the global, European and national challenges affecting the security of our country is always Hungary and the Hungarian nation

[18] The aim of Hungary's National Security Strategy is to preserve and strengthen the current level of security of our country, and as a result, to guarantee the country's further development in a changing world

[19] To this end, we outline our national vision, discuss our country's unique values and assets, its security environment, and identify the most important challenges and our responses and opportunities based on our national interests
