[154] Also, the air defense capacity will be organized by integrating the air defense systems in the equipment and those allocated in support into a coordinated response

[155] intelligence, surveillance, detection, identification and localization of targets and research (ISTAR) - the ISTAR capabilities of the Armed Forces will integrate a wide range of advanced technologies, including sensor systems, drones, radar and other specialized platforms

[156] These will facilitate the early identification and assessment of military threats, providing essential information for rapid decision-making in complex and dynamic situations

[157] The process of transforming and modernizing the respective capabilities of the Armed Forces will consider integrating, at the national level, with similar capabilities of other state institutions

[158] At the same time, it will also aim to ensure an optimal level of interoperability with various ISTAR systems and platforms of partner states

[159] maneuver (mobility and countermobility) - maneuver capabilities will provide the Armed Forces with the flexibility necessary to carry out the entire spectrum of missions, with the ability to operate in complex terrain, firepower and personnel protection

[160] The continuous and sustained process of modernization of these capabilities will represent one of the most credible deterrents to a possible military aggression

[161] The main effort will consist in the reorganization of maneuver units and subunits in order to increase their flexibility, mobility, degree of interoperability, lethality and sustainability

[162] fire support - these capabilities will ensure the coordination and continuous integration of fire support systems to neutralize, suppress and demoralize enemy forces, producing effects on the ground on a large scale and in the depth of combat devices

[163] The fire support capabilities of the Armed Forces will include the integration of automated target identification, location and engagement systems and ensuring their interoperability with those of Western partners

[164] Special Operations Forces (SOF) – the development of SOF capabilities will aim to integrate essential skills for planning and conducting specialized missions, which require unique ways of using special equipment and techniques, for which time, the level of risk accepted and confidentiality are critical factors

[165] These capabilities will be used to collect essential information in sensitive and hostile environments and to execute direct actions, as well as to support the organization of the national resistance effort in the event of armed aggression, including in territories possibly under the temporary occupation of the aggressor

[166] In this context, the aim will be to strengthen the effectiveness of the SOF in dynamic and complex missions, which require close collaboration in the field of information collection, analysis and exchange with other military units, governmental institutions and international partners

[167] The integration of advanced technologies and specialized equipment will also be a priority to ensure the constant adaptability of the forces to face emerging threats and continuous transformations of the operational environment

[168] command, control, communications and computers (C4) - these capabilities will allow the exercise of command and control over the Armed Forces across the entire spectrum of operations carried out by them, from the strategic to the tactical level

[169] The development of the Armed Forces' C4 will aim at the existence of a continuous and efficient chain of command and control by implementing advanced communication technologies, creating robust and secure systems and establishing standardized operating procedures, which will also ensure the necessary level of interoperability with governmental and international institutions, as well as with partner states

[170] cyber defense - these capabilities will ensure the counteraction of cyber threats and attacks, the identification of vulnerabilities and the reduction of associated effects in order to ensure the continuous functionality of the C4 system

[171] By developing cyber defense capabilities, the aim is to integrate the cyber defense technologies of the Armed Forces with those at the national level

[172] At the same time, the security of C4 networks will be ensured and the impact of cyber attacks on military operations and data integrity will be limited, through periodic modernizations, in compliance with national and international standards and regulations in the field; electronic warfare - the development of the electronic warfare capabilities of the Armed Forces aims to manage and synchronize the national electromagnetic spectrum by using the offensive and defensive measures necessary to achieve electromagnetic spectrum supremacy

[173] The process of developing this capability will include improving equipment and strategies for exploiting, deceiving, disrupting or destroying the enemy's electronic systems and networks

[174] At the same time, efficient, scalable and adaptable solutions will be provided to counter unmanned systems

[175] In addition, the integration of these capabilities with ISTAR and C4 systems will be pursued to support the decision-making and transmission process

[176] force protection - the development of force protection capabilities will aim to achieve safety, security and operational effectiveness

[177] This process will include measures and concepts aimed at reducing the vulnerabilities of units, personnel, equipment and military infrastructure to threats and risks, ensuring the freedom of action of the Armed Forces

[178] In this regard, combat units will be equipped with individual and collective protective equipment, and support units will be equipped with means and materials specific to the functional areas, such as: medical, military police, air defense, engineering, chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear defense, combating unmanned aerial systems

[179] sustainability - the development of sustainability capabilities is to ensure the logistical and operational support of the forces, including those detached to international missions and operations

[180] These capabilities will be achieved using an integrated concept, aligned with Western standards, to ensure mobility, modularity and interoperability with external partners and other national structures, by transforming the logistics system from a structural and managerial point of view

[181] All combat and combat support units will be responsible for the self-sufficiency of the forces for a certain period of time

[182] In addition, the units/structures that ensure sustainability will be equipped with modern equipment and means to provide services in accordance with national and international norms and procedures

[183] training - the development of these capabilities will be achieved by ensuring training in conditions similar to the modern combat space and the hybrid environment, using simulation and modeling capabilities, with an emphasis on the doctrinal and operational integration of new weapons systems and military equipment in the Armed Forces

[184] The training of the forces will be carried out through educational activities in the field of military, individual and collective training, as well as through exercises, permanently adapted to the evolution of the security environment, international good practices and technological advances, for the development of the knowledge and skills necessary to fulfill the entire spectrum of established missions; strategic communication (STRATCOM) - the development of STRATCOM will aim to integrate this field into the planning and execution of all activities of the Armed Forces

[185] At the same time, the STRATCOM structure within the Armed Forces, in cooperation with the authorities and institutions of the national security system, will develop synchronized themes and messages to promote and determine perceptions and attitudes in support of the national security objectives of the Republic of Moldova

[186] The development of this capability will require structural modernization, including the element of monitoring, analysis and response to actions of falsification, disinformation and information manipulation

[187] Also, special attention will be paid to the professionalization of personnel in the field, as well as to the provision of specific equipment

[188] The development of priority military capabilities will require a complex effort, which will include innovative actions and initiatives for the national defense system

[189] In order to ensure the coherence, legality and sustainability of these actions, special attention will be paid to the consolidation of the institutional capacity and expertise of the Armed Forces with reference to strategic planning, legal support, the military medical system, the formation and training of the reserve, and the military education system

[190] At the same time, in order to contribute to the modernization and economic development effort of the Republic of Moldova, actions to consolidate digitalization will be intensified, as well as support measures for the development of the national defense industry

[191] CONCEPTS FOR THE USE OF THE ARMED FORCES The use of the Armed Forces to fulfill the established missions will continue to be carried out on the basis of specific concepts, developed following a thorough analysis of the security environment

[192] These concepts aim to establish general procedures and principles for the engagement of the Armed Forces, which will guide and facilitate subsequent military planning, as well as define the programmatic requirements in the medium and long term, in accordance with national legislation and international norms

[193] Given the diverse nature of the military threats and risks facing the Republic of Moldova, the concepts for the use of the Armed Forces are oriented towards ensuring the military security of the state and fulfilling other missions for which they are prepared

[194] During the period of application of this Strategy they refer to

[195] military defense of the country's sovereignty, independence, unity, territorial integrity and constitutional democracy - by conducting defensive operations and countering any form of external military aggression in a joint effort of the entire nation

[196] In the light of this approach, military resilience and national resistance become fundamental components, by preparing forces for long-term operations and creating logistical and operational support networks in conflict conditions

[197] defense of the population and critical infrastructure - by conducting protective and support actions during internal crisis situations, which may also involve armed, cyber or terrorist attacks

[198] Under the provisions of national legislation, the Armed Forces may provide the necessary support both to ensure the continuity of vital services for the population, and to neutralize illegal military and paramilitary formations on the territory of the country, as well as to detain or annihilate groups/individuals recognized as terrorists

[199] support for combating hybrid threats – by actively engaging in repelling cyber and informational aggressions orchestrated by state or non-state actors, which aim to destabilize the Republic of Moldova

[200] This may involve actions to protect critical digital infrastructure and counter disinformation and propaganda campaigns, including by ensuring the exchange of information, coordinating responses and cooperating with specialized national and international institutions

[201] participation in international peacekeeping efforts - by training and seconding, including in partnership with other states, military experts and/or military contingents within international missions and operations

[202] These actions aim to ensure peace and security, promote and respect for human rights, strengthen institutional capacities, and provide other forms of assistance necessary to prevent conflicts and support international stability

[203] The basic principles of the established concepts will continue to be focused, as applicable on ensuring proportionality, surprise, deterrence, firmness, flexibility, minimal losses, and transparency in the application of military force

[204] The synergy of efforts of all state institutions, society and other actors involved in ensuring military resilience and, where appropriate, national resistance constitutes the main pillar of national military planning

[205] In this context, in order to increase the operational efficiency and response capacity of the Armed Forces, measures will be adopted aimed at

[206] implementing the concept of complex national defense, which involves both the development of a multidimensional approach to national defense, with an emphasis on the foundation of the concept of national resistance, and the implementation of awareness-raising actions within state institutions and the education of society on the importance of national defense

[207] These actions will be carried out in connection with the status of permanent neutrality of the Republic of Moldova, enshrined in the Constitution, which obliges the state to be prepared to respond autonomously to any form of armed aggression or any hostile actions

[208] In the context of the imminence or development of such threats, which may endanger the sovereignty, independence, territorial integrity and constitutional democracy, the Armed Forces, together with other structures and forces of the state, will be mobilized as a priority and exclusively for the military defense of the country, using all available resources

[209] In these circumstances, the active participation and unconditional support of each citizen in the efforts of national defense and resistance represents a fundamental duty and an act of devotion to the state and the nation, which must be assumed with responsibility whenever the situation requires it

[210] ensuring the interoperability of the forces intended for national defense, which involves the adoption of the technical, organizational, doctrinal and training measures necessary to ensure the efficient operational synchronization of the forces intended for national defense

[211] These measures include the establishment of common requirements for equipment, communication systems and operational procedures, as well as the periodic organization of military exercises and other joint training activities

[212] The aim is to ensure a rapid and coordinated response between the various components of the national defense system, including in international contexts

[213] ensuring inter-institutional collaboration, which involves the development and implementation of mechanisms that will allow close collaboration between the Armed Forces and central and local public administrations, in a well-coordinated framework, with common objectives and clear communication channels

[214] Improving this collaboration framework will contribute not only to strengthening national defense, but also to the integrated management of internal challenges, such as social crises, natural disasters and security incidents of a hybrid or terrorist nature

[215] In this regard, the Armed Forces will be able to intervene under the law, at the request of public authorities, with forces and/or expertise to ensure the protection of citizens, critical infrastructure and/or for the efficient management of the respective crisis situations

[216] the development of humanitarian partnerships, which involves the conclusion of strategic partnerships, on the humanitarian dimension, with international and non-governmental organizations to expand the capacity of the Armed Forces to support the civilian population in the event of crisis situations

[217] Chapter VII STRUCTURE OF THE ARMED FORCES The Armed Forces represent the core of the forces intended for national defense, being constituted on the principle of the defensive sufficiency of their basic component - the National Army

[218] In order to ensure national defense, this Strategy aims at implementing a new model of organization and training of the basic component of the Armed Forces, based on a structure capable of planning and carrying out defense actions at the tactical, operational and strategic levels in the land, air, information, cyber and electromagnetic domains

[219] It will be composed of standardized, deployable and flexible subdivisions within force groups, created at national or international level, to carry out specific missions

[220] The transition to the new force structure of the Armed Forces will be carried out gradually, through the implementation of the programs and development plans developed on the basis of this Strategy, which will emphasize maintaining the mixed system of personnel replenishment, multiannual acquisitions of modern military equipment, as well as ensuring multilateral training and sustainability

[221] Also, the new structure of the Armed Forces will integrate advanced components of command and control, information, planning and conduct of operations, training and generation of forces

[222] Section 1 Leadership Responsibility for adopting decisions related to the defense policy of the Republic of Moldova and the use of the Armed Forces in peacetime, upon declaring states of siege and/or war shall belong to the President (as the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces), the Parliament, the Government, the Ministry of Defense and other public authorities with responsibilities in the field of national defense within the limits established by the Constitution of the Republic of Moldova and other normative acts

[223] The Ministry of Defense shall continue to be the central public authority of specialty responsible for the organization, coordination and management of activities in the field of national defense, being responsible for the construction and development of the basic component of the Armed Forces in order to prepare for the execution of the country's defense missions

[224] The Defence General Staff, a central structure of the Ministry of Defence, directly subordinated to the Minister of Defence, shall exercise, under the leadership of the Chief of Defence General Staff, the command and control of the basic component of the Armed Forces in peacetime, as well as of the forces intended for national defence in wartime

[225] These responsibilities shall be carried out through the commands, which shall refer at least to a joint force command and a force generation command, as well as to the operational centres under its subordination

[226] Section 2 Organisation of the Armed Forces According to the employment environment and destination, the basic component of the Armed Forces shall be organised as follows

[227] Land forces structures - will continue to represent the main element of maneuver, strike and protection, as well as of deployment, presence, expansion and continuity of military actions or operations, both in peacetime and in crisis or war situations

[228] These structures will also represent the main source of personnel for contingents/forces involved in supporting public authorities and/or in international missions and operations

[229] In order to achieve this objective, the mentioned structures will prioritize the capabilities offered by the infantry brigades, the artillery regiment, the peacekeeping battalion and the engineer battalion; 34.2. air force structures - will have the role of supervising and defending the national airspace, ensuring the operationalization of air and anti-aircraft forces and means, supporting other forces in conducting operations, as well as contributing to the management of crisis situations in support of public authorities and the fulfillment of international commitments assumed by the Republic of Moldova

[230] From the point of view of destination, these forces will be composed of the air defense regiment, the aviation regiment and the radar battalion

[231] special operations forces - are intended to carry out special research, direct actions, military assistance and other complementary missions, both on the national territory and outside it

[232] These forces will act independently or in cooperation with other national forces and/or partners, in accordance with the provisions of the law, being constituted by the special operations battalion

[233] structures for communications, informatics and cyber defense - will ensure, through fixed and deployable capabilities, the infrastructure for exercising command and control, managing, operating and maintaining systems and services for communications, informatics, information security and cyber defense, providing support in the field of communications and informatics both on and outside the national territory

[234] These forces will integrate in particular the communications and informatics regiment and the cyber defense and security center. information assurance structures - will ensure support for strategic, operational and tactical decisions with reference to national defense by collecting, analyzing and disseminating relevant information/products in this regard, including geospatial information

[235] The fulfillment of these tasks will be ensured by the military intelligence battalion and the geospatial information center

[236] structures to ensure sustainability – will support the effective functioning of the Armed Forces in peacetime, in crisis and/or war situations, ensuring multilateral support for combat services, in accordance with their needs and areas of responsibility

[237] They will manage the processes of selection, recruitment, incorporation and employment of military personnel, both by contract and on time, the recording and preparation of mobilization resources, as well as the coordination of mobilization preparation processes and the mobilization of the Armed Forces

[238] Specialized structures will provide comprehensive medical support to the military and civilian personnel of the Armed Forces, and in medical crisis situations they will support the national health system

[239] They will integrate the logistics brigade, the mobilization and personnel management center with the subordinate territorial military centers, departmental medical institutions of Role 3 level (central military clinical hospital and consultative-diagnostic medical center); military training structures - will ensure the initial and continuous training of military and civilian personnel of the Armed Forces

[240] They will ensure professional military education, including joint training and the conduct of military exercises, as well as military physical training

[241] The implementation of these tasks will be ensured by the military academy, military college, sergeant school, centers of excellence, training battalion/training base and sports center of the Armed Forces

[242] institutional support structures - will perform specific tasks, which also refer to military discipline, road traffic control, internal service, VIP protection, preparation and conduct of military protocol and ceremonial activities

[243] These structures will mainly integrate the military police battalion, together with the Honor Guard and the Presidential Orchestra

[244] The structures and forces of the General Inspectorate of Carabinieri of the Ministry of Internal Affairs will continue to have a dual character, exercising duties in the field of maintaining, ensuring and restoring public order and security in peacetime and duties specific to the Armed Forces in times of siege and war, in accordance with national legislation

[245] These structures/forces will ensure the training, standardization and preparation process to ensure interoperability within the Armed Forces in order to carry out the missions of defending the country, according to the destination and operational plans in force

[246] Upon the declaration of a state of siege or war, the Carabinieri structures/forces will participate in the defense of the country exclusively under the leadership (command) of the Chief of the Defense Staff

[247] In the current security environment, achieving the objectives of this Strategy will depend on an integrated approach, based on the national resources of the Republic of Moldova, human, financial and material, as well as on the support of external partners
