[105] INTRODUCTION

[106] Brazil governs its international relations, among others, by the constitutional principles of non-intervention, the defense of peace and the peaceful resolution of conflicts

[107] This vocation for harmonious coexistence with other countries is part of the national identity and constitutes a value to be preserved by the Brazilian people

[108] On the other hand, the current context demonstrates that international relations remain unstable and sometimes have unpredictable consequences

[109] Thus, as defended by José Maria da Silva Paranhos Júnior, Baron of Rio Branco, Brazil is aware that “no State can be peaceful without being strong”, so that the growing development of the Country must be accompanied by the adequate preparation of its defense

[110] Thus, Brazilian society has become more aware of its responsibility in preserving the country's autonomy and the importance of providing it with tools that enable it to guarantee the achievement of its legitimate aspirations

[111] The State, as the provider of security for the Brazilian population, is responsible for coordinating actions related to National Defense, whose highest level document is the National Defense Policy – PND, which establishes the National Defense Objectives – OND, which must be permanently pursued by the Nation

[112] The National Defense Strategy – END, in turn, guides the segments of the Brazilian State regarding the measures that must be implemented to achieve these objectives

[113] It is, therefore, the link between the country's positioning on defense issues and the actions necessary to effectively provide the State with the capacity to preserve its fundamental values

[114] After the approval of its first version in 2008, the National Defense Strategy was submitted to its first review process in

[115] In 2016, as provided for in Decree No. 97 of June 9, 1999, and its amendments, it reached a new stage of updating, which consisted of its adaptation to the current circumstances of the national and international environments

[116] Based on the positions established in the National Defense Policy and based on the most important objectives in the field of defense, the END defines, in a clear and objective manner, the strategies that should guide Brazilian society in its actions to defend the Homeland

[117] It deals with the bases on which the country's defense should be structured, as well as indicating the articulations that should be conducted, within the scope of all instances of the three powers and the interaction between the various levels conducting these actions with the non-governmental segments of the country

[118] NATIONAL DEFENSE STRATEGY STRATEGIC DEFENSE CONCEPT

[119] The Federal Constitution establishes the foundations and Fundamental National Objectives6 of Brazil, which guide the maintenance of national identity and the preservation of the country's cohesion and unity

[120] The Magna Carta prescribes, among other aspects, social well-being, the construction of a fair, free and supportive society, which provides the conditions for national development, based on an adequate level of security promoted by the State, understood as the feeling of necessary and indispensable guarantee to a society and to each of its members, against threats of any nature

[121] In this sense, National Defense, conceptualized as the set of attitudes, measures and actions of the State, with an emphasis on military expression, for the defense of the territory, jurisdictional waters, sovereignty and national interests against predominantly external, potential or manifest threats, is essential for achieving the desired level of security of the Country

[122] Brazil's strategic defense concept foresees the prevalence of diplomatic action, in times of peace or crisis, in which understanding will be sought for possible divergences and disputes, observing the precepts of international law and the commitments entered into by the country

[123] The actions of the Defense Sector, comprised of the Ministry of Defense and the Armed Forces, contribute to the purpose of diplomacy by interacting with counterparts in other countries, increasing mutual trust and bonds of friendship

[124] National Defense, therefore, gives substance to Security and acts in accordance with the Brazilian policy of prioritizing the peaceful resolution of disputes between countries, so that the use of force through the Military Expression of National Power will only be implemented when, when national interests are threatened, the possibilities of negotiation are unfeasible, aiming at the preservation of sovereignty, territorial integrity and national interests. [130] FUNDAMENTALS 3.1 NATIONAL POWER

[131] National Power presents itself as the interdependent combination of wills and means, aimed at achieving a specific purpose

[132] Of wills, as this is an essential element for its manifestation, making it an essentially human phenomenon, individual or collective; of means, as it reflects the possibilities and limitations of the people who constitute it and the resources at their disposal

[133] Thus, National Power is understood as the Nation's capacity to achieve and maintain National Objectives, in accordance with the national will, manifesting itself in Political, Economic, Psychosocial, Military and Scientific-technological Expressions

[134] The preparation of National Power consists of a set of activities carried out with the objective of strengthening it, either by maintaining or improving existing power, or by transforming potential into power

[135] The efficiency of such preparation depends on policies and strategies that provide the necessary conditions for the country's development process

[136] In the context of National Defense, it is necessary for the Nation to be prepared to evolve quickly from a situation of peace or crisis to a situation of armed conflict

[137] The country must, therefore, be able to rapidly increase the human and material resources of the Armed Forces, which is the concept of Elasticity, one of the premises of National Mobilization

[138] In light of the analysis of current national and international scenarios, it becomes essential to adapt the configuration of expressions of National Power to new circumstances and, consequently, seek to structure the means of defense around capabilities

[139] National Defense Capabilities are considered to be those composed of different parts of the expressions of National Power

[140] They are implemented through the coordinated and synergistic participation of government agencies and, when relevant, private entities focused on defense and security in their broadest sense

[141] Thus, the following stand out among the National Defense Capabilities: Protection, Deterrence, Rapid Response, Coordination and Control, Information Management, Strategic Mobility and Mobilization Capabilities

[142] The Protection Capacity of the Brazilian territory and population expresses the most important national objective, that of guaranteeing sovereignty, national heritage and territorial integrity

[143] Therefore, it is important to provide the Nation with the capacity to respond in exceptional situations, preserving the normal functioning of the State's vital functions

[147] Strengthening Protection Capacity requires adapting surveillance means and methods over the national territory, including the Exclusive Economic Zone, the continental shelf and the overlying outer space, cyberspace and other areas of interest

[148] It also requires the improvement of communications and information systems and alert systems related to Civil Defense and Protection agencies, with a view to coordinated inter-agency action

[149] The Protection Capacity, in addition to focusing on the national territory, must consider Brazilian interests abroad, with the purpose of ensuring the observance of individual or collective, private or public rights, compliance with international agreements, and also safeguarding national heritage, economic assets, and resources existing outside Brazil, in accordance with the international legal framework

[150] Deterrence Capacity, in turn, is an essential factor for National Security, as its purpose is to discourage possible aggression

[151] It is based on the conditions that the Nation has to gather and apply its Protection and Rapid Response Capacity, in the event of possible hostile actions against the sovereignty and legitimate interests of Brazil

[152] Closely related to the Protection Capacity is the Rapid Response Capacity, which includes several elements of National Power

[153] It aims to prevent the worsening of a crisis situation or to quickly end a dispute that has already broken out, avoiding the country's involvement in a prolonged armed conflict

[154] The Coordination and Control Capacity aims to allow, under any circumstances, coordination between the various government agencies and is based on the control and integrity of information traffic

[155] It will be based on the National Critical Communications System – SISNACC, which will benefit the Public Administration with a communications network for coordination and control in the fields of National Defense, Protection and Brazilian Marine Defense/Disclosure

[158] The Information Management Capacity aims to ensure the acquisition, production and dissemination of the knowledge necessary for the coordination and control of the resources available to the Nation, providing access to Intelligence to decision-makers and those responsible for the areas of Public Security and National Defense, at all levels

[159] The Brazilian Intelligence System – SISBIN is its main structure

[160] This capacity provides conditions for preventive action by public authorities and contributes to the effectiveness of the operational resources of the Armed Forces and public security resources

[161] In turn, Strategic Mobility Capacity refers to the condition of the country's transport logistics infrastructure, with multimodal capacity, and the means of transport, to allow the Armed Forces to move quickly to the area of employment, within the national territory or abroad, when this is required in order to defend national interests

[162] This condition requires, among other aspects, that Brazil has an adequate transport structure that prioritizes the interaction and integration of the various modes, so that they can act in a complementary manner

[163] The Mobilization Capacity aims to increase the effectiveness of the use of military expression, which is closely associated with the degree of technological and logistical independence of the Country, the National Mobilization capacity and the ability to promptly employ the resources and services made available to it

[164] The Mobilization Capacity aims, on the one hand, to complement the logistics of the Armed Forces, through the use of civilian resources, using the concept of national logistics, in accordance with the National Mobilization System – SINAMOB

[165] Mobilization should consider all the capabilities available to the country (infrastructure, installed and potential, and human capital), and special attention should be given to the preparation of these capabilities, aiming at their rapid, efficient and effective use, considering that the “time factor” is critical for the results of an armed conflict

[166] In this sense, the Defense Sector must stimulate, within its scope of action, the development of the country's industrial potential, in order to increase National Power

[167] Thus, the defense and development of the country are naturally interdependent, as this is a preponderant factor in building the human and infrastructure resources that the nation needs to support the eventual use of military resources

[168] At the same time, it is imperative that the defense apparatus be in line with the most advanced practices and technologies, which requires the condition of national scientific and technological development at the state of the art

[169] In this context, Brazil's defense requires the permanent strengthening of its Defense Industrial Base - BID, formed by a group of state and private, civil and military organizations that carry out or conduct research, projects, development, industrialization, production, repair, conservation, review, conversion, modernization or maintenance of defense products in the country

[170] Regarding commercial aspects, these must be subordinated to strategic imperatives, with the purpose of enabling the fulfillment of National Defense Objectives

[171] Therefore, it is important that the development, production and commercialization network be subject to special legal, regulatory and tax regimes, in order to provide a greater level of security to national companies against the risks of commercial immediacy and greater regularity in state demands for defense products, without harming market competition and the development of new technologies

[172] Such regimes should provide the Defense Industrial Base with conditions to increase its competitiveness, especially in relation to the external market, in order to increase its production scale and, thus, promote greater regularity in the demand for products, whether exclusively for defense or for dual application

[173] The counterpart for such special regimes must be the IDB's compliance with requirements established by the State, in accordance with the relevant legal instruments, so that such condition does not constitute privileges, but rather prerogatives arising from its commitment to the preparation of the National Power. [204] NATIONAL DEFENSE STRATEGY 3.2 DIPLOMACY ACTIONS

[205] Diplomatic activity encourages mutual understanding between nations and allows for the reconciliation of any differences in perception

[206] Therefore, dialogue and cooperation with other countries are fundamental to the success of the National Defense Strategy, as they are powerful instruments for preventing and resolving conflicts

[207] In an increasingly complex international environment and with growing interdependence between nations in various domains, diplomacy is gaining increasing importance in addressing major global issues

[208] In the case of Brazil, diplomatic activity is guided by the principles established in Article 4 of the Federal Constitution: national independence; prevalence of human rights; self-determination of peoples; non-intervention; equality among States; defense of peace; peaceful resolution of conflicts; repudiation of terrorism and racism; cooperation among peoples for the progress of humanity; and granting of political asylum

[209] In the context of Brazil's foreign policy, diplomatic relations, from the perspective of National Defense, precede the use of force, through military means, to resolve disputes

[210] They aim to achieve National Objectives through compliance with the principles established in Article 4 of the Constitution, especially with regard to the peaceful resolution of disputes

[211] Diplomatic actions, therefore, differ from those adopted by National Defense in terms of their nature, but they have the same objective in essence

[212] Thus, although foreign policy is the exclusive competence of the President of the Republic, who exercises it with the assistance of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in its formulation and implementation, the international defense relationships of Brazilian government entities can contribute to the defense strategy, since they promote mutual trust, cooperation and the strengthening of bonds of friendship, which favor the peaceful resolution of any differences

[213] DEFENSE SECTOR

[214] The Defense Sector, constituted by the Ministry of Defense and integrated by the Brazilian Navy, the Brazilian Army and the Brazilian Air Force, is responsible for the preparation and employment of the military expression of National Power

[215] Thus, if, on the one hand, in times of peace, the Defense Sector acts in a way that contributes to the diplomatic actions undertaken by the Country, on the other hand, in the event of war or armed conflict, it is the sector of the Brazilian State that applies force in its entirety

[216] The Federal Constitution defines the Armed Forces as permanent and regular national institutions, organized on the basis of hierarchy and discipline, under the supreme authority of the President of the Republic, which are intended for the defense of the Homeland, the guarantee of constitutional powers and, at the initiative of any of these, law and order

[217] To fulfill their constitutional purpose, the Armed Forces must design their organizational and operational structures around capabilities, in line with the structuring of the country's defense resources, according to the characteristics of each individual Force

[218] The Military Capabilities that the Armed Forces must have to support their eventual use arise from the National Defense Capabilities

[219] Such capabilities must be integrated and consolidated to allow for the planning, structuring and definition of processes and responsibilities, aimed at preparation and employment

[220] Given the unique characteristics of modern armed conflicts, the joint use of the Armed Forces must be prioritized, streamlining all types of resources and enhancing the capabilities of each through the synergistic use of their characteristics and potential, without disregarding the possibility of their individual use. It is therefore necessary to develop monitoring and control of Brazilian airspace, territory, jurisdictional waters, and other areas of interest, as well as the ability to respond promptly to any threat or aggression

[221] Such activities increasingly require that the Forces be able to operate in a network, increasing the exchange of information, which, given the dimensions of the areas considered, will require the ability to reach the region of interest in a timely manner, in accordance with the strategic mobility capacity

[222] In addition to their constitutional powers, the three forces must be able to act, individually or jointly, in international operations, whether expeditionary, peacekeeping or humanitarian aid, to meet commitments undertaken by the country or to safeguard Brazilian interests abroad and, in this way, contribute to the objectives of Brazil's foreign policy

[223] Military Defense Capabilities must be jointly raised and consolidated, integrated and prioritized at the sectoral level of the Federal Administration

[224] These capabilities are added to the military capabilities of the Individual Forces, to fulfill their subsidiary duties and the specificities of each of them

[225] Network operation is a concept that requires the Armed Forces to operate in the context of the information age

[226] It provides conditions for the interoperability of forces, contributing to the construction, maintenance and dissemination of situational awareness in the battlespace and the improvement of the Command and Control Cycle

[227] It is characterized by the establishment of an environment for sharing situational awareness, contributing to the achievement of Information Superiority and initiative, even if the resources employed are geographically dispersed

[228] The sets of Military Defense Capabilities will be systematized and dimensioned in the Military Defense Policy and Strategy and, finally, reflected in the Defense Articulation and Equipment Plan (PAED), which will include, in addition to the macrodistribution of military installations in the national territory, the design of the equipment necessary to fulfill the duties of the Armed Forces, with the purpose of increasing interoperability between them and the defense capacity, allowing the specific needs of the Forces to be met and the rationalization of strategic planning activities

[229] In turn, the execution of projects aimed at obtaining both sets of capabilities will, in principle, be the responsibility of each individual Force

[231] The Brazilian Navy's mission is to prepare and employ Naval Power to contribute to the defense of the Homeland; to guarantee constitutional powers and, at the initiative of any of these, law and order; to fulfill subsidiary duties provided for by law; and to support foreign policy

[232] The intensification of illegal acts at sea, in the form of piracy, human and drug trafficking, smuggling, illegal fishing, environmental crimes and others, demands a state presence at sea and in waterways

[233] It follows that the Naval Power must have means capable of detecting, identifying and neutralizing actions that represent a threat in Brazilian jurisdictional waters, and maintain security in maritime lines of communication where there are national interests, in accordance with international law

[234] The fulfillment of this set of attributions will be carried out through the basic tasks of Naval Power: control of the maritime area

[235] denial of the use of the sea; projection of power over land; and contribution to deterrence

[236] Monitoring the sea, including from space, should be part of the repertoire of operational practices and training

[237] Naval Power must exploit its intrinsic characteristics of mobility, permanence, versatility and flexibility, which, favored by freedom of navigation, the availability of fixed, strategically positioned logistical support points, and the incorporation of mobile logistical support to forces in operation, allow the use of Naval Power in a wide spectrum of activities, from support to diplomatic actions, the limited use of force, to naval warfare operations

[238] The capabilities to control maritime areas, deny the use of the sea and project Naval Power will focus on increasing security and the ability to defend oil platforms, naval and port facilities, archipelagos and oceanic islands in Brazilian jurisdictional waters and respond promptly to any threat to maritime trade routes

[239] Two coastal areas deserve special attention, from the point of view of the need to control maritime access to Brazil: the strip that runs from Santos to Vitória and the area around the mouth of the Amazon River

[240] In this way, the Navy will continue the work of installing its new submarine base and studies to establish, near the mouth of the Amazon River, a multi-purpose naval complex

[241] The Navy will be structured, in stages, as a Force balanced between surface, submarine, amphibious and air components, with a high degree of mobility

[242] This will allow for increased flexibility in pursuing the priority objective of the maritime security strategy: deterrence against any concentration of hostile forces in waters of national interest

[243] The naval surface force will include both large ships capable of operating and remaining on the high seas for long periods, as well as smaller ships dedicated to patrolling the coastline and main Brazilian rivers

[244] It should also have mobile logistics support vessels, necessary to provide mobility for the naval force in areas of interest

[245] Among large ships, special attention should be paid to obtaining multi-purpose ships and aircraft carriers

[255] The Brazilian Army's mission is to contribute to guaranteeing national sovereignty, constitutional powers, law and order, safeguarding national interests and cooperating with national development and social well-being

[256] To this end, the Land Force must, among other things, be able to: neutralize concentrations of hostile forces along the land border, contribute to the defense of the coast and to anti-aircraft defense in the national territory

[257] It aims to fulfill its constitutional purpose by maintaining the Land Force in an adequate state of readiness, structured and prepared to carry out land, joint and interagency operational missions

[258] This state of readiness results from the continuous process of transformation, in the search for new capabilities, under the guidance of the doctrinal characteristics of flexibility, adaptability, modularity, elasticity and sustainability

[259] Flexibility, a characteristic resulting from structures with minimal pre-established rigidity, provides a greater number of options for reorganizing combat elements into temporary structures, with adequate logistical support, from elementary fractions to Grand Commands

[260] Adaptability, in turn, enables rapid adjustment to changes in the conditions that determine the selection and the way in which resources will be employed, in any range of the conflict spectrum, in war and non-war situations

[261] Modularity gives a combat element the ability to, from a minimum basic structure, receive modules that expand its combat power or add capabilities, enabling the adoption of adaptable structures for each employment situation

[262] Modularity is directly related to the concept of elasticity

[263] Elasticity, in turn, is the characteristic that, given a force with adequate command and control and logistics structures, allows it to vary its combat power by adding or removing structures, if appropriate, or through the mobilization of additional resources, when applicable

[264] Sustainability allows a force to last in action for as long as necessary, maintaining its operational capabilities and resisting the fluctuations of combat

[265] The Army's transformation process, however, will not be guided solely by these characteristics, but must seek compatibility with the presence strategy, especially in the Amazon

[266] The ability to be present, or to make oneself present, will be achieved through the mobility of its resources, especially its light, mechanized and armored brigades

[267] Thus, the Army, starting from a device of expectation and together with the other Singular Forces, must have the capacity to concentrate the necessary forces to guarantee decisive superiority in combat, maintaining the inviolability of the national territory

[268] As a result of the presence strategy, the Army will act episodically and punctually in Law and Order Guarantee operations and will collaborate with Public Security agencies in actions against transnational crimes perpetrated in the border area

[269] In addition to participating in the integrated protection of Strategic Structures and the execution of engineering works throughout the national territory, to the benefit of the country's development

[270] The Army must also have the capacity to project power, constituting an Expeditionary Force, whether for peacekeeping operations, humanitarian aid or other operations, to fulfill commitments assumed under the auspices of international organizations or to safeguard Brazilian interests abroad

[295] Strategic sectors

[296] Three technological sectors are essential for National Defense: nuclear, cyber and space

[297] Therefore, they are considered strategic and must be strengthened

[298] As a result of their very nature, they transcend the division between development and defense and between the civil and the military

[299] In this context, it is important to empower the country as a whole, as well as to provide the National Power with the conditions to adapt to the circumstances and to make use of the employment potential they hold

[300] These strategic sectors are highly complex, meaning that, while they demand centralized leadership, they also require close coordination and integration of various actors and areas of knowledge

GLOSSARY ADAPTABILITY - A characteristic that enables rapid adjustment to changes in the factors that determine the selection and use of assets, across any spectrum of conflict, in both war and non-war situations. C3I - Command, Control, Communication, and Intelligence

[455] DEFENSE CAPABILITY

[456] – The country’s capacity to generate a deterrent effect and support the preservation of national interests, compatible with its political-strategic stature and its responsibilities to defend the territory, jurisdictional waters, continental shelf and Brazilian airspace

[457] PROTECTION CAPACITY - expresses the most relevant national objective, that of guaranteeing sovereignty, national heritage and territorial integrity

[458] Therefore, it is important to provide the Nation with the capacity to respond in exceptional situations, preserving the normal functioning of the State's vital functions

[459] DETERRENCE CAPACITY - is an essential factor for National Security, as its purpose is to discourage possible aggression

[460] It is based on the conditions that the Nation has to gather and apply its Protection and Rapid Response Capacity, in the event of possible hostile actions against the sovereignty and legitimate interests of Brazil

[461] COORDINATION CAPACITY

[462] AND CONTROL – capacity that aims to allow, under any circumstances, coordination between the various government agencies and is based on the control and integrity of information traffic

[463] INFORMATION MANAGEMENT CAPACITY – capacity that aims to guarantee the acquisition, production and dissemination of the knowledge necessary for the coordination and control of the resources available to the Nation, providing access to Intelligence to decision-makers and those responsible for the areas of Public Security and National Defense, at all levels

[464] The Brazilian Intelligence System (SISBIN) is its main structure. STRATEGIC MOBILITY CAPABILITY – the country's multimodal transportation logistics infrastructure and transportation capabilities enable the Armed Forces to quickly deploy to their intended areas, within the country or abroad, when required to defend national interests

[465] NATIONAL DEFENSE STRATEGY MOBILIZATION CAPABILITY – is the capability that aims to increase the effectiveness of the use of military resources, closely associated with the country's degree of technological and logistical independence, its National Mobilization Capability, and its ability to promptly employ the resources and services made available to it. MARITIME AREA CONTROL – Control that aims to ensure a certain degree of utilization, even if temporary, of limited maritime areas, whether stationary or mobile, exercised at the intensity appropriate to the execution of specific activities. NATIONAL DEFENSE CAPABILITIES

[466] – are those composed of different parts of the expressions of National Power

[468] DESUASION - Strategic attitude that, through means of any nature, including military, aims to discourage or divert adversaries, real or potential, from possible or presumed warlike intentions

ELASTICITY - A characteristic that, given a force's adequate command and control and logistics structures, allows it to vary its combat power by adding or removing structures, if appropriate, or by mobilizing additional resources, when appropriate. FLEXIBILITY - A characteristic that a military force must have in order to organize itself to fulfill a specific mission, to meet both the different phases of a plan or order of operations, and to adapt to changing situations that may arise during the course of combat or the assigned mission

[470] Ability to organize operational groups of different values, depending on the mission

[471] Ability to adapt quickly to changing situations, using air units to carry out a wide range of mission types, employing, in each case, tactics and weapons appropriate to the operation to be carried out

[473] MODULARITY - Division of a system into components, called modules, which are named separately, have common internal characteristics, and can be operated independently of one another.
