[32] I. INTRODUCTION

[33] This National Defense Strategic Concept (CEDN) aims to update the previous one, approved in 1993, by Internal Resolution No. 9/93, of the Council of Ministers, taking into account the significant changes that have occurred in the national and international situation

[36] In this way, the CEDN is an indispensable instrument for mobilizing Angolans around the defense of the country, providing an overview of the National Strategy, including a conceptual approach on the foundations that frame it and give it consistency, namely

[46] Objectives of the National Defense Policy

[242] To this end, it must define a framework of alliances that guarantee the defense of national interests

[243] Adapt the Structure of the surrounding Security and Defense Forces

[244] Ensure the capacity to carry out missions that guarantee the defense of the achievements of the Angolan people, territorial integrity and national independence; * Provide integrated responses between the various State bodies to threats and the pursuit of national objectives

[245] Ensure an effective national capacity for the control of land, air and sea space

[246] Ensure Strategic Reserves capable of facing changes in the internal and external situation; * Maintain the population's ability to mobilize in the event of a change in the Peace situation

[247] Organization and Improvement of the Main Vectors that Contribute to the Success of the National Defense Policy

[248] Improve the organization of the National Education and Teaching System

[249] The country's educational policy must promote human and educational development, based on quality education and lifelong learning for each and every Angolan. b

[250] Valuing Culture as a Factor of National Identity Citizens must be guaranteed respect for all their cultural values with equal opportunities, and their creativity must be stimulated, promoting the spirit of tolerance, solidarity, fraternity and active participation in national life, as well as contributing to patriotic and civic education, economic and social progress

[251] The Angolan State must continue to encourage the promotion of national and common values, with respect for the diversity of the various ethnolinguistic specificities within the socio-cultural diversity of Angola

[252] Promote access by all citizens to the benefits of culture without any kind of discrimination, taking into account the aspirations of different segments of the population, thus promoting freedom of expression and the broadest participation of citizens in the cultural life of the country, in the free and harmonious strengthening of their thought and respect for uses and customs favorable to development, which contributes to the consolidation of national identity

[253] Improve the National Health System

[254] The State must continue to promote sustained improvements in the quality of basic health care for the Angolan population, seeking to extend life expectancy and reduce birth rates, supporting the most disadvantaged social groups; Improve, within the scope of the National Health System, the capacity for Safety and Disaster Medicine, which ensures an adequate workforce to respond to Public Health emergencies of national and international interest, whether of natural or autogenic cause

[255] Create the basis for the national production of essential medicines and medical resources to address the pathologies that most affect the population, emergency situations or major difficulties in acquiring resources abroad. d

[256] Adopt a Development Model

[257] Sustainable and Strengthen the National Financial System

[258] Angola must continue to envision human development through the adoption of greater balance and equity in economic development, allowing for greater growth in employment and greater domestic savings

[259] Therefore, the Angolan State must: * Promote the development of the Manufacturing Industry Sector, particularly in the context of goods and services, on a sustainable basis, contributing to the generation of jobs, the use of agricultural and mineral raw materials, the territorial distribution of activities, the balance of trade and the saving of foreign currency; * Mobilize and strengthen industrial entities in the interest of National Defense

[260] Improve the Management of Territorial and Environmental Administration

[261] Define the options that guarantee the planning of the national space and the natural environment, taking into account the needs of National Defense, particularly in the integrated use of infrastructures

[262] Improve and integrate the Transport and Communications System * Provide the country with an integrated transport network suited to national and regional development objectives, facilitating the economic development process and enhancing territorial and population policies; * Continue to develop the main axes and corridors that enable regional integration

[263] Develop actions that allow the provision and effective use of transport infrastructure to support a potential crisis or war effort

[264] In the field of communications, provide the country with modern infrastructure, with a view to improving services provided to the population; Implement the strategy for electronic governance; Implement a Technological Modernization Program throughout the National Defense System; Continue to implement the National Space Program (PEN), safeguarding the Angolan State's strategy in the medium and long term, guaranteeing access for citizens in the most remote areas to the services that the program will provide, thus improving the geographic cohesion of the country and its people

[265] Define the National Cybersecurity Strategy with the aim of ensuring

[266] National Sovereignty in cyberspace, promoting crisis management measures and combating cybercrime, cyberterrorism, cyberespionage, and cyberwarfare; * Promoting the National Cybersecurity and Cyberdefense Strategy to respond to incidents related to cyberattacks, safeguarding national interests and the protection and security of national cyberspace. g

[267] Encourage and Develop the National Agricultural and Livestock Sector

[268] Promote the integrated and sustainable development of the Agricultural and Livestock Sector, taking as a reference the full use of the potential of productive natural resources and the competitiveness of the Sector, aiming to guarantee: food security and domestic supply, as well as taking advantage of opportunities related to regional and international markets; Promote the gradual and sustainable transformation of subsistence agriculture into market-oriented commercial agriculture, aiming to achieve food security and the dynamization of the national agro-industry

[269] Promote the socio-economic development of rural and peasant communities, significantly increasing their levels of well-being and simultaneously contributing to raising the levels of food security of the Angolan population and the eradication of poverty

[270] Permanently Raising Society's Awareness of the Importance of National Security and Defense

[271] The defense of the Homeland is the highest and most inalienable right and duty of every citizen, contributing to National Security in the form of fulfilling Compulsory Military Service or Civic Service

[272] The National Defense Policy must ensure the strengthening of national consciousness, through respect for State institutions, as well as include in the Education and Teaching school curriculum, in conjunction with the provisions regarding the preparation, management and control of Public Administration personnel frameworks; subjects that portray the ethical, moral and cultural values that exalt the entity of the Nation, respect for national heroes, through the development of education, teaching, culture and social communication

[273] The safeguarding of individual freedoms and pluralism of expression, the effectiveness and prestige of the Security and Defense Bodies as national institutions at the service of the people, and therefore deserve special attention

[274] Implement a policy that guarantees plural, impartial, independent, and responsible information and that expands on the achievements made regarding the rights and guarantees of freedom of expression, within the framework of the principles of a democratic and law-based state

[275] FOR THE OPERATIONALIZATION OF THE STRATEGIC CONCEPT OF NATIONAL DEFENSE

[276] The Strategic Concept of National Defense, as determined by law, establishes the main guidelines and priorities that the State and society must carry out, to maintain Peace and guarantee the Unity and Territorial Integrity of Cabinda to Cunene do Mar to the East, consolidate the ongoing democratic process, safeguard the material and immaterial heritage, in short, defend national interests and guarantee the construction of a prosperous and indivisible Nation

[277] For this reason, the Strategic Concept of National Defense must be seen as an instrument and a National strategy of the State, designed to fulfill its fundamental tasks, in which all state and private institutions and civil society contribute

[278] The methodology to be followed is one that meets international best practices, ensuring integration, information sharing and accountability of the entities responsible for implementing the different lines of action, both internally and externally, through specific sectoral strategies
