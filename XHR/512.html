[28] Principles of the Security and Defence Policy of the Republic of Hungary In recent years, a significant change has occurred in the external security situation of Hungary, mainly as a result of the achievements in the field of Euro-Atlantic integration

[29] With the attainment of NATO membership and the practical progress of the accession process to the European Union, the country has become an institutional member of the community of democratic, developed and stable countries of the Euro-Atlantic area

[30] All of this necessitates the reformulation of the principles of the security and defence policy of the Republic of Hungary

[31] The Republic of Hungary interprets security in a comprehensive way, which, in addition to traditional political and military factors, also includes other dimensions of security in the broad sense - economic and financial, human rights and minority, information and technological, environmental and international legal - in addition to these

[32] In the world of global challenges, international political and economic interdependence, and technological development, the security of the Euro-Atlantic area is indivisible

[33] Compared to the period of the bipolar international system, the threat of a global armed conflict has been reduced to a minimum, while the range of risks and sources of danger has increased significantly and become more complex

[34] Hungary's security is primarily affected by the development of the security of the Euro-Atlantic region and the political, social and economic processes taking place in its region, but the country cannot isolate itself from global sources of danger either

[35] Its connection to a federal system creates additional responsibility and, at the same time, increased opportunities in managing these

[36] The growing risks are posed by temporary or permanent conflicts between countries and groups arising from different levels of social development, economic, financial and social crises, ethnic and religious tensions, terrorism, organised crime, illegal drug and arms trafficking, demographic tensions, mass migration and severe environmental damage

[37] The proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and their means of delivery, as well as the possibility of attacks on information systems, pose an increasing challenge and threat

[38] In addition, tensions between states and armed conflicts within states are still present in Europe today

[39] In our region, instability and unpredictability arising from transformation, and the fragility of democratisation processes, are emerging as specific sources of danger

[40] The specificity of our security environment is that new types of and traditional challenges often appear simultaneously and mutually reinforcing

[41] The main objective of the security policy of the Republic of Hungary is to - guarantee the independence, sovereign statehood and territorial integrity of the country; - create appropriate conditions for the implementation of the principles laid down in the Constitution, promote the rule of law, the smooth operation of democratic institutions and the market economy, and contribute to the internal stability of the country; - promote the full implementation of civil, human, and national and ethnic minority rights in the Republic of Hungary; - ensure appropriate conditions for the enforcement of the life, property, and social security of those living in the territory of the Republic of Hungary, and the preservation of national wealth; - contribute to the implementation of the provisions of the North Atlantic Treaty and the security of its allies; - promote the maintenance of international peace, the strengthening of the security and stability of the Euro-Atlantic area, Europe, and its narrower region; - contribute to ensuring the conditions for the international economic, political, cultural, and other relations and cooperation of the Republic of Hungary

[42] The Republic of Hungary intends to achieve the above objectives in accordance with the Constitution, in accordance with the norms of international law, in particular the principles and commitments set out in the Charter of the United Nations, the documents of the Organization for Security and Cooperation in Europe, the Council of Europe and the North Atlantic Treaty, and in compliance with its obligations under other relevant international documents, regional and bilateral treaties

[43] The Republic of Hungary does not consider any state to be its enemy, and treats all actors in international politics who adhere to the principles of international law as partners

[44] It intends to resolve disputed issues in accordance with international law, taking into account European practice

[45] The security of the Republic of Hungary is built on two fundamental pillars: on the one hand, its national self-reliance, and on the other hand, Euro-Atlantic integration and international cooperation

[46] The Republic of Hungary intends to achieve the security and prosperity of its citizens on a lasting basis as a member of the democratic community of Euro-Atlantic countries

[47] The Republic of Hungary can maintain its security most effectively as a member of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (hereinafter referred to as the Alliance), which is based on the principle of collective defense

[48] Hungary, as an integral part of the political and military structure of the Alliance, undertakes the tasks devolving upon it from the common defense

[49] Hungary views constructive participation in the Alliance's consultation and decision-making system as a means by which it can contribute to strengthening the security and stability of the Euro-Atlantic area and to the effective enforcement of its national interests

[50] Our country considers transatlantic cooperation to be a fundamental factor of European security in the long term

[51] Within the framework of cooperation within the Alliance, it supports the development of a European security and defense identity, which constitutes an important element of Hungary's integration policy

[52] Throughout its history, European integration - and the European Union, which serves as its most important framework - has created the economic and political foundations for the security of its member states

[53] Based on the ongoing negotiations with the European Union, it is a realistic objective for the Republic of Hungary, keeping in mind its national interests, to become a full member of the European Union within a few years and to participate in the development and implementation of the Union’s common foreign and security policy

[54] As part of its Euro-Atlantic strategy, the Republic of Hungary also seeks full membership in the Western European Union, which it sees as an important future tool for addressing regional challenges

[55] The Republic of Hungary continues to play an active role in the activities of the United Nations, the Organization for Security and Cooperation in Europe, the Council of Europe, the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, and international arms control and non-proliferation regimes

[56] These organizations and forms of cooperation serve as an important framework for the implementation of Hungarian security policy goals in the areas of norm-setting, security and confidence-building, conflict prevention and management, and the continuous monitoring of the implementation of undertaken international obligations

[57] The Republic of Hungary attributes an important role to regional and subregional, as well as traditional bilateral forms of international cooperation in ensuring the security of the Republic of Hungary

[58] International cooperation can only be effective with effective national involvement, so national self-reliance continues to play a decisive role in ensuring the security of the country

[59] The active participation, support and willingness to sacrifice of citizens are of fundamental importance in ensuring the security of the Republic of Hungary

[60] The Hungarian security policy and institutional system must function in such a way that it is able to recognize in a timely manner, continuously and reliably assess the factors threatening the security of the country, and on this basis make timely decisions necessary to prevent and avert emergencies, and implement the measures resulting from them

[61] The Republic of Hungary pursues its security policy goals primarily through foreign policy, economic policy and defence policy

[62] In addition to Euro-Atlantic integration, Hungarian foreign policy focuses on neighbourhood policy and the prosperity of Hungarian communities across the border

[63] Hungary is a stable point in the Central and Eastern European region and plays an initiative role in expanding regional stability; it strives to foster good-neighborly relations with all countries in our region and to develop regional and sub-regional cooperation

[64] From the perspective of balanced, good-neighborly relations with neighboring states, it considers strengthening economic, cultural and human rights cooperation - including minority cooperation - between the countries of the region to be of fundamental importance

[65] The Republic of Hungary intends to build its relations with other countries in the region on the basis of Euro-Atlantic values, the norms of international law and mutual interests

[66] It is interested in its neighbors becoming members of Euro-Atlantic organizations as soon as possible by fulfilling the membership criteria

[67] Hungary actively supports their efforts in this direction and the maintenance and strengthening of their Euro-Atlantic commitment
