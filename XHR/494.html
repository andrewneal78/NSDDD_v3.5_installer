[204] Therefore, it is important to provide the Nation with the capacity to respond in exceptional situations, preserving the normal functioning of the State's vital functions

[205] Strengthening Protection Capacity requires the adaptation and structuring of surveillance systems in areas of interest and control over the national territory, Brazilian Jurisdictional Waters, overlying airspace and outer space, cyberspace and other areas of interest

[206] Examples of its structures include the Brazilian Aerospace Defense System - SISDABRA, the National Civil Defense and Protection System - SINPDEC and the Amazon Protection System - SIPAM

[207] The Protection Capacity, in addition to focusing on the national territory, must consider Brazilian interests abroad, with the purpose of ensuring the observance of individual or collective, private or public rights, and compliance with international agreements, in order to also protect the heritage, economic assets and national resources existing outside Brazil, in accordance with the international legal framework

[209] Closely related to the Protection Capacity is the Rapid Response Capacity, which includes several elements of National Power

[210] It aims to prevent the worsening of a crisis situation or to quickly end a dispute that has already broken out, avoiding the country's involvement in a prolonged armed conflict

[211] Deterrence Capacity, in turn, is an essential factor for National Security, as its purpose is to discourage possible aggression

[212] It is based on the conditions that the Nation has to gather and apply its Protection and Rapid Response Capacity, in the event of possible hostile actions against the sovereignty and legitimate interests of Brazil

[213] Deterrence capacity, which consists not only of the availability and readiness of adequate military resources, but also of the training of its personnel, is a tool of diplomacy

[214] The Coordination and Control Capacity aims to allow, under any circumstances, coordination between the various government agencies and is based on the control and integrity of information traffic

[215] The Information Management Capacity aims to guarantee the acquisition, production and dissemination of knowledge necessary for the decision-making process and the coordination and control of the resources available to the Nation, providing access to Intelligence to decision-makers at all levels

[216] This capacity provides conditions for preventive action by public authorities and contributes to the effectiveness of the Armed Forces' operational resources

[217] The Logistics Capacity for National Defense is based on Military Logistics and its systematized actions (military and business logistics), creating the direct and indirect involvement of the various national sectors capable of the seven logistics functions (Engineering, Maintenance, Human Resources, Rescue, Health, Supplies and Transportation), which, in a harmonious way, is coordinated by the Defense Logistics System - SisLogD, maintaining the military logistics, specific to each Singular Force, with the interoperability necessary to meet the interests of National Defense

[218] In turn, Strategic Mobility Capacity refers to the condition of the country's transport logistics infrastructure, multimodal capacity and means of transport, which allow the Armed Forces to move quickly to the area of employment, within the national territory or abroad, when this is required in order to defend national interests

[219] This condition requires, among other aspects, that Brazil has an adequate transport structure that prioritizes the interaction and integration of the various modes, so that they can act in a complementary manner

[220] The Mobilization Capacity at the national level complements national logistics, with the purpose of enabling the National Power to carry out strategic actions, in the field of National Defense, to face a situation of political-strategic crisis and, at the military level, it complements military logistics with the purpose of preparing the military expression for the transition from the peace structure to the war structure, to face an emergency situation resulting from the imminent realization or implementation of a hypothesis of employment of the Armed Forces

[221] Its main foundation is the National Mobilization System - SINAMOB, created by Law No. 11,631, of December 27, 2007, which has the Ministry of Defense as its central body and is structured in the form of sectoral directorates that will respond to the needs of National Mobilization in the political, economic, social, psychological, security and intelligence, civil defense, scientific technological and military areas, through subsystems in the federal administration, among them the Military Mobilization System - SISMOMIL

[222] The Military Expression of National Power is closely associated with the degree of technological and logistical independence of the Country, the capacity for National Mobilization and the ability to promptly employ the resources and services made available to it

[223] Mobilization should consider all the capabilities available to the country (infrastructure, installed and potential, and human capital), and special attention should be given to the preparation of these capabilities, aiming at their rapid, efficient and effective use, considering that the “time factor” is critical for the desired results

[224] The Defense Technological Development Capacity provides the development and/or modernization of Defense Products - ProdE and Defense Systems - SDs, through integrated actions undertaken by the State, industry and academia, in a synergistic manner, seeking technological updating and independence

[226] The Defense Sector must stimulate, within its scope of action, the development of the country's industrial potential, in order to increase National Power

[227] Thus, the defense and development of the country are naturally interdependent, insofar as the latter is a preponderant factor in providing the human and infrastructure resources that the nation needs to support the eventual use of military resources

[228] At the same time, it is imperative that the defense apparatus be in line with the most advanced practices and technologies, which requires the condition of national scientific and technological development at the state of the art

[229] In this context, Brazil's defense requires the permanent strengthening of its IDB (Industrial Defense Base), formed by a group of state and private, civil and military organizations that carry out or conduct, in the country, research, projects, development, industrialization, production, repair, conservation, overhaul, conversion, modernization or maintenance of defense products

[230] Regarding commercial aspects, these must be subordinated to strategic imperatives, with the purpose of enabling the fulfillment of National Defense Objectives

[231] Therefore, it is important that the development, production and commercialization network be subject to special legal, regulatory and tax regimes, in order to provide a greater level of security to national companies against the risks of commercial immediacy and the lack of regularity in state demands for defense products, without harming market competition and the development of new technologies

[232] The competitiveness of the IDB should also be taken into account, aiming at increasing the export of military goods, services and technologies and their possible adaptations for use in the civilian sector

[233] Such regimes should provide the IDB with conditions to increase its competitiveness, especially in relation to the external market, in order to increase its production scale and, thus, promote greater regularity in the demand for products, whether exclusively for defense or for dual application

[234] The counterpart for such special regimes must be the IDB's compliance with requirements established by the State, in accordance with the relevant legal instruments, so that such condition does not constitute privileges, but prerogatives arising from its commitment to the preparation of the National Power

[235] Promoting the IDB encourages the country's economic growth, as it generates direct and indirect jobs and develops products that will also be useful to the civil sector

[236] Therefore, investing in defense means guaranteeing sovereignty, promoting scientific and technological development and stimulating the country's growth

[237] Considering the context of globalization in the commercial and industrial areas, the search for strategic partnerships with other countries must be a priority, which demands coordinated action from various government agencies and private entities, with the purpose of achieving and consolidating the capacity to develop and manufacture defense products, minimizing dependence on the import of critical components, products and services, encouraging the acquisition and transfer of technologies, maintaining national sovereignty over the authority of rights and patents of goods and services

[238] Such partnerships should be based on the premise of strengthening national autonomous capabilities

[239] The Strategy therefore considers that a substantial part of the development, production and maintenance will be carried out in Brazil

[240] The state component of the DIB should, in principle, design and produce what the private sector cannot do profitably in the short and medium term

[241] In this way, the State will seek to act at the technological ceiling, in close collaboration with the advanced research centers of the Armed Forces and Brazilian academic institutions

[242] The search for new markets is one of the biggest challenges for the Defense Industrial Base and a relevant factor for its development and sustainability

[243] In this context, the State's actions constitute an important facilitating agent, insofar as the foreign policy practiced by Brazil, as well as state support for financing programs, research projects, development, production, acquisitions and marketing of national defense products, tend to provide greater confidence to potential buyers

[244] The State must use its purchasing power to guarantee minimum conditions of sustainability and improvement of the IDB's capabilities, so that the production chain is relatively independent of the export policy and the marketing of dual products

[245] Regarding national technological capabilities in the field of defense, their improvement occurs as a result of both the development of science and technology infrastructure and the training of human resources

[246] Hence the importance of implementing a training policy in basic and applied sciences, prioritizing the approximation of scientific production with activities related to the technological development of the IDB

[247] While safeguarding the State's security interests regarding access to information, joint initiatives between Armed Forces research organizations, national academic institutions and Brazilian private companies should be encouraged

[248] In this context, the Defense Sector should monitor advanced research in defense technologies at Armed Forces institutes or other subordinate or associated organizations, aiming, above all, at the synergistic performance of such initiatives. This seeks integration that avoids duplication of efforts, shares teams and ideas, and rationalizes the use of resources, as well as prioritizes the building of links between research and production, without losing touch with advances in basic sciences

[249] To achieve this condition, research projects must, as a priority, be carried out jointly by the advanced technology institutions of the three Singular Forces

[250] Projects of common interest to more than one Force must have their efforts integrated, defining, for each of them, an integrating pole

[251] These projects may be organized with their own personality, either as special purpose companies or under other legal forms, and should be selected and evaluated not only for their immediate employment potential, but also for their technological fruitfulness, that is, their usefulness as a source of inspiration and training for similar initiatives

[252] Furthermore, such projects must consider the desired commonality and interoperability of the Armed Forces' equipment, while safeguarding the peculiarities of each Force

[253] Likewise, the Defense Product Procurement Policy must observe this criterion, aiming to rationalize the use of budgetary resources and ensure, in procurement decisions, the prevalence of the commitment to the development of national technological capabilities in defense products

[254] With regard to human resources, a qualified reserve must be maintained and ready to be called upon to act in defense of the Homeland, when necessary

[255] In this context, the mandatory nature of Military Service should be maintained, as it is one of the conditions for mobilizing the Brazilian population in defense of national sovereignty, in addition to serving as an instrument of integration, cohesion and affirmation of national unity, regardless of social classes, since it generates opportunities for personal and professional improvement and encourages the exercise of citizenship

[256] In order to encourage the emergence of civilian specialists in the field of Defense, the involvement of Brazilian society in matters in this area should be increased, through classes, lectures, seminars, courses and related activities, in addition to joint work on development projects and those of interest to the defense sector, as well as in the creation of a career for specialists in this area

[257] Compulsory Military Service must be carried out in accordance with criteria established within the scope of the Singular Forces, based on the functional and professional characteristics and needs of each of them

[258] However, its educational, social and professionalizing nature must be observed in order to provide society with citizens committed to the country and better prepared for the job market, and qualified and motivated military personnel to serve the country well

[259] The composition of the personnel must, therefore, be in line with the policy of rational use of human resources, and it is imperative to seek a balance between the entry of career military personnel and temporary military personnel, aiming to balance the need to form reserves and meet the functional needs of each Force

[260] The military career, given its universal nature, reflects the whole of Brazilian society, and should, therefore, maintain its accessibility to all social classes

[262] National capabilities provide support for actions, both for diplomacy and for military expression

[263] Therefore, the development of the Country, an essential condition for the generation of National Power in all its fullness, constitutes a fundamental piece for the effective deterrence against possible threats to National Defense and Security

[271] DEFENSE SECTOR

[275] replace the Armed Forces' obsolete equipment
