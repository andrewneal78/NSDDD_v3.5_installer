[145] Strategic Communication During the first decade of the 21st century, the Colombian State and society managed to dismantle the main armed threats against the existence, independence and territorial integrity of the country

[146] Colombia is an example of how it is possible to transform a situation of imminent state failure into a new scenario of opportunities for development with equity, economic growth, and the rule of law

[147] Three factors were decisive in defeating terrorism, restoring security and authority to large swathes of territory that had been controlled by criminal organizations and subversive groups linked to drug trafficking and transnational crime

[148] The first of these was civilian leadership in the design and implementation of the Defense and Security Policy (PDS), which translated into the Government's decisive commitment to restoring security and the political, economic, and social support received by the Military and Police Forces

[149] The second factor was the modernization and professionalization of the Armed Forces and the National Police, to the point of becoming a global benchmark in counterinsurgency and counterterrorism capabilities, dismantling armed groups of transnational crime, and combating drug trafficking

[150] The third determining factor was a society united against crime, one that did not condone violence, terrorism, or crime, while supporting the institutions' willingness to enforce the law, recognizing the sacrifice of soldiers and police officers, and providing them with active support

[151] The citizen mobilization against crime and violence delegitimized their use, regardless of the purpose claimed, and strengthened the legitimacy of the Military Forces and the National Police, the Government, and the State

[152] This policy subdued the criminal activities of the United Self-Defense Forces of Colombia (AUC) and the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC), disbanding them as terrorist and drug-trafficking organizations. [154] Today, a new vision of security is needed that broadens the objective of confronting these armed groups and illicit economies and, in turn, adopts a multidimensional policy that addresses national interests, consolidates the rule of law, strengthens the democratic legitimacy of institutions, guarantees respect for human rights, and becomes the driving force behind the structural transformation of territories affected by crime and violence, ensuring their full incorporation into the nation as a whole and denying them to illegal groups

[282] From military control to institutional control of the territory The main threat to the country's security and stability lies in geographical areas devoid of institutions or with precarious institutions

[283] The changing threats, the spread of illicit crops, the illicit extraction of minerals, the persistence of organized armed groups and organized criminal groups, in addition to the weak or lacking presence of institutions, the lack of a social rule of law in the areas most affected by violence and crime, as well as the lessons learned and a multidimensional vision of security, all indicate the need for a new approach that transitions from the traditional concept of military control of territory to institutional control

[823] It will be a priority to strengthen and deepen this cooperation in the various areas established in the National Intelligence Plan, in accordance with the National Intelligence Strategy, under the coordination of the Joint Intelligence Board

[824] Institutional strengthening, efficiency, and well-being The implementation of the Defense and Security Policy requires high levels of efficiency, effectiveness, austerity, transparency, alignment, control, and synergy in the sector

[825] To achieve this, civil institutions will be strengthened and modernized, while the transformation processes of the Military Forces and the National Police will be promoted

[826] To this end, the following strategic initiatives will be undertaken

[827] Strengthen the National Security Council and adopt a national security strategy

[828] The National Security Council is the highest advisory body to the President of the Republic for decision-making on matters of national security

[829] It is responsible for advising on the formulation, implementation, and monitoring of national security policies, with the aim of coordinating the efforts of ministries and other state entities

[830] The Technical Secretariat is under the responsibility of the National Security Council

[831] Strengthening the institutional framework of the security and defense sector requires strengthening the Council's capacity to fulfill these tasks

[832] To this end, its operations will be reorganized; the heads of the Ministry of the Environment and the National Planning Directorate will be integrated into its current structure; a high-level, inter-institutional, and permanent technical team will be created; and a system will be created to monitor and follow up on the country's security situation

[833] In particular, the capacity to effectively coordinate the various state agencies and monitor the implementation of policies relevant to national security must be strengthened

[834] Under the new legislation promoted by the National Government, the Council will intervene in the implementation of aspects of Law 418 of 1997 and its extensions related to peace processes, in order to ensure the coherence of all legal instruments and different policies, in order to safeguard the country's internal and external security

[835] It will also have to define the Strategic Zones of Comprehensive Intervention (ZEII) and their special plans, as well as monitor their implementation

[836] The Council will play a strategic role in addressing factors impacting national security, particularly those related to water, biodiversity and the environment, organized armed groups, drug trafficking, illicit mineral extraction, cyberspace, borders, and foreign threats

[837] To this end, a National Security Strategy will be adopted for the first time, with a ten-year horizon, which expressly outlines Colombia's national interests, identifies the threats and challenges they face, and establishes strategic lines of action and those responsible

[838] Strengthen the Ministry of National Defense

[839] For its part, the Ministry of National Defense is responsible, within its scope of responsibility, for implementing the Defense and Security Policy, which entails aligning the Armed Forces, the National Police, and the institutions attached to and linked to the sector with the achievement of the established objectives and coordinating with other government departments

[840] This task requires improved technical capabilities and new control, evaluation and monitoring mechanisms

[841] In fulfilling these functions, the Ministry must establish technical bodies and mechanisms to ensure that government direction is fulfilled and that the Military Forces and the National Police carry out their functions within the strict framework of the Sectoral Policy and the constitutional order

[842] To this end, work will be done in the following areas: Raise the technical competency level of civilian personnel working in the defense sector and strengthen the technical and management capacity of the vice-ministries and directorates of the Ministry of National Defense

[843] • Ensure administrative coherence and avoid functional duplication between the Ministry of National Defense and the Armed Forces and the National Police, within the scope of the legal powers that correspond to each. • Establish mechanisms for comprehensive evaluation of the Armed Forces and the National Police that go beyond operational results. • Guarantee the transparency and technical nature of statistical information and information relevant to the design and evaluation of public policies. • Create and strengthen their own information systems to monitor the implementation of the Policy and progress in security in the territories. • Create an interagency commission to monitor the planning and execution of the budget of the Ministry, the Armed Forces, the National Police, and the attached and related entities. • Establish mechanisms so that, exclusively through the Ministry of National Defense, as head of the sector, the Armed Forces and the National Police are coordinated with other government agencies and branches of public power within their scope of competence

[844] • Develop and implement the technology management policy and model to transform the Ministry of National Defense into an interconnected, secure, and efficient digital organization

[845] • Move toward the creation of a single purchasing authority for all National Forces and the National Police, in accordance with international standards of transparency and efficiency for the sector

[846] • Strengthen the national maritime authority as a dependency of the Ministry of National Defense

[847] Articulate and strengthen existing transformation processes in the Military Forces and the National Police

[848] The Armed Forces and the National Police are carrying out independent and parallel transformation processes that seek to optimize their capabilities and administrative processes, and achieve increasingly efficient management

[849] To strengthen these processes and generate synergies in the sector, the Ministry of National Defense will develop and implement a roadmap to coordinate them, prioritizing the strengthening of differential, joint, and coordinated capabilities, as well as the institutional strengthening of the sector's leadership

[850] In terms of management, it will identify areas where it will be necessary to improve processes, specialize personnel, strengthen information systems and monitoring and evaluation mechanisms, generating efficiencies in the use of sector resources

[851] Additionally, strengthening the General Command of the Military Forces will be a priority, as an advisory body to the Ministry of National Defense and responsible for integrating the strategies and capabilities of each of the Military Forces, under the concept of jointness and efficiency

[852] To this end, strategic direction processes, technical capacity, information systems, human talent management and administration, among other aspects, will be strengthened

[853] This should allow for optimal coordination of the Armed Forces, ensuring that this body fulfills its legal obligations and contributes to achieving the objectives defined by the Sectoral Policy

[854] Advance the Planning and Development Model for the Capabilities of the Military Forces and the National Police

[855] Work will be done to project and develop the force structure in the medium and long term in accordance with the strategic vision of the Defense and Security Policy, the military and police strategy, the fiscal framework, and the definition of roles and missions between the Armed Forces and the National Police, based on criteria of jointness, coordination, complementarity, efficiency, and the elimination of potential duplication

[856] Methodologies will also be implemented to measure, estimate, and monitor the differential and complementary capabilities of the Military Forces and the National Police, as well as to train administrative and logistical personnel in this planning model

[857] The development of capabilities and the acquisition of new assets by each of the Armed Forces and the National Police must be framed within the military and police strategy, requirements that the Ministry of National Defense is responsible for ensuring in order to ensure efficient spending

[858] To make substantial progress in this effort, it will be necessary to prioritize the review of the roles and missions of the Military Forces and the National Police, prioritizing critical areas, achieving the objectives of this Policy, and implementing the National Security Strategy

[859] It will be essential to focus this exercise on the country's national interests, reducing crime rates, and strengthening national defense

[860] Information, transparency, accountability, monitoring and comprehensive evaluation

[861] Information is one of the most important tools for managing an organization, supporting decision-making, evaluating results, and managing change

[862] However, due to the size of the sector and compliance with information security standards, much of it rests independently with the Armed Forces, the Police, or the Ministry

[863] One of the main objectives will be to strengthen the integration of operational information systems and those related to the management of the Ministry, the Armed Forces, the National Police, and affiliated and related entities, in order to consolidate the information required for the continuous improvement of the sector and the fulfillment of the Policy's objectives

[864] On the other hand, to continue strengthening systemic actions that prevent, control, and punish malpractice that hinder the normal functioning of the sector, it is essential to continue working on the implementation of strategies regarding integrity and access to information, as self-control measures, as well as on strengthening internal control and inspection practices and imposing severe sanctions

[865] Additionally, the sector will continue to implement integrity and transparency measures in accordance with the international standards of the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) and the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO

[866] For a proper and timely execution of this Policy

[867] The Ministry of National Defense will evaluate the current monitoring and evaluation systems with the aim of implementing a new integrated system that ensures adequate, efficient, and timely development of strategic objectives and areas of effort, in order to achieve sectoral and national objectives, both in terms of mission and administrative management

[868] To achieve this, it will be essential to create new indicators that measure the joint work between the Military Forces, coordinated with the National Police, and interagency efforts with other state institutions

[869] New indicators are also needed that, in addition to measuring national crime reduction rates, allow for the evaluation of threat reduction results on a territorial and differentiated basis, as well as ensuring that achieving these goals does not lead to the transfer, expansion, or growth of threats to other areas

[870] Similarly, indicators will be available to record comprehensive progress against crime and illegal armed threats, going beyond statistical records and allowing for an assessment of the structural impact on such phenomena

[871] Innovate in systems for linking members of the Military Forces and the National Police

[872] The lack of state resources to continue expanding and maintaining the current force requires adopting new models for linking professional soldiers and police officers

[873] Regarding professional soldiers, whose essential characteristic is their combat capability, formulas will be studied to enable future processes to initiate personnel contracts with fixed-term contracts of 10 years and different contract modalities than the current one, which has a 20-year term

[874] Regarding the increase in the National Police force, work will be done to design formulas that will allow, also in the future, the recruitment of new officers through funding from regional entities, while preserving the national character of the Police

[875] Finally, developing the principle of complementarity between private security and the National Police, under strict state regulation and oversight, will contribute to expanding the capacity to prevent and guarantee citizen security

[876] Efficiency and rationalization of spending

[877] Fiscal difficulties require a review of the efficiency with which the budget is planned and executed

[878] It is essential to conduct a rigorous review of this process to seek opportunities for austerity and efficiency improvements, in order to make the best possible use of scarce resources in a sector with very limited budgetary flexibility

[879] To this end, the Ministry of Defense, together with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit and the Presidency of the Republic, will establish an interagency commission to strengthen the use of resources and guarantee the financial sustainability of the sector

[880] The full operation of the Defense Sector Logistics Information System (SILOG), head by the Ministry of National Defense, must be ensured in order to manage the supply and distribution chain, from the acquisition of goods and services to their final disposal

[881] This should enable effective control of allocated budgetary resources and support planning and decision-making

[882] In order to guarantee the operation of the System (SILOG), the Military Forces and the National Police will assign the required personnel, based on their profile and skills, to record data in a timely and quality manner

[883] This should enable real-time management of logistical and financial information, facilitating, in turn, insight into the readiness and control levels of equipment and materials, as well as a reflection of resource execution and its impact on the sustainability and efficiency of expenditure

[884] Strengthen the competitiveness of the business sector, as well as science, technology, and innovation

[885] The Social and Business Group of the Defense Sector (GSED) is a strategic reserve and an opportunity

[886] Under this framework, the following lines of work will be developed: Establish Corporate Governance for the companies and entities that are part of the (GSED) in order to improve competitiveness and enhance the capabilities of the Defense and Security Sector

[887] • Develop the industrial and commercial capabilities of GSED entities through the strengthening of science, technology, and innovation, knowledge transfer, and cooperation among the Group's entities, in order to position themselves in the natural market of the Armed Forces and National Police, and in the Defense Sector, and to enter new markets under more competitive conditions. • Advance strategic alliances with the national and international private sector, seeking to consolidate the sector in the security and defense market

[888] • In the field of science, technology, and innovation, we will seek to promote and strengthen the sector's scientific and technological capabilities through the implementation of strategic programs, the management of activities, the strengthening of intellectual property, and the consolidation of the Industrial and Social Cooperation Policy (Offset), through legal instruments that promote the transfer of technology and knowledge, in addition to seeking the optimization of cost systems

[889] • Similarly, it is necessary to modernize the acquisition, development and innovation, management, and logistics processes. To this end, policies and guidelines will be developed and implemented to develop capabilities in supply, maintenance, and infrastructure, enabling the strengthening of joint and coordinated logistics to sustain operations and maintain expenditures

[890] • Examine growth opportunities with models that involve the private sector, both national and international, in specific areas where investment is deemed necessary to be competitive and expand in the market

[891] Towards the total professionalization of the Military Forces

[892] To advance the professionalization of the Military Forces, that is, the transition from regular soldiers to professional soldiers, technical studies will be developed and a roadmap will be built and implemented to have one hundred percent (100%) professional Military Forces in 10 years

[893] In order to improve the quality of training, capacity building, and coaching programs, their content will be reviewed in accordance with constitutional and legal guidelines and the sectoral policy

[894] Likewise, training programs will be implemented for sector officials in military, police, management, and public administration matters

[895] Justice and well-being of members of the Military Forces and the National Police

[896] Improving the well-being of members of the Armed Forces and the National Police is a priority objective of this Policy, as is responding to the serious difficulties faced in healthcare or the demands for improvements in access to housing or education

[897] Regarding the health subsystems of the Military Forces and the National Police, the current status of their operations and service satisfaction will be reviewed, as well as the lines of action currently being developed in these areas

[898] In any case, a comprehensive care model must be guaranteed, focused on the individual, health promotion and maintenance, financial sustainability, and the improvement of installed capacity and human talent

[899] Similarly, policies will be adopted to strengthen transparency and spending efficiency

[900] Oversight over the provision of health services by the Military Forces and the National Police will be strengthened with enhanced inspection and surveillance by the Health Superintendency and the creation of monitoring and control mechanisms by the Ministry of National Defense

[901] Additionally, work will be done to design a sustainability model for the pension liabilities of the Military Forces and the National Police

[902] Access to education and housing will be reviewed to optimize existing resources and align the defense sector with the programs implemented by the National Government through the Ministries of Education and Housing

[903] The career statutes of members of the Armed Forces and the National Police will be updated

[904] Legal security will be guaranteed for soldiers and police officers in the performance of their duties, and a high-level commission will be created to make recommendations aimed at strengthening the Military Criminal Justice system

[905] Veterans of the fight against terrorism will receive special treatment as a demonstration of recognition and gratitude from Colombian society for their sacrifice and commitment to their country

[906] The Ministry of National Defense will process a law on veterans' benefits and will ensure that legal certainty exists for the actions of members of the Armed Forces and the National Police

[907] Regarding justice, the Security Policy considers it a priority to guarantee the due process rights of members of the Armed Forces and the National Police who appear before the Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP), as well as to respect the principle of the presumption of innocence

[908] Likewise, within the scope of its powers, the Ministry of National Defense will develop a program to ensure that the processes of constructing historical memory and truth also incorporate the perspective of the Armed Forces and the National Police

[909] As a contribution to nonrepetition, the historical memory component will include the construction of monuments in public spaces by military and police units to commemorate the victimizing acts committed by criminal and subversive organizations against the civilian population and members of the Armed Forces and the National Police, as well as the development of written and audiovisual records on the crimes against humanity and war crimes perpetrated by these criminal groups

[910] Soldiers and police officers who have been murdered, injured, kidnapped, disappeared, tortured, or subjected to any other violation of their rights, and their families, are considered, without distinction, in light of this Policy, to be victims and therefore deserve special attention from the Defense sector to guarantee their rights to truth, justice, and reparation

[911] Military Criminal Justice will be strengthened within the framework of its jurisdiction and the jurisprudence of the Constitution and the Inter-American system

[912] This jurisdiction is a very important control and justice mechanism for the sector

[914] To this end, the following strategic initiatives will be undertaken

[916] The strategic communication of the Defense and Security Policy will have a strong pedagogical focus, aimed at legitimizing the actions of the Armed Forces and the National Police and conveying to new generations that violence and crime are unacceptable as a means of political action

[917] The media available to the Armed Forces and the National Police are very important instruments in building a culture of legality and democratic values

[918] A single message from the Government

[919] The National Government has a single message on matters of security and defense

[921] The defense sector is one and the person in charge of the Ministry of National Defense is in charge

[922] Communications and messages must comply with the guidelines established by the Ministry, which, in turn, comply with those established by the Presidency of the Republic

[923] The various branches of the Military and Police Forces that issue messages must ensure discipline in communication

[924] The spokesperson for the sector is the person who holds the Ministry of National Defense

[925] Within the framework of the communications strategy and for operational matters, the Ministry of National Defense will determine who in the Armed Forces and the National Police may serve as spokespersons, in what areas, and under what conditions

[926] Explain the actions in accordance with the Defense and Security Policy

[927] The Policy serves as a guideline for the actions undertaken by the Armed Forces and the National Police, and serves as a framework for explaining the efforts carried out through military and police operations

[928] At all times, communication will articulate the messages sent to develop the Policy

[929] Direct communication with citizens

[930] The interaction between soldiers and police officers with citizens is the best opportunity for communication that builds trust, strengthens institutional legitimacy, and is fundamental to the successful implementation of this policy

[931] The Armed Forces and the National Police will implement collective communication mechanisms and optimize the use of social media to develop information schemes that directly involve communities in addressing their needs, interests, and expectations

[932] Supported by the development of new technologies, they will have communication channels that allow for interaction with citizens, soldiers, and police officers

[933] Defense and security officials will be committed to providing citizens with comprehensive, sufficient, and accurate information that will allow them to understand events in detail, identify threats and risk levels, and participate in the detection of fake news and manipulation attempts by state and non-state threat actors

[934] Transparency in communication is the rule

[935] Communication for regional integration

[936] The media available to the Armed Forces and the National Police should be geared toward integrating the country's most remote areas and be an instrument that contributes to institutional control of the territory

[937] All resources under a single communication and management strategy

[938] The Armed Forces and the National Police have radio stations throughout the country and other media outlets that constitute a strategic resource that must be consistent with the implementation of the sector's policy and useful for achieving the objectives established in the National Development Plan

[939] The Ministry of National Defense will ensure the coordination, control, management, and evaluation of these media, with the participation of the communications offices of the Armed Forces and the Police

[940] Communication with indigenous peoples

[941] The Ministry of National Defense will define and implement a communication strategy for indigenous peoples in their language, and will coordinate all media outlets of the Armed Forces and the National Police to implement it

[942] The same will be done, especially with the native and Afro-descendant communities

[943] The defense sector's advertising budgets will be the minimum and strictly necessary, in accordance with the National Government's goal of spending efficiency

[944] Advertising in the sector will be regulated by the Ministry of National Defense to ensure the principles of austerity and transparency
