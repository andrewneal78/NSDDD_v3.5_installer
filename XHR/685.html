[4] MILITARY DOCTRINE OF THE REPUBLIC OF BELARUS SECTION I GENERAL PROVISIONS CHAPTER 1 FUNDAMENTALS OF THE MILITARY DOCTRINE This Military Doctrine of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter referred to as the Military Doctrine) is a system of views officially adopted in the Republic of Belarus on maintaining international peace and security, ensuring military security of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter, unless otherwise specified, ensuring military security), its armed protection within the framework of state defense

[5] The military doctrine reflects the foundations of the military policy of the Republic of Belarus

[6] The provisions of the Military Doctrine are based on the results of an assessment of the military-political situation in the world and the European region, a forecast of its development, scientific research in the field of military security and defense, as well as on the experience of military development and the use of military force in modern conditions

[7] They are specified in the messages of the President of the Republic of Belarus to the people of the Republic of Belarus on the situation in the state and on the main directions of domestic and foreign policy, legislative acts, the Defense Plan of the Republic of Belarus and other documents of strategic defense planning

[8] By adopting the Military Doctrine, the Republic of Belarus, as an independent, sovereign, and peace-loving state, confirms the immutability of its military policy, its readiness to serve as a platform for the peaceful resolution of conflicts, and the launch of a global dialogue on security

[9] At the same time, it declares its determination to protect its national interests and the common interests of the member states of military-political associations with the participation of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter referred to as the allied states of the Republic of Belarus) using all means at the disposal of the state

[10] The legal basis of the Military Doctrine is the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, the Concept of National Security of the Republic of Belarus, approved by the decision of the All-Belarusian People's Assembly, other decisions adopted by the republican referendum or the All-Belarusian People's Assembly on military policy issues, and international treaties of the Republic of Belarus

[374] The Republic of Belarus considers any actions involving the use of military force directed against any of the member states of the Union State as an encroachment on the Union State as a whole and will take appropriate retaliatory measures using all forces and means at its disposal

[375] Issues of organizing the use of military force by the Republic of Belarus for the purpose of ensuring the military security of the Union State and its armed defense are determined by international treaties of the Republic of Belarus concluded with the Russian Federation and are resolved within the framework of a regional grouping of troops (forces) of the Republic of Belarus and the Russian Federation

[376] The Republic of Belarus considers aggression by any state (coalition of states) against one of the CSTO member states as aggression against all CSTO member states and will provide the state against which aggression has been committed with the necessary assistance, including military assistance

[377] In order to ensure, on a collective basis, military security and armed protection of the independence, territorial integrity and sovereignty of the CSTO member states, the Republic of Belarus contributes military contingents to the CSTO Collective Forces

[378] The Republic of Belarus uses military force in the context of fulfilling its international obligations to assist the efforts of the world community and collective security bodies in maintaining or restoring international peace and security by providing a military contingent

[379] to the UN military forces to participate in relevant activities carried out on the basis of a decision of the UN Security Council in areas of potential or existing military conflicts

[380] to the Collective Peacekeeping Forces of the CSTO to participate in peacekeeping operations on the territory of the CSTO member states, as well as to perform other tasks assigned to these forces by the decision of the CSTO Collective Security Council, including in the interests of resolving military conflicts on the territory of states that are not members of the CSTO, in accordance with the UN mandate

[381] The Republic of Belarus considers it possible to provide military assistance to friendly states on the basis of bilateral and multilateral international treaties of the Republic of Belarus, including by sending a military contingent to participate in relevant activities to maintain international peace and security

[382] The decision to send military personnel, employees of paramilitary organizations, and other persons outside the Republic of Belarus to participate in ensuring collective security and activities to maintain international peace and security is carried out in accordance with legislative acts

[383] SECTION IV MILITARY ORGANIZATION OF THE STATE CHAPTER 10 FUNDAMENTALS OF MANAGEMENT OF THE MILITARY ORGANIZATION OF THE STATE, FUNCTIONS AND TASKS OF ITS STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS The achievement of the Republic of Belarus's goals in military conflicts in the event of their outbreak, as well as the implementation of measures to ensure military security are carried out through the joint activities of the structural components of the MOG

[384] The highest state authorities, taking into account the delimitation of their powers, exercise leadership over the All-Belarusian People's Assembly and regulate the activities of its structural components

[385] In this case

[386] The President of the Republic of Belarus – Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, within the framework of general management of the system of ensuring national security and protection of the state, exercises his powers to ensure military security and defense

[387] The All-Belarusian People's Assembly, in implementing its functions to determine the strategic directions for the development of society and the state, directly participates in the formation of the state's military policy and facilitates its implementation through the approval of the Concept of National Security of the Republic of Belarus and the Military Doctrine of the Republic of Belarus

[388] Parliament - the National Assembly of the Republic of Belarus as a representative and legislative body of the Republic of Belarus in the course of implementing rule-making activities adopts laws aimed at implementing military policy and regulating other issues in the interests of ensuring military security and defense, participates in ensuring military security, preventing military conflicts through the development of interparliamentary relations and dialogue

[389] The Administration of the President of the Republic of Belarus, as a government body that ensures the activities of the President of the Republic of Belarus, and the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, as the central government body, within the framework of their competence, take measures to implement military policy and solve other problems in the interests of ensuring military security and defense

[390] The principles of the functioning of the VOG, its composition and structure are determined in the process of military development by the relevant regulatory legal acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus

[391] The tasks of the structural components of the All-Belarusian Society of the Deaf are established by legislative acts

[392] In this case

[393] The Security Council of the Republic of Belarus considers issues of military policy and makes fundamental (most important) decisions on issues of ensuring military security and defense

[394] The State Secretariat of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus prepares draft decisions of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus, decrees and orders of the President of the Republic of Belarus on issues of ensuring military security and defense, fulfillment by the Republic of Belarus of its allied obligations, participation in activities to maintain international peace and security

[395] The Ministry of Defense organizes interaction and coordination of the activities of government agencies and other organizations on issues of ensuring military security and defense

[396] In the event of a military threat, the Ministry of Defense, in accordance with its powers, prepares proposals for the adoption by the President of the Republic of Belarus of decisions on issues of organizing defense, the use of non-military measures to resolve a military conflict, as well as on resource provision for military operations

[397] The General Staff of the Armed Forces organizes interaction and coordination of the activities of the security component of the VOG on issues of ensuring military security and armed protection

[398] With the introduction of martial law, the General Staff of the Armed Forces performs the functions of the executive body of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus in the system of strategic command of the Armed Forces and other troops under the direct leadership of the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces

[399] State bodies and organizations that are part of the Republican Society of the Deaf, in their areas of activity, carry out tasks to ensure military security and armed protection

[400] The VOG force component, within the limits of its authority, resolves defense tasks

[401] It, as the basis of the military force of the Republic of Belarus, is intended to ensure military security by military measures, as well as to implement armed defense by using forces and means of armed combat in the appropriate forms of military action

[402] The Armed Forces and other troops must be prepared

[403] exclude the sudden emergence of external and internal military threats to the Republic of Belarus

[404] ensure constant readiness to use allocated forces and means in order to eliminate crisis situations that could lead to the outbreak of armed conflicts on the territory of the Republic of Belarus or states allied with the Republic of Belarus

[405] carry out a set of deterrent, preventive and preparatory measures to increase their readiness for armed defense in accordance with the level of military danger

[406] within the framework of the implementation of a set of measures for armed protection, ensure the achievement of the goals defined by this Military Doctrine

[407] In order to increase the level of the state's defense capability, to give the fight against the enemy a universal (nationwide) character, to improve the effectiveness of the actions of the Armed Forces and other troops, and to ensure the sustainability of the functioning of state bodies and organizations, branches and facilities of the economy, and the infrastructure of the Republic of Belarus, territorial defense and civil defense are organized in the event of a military threat

[408] The tasks of territorial and civil defense, the procedure for their preparation and implementation are determined by legislation and specified in the Defense Plan of the Republic of Belarus

[409] State bodies and other organizations that are not members of the All-Belarusian Society of the Deaf, as well as citizens of the Republic of Belarus, participate in ensuring military security in accordance with the law

[410] Local executive and administrative bodies, within the limits of their authority, carry out a set of measures for mobilization preparation and mobilization, preventing destabilization of the situation in the regions, carry out direct preparation and management of territorial and civil defense in the relevant territory, create the necessary reserves of material resources, and solve other tasks in the interests of ensuring military security and defense in accordance with the law

[413] improvement of the regulatory framework in the field of military security and defense

[414] increasing the efficiency of the functioning of the system of state and military administration and the interaction of its elements in solving joint tasks of ensuring military security and defense

[415] improving the state's military planning system

[416] increasing the effectiveness of non-military and military measures of strategic deterrence, their balanced and comprehensive application in accordance with the level and nature of military danger

[417] Conducting an active state policy and improving ideological work aimed at consolidating Belarusian society around the national idea of defending the Fatherland

[418] increasing the capacity of the structural components of the VOG to take coordinated actions to prevent the escalation of military threats and the prevention of military conflicts, improving interdepartmental cooperation

[419] bringing the structure, composition, and number of structural components of the VOG and the system of their staffing into line with the tasks of ensuring military security and armed protection, and the economic capabilities of the state

[426] improvement of territorial and civil defense

[427] increasing the prestige of military service, preparing citizens of the Republic of Belarus for it

[428] improving the system of training military-trained reserves and mobilization deployment

[430] SECTION V MILITARY-ECONOMIC FOUNDATIONS OF MILITARY SECURITY AND ARMED DEFENSE CHAPTER 12 FOUNDATIONS OF ECONOMIC SUPPORT OF MILITARY SECURITY AND ARMED DEFENSE The goals of economic support of military security and armed defense are the comprehensive and timely satisfaction of the needs of the All-Russian Society of the Deaf in the necessary financial, material and other resources, as well as the implementation of nationwide measures to prepare for defense

[431] The main principles of economic support for military security and armed defense are

[432] compliance of the level of financial and material support of the All-Russian Society of the Deaf with the needs necessary to ensure military security and armed protection, in accordance with the law

[433] centralized state management of military-economic processes in the state, ensuring high efficiency of enterprises and organizations that determine the technological sustainability of the defense sector of the economy

[434] concentration of financial, material and other resources on solving key tasks of ensuring military security and armed protection

[435] development of competitive and import-substituting technologies and production that are a priority for ensuring military security and armed protection

[436] a comprehensive set of economic measures aimed at ensuring military security and armed protection

[437] The main directions of economic support for military security and armed protection are

[438] improvement of legislation and coordination of functions of state bodies and organizations in solving problems of economic support of military security and armed protection

[439] improving the system of assessing, planning and financing the military needs of the state within the framework of a comprehensive system of strategic planning for ensuring national security based on the parameters of macroeconomic forecasting of the country's socio-economic development

[440] full implementation of state defense order activities

[441] optimization of expenditures of material, financial and other resources directed towards ensuring military security and armed protection

[442] preparation of the economic management system for sustainable functioning during periods of increasing military threat and during wartime

[443] creation of the necessary reserves of material resources in the state and mobilization material reserves to meet the needs of the VOG and the stable functioning of the economy during periods of increasing military threat and in wartime

[444] improving the system of mobilization preparation of the state economy

[445] rational use, further development and state support of the scientific, technical and production potential of the defense sector of the economy

[446] social security of military personnel, employees and civilian personnel of the Armed Forces and other troops

[447] comprehensive provision of activities for the patriotic education of youth, increasing the prestige of military service in society

[448] further improvement and development of mechanisms for military-economic (military-technical) cooperation

[449] To ensure readiness for armed defense in peacetime, a set of measures is being implemented to mobilize the economy and the armed forces to address this challenge, meeting the needs of the Republic of Belarus and the needs of the population during periods of increasing military threat and in wartime

[450] The mobilization preparation of the economy of the Republic of Belarus is consistent with the systems of mobilization deployment of troops (forces), territorial and civil defense in order to increase the sustainability of the functioning of industries and economic facilities, and the timely deployment of production of military and civilian products in accordance with mobilization plans

[451] The following is provided

[452] regulation of financial and economic relations, including the functioning of the monetary, budgetary, financial and tax systems, entrepreneurial and economic activities

[453] maintaining the economic potential of the Republic of Belarus, which would allow it to sufficiently satisfy the needs of the VOG and the needs of the population in wartime

[464] The main directions of development of the defense sector of the economy are

[465] improvement of regulatory legal acts in the field of functioning of the defense sector of the economy

[466] development and implementation of a strategy for the development of the defense sector of the economy, a set of measures aimed at creating conditions for the effective operation of enterprises and organizations, and measures to provide economic incentives for executors of state defense orders

[467] implementation of a unified state military-technical policy and comprehensive provision of fulfillment of the state defense order

[468] increasing the efficiency of production in the defense sector of the economy, introducing advanced technologies, creating innovative units and organizations that will significantly reduce the time required to develop and produce new, modern, and most in-demand types of military and technical equipment

[469] Ensuring sustainability and expanding the production of basic types of military equipment for the Armed Forces and other troops

[470] Development, at the request of the Armed Forces and other troops, of multifunctional (multipurpose) models (systems) of weapons and special equipment using standardized components and the possibility of long-term modernization, creation of a system for managing the full life cycle of weapons and special equipment products according to the following priorities: automated systems for command and control of troops and weapons; communication systems (means); systems (means) of electronic reconnaissance, electronic warfare, counter-battery warfare; unmanned aerial systems for various purposes; optical and optical-electronic means, reconnaissance and fire modules; missile technology and equipment; artillery systems and ammunition therefor; armored vehicles; standardized (modular) mobility vehicles; small arms and ammunition therefor; systems (means) of ensuring information security, means of information support of troops and information warfare; items of equipment and gear for military personnel, personal protective equipment

[472] development of import substitution, reduction of import intensity and import dependence, maximum localization of joint and foreign production for the manufacture, repair and modernization of military equipment, gradual minimization of imported components, raw materials and materials in the composition of military products manufactured by domestic enterprises

[474] maintaining state control over strategically important organizations in the defense sector of the economy

[475] training and recruitment of qualified personnel to meet the needs of the defense sector of the economy

[476] development of standards for organizations in the defense sector of the economy that allow for the optimization of processes and increased efficiency in conditions of a shortage of time, resources, and coordination
