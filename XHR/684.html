[5] NATIONAL SECURITY CONCEPT OF THE REPUBLIC OF BELARUS SECTION I GENERAL PROVISIONS This Concept represents a set of official views on ensuring the security of the individual, society, and the state from external and internal threats in all spheres of life in the Republic of Belarus

[6] This document defines unified approaches to the formation and implementation of state policy on national security and constitutes the methodological basis for the development of strategic planning documents and legislative acts in this area

[7] The Concept is based on the interconnection between national security and the socio-economic development of the Republic of Belarus, maintains continuity with previously adopted fundamental documents in this area, and is based on the main trends in the country's development, its place and role in the modern world, and the desire of the Belarusian people to preserve their sovereignty and national identity

[8] The following key concepts are used in this Concept: National security – the state of protection of the national interests of the Republic of Belarus from internal and external threats, ensuring its sustainable development; National interests – a set of balanced interests of the individual, society, and the state that ensure constitutional rights and freedoms, a high quality of life for citizens, social harmony, the unshakable foundations of democracy and the rule of law, its independence, territorial integrity, and sovereignty; Source of threat to national security – a factor or set of factors, as well as the entities that generate them, capable, under certain conditions, of giving rise to a threat to national security; Threat to national security – a real and existing danger of harm to the national interests of the Republic of Belarus; A challenge to national security is an objectively emerging danger of harm to the national interests of the Republic of Belarus; A risk to national security is an emerging danger of harm to the national interests of the Republic of Belarus; Political security is the state of protection of the political system of the Republic of Belarus from internal and external threats, ensuring the implementation of independent state policy, the harmonious development of society and the state, the preservation of the traditional fundamental values of the Belarusian people, and the observance of constitutional rights and individual freedoms; Economic security is the state of protection of economic sectors and sectors from threats that impede the sustainable socio-economic development of the Republic of Belarus; Scientific and technological security is the state of protection of scientific, technological, and educational potential from threats that impede the development of scientific activity, the creation and implementation of innovations and advanced technologies in the real sector of the economy, and other areas; Social security is the state of protection of individuals, society, and the state from social threats, ensuring the preservation of the life, health, and well-being of citizens, as well as the spiritual and moral values of the Belarusian people; Demographic security – the state of protection of individuals, society, and the state from the impact of demographic threats, ensuring population stabilization, the development of the country's human potential, and the preservation of family and intergenerational ties; Biological security – the state of protection of the population, animals, plants, and the environment from the impact of hazardous biological factors, ensuring an acceptable level of biological risk; Information security – the state of protection of the information space, information infrastructure, and information resources from external and internal threats in the information sphere; Military security – the state of protection of the national interests of the Republic of Belarus from existing and emerging military threats; Environmental security – the state of protection of the environment, the life and health of citizens from threats arising from anthropogenic impacts, as well as natural and man-made factors, processes, and phenomena; The electoral sovereignty of the Republic of Belarus is the inalienable and exclusive right of the state to independently organize and conduct elections and referendums in order to ensure the exercise of the people's sovereignty and freedom of choice under the supremacy of the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus and national legislation, and to prevent interference in the electoral process; National security system entities – government bodies and organizations, civil society actors, and citizens exercising their rights and responsibilities in the sphere of national security in accordance with the law

[213] CHAPTER 9 NATIONAL SECURITY SYSTEM The national security system is the set of interacting national security entities and the means they use to protect and realize the national interests of the Republic of Belarus and ensure the security of individuals, society, and the state

[214] The legal basis of the national security system consists of the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, laws of the Republic of Belarus, regulatory legal acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus, other legislative acts governing public relations in the sphere of national security, as well as international treaties of the Republic of Belarus

[215] National security entities carry out coordinated activities to achieve the goals and solve problems of national security in accordance with their legal status, as determined by law

[216] Furthermore: The President of the Republic of Belarus exercises overall leadership over the national security system by exercising his or her powers in this area both directly and through the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus and its working body, the State Secretariat of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus, as well as through the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus; The All-Belarusian People's Assembly, based on the strategic directions for the development of society and the state, approves the main directions of domestic and foreign policy, the military doctrine, and the concept of national security, and exercises other powers related to national security in accordance with legislation; The National Assembly of the Republic of Belarus adopts laws in the sphere of national security; The Security Council of the Republic of Belarus considers domestic and foreign policy issues of the Republic of Belarus affecting national security interests and makes decisions on them, including determining the state bodies responsible for ensuring national security in key areas of individual, societal, and state activity, as well as the threshold values for national security indicators, and organizes the effective functioning of the national security system

[217] The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, within its competence, develops specific measures to ensure national security and organizes and oversees their implementation

[218] State bodies subordinate (accountable) to the President of the Republic of Belarus and national government bodies subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus, in accordance with their competence, implement measures aimed at achieving national security objectives and maintain available forces and assets in a state of readiness for use

[219] The prosecutor's office oversees the precise and uniform implementation of laws, decrees, and other regulatory legal acts by national government bodies and other state organizations subordinate to the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, local representative, executive, and administrative bodies, public associations, religious organizations, and other organizations, officials, and other citizens, including individual entrepreneurs, in the area of national security

[220] They coordinate the law enforcement activities of state bodies combating crime and corruption, as well as the anti-crime activities of other organizations involved in the fight against crime, and crime prevention activities in the area of national security

[221] The courts administer justice in cases involving matters of national security

[222] The State Control Committee exercises state control over the execution of the national budget, the use of state property, the implementation of acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus, the Parliament of the Republic of Belarus, the Government of the Republic of Belarus, and other state bodies regulating state property relations, economic, financial, and tax relations, as well as other matters in accordance with laws and decisions of the President of the Republic of Belarus in the area of national security

[223] Local government and self-government bodies ensure the implementation of national security issues assigned to their competence by law

[224] Citizens participate in ensuring national security by exercising their rights and obligations (including fulfilling their sacred duty to protect the Republic of Belarus) as stipulated by the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, laws of the Republic of Belarus, and regulatory legal acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus

[225] They realize their vision of national interests and the methods and means of protecting them by participating in elections, referendums, and other forms of direct democracy, as well as through state bodies and local governments

[226] Citizens and civil society actors may also participate in ensuring national security by: informing state bodies about the existence (emergence) of sources and factors that create threats to national security; directly protecting the rights and freedoms of citizens and the interests of society through legal means and methods; developing and submitting proposals to state bodies for improving regulatory legal acts governing public relations in various areas of national security; participating in shaping public opinion on national security issues; enhancing political and legal culture, citizen responsibility, civic awareness, and fostering patriotism; Assistance by lawful means and methods to government agencies in ensuring national security, achieving public harmony, and stability

[227] To address national security issues in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, laws of the Republic of Belarus, and regulatory legal acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus, national security forces are created

[228] These forces include the Armed Forces, state security agencies, border service agencies, internal troops of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Security Service of the President of the Republic of Belarus, the Operational and Analytical Center under the President of the Republic of Belarus, other troops and military formations of the Republic of Belarus, the Investigative Committee, the State Forensic Examination Committee, internal affairs agencies, emergency response agencies and units, financial investigation agencies, customs authorities, the State Inspectorate for Wildlife Protection under the President of the Republic of Belarus, the financial monitoring agency, and units (services) of other government agencies that ensure the safe conduct of work in industry, energy, transport, communications and information security, and more

[229] The main functions of the national security system are: improving the conceptual, organizational, and legal foundations for ensuring national security; refining the interests of individuals, society, and the state in accordance with the evolving situation, and establishing and maintaining the necessary balance between them; developing and promptly adjusting indicators (indicators) of the state of national security and performance criteria for those implementing it; organizing and conducting monitoring, analysis, and assessment of the state of national security; defining priority areas and objectives for ensuring national security in key areas of individual, social, and national activity; forecasting, promptly identifying increasing levels of danger, and assessing internal and external risks, challenges, and threats to national security; developing and implementing a set of operational and long-term measures to prevent and neutralize risks, challenges, and threats to national security, and to prevent harm to the national interests and development of the Republic of Belarus; Managing existing forces and assets, maintaining them in constant readiness based on organizational, personnel, financial, material, technical, informational, and other resource support; Organizing interaction and coordinating the actions of all national security entities, and assessing their effectiveness; Informing citizens about the state and provision of national security

[230] Interaction between national security entities is carried out taking into account their legal status, competence, and the nature of the tasks being performed, with the coordinating role of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus and its working body, the State Secretariat of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus

[231] The national security system is organized and developed based on the further improvement of existing subsystems and mechanisms that ensure the reliability and sustainability of its functioning, as well as taking into account the actual financial and material capabilities of the state

[232] National security objectives are addressed through the systematic implementation of a range of interconnected political, diplomatic, economic, legal, military, informational, and other means aimed at identifying, preventing, and neutralizing internal and external risks, challenges, and threats to the security of individuals, society, and the state, as well as preventing or minimizing the scale of harm to the national interests of the Republic of Belarus

[233] The degree of concentration and the scope of use of available resources are determined based on the likelihood and impact of specific threats on the Republic of Belarus, as well as the actual capabilities of the state and society

[234] The effectiveness of national security is achieved through targeted activities to protect and realize national interests, the ability of the national security system to promptly identify changes in the internal and external environment that create challenges and threats to national security, and to prevent or minimize the consequences of crises

[235] All national security entities organize an anti-crisis response to risks, challenges, and threats to national security, which includes: collecting, processing, and analyzing information (monitoring) on the development of the situation (environment) in the relevant areas of national security; Assessing the situation, forecasting its development and possible negative consequences; Developing proposals to improve operational responses to national security challenges and threats

[236] Anti-crisis response is implemented through the application of special legislative and other regulatory legal and administrative acts, operational, medium-term, and long-term organizational and practical measures, the deployment of relevant forces and resources of the national security system of the Republic of Belarus, and other capabilities, as well as their integration, if necessary, into interdepartmental and national systems

[237] During the implementation of anti-crisis response measures requiring maximum state effort, special legal regimes are temporarily introduced, under which, in accordance with the law, the constitutional rights and freedoms of citizens, as well as the activities of political, economic, civil, and other entities, may be restricted

[238] When an immediate threat to national security arises, proposals are prepared for the President of the Republic of Belarus and the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus on the adoption of urgent measures aimed at protecting against the threat(s), preventing crises, or minimizing their possible negative consequences

[239] The implementation of adopted decisions is organized, their execution is monitored, and the national security system is regulated

[240] A comprehensive strategic planning system operates in the interests of ensuring national security

[241] Strategic planning documents are developed, reviewed, and approved in accordance with the procedure established by law

[242] The key indicators (indicators) of the state of national security are: the share of gross fixed capital formation; the ratio of short-term payments for the repayment and servicing of public debt in foreign currency to gold and foreign exchange reserves (in the international definition); gross external debt; the degree of consideration of international law and the political and legal mechanisms provided therein in resolving issues affecting the interests of international actors; The availability and effectiveness of international legal and political-legal guarantees of the security and sovereignty of the Republic of Belarus; The level of trust in government institutions; The ratio of the Republic of Belarus's per capita GDP at purchasing power parity to the average in neighboring countries; The share of innovative organizations in the total number of manufacturing organizations; Domestic expenditure on research and development; Human Development Index; Decile coefficient; Depopulation rate; Migration increase; Life expectancy at birth; Biological risk level

[243] The level of information technology and telecommunications development; The military-political situation; The readiness of the state's military organization to fulfill military security tasks; The level of the state's military power; The elasticity coefficient of pollutant emissions, wastewater discharges into the environment, waste generation, and GDP growth

[244] The list of indicators, their threshold values, and the procedure for assessing the state of national security shall be determined by decree of the President of the Republic of Belarus

[245] SECTION V FINAL PROVISIONS This Concept shall be implemented under the leadership of the President of the Republic of Belarus through the consolidation of the efforts and resources of the state, civil society actors, and citizens to protect and advance the national interests of the Republic of Belarus

[246] To enhance the effectiveness of these activities, modern methods of analysis, forecasting, and comprehensive assessment of the situation are widely used

[247] The State Secretariat of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus shall monitor the implementation of this Concept, including through the preparation of the annual report of the State Secretary of the Security Council of the Republic of Belarus to the President of the Republic of Belarus on the state of national security and measures to strengthen it
