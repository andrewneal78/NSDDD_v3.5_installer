[1] National Defence Concept Approved by the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Latvia on 20 May 2008 Adopted by the Saeima of the Republic of Latvia on 19 June

[2] The National Defence Concept (hereinafter – the Concept) has been developed on the basis of Article 29 of the National Security Law

[3] Its aim is to determine the strategic basic principles of national military defence, priorities and measures for ensuring national defence

[4] The Concept has been prepared in accordance with the geopolitical situation of Latvia, national security and foreign policy objectives and Latvia’s obligations arising from its membership in the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the European Union

[5] The Concept has been prepared taking into account the assessment of national security threat factors, as well as the analysis of regional and international security development trends and the documents defining the security and defence policy of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the European Union

[6] The Concept determines the guidelines for the implementation of Latvia’s national defence tasks and the development planning of the Latvian National Armed Forces (hereinafter – the National Armed Forces

[7] Description of Latvia's security and international security

[8] With its membership in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union, Latvia has significantly strengthened its national security and defence

[9] The basis for national security and defence is the strengthening of Latvia's military capabilities, the principle of collective defence of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and military cooperation with allied countries within the framework of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union

[10] Latvia's security is inseparable from the security of other Baltic Sea region countries of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union, which is facilitated by close mutual cooperation

[11] Military cooperation with the Baltic States, the Nordic countries, as well as the United States of America, which will continue to be an important strategic partner of Latvia, plays an important role in promoting the security of Latvia and the entire region

[12] The security and defence policy of the European Union plays an important role in the international security environment, and it is an important element in strengthening Latvia's security and defence

[13] When implementing national defence policy, it is necessary to take into account the possible uncertainty of the domestic and foreign policies of individual countries, which may negatively affect the security of Latvia and its allied countries

[14] One of the security risks is the possible changes in the balance of power in international politics, which are related to the rapid economic development of certain countries, the struggle for natural resources, the increasing armament trends, as well as efforts to regain influence in world politics

[15] At the same time, the security of Latvia (as well as that of other member states of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union) is significantly affected by the development trends and security challenges of the international security environment

[16] For example, international terrorism, the spread of weapons of mass destruction, organized crime, cyberattacks, as well as instability in various regions of the world and the desire of individual countries to engage in military rivalry

[17] Modern threats are characterized by global spread, they are difficult to predict, and they are not only of national origin

[18] The changing nature of global security challenges determines that effective ensuring of Latvia's security and defense must be based on readiness to prevent situations of national threat in peacetime, national threat and wartime

[19] Ensuring national defense

[20] National Defence Basic Task

[21] The national defence basic task of Latvia is to guarantee the independence of the state, territorial indivisibility and security of the population

[22] In order to ensure the most effective national security and defence, Latvia has joined the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the European Union

[23] An effective warning system and a set of preventive measures are necessary for the timely prevention of national threats

[24] In the event of a national threat, the National Armed Forces, in cooperation with the armed forces of allied countries, ensure the inviolability of the land territory, sea area and airspace of the state of Latvia

[25] The National Armed Forces, together with civil state institutions, must be ready to receive the armed forces of allied countries on the territory of Latvia

[26] The National Armed Forces provide support to the civil institutions of the state in preventing threatening situations and in overcoming and liquidating their consequences

[27] The involvement of the National Armed Forces in these measures promotes the effective use of state resources and increases the practical readiness of the National Armed Forces

[28] Strategic Basic Principle of National Defence

[29] The strategic basic principle of Latvia's national defence is participation in the collective defence system of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation, the task of which is to protect its member states against any type of military threat

[30] Consequently, it is in Latvia's interests for the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation to maintain and develop its capacity and effectiveness to implement its tasks, thereby also strengthening Latvia's defence

[31] In order to implement this, it is important for Latvia to strengthen its influence in the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation by participating in the decision-making and consultation process of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation

[32] Basing its security on the collective defence system, Latvia has been actively involved in both the strengthening of the capabilities of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and in the international operations and other security promotion measures it implements

[33] The national armed forces are included in the military combat units of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation, paying special attention to contributing to the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation's rapid reaction forces

[34] By participating in the rapid reaction forces, the National Armed Forces gain experience necessary for improving combat capabilities

[35] The development of Latvia's defense capabilities takes place in accordance with the North Atlantic Treaty Organization's Capability Development Guidelines, the basis of which is the North Atlantic Treaty Organization's Comprehensive Political Guidelines, determining the obligations of the member states to implement military capabilities

[36] In order for Latvia to operate successfully in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, the interoperability of the armed forces is necessary

[37] It promotes cooperation between the types and units of the armed forces of the member states of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization to perform common tasks

[38] For the effective operation of the national defense system in overcoming military and non-military threats, coordination of the activities of all involved state institutions and mutual cooperation in predicting, planning, preventing and overcoming these threats are essential in order to effectively use the resources at the disposal of the state and to quickly respond to each possible threat or its consequences

[39] Measures to ensure military defence

[40] The basis of the state's military defence is formed by the National Armed Forces, which ensure the inviolability of the country's land territory, sea waters and airspace, participate in international operations, ensure the training of personnel and military service reserves, as well as engage in the prevention of situations threatening the state

[41] During war or a state of emergency, the Defence Department of the Bank of Latvia is included in the National Armed Forces, and during wartime, the State Border Guard is included in the National Armed Forces

[42] The National Armed Forces are developed as a professionally trained, capable of adapting to combat situations and various security situations, modernly equipped, compatible with the military units of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the battle groups of the European Union, and socially secured national defence instrument with balanced tasks, structure and funding

[43] Primarily, those combat capabilities of the National Armed Forces are developed that can be used in various security situations for the performance of both national and collective defense tasks and meet the following requirements: a) effectiveness – mobility of units, compatibility of military capabilities with allied forces, readiness to operate in multinational military formations and in wide-ranging operations, b) deployability – the ability to create, prepare and move units in Latvia, as well as to create, prepare and send them to international operation areas, c) sustainability – readiness to maintain units and ensure their operation with resources throughout the operation, d) multifunctionality – the ability of units to engage in the prevention of all types of threat situations both in Latvia and abroad, e) survivability – protection of units from the threat of weapons of mass destruction, f) information superiority – rapid acquisition and use of strategic and operational information for the performance of tasks

[44] The National Armed Forces shall carry out national defence measures with the high combat readiness units at their disposal in accordance with the procedure laid down in the national and collective defence plans

[45] Lower combat readiness units shall, as necessary, prepare individual specialists and specialised sub-units for the performance of the tasks of the National Armed Forces

[46] The National Armed Forces shall maintain a structure in which the administrative element is subordinate to combat and combat support tasks

[47] The National Guard is a component of the National Armed Forces, and its aim is to involve the citizens of the Republic of Latvia in the defence of the territory and society of the country

[48] Within the framework of the national defence system, the National Guard provides support to the regular forces by maintaining the necessary specialised capabilities

[49] Within the framework of the collective defence system, it fulfils the functions of supporting Latvia as a host state and participates in international operations

[50] For the purpose of fulfilling national defence tasks and developing appropriate military capabilities, the total numerical strength of the National Armed Forces shall not exceed 20,000, of which 5,800 are professional service soldiers, the remainder being national guardsmen, military and civilian employees

[51] When approving the state budget, the Saeima shall determine the numerical strength of the National Armed Forces each year

[52] The Defence Department of the Bank of Latvia and the State Border Guard shall participate in ensuring national defence in cases of national threat provided for in the National Armed Forces Law, by becoming part of the National Armed Forces and performing the tasks specified in legal acts and action planning documents

[53] The development of the national defence system shall take place in the following main directions: 24.1. professional military service: 24.1.1. professional armed forces shall ensure the readiness of the National Armed Forces for national and collective defence with effective operational capabilities, high combat readiness and modern technologies; 24.1.2. in developing the armed forces based on professional service, the military service must attract appropriate personnel

[54] In order to provide the National Armed Forces with the necessary number of personnel, the recruitment system must be flexible, modern and effective

[55] Strengthening the Young Guard movement and the development of its activities contribute to attracting appropriate personnel to the military service

[56] in order to attract and motivate personnel to serve in the National Armed Forces, a personnel career planning system must be developed, which ensures clear future career growth in the National Armed Forces and effective filling of the positions of the National Armed Forces with the necessary personnel

[57] in order to promote the competitiveness of the position of a soldier in the labor market, appropriate remuneration must be ensured for military personnel, continuous replenishment of professional knowledge, prerequisites must be created for successful integration into civilian life after the end of service, and soldiers and their families must be provided with appropriate social guarantees

[58] Armament, infrastructure and training that meet the tasks to be performed and modern standards

[59] For the development and modernization of the National Armed Forces, modern armament equipment and weapon systems must be purchased and high-quality training that meets the technologies used must be ensured

[60] The purchase of modern armament and equipment is aimed at increasing the combat capabilities of the Armed Forces and the protection of soldiers

[61] For the deployment, maintenance and support of the training process of the National Armed Forces units, it is necessary to develop an appropriate multifunctional infrastructure

[62] taking into account that the National Armed Forces are numerically small, for their development and modernization, such military-technical systems shall be introduced that do not require significant personnel resources

[63] They must be effective in area surveillance, airspace and territorial sea control and protection, in order to ensure information superiority

[64] in order to develop the National Armed Forces' ability to support society in various situations of national threat in peacetime, specific equipment and appropriately trained personnel are required

[65] in order to ensure the mobility of units within the national territory and transportation to areas of operation in the long term, as well as their transportation and long-term maintenance in areas of international operations, units shall initially be formed with the minimum necessary combat support and combat support, gradually increasing these capabilities over time

[66] In order to increase the professionalism of the National Armed Forces, the training system shall be improved to provide each soldier with the knowledge and professional growth opportunities necessary for military service, while the field tactical exercises of the units shall be closer to possible combat conditions

[67] combat capabilities

[68] The National Armed Forces shall develop such combat capabilities that, in the event of a national threat, they shall be able to ensure the inviolability of the land territory, sea waters and airspace of the State of Latvia

[69] At the same time, the National Armed Forces improve their ability to participate in search and rescue activities and provide support to state civil institutions in situations of threat

[70] one of the directions of development of the National Guard is to ensure the ability to implement national defense tasks in cooperation with regular forces until the arrival of the armed forces of allied countries in the territory of Latvia and to ensure the support of the host country to the allied forces

[71] taking into account Latvia's membership in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union and involvement in strengthening international security, Latvian soldiers must be prepared for participation in international operations in geographically distant areas with a climate uncharacteristic for Latvia, different socio-political and economic conditions and a cultural environment

[72] By participating in international operations, the National Armed Forces develop operational experience and improve the combat support system and combat cooperation with allied forces

[73] The development of the National Armed Forces is carried out in accordance with the following guidelines for the development of military capabilities of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization: a) creation of a modern command and control system, development of effective communication and information transmission systems and their security, b) increasing the effectiveness of combat capabilities, c) improving protection against weapons of mass destruction, d) creation of a modern training infrastructure

[74] in accordance with the common policy of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, Latvia is developing capabilities to ensure the permanent presence of not less than 8% of the personnel of the National Armed Forces in the areas of operations, which is 450 soldiers

[75] Not less than 40% of the total number of personnel of the National Armed Forces must be prepared for participation in operations led by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, the European Union and other international organizations

[76] The National Armed Forces must ensure in the medium term the ability to deploy and independently maintain a platoon-level unit in the area of operations up to 15,000 kilometers from the Latvian border, a company-level unit up to 5,000 kilometers away, and two company-level units with integrated combat support and combat support capabilities up to 3,000 kilometers away

[77] If necessary, the National Armed Forces must be ready to participate in full-spectrum combat operations led by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization with one infantry battalion for up to six months without rotation or with subunits not exceeding two infantry companies, or with specialized subunits of equivalent size for up to six months with rotation

[78] The Navy of the National Armed Forces must improve its ability to participate in operations led by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization

[79] within the framework of a timely implemented and effective warning system, intelligence, command, control and information systems must be established and developed, and their operational security must be ensured, achieving information superiority and the possibility of using it immediately to achieve one's goals

[80] Ensuring host nation support

[81] In order to fully implement national defense, Latvia must ensure the reception of allied armed forces on the territory of Latvia within the framework of the host nation support system

[82] Host nation support must ensure the rapid arrival and deployment of allied forces, which is based on compatible and coordinated planning of the supply and support system

[83] The host nation support system is essential not only in cases of military threats to the state, but also in cases of prevention of the consequences of natural and man-made disasters and international exercises

[84] The National Armed Forces shall ensure the support of the host country in close cooperation with the civil institutions of the state and private institutions, which, in accordance with the level and intensity of the threat, ensure: a) the rapid and effective implementation of border crossing procedures, b) the provision of medical support, c) the use of civil aviation and airfields, the use of the maritime transport fleet and ports, as well as the use of railways and related infrastructure, d) the use of civil transport and other infrastructure necessary for the deployment of forces

[85] All responsible institutions shall ensure the maintenance of the host country support system and mutual coordination so that, if necessary, allied forces can be quickly deployed

[86] Personnel reserve and mobilization systems

[87] Along with the transition to professional armed forces, appropriate personnel reserve and mobilization systems must be established and maintained to ensure the performance of national defense tasks in a situation of national threat

[88] Latvia maintains personnel reserves and mobilization systems in accordance with the level of threat, and the amount of funds invested in them is adequate for the tasks to be performed by the National Armed Forces

[89] They are based on the duty of citizens to defend the independence, freedom and democratic state system of the country, do not require significant funds for maintaining a large and high-readiness mobilization reserve

[90] The primary maintenance base of the personnel reserve system of the National Armed Forces is the National Guard

[91] It is necessary to develop a personnel reserve system that would allow planning the attraction of resources for the performance of the tasks of the National Armed Forces not only in the event of a threat to the country, but also in peacetime, as well as for ensuring the operation of the National Armed Forces in collective defense operations of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and peacekeeping

[92] When creating such a personnel reserve, it should be taken into account that the National Armed Forces must be able to compete in the Latvian labor market and an appropriate training, supply and support system for these reserves must be created

[93] Participation in strengthening international security

[94] Taking co-responsibility for promoting common security in the Euro-Atlantic area, Latvia must engage in strengthening international security, which is at the same time an investment in Latvia's security

[95] Given the existence of global threats, timely action and close mutual cooperation between states and international organizations in mitigating potential threats and promoting international security are becoming increasingly important

[96] Coordinated cooperation between the United Nations, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, the European Union and the Organization for Security and Cooperation in Europe is in Latvia's interests, as the crisis management resources at the disposal of these organizations allow for the most appropriate solution to be found for resolving international crises

[97] Latvia supports the development of harmonious cooperation between the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union without overlapping functions and capabilities between these organizations

[98] Latvia engages in strengthening international security by participating in international operations, implementing regional cooperation, as well as providing support to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization partner countries in implementing defense reforms

[99] Participation in international operations

[100] Latvia's participation in international operations strengthens international security and at the same time contributes to national security

[101] By participating in international operations, Latvia promotes cooperation with its allies in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union, providing them with political and practical support in resolving current security issues, and gains useful experience in the military field

[102] Latvia plans its participation in international operations in accordance with the priorities of its national security policy and foreign policy

[103] Basing security on the fundamental principle of collective defense, Latvia's priority is participation in operations led by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization

[104] At the same time, taking into account the available personnel and financial resources, Latvia participates in operations led by the European Union and other international operations

[105] Stabilizing the situation and restoring security in international crisis areas requires a comprehensive approach, attracting military and civilian resources and financing

[106] Consequently, Latvia participates in strengthening international security not only with military investment, but - to the extent possible - also with civilian resources

[107] The capabilities at the disposal of the European Union are important for the timely prevention of international crises and the stabilization of the post-conflict situation

[108] It is in the interests of Latvia to promote the coordinated use of civil and military assets of the European Union and the North Atlantic Treaty Organization in solving security problems

[109] Regional cooperation (Baltic cooperation, cooperation with the Nordic countries

[110] Baltic cooperation

[111] The military cooperation of the Baltic States is based on their common interests and goals, which are aimed at increasing the security and well-being of the countries, as well as effective participation in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union

[112] Close cooperation between the Baltic States is a fundamental element of strengthening the stability and security of Latvia and contributes to the development of the armed forces of the Baltic States and their mutual interoperability

[113] The Baltic States must continue to develop and improve joint military projects that ensure more efficient use of limited resources and the improvement of common military capabilities

[114] Cooperation should increase the ability of the armed forces of the Baltic States to participate in international operations and develop readiness to provide host nation support

[115] Cooperation with the Nordic countries

[116] Cooperation between Latvia and the Nordic countries is based on common interests – to promote stability and security in the Baltic Sea region, to promote the strengthening of the countries’ military capabilities and readiness to participate in international operations, as well as to effectively use human and financial resources in the development of military capabilities

[117] It opens up opportunities to form a common stance and common positions on various security and defense policy issues, increasing the opportunity to influence decision-making processes in international organizations, as well as improving the defense capabilities of countries

[118] It is important for Latvia to continue cooperation with the Nordic countries in the following key areas – pre-operational training and joint operations in areas of operations, personnel training and personnel exchange, logistics issues and procurement, consultations on host nation support issues, military capability planning and environmental protection

[119] Support for NATO partner countries in implementing defence reforms

[120] As a full member state of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, Latvia forms part of a much broader common security area and has taken on new responsibilities in strengthening international security and stability

[121] Following the policy implemented by the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, Latvia actively cooperates with countries whose foreign and security policy objectives are to develop closer cooperation with the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and the European Union

[122] Latvia's goal, by sharing its accumulated experience in the development of democracy, Euro-Atlantic integration, and the formation of the security and defence sector, is to provide the necessary support to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization partner countries so that they can successfully implement the necessary security and defence sector reforms

[123] Based on the interest expressed by the partner countries in closer cooperation on defence and security issues, Latvia is ready to share its experience in the field of national defence reforms with the countries of the South Caucasus and the Western Balkans, Moldova and Ukraine

[124] The main areas of cooperation concern personnel training, consultations with defence experts of partner countries and support in strengthening military capabilities

[125] In order to implement national defence tasks, fulfil commitments to the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the European Union, cooperate with allies and participate in international operations, as well as taking into account changes in the international security situation and the changing and unpredictable nature of security challenges, national defence financing is envisaged at 2% of the gross domestic product

[126] At the meeting of the Heads of State and Government of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation in Riga in November 2006, the countries committed to ensuring adequate funding for national defence tasks

[127] Latvia, within the framework of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the European Union, is involved in multinational initiatives and other military capability development activities aimed at the effective use of resources and in line with the national defence development priorities

[128] Action priorities

[129] The development of the national defence system and the strengthening of national defence are taking place in the following directions and in accordance with the following action priorities: a) development of professional service, strengthening the recruitment and selection process, continuing to increase the professional growth of soldiers and strengthening the prestige of the soldier's profession, establishing an effective training system, ensuring competitive remuneration, social guarantees and construction of housing stock, b) acquisition of armament and equipment appropriate to the tasks to be performed, as well as ensuring appropriate training to increase the combat capabilities of the National Armed Forces units and the safety of soldiers, c) preparation of the National Armed Forces units for participation in international operations, improving their operational experience and supply and support capabilities; Ensuring the readiness of the national armed forces to participate in full-spectrum combat operations of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization with one battalion for up to six months without rotation of this unit, d

[130] Planning and ensuring the participation of the national armed forces in the North Atlantic Treaty Organization rapid reaction forces and the European Union battle group, e) Establishing an inter-institutional cooperation mechanism and a civilian personnel training system to ensure the involvement of civilian personnel and financial resources in post-conflict stabilization and reconstruction measures in international operation areas, as well as effective civil-military cooperation in international operations, f

[131] Development of the host country support system to ensure the reception of allied armed forces in the territory of Latvia, g) Development of the reserve and mobilization system in accordance with the requirements of the modern security situation and the basic tasks of the National Armed Forces, h) Continuing and improving the joint military projects of the Baltic States, which ensure more efficient use of limited resources and improvement of common military capabilities, i

[132] Continuing cooperation between Latvia and the countries of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization in the Baltic Sea Region and the European Union, which contributes to the strengthening of their military capabilities and readiness to participate in international operations, j) providing support to partner countries of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization in adopting the experience of security and defense sector reforms in accordance with the established cooperation priorities, k) ensuring state budget financing for the national defense system in the amount of 2% of the gross domestic product, achieving the most efficient use of funds, l) patriotic education of youth, ensuring the preservation and inheritance of historical memory, perpetuating the evidence of the past and including it in active circulation

[133] Closing questions

[134] The strategic basic principles and guidelines for ensuring national defense and developing military capabilities set out in the Concept have been developed for the medium and long term

[135] Based on the Concept, a National Defense Plan is being developed, which determines specific national defense measures, priorities and necessary resources, as well as the necessary readiness and activity of the institutions and natural and legal persons involved in national defense

[136] Based on the National Defense Plan, development plans for the National Armed Forces are being developed

[137] The implementation of the Concept is ensured by the state institutions of the Republic of Latvia in accordance with their competence and the tasks set out in regulatory enactments

[138] In accordance with the National Security Law, the Concept is reviewed and approved no less than once every four years
