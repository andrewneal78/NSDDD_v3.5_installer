[174] Broad-spectrum influencing Changes in the security environment have made the concepts of hybrid influencing and hybrid warfare a permanent part of Western threat discourse

[178] Broad-spectrum influencing is a threat perception used in the context of Finnish military planning

[179] It includes hybrid influencing, but it also contains the open use of military force

[180] Broad-spectrum influencing is systematic, combines various methods and is often used over a long period of time

[181] Influencing may be difficult to identify, and it exploits society’s vulnerabilities even during normal conditions

[182] The influencing party tries to create circumstances that are advantageous to it, and to further its own goals by using means that are suitable for the current situation

[183] The goal is to shake the foundations of the target country’s defence capability, for example by creating uncertainty among the population, by weakening the people’s will to defend the country, and by weakening the political leadership’s ability to act

[186] Broad-spectrum influencing may include exerting military pressure, draining the resources of the defence system, and those of society as a whole
