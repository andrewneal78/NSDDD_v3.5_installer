The Security Council of the Russian Federation - carries out work on the proactive identification and assessment of threats to the national security of the Russian Federation, promptly prepares draft decisions for the President of the Russian Federation to prevent them, develops proposals in the field of ensuring the national security of the Russian Federation, as well as proposals for clarifying certain provisions of the National Security Concept of the Russian Federation, coordinates the activities of national security forces and bodies, monitors the implementation of decisions in this area by federal executive bodies and executive bodies of the constituent entities of the 09/10/2023, 16:05 National Security Concept of the Russian Federation (approved
