[261] Basic principles of armed forces development

[262] In addition to the military security and risk situation, the budgetary situation is crucial for the provision of the ÖBH's services

[263] Based on the strategic assessment of the situation, the Austrian Armed Forces (ÖBH) must be aligned with priority according to the operational requirements and the likely military tasks that can only be guaranteed by military forces in the sense of a unique selling point

[264] The multi-role capability of units for domestic and international tasks must be fundamentally maintained, with resource-oriented task modularity and gradation

[265] Missions abroad must always be carried out in a multinational context, although evacuation operations in a cooperative environment, humanitarian and disaster relief, and search and rescue missions abroad may be exceptions

[266] Military knowledge must be continuously improved through capability-relevant evaluation of missions to ensure early recognition of future operational requirements

[267] The emphasis of resources must be placed on the operational organization

[268] The freedom of action of the management levels must be increased with the associated strengthening of responsibility, including budget responsibility

[269] International exercises must be used to ensure military interoperability, to further develop our own capabilities and to ensure the best possible preparation for operations both within the EU and within the NATO/PfP framework, including participation in training projects for rapidly available crisis management forces

[270] Capability maintenance and capability development must focus on military operational tasks (domestic and foreign) and their probability of deployment, taking into account the risk and threat profile

[271] The necessary capabilities of the Austrian Armed Forces are to be derived within the framework of a capability-based planning process

[272] Taking into account the available resources and the likelihood of deployment, the future capability spectrum must be adjusted so that there is a clear emphasis on the military tasks that are likely to be deployed, which should enable the maintenance of a high-quality, albeit quantitatively limited, capability portfolio due to resource constraints

[273] By clearly focusing on defined strengths, which also take international needs into account, a military profile that is as high-quality as possible and quantitatively resource-oriented must be developed

[274] • The qualitative focus of the Austrian Armed Forces' ambition in the land area is the ability to conduct combined arms combat within the framework of the battalion combat group

[275] The brigades are the military home of the battalions and prepare them for their operational tasks

[276] • The comprehensive approach to security must also be implemented at the operational and tactical levels, and the integrated civil-military planning approach must be further improved

[277] The concrete missions, including contributions to the EUBG and to multinational reaction forces, must continue to be used as a driving force for capability development and maintenance, as well as for increasing interoperability

[278] The armed forces of the Austrian Armed Forces must enter into in-depth cooperation with preferred partners

[279] Cooperation with these partners must take place throughout the entire life cycle

[280] This means from conception through capability development to deployment

[281] Cooperation and partnerships must be further developed both in the national (especially cross-departmental) and international (bi- and multinational) environment

[282] For this purpose, appropriate portfolios must be created while maintaining the obligation of neutrality

[283] In the regional context and in international organizations, a military foreign and cooperation portfolio provides the framework for this

[284] Cooperation should primarily lead to the supplementation of missing capabilities, the targeted use of resources and cost optimization within the Austrian Armed Forces, as well as to the improvement of civil-military cooperation capabilities and interoperability

[286] The Austrian Armed Forces (ÖBH) will consequently participate in selected projects in the course of future joint procurements and thus also act as a door opener for Austrian small and medium-sized enterprises

[294] The focus is on those stand-alone competencies that the Austrian Armed Forces must necessarily maintain or develop

[306] Human Capital and Defense Policy

[307] An essential basis for a socially broadly anchored and accepted Austrian Armed Forces (ÖBH) is an attractive, modern and operationally oriented basic military service

[308] The fitness system, training and service operations of conscripts must be designed and further developed in accordance with the measures contained in the report on the reform of military service in such a way that, on the one hand, they correspond to the changed security policy framework conditions and, on the other hand, they take the abilities and interests of young citizens into account as best as possible and have a meaningful and motivating effect on them

[309] This must be done in such a way that, even during basic military service, different and, if possible, concrete contributions to Austria's security are increasingly made, and that those serving in the basic military service can also derive personal benefits for their later lives from their time with the Austrian Armed Forces

[310] In addition, they should be specifically motivated to pursue a temporary or militia career after completing their basic military service

[311] In this sense, basic military service must be further reformed and evaluated annually, in accordance with the report on the reform of military service

[312] The militia, with its adapted readiness, is an essential part of the organizational structure of the Austrian Armed Forces

[313] According to the report on the reform of military service, it is to play an important and supporting role within the overall organisation of the Austrian Armed Forces (OBH) within the framework of the implementation of the reform of military service

[314] In order to make it more attractive, the structured militia in particular should be given a clear basic mandate

[315] This is also intended to achieve a regional connection and improved identification of the militia soldiers with their respective tasks

[316] The assignment of the structured militia is intended to take into account a strong link with the presence organization and a corresponding "military home

[317] Militia soldiers should, in principle, continue to participate in all operational tasks, with the emphasis being on fulfilling domestic tasks that require a large number of forces with, in some cases, a high level of sustainability (particularly in the context of security police assistance or assistance in disaster relief) or special expertise (e.g., in the context of cyber defense or NBC defense

[318] Furthermore, the militia will be used to ensure the sustainability of peacekeeping operations and to contribute its civil-military expertise within the framework of international military security cooperation

[319] Regular exercises as well as measures for operational preparation and improvement of equipment are to be provided in light of the above-mentioned premises of “military home”, “regional relevance”, “basic mission” and strong “interlinkage with the presence organisation”

[320] People are the greatest strength and most valuable asset of the Austrian Armed Forces

[321] Their quality, professionalism and motivation are crucial for the fulfilment of the ÖBH's missions and tasks

[322] Building on previous achievements, innovative measures must be further developed in the spirit of a modern corporate culture

[323] The training system in the Austrian Armed Forces (ÖBH) should be increasingly geared to distinguishing between the necessary educational level and subject-specific training

[324] The effects of the time course must be taken into account

[325] The training to be carried out by the Austrian Armed Forces itself must concentrate on the core military area

[326] Any additional requirements must be met through cooperation with national and international educational institutions to ensure the necessary level of education

[327] Particular attention should be paid to academic qualifications in accordance with the principle of lifelong learning and to improving opportunities for transition (vocational development measures) into the civilian labour market

[328] The service for women in the Austrian Armed Forces is to be made more attractive through, among other things, specific improvements in training, consistent career planning and targeted support in the allocation of leadership and management positions

[329] The long-term goal is to increase the proportion of female soldiers to 10 percent

[330] The further development of the Austrian Armed Forces is also to be pursued in the area of service and salary law, which should take into account the requirements of the military profession

[331] The specific psychological and physical stresses, especially those associated with periods spent abroad, must also be taken into account
