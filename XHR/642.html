[1633] Conclusions for the development of the Bundeswehr

[1634] The Bundeswehr&#39;s need for adaptation stems from the necessity of effectively building up its core crisis management capability, ensuring the meaningful fulfillment of compulsory military service, and gaining planning certainty in light of scarce resources

[1635] Mission and resources must be aligned

[1636] Task-oriented, targeted, and demanding training and the operation of the armed forces will be ensured

[1637] Furthermore, the national capability to participate in international crisis management and peacekeeping missions and to provide humanitarian aid will be improved

[1638] The Bundeswehr will be further developed as an organic, integrated system

[1639] Priorities will be set that reflect the Bundeswehr&#39;s changing tasks and initially concern the core military capabilities required for these tasks

[1640] Key elements include command and strategic reconnaissance capabilities, long-range mobility for deploying and supplying force contingents via sea and air transport, general supply and communication capabilities, and effective missile defense

[1641] Realizing these capabilities requires inter-service approaches, and multinational solutions are necessary for development, procurement, and operation

[1642] Existing gaps in military capabilities, which are crucial for effective contributions to crisis management, must be closed jointly within the alliances

[1643] NATO and the WEU are therefore seeking common solutions

[1644] The European states allied in NATO and the WEU cannot afford to continue developing multiple battle tanks, helicopters, aircraft, frigates, and submarines independently, thereby competing with one another

[1645] They all face the same fundamental problem: less and different equipment is needed than before, and all have fewer available resources

[1646] This situation demands joint solutions more urgently than ever

[1647] The financial resources, performance, length of military service, size, and structure of the Bundeswehr (German Armed Forces) are closely interrelated

[1648] Changing one factor always alters the entire Bundeswehr system

[1649] Eliminating individual deficiencies can exacerbate the situation elsewhere and does not necessarily improve the overall situation of the armed forces

[1650] Individual adjustments can therefore only be made within a holistic approach that always considers all factors

[1681] Operational Cost Limitation

[1682] In March 1993, the Federal Minister of Defense established the working group &quot;Operational Cost Limitation.&quot; This group has since developed proposals on how operational costs within the armed forces and parts of the Federal Armed Forces administration can be limited or reduced in order to save budgetary resources and thus free them up for urgently needed investments

[1683] The working group has determined that there is considerable potential for savings

[1684] Realizing this potential in a cost-effective manner requires a two-pronged approach: On the one hand, it is essential to develop business management principles even more strongly than before, down to the individual units and formations

[1685] On November 9, 1993, the Federal Minister of Defense therefore set the necessary course for this long-term process

[1686] He ordered the development of a &quot;system for cost and performance accountability&quot; intended to create transparency and unleash the troops&#39; creativity for optimization

[1687] In doing so, the Bundeswehr is following the path of large industrial companies, which see the greatest potential for rationalization in a gradual but continuous improvement of performance and the decentralization of responsibility

[1688] On the other hand, it is important to promote competition, both within the Bundeswehr and with industry

[1689] In the British Armed Forces, this approach is being tested under the term &quot;market testing.&quot; Since mid-1993, the Federal Ministry of Defense has been engaged in a dialogue with the German defense industry regarding privatization solutions

[1690] The resulting comparison of industrial services with those provided by the Bundeswehr has yielded proposals for cost reduction from the armaments sector, particularly the naval arsenal, and from within the troops themselves

[1691] In line with this approach, the Army, Air Force, and Navy are examining possibilities for better utilizing logistical and technical facilities and reducing their capacity through centralization and the elimination of redundant resources

[1692] All considerations are based on the premise that the changed security situation allows for further cadre development of logistics units, the transfer of tasks to industry, and the consolidation of supplies and logistics facilities

[1693] The aim is to improve the current ratio of investments to operating expenses in favor of investments in order to enable the restructuring of the Bundeswehr

[1694] For this to happen, it is necessary that funds generated internally through cost-cutting and rationalization measures can be used for investment

[1695] The future size of the crisis response forces, the greater differentiation of the main defense forces, the rationalization of operations, and the streamlining of structures open up options for the future internal structures of the armed forces, personnel levels, and deployment

[1827] CHAPTER 5 Cost Management and Use of Civilian Capacities

[1828] The Bundeswehr&#39;s weapon systems must be developed, procured, and used at reasonable costs

[1829] Effective cost management and a set of modern management tools, especially for measuring progress and controlling costs, are essential for this

[1830] A key element is a concept for minimizing the life-cycle costs of military equipment

[1831] This concept stipulates that costs are considered on an equal footing with other parameters, such as operational and technical requirements and implementation timeframes

[1832] All costs incurred by the weapon system throughout its entire life cycle must be taken into account

[1833] The data and cost reporting procedure agreed upon in 1990 between the Federation of German Industries (BDI) and the Federal Ministry of Defense (BMVg) provides the framework for monitoring performance, time, and costs

[1834] This cost monitoring, together with cost transparency in projects and the ability to reliably estimate costs, forms the basis for continuously minimizing costs and preventing project failures

[1835] A needs-based and efficient controlling system built upon this foundation makes it possible to comply with the framework conditions across projects and to plan, monitor, and control all projects appropriately

[1836] Costs can also be limited by the increasing use of civilian technology in the armed forces

[1837] In some sectors of military equipment, the succession of generations is influenced by increasingly rapid and significant leaps in innovation in civilian technology

[1838] This is particularly true for command, communication, and reconnaissance systems

[1839] In the future, systems or components of civilian technology will be increasingly used in the armed forces

[1840] The Bundeswehr thus benefits from the innovative strength and competition of the civilian markets
