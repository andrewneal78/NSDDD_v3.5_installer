[152] An obvious corollary of this is ensuring resources at an appropriate level

[153] The components of integrated defense resource management are: human resource management, financial resource management, defense procurement management, research, development and innovation system management, defense infrastructure management. The economic developments in recent years and the financial difficulties at European level have highlighted the need to rethink the way resources are allocated, but especially their use, efficiency being the main criterion

[154] At national, allied and EU levels, the opportunity to develop multinational approaches in the field of defense ("Smart Defence", "Connected Forces Initiative", "Framework Nation Concept", respectively "Pooling and Sharing") was highlighted, which involve the adoption of innovative solutions in the optimal management of resources, by prioritizing investments and multinational financing of capabilities of common interest, all with the objective of increasing the security of the member states

[157] improving the activity planning process, especially by achieving a better correlation with the available resources and the estimation of the resource requirements; * improving the system of recording and allocating resources, with an emphasis on material and financial ones; * ensuring a balanced proportion between the expenses for the provision of goods and services and personnel so as to ensure a fair balance, according to the models in the NATO space; * continuing to develop capabilities within the multinational framework provided by NATO and the EU by using multinational initiatives in the field of defense ("Framework Nation Concept", "Smart Defence", "Connected Forces Initiative", respectively "Pooling and Sharing"); * adopting common endowment programs for several categories of forces and developing those capabilities that have dual-use capability (civilian and military
