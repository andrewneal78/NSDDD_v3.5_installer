[895] ZAKONCZENIE CONCLUSIONS The main objective in the restructuring of a national defence system is the ability to overcome the geopolitical as well as technological challenges which the XXI century brings

[896] The aim of the Government of the Republic of Poland is to build for today and for the future an effective well commanded and managed, appropriately trained and equipped modern Armed Forces

[897] An effective and efficient National Defence System has to defend our country against threats which may appear in the near and distant future

[898] The goals imposed on the Armed Forces are intended to define a clear vision of their role in the changing world

[899] An appropriate long-term perspective is a necessity as decisions made today regarding the type, structure and equipment of the Armed Forces must define their capabilities for at least the next two decades

[900] The excessive attachment to classic Armed Forces activities and the form and conditions of their achievement show ignorance of the dynamic trend in the changing nature and location of military activity and armed combat

[901] It is normal that after the end of one war most armies start preparing for the next war; the concept of this is based on experience of the past war

[902] Analysing the shortfalls of past operations, accompanying successes and failures and improve methods and means, as if history was to be repeated and as if once again the same goals and targets were to be achieved under similar conditions

[903] In answer to the question, what should be done today to ensure our ability to function efficiently tomorrow

[904] We should start by stating that, in the contemporary world power and authority will be to a large degree, and increasingly commonly not only connected with threat avoidance or forming capabilities to oppose them but also in the creation and exploitation of chances

[905] It is this characteristic that today takes on a significant meaning in the face of confrontation with the emerging image of reality formed by dynamic, political, economical, social, cultural and religious, and maybe especially - military transformation

[906] The Armed Forces must possess the ability to adapt to new solutions in the field of tactics and strategy as well as to new technological and educational solutions

[907] On analyzing on one hand the military operations which took place in the not too distant past in various parts of the world and on the other - the development of the art of operation and above all the destruction means, one can and should expect vital changes in the way future wars are conducted

[909] The greater efficiency of modern weapons will demand increased emphasis on mobility, force dispersal and camouflage as well as on the pace of conducted operations

[910] The maintenance of a higher tempo than the enemy helps to seize the initiative during war operations

[911] The dispersal of forces requires the employment of secure and reliable communications and cooperation

[912] The synchronization of operations gains importance

[913] There follows an increasing importance on the role and possibility of independent action by small units with a simultaneous increase of requirements concerning their capability for integration

[914] These trends can only become a reality, as well as the necessary equipment, if the Armed Forces have professionally trained and well prepared personnel
