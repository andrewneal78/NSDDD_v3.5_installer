General Considerations Defense is a pure public good; the resources to invest in it are finite and must be balanced with the other needs of the State. A pure public good is understood to be one whose consumption is indivisible and which can be shared by all members of a community without exception. The generation, development, and maintenance of strategic capabilities is a medium- and long-term process that requires the investment of economic resources

[615] The determination of the level and type of strategic capabilities that the country requires, as well as the degree of operational readiness of the means, occurs within a framework defined by the security environment, the possible employment scenarios and the resources that the State has available to invest in Defense

[616] Understood as the temporary condition that enables a capacity to effectively and efficiently develop the operations inherent to it

[720] In this regard, the Policy has defined the Mission Areas as including the defense of sovereignty and territorial integrity, security and territorial interests in our country, international cooperation and support for foreign policy, participation in the national emergency and civil protection system, and, finally, contribution to national development and support for civil authorities

[723] B. Political Leadership of Defense as a Military Instrument As defined above, Defense provides the military instrument of the State for its integrated use in a national strategy, together with other instruments of power and influence, directed by the President of the Republic

[724] In this regard, it is necessary to update, institutionalize, and maintain the mechanisms for providing ongoing advice and support to the President of the Republic in the decision-making process regarding matters affecting national security, where Defense is one of the components that must act in a highly integrated and coordinated manner with the rest of the State's capabilities and instruments of power

[725] The political management of the military instrument, as part of national strategic management, is intended to achieve both deterrence and the attainment of political objectives in an international crisis or conflict, in addition to the preparation and effective and efficient use of the Armed Forces

[726] in all other Mission Areas which, in the case of Chile, are essential for its security and development

[727] This has highlighted the role of the Minister of Defense as the President of the Republic's direct collaborator in the use of military instruments as part of a national strategy

[728] The Minister of National Defense has also assumed, by delegation of the President, the role of guiding, organizing, and arranging the operational use of the Armed Forces in normal times

[729] for different tasks, which represents an evolving organizational and institutional challenge

[730] The senior ministerial leadership in this area has materialized through plans, directives, and ministerial instructions in the areas of employment of force in crisis situations, emergencies, international cooperation, peacekeeping operations, Antarctic operations, cyber defense, and support to other civilian or police authorities, which guide the ongoing planning process in these areas

[731] In recent years, the capacity for joint strategic leadership has been strengthened in the planning, execution, and control phases for employment scenarios in different Mission Areas

[732] This, in turn, has helped guide the definition of strategic capabilities, the planning of force development, and the programming of defense investments

[733] Notwithstanding the progress made in recent years, the challenge of continuing to deepen the joint vision and leadership of the Armed Forces has been taken on
