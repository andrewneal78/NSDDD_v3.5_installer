[500] The process will involve the Ministries of Government and Foreign Affairs to articulate the three components of security: public security, foreign policy, and defense policy

[501] As part of a democratic culture, emphasis will be placed on strengthening civil-military relations, ensuring military subordination to legally constituted political power, incorporating civil society into the dialogue process to raise awareness of security and defense in Ecuadorian society, increasing participation in the formulation of public policies, ensuring transparency and accountability in defense management, and broadly disseminating the importance of building a consensual Defense Policy as a State Policy

[502] Regarding the internal conflict in the sister Republic of Colombia, the following premises will be considered: non-intervention in the internal affairs of the Colombian State and the non-militarization of Ecuador's foreign policy

[503] The National Defense Agenda has a 16-month time horizon, from September 2005 to December 2006, and will be adjusted to the following priorities: Updating the legal framework of the Armed Forces with special attention to the Organic Law, Personnel Law, and ISSFA Law

[504] • Evaluation and Update of the National Defense Policy

[505] • Curricular reform of the Military Education System with priority given to the implementation of the “Plan to strengthen institutional values and democratic culture.”

[506] • Updating the Joint Military Doctrine and strengthening the Military Intelligence System

[507] • Institutional strengthening and improvement of the operational, logistical, and administrative capacity of the Armed Forces. • Strengthening Social Assistance and Welfare for Military Personnel

[508] FINAL PROVISION The Minister of National Defense is entrusted with the coordination of inter-institutional actions for the fulfillment of the National Government's Defense Agenda

[509] The Minister of National Defense is empowered to issue complementary and clarifying regulations, and also to promulgate specific guidelines that develop this Defense Directive
