[7] PREFACE

[8] Our Legislation defines National Defense as a set of civil and military activities directed at the sovereignty and Independence of our country, the security of the territory and its strategic resources, as well as the Republic, within the framework of the Constitution and going to generate the conditions for the future well-being of the population

[9] It covers a large dimension and requires medium and long term, which implies a political support, characterizing the National Defense of the State

[10] Its fundamental instruments are: the Diplomacy, Public Security and Military Defense

[11] National Defense must be conceived and conducted by the Executive Branch, through: the Council of Ministers, the Presidency of the Republic, the Ministries of Foreign Affairs, Economy and Finance, National Defense, and the International; and the Legislative Branch, thus ensuring institutional balance

[12] In the process of design, implementation, execution and evaluation of public policies on National Defense, all the actors mentioned interact, in addition to those specifically involved

[13] This National Defense Policy contributes to achieving the objectives of economic and social development

[14] For this to be possible, the following are necessary: to achieve adequate educational, scientific-technological and industrial development in our country that allows for the generation of democratic strengthening, social equity, economic and financial stability, an adequate distribution of income and a situation of long-term trade balance

[15] All of the above leads to strong coordination between the National Defense Policy and the National Development Policy

[16] Although the National Defense Policy, as a Public Policy, is implemented by the State, the broadest possible participation and awareness among social actors must be encouraged

[17] Only with a strong understanding by all Uruguayans of the meaning and importance of the development process of our National Defense can it be successfully achieved in achieving its objectives

[18] Thus, citizen involvement and participation in the implementation of the country's defense, in its many forms, are a fundamental condition for facing present and future challenges

[32] INTRODUCTION The National Defense Policy is characterized according to art

[33] rd of National Defense No. 18,650, as public policy

[34] According to its own standard, it must promote broad agreements and must comply with the general principles of international law in coordination with the foreign policy of the various social groups. In this sense, it is understood as public policies and actions that the government plans and develops, together, empowered by the regulatory framework, which preserve the conditions in which the economy develops

[35] Furthermore, the plans pursue national objectives and interests; therefore, they must be designed with a view to adequately informing society and, consequently, ensuring public protection

[36] The National Defense Policy has as its purpose the State in matters of National Defense, concepts contained in the Defense Framework Law unanimously by all political parties with respect to the matter, politically agreeing on existing resources and effectively managing the National Interests that are in this document; It constitutes the highest level of government document, the process of planning of National Defense, by which political ideas and strategies are established, seeking to develop the capabilities that are necessary to achieve the objectives set out

[37] • This Policy is based on the principles, values and characteristics of the Uruguayan State, validated with the broadest political and social support

[38] age as a whole, to constitute an authentic State Policy

[39] The above introduces the National Defense Policy within the compliance of the national and international regulatory framework and links it with the objectives and priorities of the Government

[40] It is determined as multisectoral and multidimensional, highlighting the importance of foresight, planning and coordination between the different State agencies

[41] The National Defense Policy, as a public policy, also tends to reduce or eliminate vulnerabilities and, through them, mitigate risks and threats, which is why it is closely linked to the identification of our national interests

[42] In this sense, these characteristics link National Defense with a vision that extends beyond government periods, as well as the establishment of priorities for the historical moment and the analysis of existing and planned resources

[43] The participation of society becomes a highly valuable element in carrying out State action, since it contributes to obtaining accurate and up-to-date information, channels additional economic and human resources to public resources, provides social support for policies, and allows for greater capacity for public management

[44] To this end, the development of a culture of "National Defense" within Uruguayan society is essential to raise awareness about the importance of issues related to our sovereignty and territorial integrity, National Interests and Objectives

[45] The National Defense Policy, as a public policy, strengthens democracy, the rule of law, and the constitutional mandate regarding the realization of human rights—civil, political, economic, social, and cultural—based on the principle of equality

[46] Therefore it is generated and executed democratically

[47] The Law determines to plan and guide the State's foreign policy in accordance with compliance with international law, with the guidelines provided by the norm

[48] the principles of self-determination of peoples, preservation of peace, non-intervention in the internal affairs of other nations, peaceful settlement of disputes and cooperation among States

[49] Diplomatic action is established as the first instrument for conflict resolution

[50] The National Defense Policy then presents such characteristics in compliance with the basic principles of international law and lays the foundations for the exercise of the right to self-defense, since Article 4 of Law 18,650 establishes that the use of force is reserved only for cases of "military aggression", so that and according to the mandate of the article itself, "all deterrent and preventive means that are appropriate" may be exercised, which will be determined through the military defense policy

[51] The nature of current threats and the high degree of uncertainty that exists, resulting from the speed with which changes occur, require emphasis on analysis and the rapid response capacity of the different sectors of the State

[52] To achieve this rapid response, there are underlying characteristics such as versatility, interoperability, sustainability, and mobility of the instruments used

[53] The formulation of this document was structured on the basis of different phases

[54] The first phase consisted of describing the global, regional and national context from which the declaration of our National Interests and the Objectives of National Defense are derived

[55] The second phase involves defining the threats and risks that arise in achieving the defined objectives, based on the previously developed strategic context

[56] In the third phase, the strategic guidelines that will allow the achievement of the established objectives are defined

[57] While no immediate conflicts affecting the integrity of our territory are perceived, given the geopolitical and strategic context in which our country finds itself, disputes or pressures of various kinds cannot be ruled out in the future, in order to preserve our national interests

[58] In this sense, and in light of this reality, it is necessary to emphasize that National Defense constitutes a public good, an essential, permanent, non-delegable, and integral function of the State, in whose implementation the energies and resources of society as a whole converge in a coordinated manner

[59] The challenge is for all Uruguayans

[60] URUGUAY: SECURITY AND DEFENSE The Uruguayan State is constituted by its territory, its people and has the same independence in its foreign relations

[61] He is the only one who exercises the legitimate monopoly of the available means to enforce the established legislation and order. In Uruguay, the definitions and scope of the Seguri address the conceptual and institutional dimensions, coexistence of different levels, forms of application and the State, where specific systems and practices are deployed and of other actors of the national reality

[62] Security, in general terms, is understood as the necessary and indispensable guarantees for all its members against threats of any nature. Defense is an act or set of acts carried out to ensure or conditions that provide the feeling of Security

[63] The concept of Security addressed aspects of public defense, the practice and institutional development of our country, progressively expanding its areas in different ways, typical of processes at the national level

[64] Currently, and in accordance with the provisions of our legislation, particularly the National Defense Framework Law No

[65] the concept is captured in the concept of National Defense, which encompasses all civil and military activities aimed at preserving the sovereignty and independence of our country, preserving the integrity of the territory and its strategic resources, as well as the peace of the Republic, within the framework of the Constitution and the laws; contributing to generating the conditions for the present and future social well-being of the population

[66] National Defense constitutes a right and a duty of all citizens, in the manner and terms established in the Constitution of the Republic and in the laws

[67] It is a public good, an essential, permanent, non-delegable and integral function of the State

[68] Its implementation involves the coordinated use of the energies and resources of society as a whole

[69] Military Defense is an essential function of the State linked to guaranteeing and maintaining its sovereignty and the integrity of its population, territory, strategic resources, and institutions; it encompasses security issues related to the external sphere, in terms of the strategic environment of our country's foreign policy, and is configured as the specific and exclusive space for organization and

[70] use of the State's military force, based on risks or threats to its very integrity and independent and sovereign existence

[71] National legislation has consolidated the principles of leadership, governance, civil leadership and democratic control of the Armed Forces, generating an institutional system with hierarchies of political decision-making and operational action, tending towards the joint action of its components, endowed with a budget that must correspond to the functions and missions provided for in its legal and institutional frameworks, under the responsibility of the Ministry of National Defense

[72] For Uruguay, Public Security includes all instruments

[73] that the State has to ensure the normal development of social life and prevent the rights of individuals from being violated

[74] In the performance of this State function, in its missions and political and technical coordination responsibilities, public security systems are established, with the operational element being the National Police, whose actions are limited to the territory, which is regulated by its own legal framework and endowed with a specific position

[75] exceptional character

[76] The participation of the Forces is contemplated, in support of public security actions, with prior authorization

[77] the political authorities responsible and delimited by laws of justice, established in our Constitution

[78] On the other hand, Regional Security articulates both Public Security and Military Defense, in the understanding that maintaining the territorial integrity, sovereignty and peace of Uruguay requires a scenario of stability and balance in interstate relations, understood as international or regional security, which requires from our country and reciprocally from other States, a willingness to cooperate in their strategic environment

[79] Uruguay considers that risks and threats to its sovereignty, territorial integrity and the peace of the Republic may come from outside its own borders, so Public Security and Military Defense are related to security aspects related to the regional and international environment, constituting a privileged area for interstate cooperation in integration processes, with the objective of safeguarding its own stability and security and that of the region as a common space

[80] Uruguay believes that regional cooperation and integration in the Military Defense and Public Security sectors must advance on the basis of recognition and respect for national differences and particularities, while exploring the formulation of common elements to achieve approaches that enable regional concurrence for the construction of spaces for cooperation and complementarity

[81] THE STRATEGIC SCENARIO GENERALITIES The strategic scenario, at the Global, Regional and National levels, is characterized in the first two by instability and the reduced degree of certainty, where different power relations are identified at the international level, a strengthening of the interdependence between States, the emergence of new non-state actors with influence, the appearance of new technologies, opportunities and threats, among many other factors that intervene in this reality that we face

[82] THE INTERNATIONAL CONTEXT Its Description The International Context is characterized by high uncertainty and a deterioration of expectations for sustainable development, where several poles of power and decision coexist, with the emergence of new actors that participate in defining the balance of power in the world

[83] We observe an international environment with countries where their political borders are more flexible and economies are integrated, strengthening relationships that generate interdependence

[84] Thus, in this environment, the concept of cooperation and the relationship between the Blocs acquires relevance, building spheres of influence throughout the world, actively participating in conflict mediation, shaping markets and transforming cultures

[85] The global economy is undergoing a critical structural process, which erupted in 2008 and has had developed countries as its main stage

[86] The main causes of the crisis are the international financial actors who generated it through speculation with highly complex financial products or by attacking the most indebted banking systems in Europe

[87] Additionally, the global system is very vulnerable to a decline in the growth rate of the Chinese economy, given that if it were to converge to the global average, the system would experience a severe demand crisis

[88] Faced with this crisis, in 2014, the timid push for financial regulation in the United States of America and the anti-crisis policies in Europe have not yielded the expected results, conditioned by the troika of institutions made up of the European Central Bank, the European Union and the IMF, which have placed emphasis on austerity and not on the growth that some European countries demand to maintain social welfare

[89] As a result, the US economic recovery has slowed, and millions of jobs and the social well-being of large sectors of the European population have been sacrificed to maintain low inflation

[90] Within this global framework, our region's performance has been diverse: while South America has enjoyed good growth, the rest of Latin America (due to its highly dependent trade integration on the US market) has suffered to a greater extent

[91] These vulnerabilities represent a serious threat to the region

[92] Hence, strengthening regional integration processes, on the one hand, and ties with other emerging powers, on the other, is an unavoidable strategic commitment

[93] While global competition is emerging, defined by access to advanced technologies and new markets, located based on low labor costs and the possibility of access to raw materials and other natural resources of strategic value, there are also processes of international cooperation between emerging powers

[94] The country faces a politically, socially, economically, culturally, and demographically diverse world, with the possibility that opportunities and threats may simultaneously exist within and outside the country

[95] New technologies and the interconnection of communication networks open the way to a strong development potential, becoming a central issue for education and information

[96] These tools have also introduced a new dimension in the field of security and defense, generating vulnerabilities that can interrupt or condition the normal functioning of a State

[97] On the other hand, the dimensions of power include, in addition to military might, economic productivity, participation in the global market, technological innovation, access to strategic natural resources, as well as intangible factors such as national will and diplomatic skill

[98] The interaction of the different actors at a global level with each other and with the elements that make up the various dimensions of power, determine a complex reality vulnerable to various threats that require timely evaluation

[99] These threats are not, in general, fully controlled by the countries of the region in isolation; they can be large-scale, multidimensional, and transnational in nature

[100] Human trafficking, arms and drug trafficking, terrorism, border security, and the preservation of strategic natural resources constitute, among others, problems that require addressing with a strategy of regional and international cooperation that allows for the creation of specific areas, clearly delimiting the fields of security and defense

[101] The world's population continues to grow, with the urban population growing more than the rural population, with the consequent increase in poverty and social inequality, where the disturbing evidence of having to face major challenges is particularly evident

[102] There is a growing perception of future shortages of natural resources and food on the planet, which will undoubtedly test the various instruments of national defense in each country

[103] Human behavior has generated progressive climate change, which is already significantly affecting the global hydrological cycle, causing health, social, environmental, and geopolitical consequences

[104] On the other hand, the invocation of fundamentalisms continues to be one of the central elements in conflicts, as well as the multiplication of violence

[105] Isolated from the rest of the world, the Arctic and Antarctica are decisive objectives, due to the potential use of their strategic natural resources and the new navigation prospects, a product of the progressi

[106] I thaw the white spaces

[107] The increase in global energy consumption has generated the need to transition from fossil fuels to renewables, seeking to diversify energy sources to meet the development of domestic and industrial activities of societies

[108] The debate on nuclear energy is still ongoing, considering that many countries are seeking to satisfy their energy needs through this means

[109] Additionally, the use of nuclear energy for military purposes remains at the center of the global political agenda

[110] • THE REGIONAL CONTEXT URUGUAY'S TRENDS AND CHALLENGES IN THE REGION Signs of slowdown or a reduction in the activity levels of their economies are observed in the most relevant actors of the subcontinent, due to domestic problems, the cooling of some economies of global actors, the downward correction of the raw materials markets and the intensification of tensions in the financial markets

[111] But beyond economic aspects, political or ideological differences between the countries in the region, Uruguay observes that there is a general awareness that regional development will be achieved if there is progress towards greater integration in productive, energy, economic and commercial infrastructure, as well as by deepening cooperation in the social, scientific-technological, cultural, military and, fundamentally, political-institutional areas

[112] The incorporation of defense issues into regional integration processes requires us to rethink National Defense in this new context

[113] At the hemispheric level, Uruguay believes it is necessary to promote and contribute to the debate regarding the current performance and relevance of the Inter-American Defense System, of which it is a member, particularly the Inter-American Defense Board, the Inter-American Defense College, and the Inter-American Treaty of Reciprocal Assistance

[114] This debate should take place within the framework of the Organization of American States, as suggested to the General Assembly of that organization

[115] Our country envisions a Latin American framework ideal for preserving peace and security, based on defense cooperation, marked by democratic institutions and the role of defense as a tool at the service of peace and global development

[116] The ultimate goal of this process is to coordinate among all Latin American nations the most appropriate instruments and mechanisms that will allow us to consolidate mutually beneficial understanding and cooperation in matters of defense

[117] The existence of the South American Defense Council (CDS) within the framework of the Union of South American Nations (UNASUR) creates a space for political dialogue and coordination for the region's defense ministries, while also enabling the development of common regional agendas in this area of public policy

[118] The CDS is considered of high geopolitical value due to its strategic impact on the preservation of strategic natural resources and biodiversity and the consolidation of South America as a zone of peace and cooperation

[119] The creation of the Center for Strategic Defense Studies is another important space for thinking about regional defense in a combined manner, in an environment of mutual trust

[120] In this process of regional integration and cooperation, the CDS has defined the creation of a regional military industry as relevant, as a way of acquiring greater autonomy, which entails the need to develop new technologies in South America

[121] Participation in the development of cooperation in this regard is revealed as key for our country

[122] Within the peaceful nature of the nations of the American continent, Uruguay expresses its willingness to promote peace and cooperation in the South Atlantic region, including the space between Africa and Latin America, where borders should not be elements of separation between countries or peoples, but rather factors that facilitate unity, brotherhood, and the development of the coastal nations

[123] Our work in the South Atlantic Zone of Peace and Cooperation not only encompasses the pursuit of sustainable development through human rights and the right to development, but also the necessary governance of natural resources to ensure that their use truly benefits our peoples

[124] It also includes special considerations for Africa, which once again calls on us to be creative in the search for formulas for South-South cooperation aimed at achieving genuine access to development, as an essential condition for peace and security

[125] THE NATIONAL CONTEXT: URUGUAY THE VALIDITY OF ITS GEOPOLITICAL VALUE The Eastern Republic of Uruguay is a country with an extensive coastline, maintaining in its capital the core of predominance not only geoeconomically, but also geohistorically, geopolitically and geostrategically

[126] The Río de la Plata basin is presented as the gateway to the vital core of the Southern Cone

[127] Our country enjoys a privileged regional position with respect to the internal watersheds, ensuring its access to the external basin of the Río de la Plata, shared with Argentina, both for maritime trade and for the exploitation of its natural resources

[128] It has various exit options to the Atlantic Ocean, which constitute access to maritime communication lines for its full development

[129] Although it lacks direct access to the Pacific Ocean, the country is part of a bioceanic corridor, which presents an opportunity for economic and social development

[130] The country's condition of relative geographical, economic and demographic dimensions means that it does not have a strong "Presence" in the International Community, but it does have significant prestige and recognition for its positions in the international system, as well as its value for its strategic position in the region, that is, a country with "Positioning

[131] The full exercise of state sovereignty over national land, air, sea, river, and lake spaces requires certain levels of presence in them

[132] The land space does not present major difficulties, notwithstanding the exceptional existence of areas with little or no population and a lack of public services

[133] Air, sea, river, and lake spaces allow for a greater level of complexity in terms of their surveillance and control; the latter in particular require special attention due to the necessary protection and proper use of the resources they contain

[134] National Defense planning is particularly oriented toward the most sensitive strategic areas and those with a wealth of strategic natural resources

[135] It not only includes its territorial integrity and the free exercise of the rights of jurisdiction and sovereignty in the land, sea and air spaces of the Uruguayan State, but also in other spaces of National Interest, such as Antarctica

[136] It also covers those land, air and sea spaces for which the State has acquired responsibility before the International Community

[137] URUGUAY AND THE WORLD

[138] Uruguay has positive recognition in the regional and international context, constituting a privileged connection with the region in the world as a result of its political and economic stability, in addition to its outstanding international geographical and political position

[139] Strict compliance with the general principles of International Law, such as the self-determination of peoples, the peaceful settlement of disputes and non-intervention in the internal affairs of States as guidelines of the State's Foreign Policy, clearly define its defensive position

[140] Diplomatic action constitutes the first instrument for conflict resolution

[141] In exercising the right to self-defense, enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, the country reserves the right to use force in cases of military aggression, without prejudice to exercising all appropriate deterrent measures

[142] Democracy and respect for human rights are the guiding principles of our daily foreign policy development, and their affirmation is our ongoing responsibility

[143] The State has a strong commitment to supporting the international community, with early participation in international conferences, as well as in the former League of Nations and as a founding member of the United Nations

[144] Uruguay is integrated into the international community through international organizations such as the UN, the OAS, and UNASUR, with which it has assumed responsibilities related to security and defense

[145] Our country's international projection is manifested primarily through diplomatic action and through its policy of participating in UN peacekeeping missions, as a commitment to the collective security established in the United Nations Charter

[146] Our country's foreign policy gives high priority to relations with neighboring countries and will continue to do so

[147] In this sense, MERCOSUR and UNASUR are its main areas of integration

[148] Thus, strengthening the regional bloc becomes an unavoidable strategic priority, and it is of utmost importance to promote it as a platform to enhance the country's capacity for external integration

[149] In order to achieve the National Objectives, it is necessary to continue efforts aimed at strengthening ties with key global players and, in doing so, continue promoting the diversification of Uruguay's political, trade, and cooperation relations with the world

[150] Likewise, there is a strong commitment to promoting and disseminating national cultural values around the world, as well as to connecting Uruguayans abroad

[151] Finally, Uruguay is a signatory to the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, with the exception of the use of nuclear technology as an economic good for peaceful purposes

[152] PUBLIC SAFETY For Uruguay

[153] Public Security includes all the instruments available to the State to ensure the normal development of life in society and prevent the violation of people's rights

[154] It is a social necessity, indispensable for human coexistence and an essential task, and as such, a non-delegable responsibility of the State which, through the Ministry of the Interior as the governing body of public security policies, calls on society as a whole to get involved and participate in the formulation and implementation of specific policies that improve the quality of society and coexistence throughout the national territory

[155] According to this vision, it is not an end in itself, but a condition for sustainable human development

[156] The citizen security promoted by the State combines the actions of the National Police, as a civil and public force dedicated to the prevention, deterrence, and suppression of crime, with the design and implementation of social policies that promote the deepest integration of society

[157] Public safety, understood in this way, is reflected in the ongoing actions of the National Police as a civil, hierarchical, and professional force that ensures the rule of law throughout the country

[158] This police action is a complex process that ranges from the design and execution of intelligence activities that allow for the evaluation and anticipation of threats to social peace, with coordination at the ministerial level of coexistence policies that articulate with society through the political and social institutions that strengthen it

[159] On the current geopolitical map, international criminal organizations possess sufficient influence and economic power to destabilize governments and substantially affect societies

[160] In this scenario, public security faces increasingly complex challenges. In addition to traditional missions such as controlling the movement of goods and people, there is also the need to monitor organizations operating globally for the purposes of illegal trafficking of substances and people, tax evasion, various economic and cybercrimes, and theft of sensitive information affecting national interests

[161] Combating these higher levels of transnational crime requires the State to develop increasing capacities for international coordination and to continuously improve its material resources and the professional level of its members

[162] During this historical stage, the Executive Branch has systematically and progressively applied the definitions agreed upon with national political forces in the "Consensus Document" generated by the Interparty Commission on Public Security, thus achieving the objective of promoting a genuine state policy

[163] MILITARY DEFENSE The Military Defense of the Country is based on the capacity of its Armed Forces and the potential of mobilizable national resources, in accordance with the provisions of the regulations, particularly the Military Defense Policy

[164] Under the political leadership of the Ministry of National Defense, these are constituted as the organized, equipped, instructed, and trained branch to carry out military acts imposed by National Defense, within the framework of the Constitution and the laws

[165] In times of peace, they may provide services or collaborate in activities that are requested of them due to their specialty, social relevance or public benefit, without this implying detriment to the fulfillment of their fundamental mission

[166] The Constitution of the Republic and the National Defense Framework Law No

[167] They constitute the highest level framework documents related to National Defense and Military Defense in particular

[168] Military Defense being an integral part of National Defense, it is also a right and a duty of all citizens, in the manner and under the terms established in the Constitution of the Republic and the laws, constituting an essential, permanent, non-delegable and integral function of the State

[169] Therefore, in the implementation of Military Defense, the energies and resources of society as a whole must also be coordinated, ensuring maximum support and identification with its Armed Forces, contributing, within the framework of its powers, directly or indirectly to national development

[170] This new framework inevitably involves the design of a Military Defense Policy that considers the modernization of Military Defense and includes the adaptation of the different components of the Ministry of National Defense, indicating the relationship that should prevail between said components, defining the design and development of the military forces, adjusted to the political-strategic stature of Uruguay, considering, among other factors, its geographical dimension, its economic capacity and its population

[171] The structure and management of Military Defense will be inspired by the criterion of joint efficiency, which will guide the dynamic and ongoing modernization process of the Armed Forces, ensuring their continuous adaptation to the strategic context

[172] It will involve the execution of strategic actions aimed at strengthening the land military presence and collaboration, within the frameworks of action given by the National Defense Law, in socio-economic development

[173] In the South Atlantic, the country must have the means to exercise surveillance and control over Uruguayan jurisdictional waters, as well as maintain the security of maritime communication lines, while also considering river and lake areas

[174] It also requires the means and capacity to exercise surveillance and control of its airspace, as well as maintaining the safety of air navigation lines

[175] In these three areas, cooperation with neighboring countries must be strengthened to protect natural resources and the environment, among other things

[176] As part of reaffirming its commitment to the defense of peace and cooperation among peoples, Uruguay will participate in humanitarian actions and peacekeeping missions in accordance with the provisions of the National Defense Law

[177] The contents of the Military Defense Policy must be in accordance with the State's Foreign Policy

[194] THE NATIONAL INTERESTS THAT INSPIRE URUGUAY International relations, in general terms, are guided by a complex action of actors, interests and norms that stimulate or limit the power and prestige of Nations, and the country does not escape this reality

[195] In this context of multiple influences and interdependence, Uruguay seeks to achieve its National Objectives, which may generate scenarios of cooperation or competition of varying intensities

[196] Thus, it becomes essential to structure National Defense in accordance with the State's capabilities, to preserve sovereignty and National Interests in compatibility with those of the region, particularly UNASUR

[197] Thus, from the evaluation of the different scenarios described above, the National Interests and Objectives emerge

[198] The preservation of National Interests is closely linked to the viability of Uruguay as a Nation State, defining itself as of vital and strategic importance for the country

[199] Vital National Interests are characterized by being of fundamental importance to the country

[200] They include: - National Sovereignty and State Independence

[201] Territorial integrity

[202] The life and liberty of its inhabitants

[203] The Democratic Republican form of government and the rule of law enshrined in the Constitution of the Republic

[204] Cultural identity

[205] Strategic National Interests are those that contribute to ensuring Vital National Interests

[206] They include: - International Law and the promotion of peace

[207] International integration and regional integration

[208] Girls and boys as the country's main strategic interest

[209] The preservation of democracy in the region

[210] The economic, cultural and social development of the country

[211] Environmental protection

[212] The presence on the Antarctic Continent

[213] Strategic renewable and non-renewable natural resources

[214] The development of knowledge

[215] THE OBJECTIVES OF NATIONAL DEFENSE PERMANENT OBJECTIVES - Guarantee the Sovereignty, Independence and Peace of the Republic, its Constitution and its Laws

[216] Guarantee Territorial Integrity

[217] Protect the life and integrity of its inhabitants and their cultural identity

[218] Support the Republican-Democratic System of Government and the Rule of Law, as well as the country's economic and social development

[219] STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES - Contribute to maintaining peace, international security, solidarity and respect for human life

[220] Deepen relations of cooperation and mutual trust with other countries in the region

[221] Strengthen the country's educational and social infrastructure to ensure children's capabilities, providing opportunities for their development

[222] Strengthen democratic commitment in the region

[223] Promote the development of knowledge and scientific research in the country and the acquisition of technology

[224] Protect strategic renewable and non-renewable natural resources

[225] Contribute to environmental protection

[226] Maintain and promote a greater presence of the Uruguayan State in the Antarctic Continent

[227] strengthen the presence of the State in land, sea and national interest areas, as well as in international organizations

[228] • r The Obstacles that we could face THE OBSTACLES THAT r WE COULD FACE The Eastern Republic of Uruguay identifies the eventual contingency of the emergence of obstacles in the planning time horizon (period 2014-2030), which could acquire different magnitude, making it difficult or preventing in such case, to achieve and/or preserve its National Interests and Objectives

[229] These obstacles could eventually become threats that affect national sovereignty, independence, territorial integrity, and resources; ultimately, disrupting the peace and social well-being of the population

[230] In view of this, although the nature of these threats is different, they are considered in an integral manner, without this enumeration showing any type of priority order or excluding other eventualities

[231] Threats are understood to be all real or perceived actions that have an intrinsic potential to negatively affect national interests and objectives

[232] They can be of natural origin, but those caused by a State, organization, group of people or person, have an intentionality

[233] In this way, Uruguay identifies, among others, the following threats: ENVIRONMENTAL DETERIORATION Environmental care and national development are not opposing objectives but rather complementary ones

[234] The growth-led development model promoted by Uruguay must prevent the contamination of our country's water, air, and soil, all of which are strategic resources threatened by potential misuse

[235] Natural disasters, with various causes, constitute a serious threat to the country of increasing magnitude

[236] The negative effects of climate change, which manifest themselves unexpectedly and with greater frequency, produce natural disasters of major magnitude, affecting infrastructure, economic and productive activity, as well as the lives and health of our population, constituting a new scenario of dangerous phenomena to which Uruguay is exposed

[237] Additionally, this environmental deterioration aggravates the effects of natural disasters already occurring in the region and the world and is connected to the problem of the progressive scarcity of natural resources (and therefore to conflicts related to them), with food insecurity and new social and health vulnerabilities that are emerging

[238] THE APPEARANCE OF PANDEMICS These diseases affect a significant proportion of the population and in many cases generate serious consequences, negatively affecting countries, even with significantly high mortality rates among those infected

[239] Environmental deterioration, climate change, or the use of biological weapons can affect humans directly or indirectly through the contamination of plants, animals, food, air, or drinking water, acting as vectors for a pandemic that could threaten the country, affecting everything from the health of its citizens to its economic and social structure

[240] ORGANIZED CRIME Organized crime is defined as the association of individuals with a certain logistical and economic infrastructure that allows them to expand internationally, carrying out clandestine operations for profit; these can lead to the destruction of social cohesion through crimes such as drug trafficking, illegal arms trafficking, money laundering, human trafficking, corruption, and cybercrime, among others

[241] These types of activities threaten the stability of the State's democratic institutions, harm its international image, and undermine the country's economic, social, and cultural development, contributing to the proliferation of domestic crime and increasing its effects

[242] Terrorism is considered to be the use of violence and threats by individuals or an organized group, premeditatedly directed at the civilian population or facilities, generating terror and spreading it through the mass media; with the purpose of intimidating the population or forcing the Government or an International Organization to carry out an act or refrain from doing so

[243] Individuals, groups, and other entities are increasingly using the tools and instruments provided by globalization, particularly those related to communications (recruitment and dissemination of their attacks), and are also making use of the most advanced financial and logistical techniques to achieve their goals

[244] THE MATERIALIZATION OF ESPIONAGE AND CYBER ATTACKS Currently, there is repeated espionage by extra-regional companies, organizations or states on the governments of the region, public companies, as well as private companies or civil society organizations with the aim of illicitly capturing information to obtain economic advantages and political, military or social control, at the strategic level of the countries

[245] As a result of scientific and technological dependence, resulting from the use of technologies developed under licenses and patents that restrict the influence of computer networks and media as a support for these technologies, and from the increase in electronic support for state databases and the digitalization of state management, there is an increase in vulnerability to cyberattacks capable of generating economic, political, and social upheaval

[246] DEMOCRATIC INSTABILITY IN THE REGION In the present decade there have been several episodes of rupture or attempted rupture of the democratic order in the region

[247] In it, profound processes of economic and social transformation are manifested, and simultaneously other mechanisms emerge that damage democracy and the institutions that support it in a more subtle and complex way than those used in the past

[248] The validity of democracy is an essential condition of the regional integration process

[249] The weakening or loss of this in any of the countries in the region creates obstacles to the consolidation of integration, and may even lead to setbacks in the process and a loss of legitimacy of the regional bloc's external agenda vis-à-vis other blocs or countries

[250] THE EMERGENCE OF EXTRA-CONTINENTAL WARS The possibility of wars breaking out in regions outside our continent, due to the existence of latent conflicts of medium and long duration that can be observed historically, with the potential to negatively affect our economy

[251] The use of weapons of mass destruction (nuclear, biological or chemical) can generate environmental and biological impacts that could constitute serious threats to Uruguay

[252] THE AGGRAVATION OF REGIONAL CONFLICTS Two situations must be taken into account: firstly, the particular situation that is manifested in the Malvinas Islands, due to the presence of extra-regional powers and therefore, which negatively impact the South Atlantic as a zone of peace and cooperation, and secondly, the aggravation of possible border conflicts between countries in the region

[253] Both the first and second situations constitute latent threats to the country. In the first case, because they affect the oceanic zone where the country's maritime communications and economic activity naturally occur; in the second case, because any regional conflict weakens the ongoing integration process, generates arms races that are contrary to development, and jeopardizes the mutual trust that has been built between UNASUR countries

[254] ECONOMIC CRISES The global economy has multiple relationships of interdependence, where international financial actors participate that could generate economic crises through speculation with highly complex and low-quality financial products or by eroding banking systems

[255] Additionally, the global system is highly vulnerable to a decline in the pace of economic growth of the major emerging powers, which generates potential crises in the demand for products in the countries of the region. This has negative effects on exports, employment, inflation and tax revenue, leading to a deterioration in the well-being of the population, primarily at the economic and social levels

[256] THE APPROPRIATION AND IMPROPER CONTROL OF STRATEGIC RESOURCES In the current international context, the appropriation of certain resources considered strategic constitute a potential threat to Uruguay and the region, taking into account that it is a geographical area rich in energy, mineral, food, drinking water and biodiversity resources

[257] Considering that they are located in specific places, both politically and territorially, it is reasonable that States that have reserves of strategic natural resources feel threatened by the possibility of being immersed in a dispute over their control

[258] Additionally, natural resources, such as those found in Antarctica and the seabed, that are not currently under the formal sovereignty of any country, must be taken into account

[259] STRATEGIC GUIDELINES FOR NATIONAL DEFENSE INTRODUCTION

[260] The strategic guidelines are defined based on the objectives to be achieved, describing a set of actions aimed at achieving and maintaining them, considering the magnitude of the obstacles that have been exposed

[261] In light of the above, general guidelines are established to protect Vital and Strategic National Interests by achieving and maintaining the National Defense Objectives, both established in this document, anticipating and mitigating temporary and structural vulnerabilities

[262] Likewise, there are national interests that generate objectives that have a transversal impact on various areas of the Uruguayan State

[263] STRATEGIC GUIDELINES FOR NATIONAL DEFENSE 1. NATIONAL ASPECTS Preserve the sovereignty, independence and integrity of the national territory

[264] Preserve the rule of law and strengthen the State and government institutions as an expression of democracy, as representatives of the sovereign people

[265] Promote knowledge of Human Rights - civil, political, economic, social and cultural - as the best way to protect and deepen them

[266] Promote care, family affection and protection from childhood, as a contribution to the present, but above all, future well-being of the population

[267] To enhance the proper development of children and adolescents and promote their educational inclusion, as a determining factor for the nation's future

[268] Promote education in all its aspects and aimed at the general population, as a contribution to the national development strategy; as well as deepen technical and scientific education as a contribution to such development

[269] Promote cultural identity, as well as the various aspects of national and institutional culture, among the inhabitants of the Republic

[270] Promote a culture of National Defense at all levels and in all spheres of society and develop a strategic vision to optimize it

[271] Promote within the national territory, integration, development and cooperation in border areas, as well as enact the necessary legislation in this regard

[272] Promote and strengthen the establishment of settlements in border areas and in those areas considered strategic by the competent authority, encouraging the installation of public services, promoting employment policies, and creating the necessary infrastructure

[273] Protect and strengthen the country's vital and strategic infrastructure, upon which the provision of essential services and resources, such as energy, water, transportation, and communications, depends

[274] Protect national maritime, river, and lake resources by strengthening surveillance and control of lagoons, rivers, territorial waters, and the exclusive economic zone

[275] Also promote the sustainable use of these resources

[276] Promote a regulatory framework and policies that guarantee food sovereignty, allowing the population access to the resources necessary for adequate nutrition

[277] Promote policies that encourage the valorization of our strategic natural resources as a factor in national economic development and exercise the necessary controls to ensure their sustainable exploitation, adapting the regulatory framework and the State's capabilities to this end

[278] Encourage the development and use of renewable energy sources, facilitating access to them and diversifying the energy mix

[279] Encourage the exploration and exploitation of non-renewable energy sources such as oil and natural gas

[280] Continue developing and managing research projects related to environmental care, considering new technologies and scientific advances in this regard

[281] Promote a regulatory framework, policies and capabilities to achieve sustainable levels of industrial and agricultural use of polluting products, as well as to prevent their discharge into waterways

[282] Generate policies aimed at reducing levels of consumerism and waste generation

[283] Encourage recycling mechanisms

[284] Guide scientific and technological research programs to strengthen the capabilities of National Defense

[285] Continue and encourage scientific research and cooperation with other nations in Antarctica, under the terms established in the Antarctic Treaty and complementary international conventions

[286] Coordinate state policies and plans that address the improvement of the social situation in the most disadvantaged areas of the country, generating a synergy that multiplies the impact that each state actor can achieve on its own

[287] Deepen policies for active citizen participation, coordinating them at the national, departmental, and local levels

[288] Ensure adequate access of the population to public services

[289] Increase the levels of professionalization in security and defense, continuing to improve and modernize doctrinal training, the quality of salary income, the updating of legal frameworks, technical development, the quality of equipment, communications, mobility capacity, and permanent deployment throughout the country

[290] Strengthen the State Intelligence system

[291] Prevent organized crime activities by strengthening state presence and control in all areas of national jurisdiction

[292] Strengthen the quality of police relations with the most vulnerable communities

[293] Continue with policies to combat hotbeds of police corruption

[294] Generate a culture of transparency and periodic accountability within the Police, before the population of each jurisdiction

[295] Continue developing the comprehensive prison care model, promoting the comprehensive rehabilitation of prisoners

[296] Protect the Uruguayan state, government, and population from all forms of espionage, reaffirming national sovereignty and the right to privacy and security of the country's internal and international communications

[297] Protect Uruguay from cyberattacks and preserve the confidentiality of data resulting from state and private management, both at the national and regional levels, as appropriate

[298] Encourage the use of free software in the Uruguayan State

[299] INTERNATIONAL ASPECTS Promote ongoing coordination between the Ministries of National Defense, Interior, Economy and Finance, and Foreign Affairs to update information on potential obstacles and risks arising from the international situation

[300] Deepen our country's active participation in multilateral bodies that aim to achieve democratic stability and peace, both at the international level (United Nations) and at the regional level (UNASUR and CELAC

[301] Strengthen the political cooperation mechanisms of MERCOSUR and UNASUR, particularly those related to the application of the democratic clause and the Additional Protocol to the UNASUR Constitutive Treaty on commitment to democracy, in the event of institutional breakdown in the countries of the region

[302] Consolidate bonds of trust and institutional ties, especially among the region's presidents and ministers

[303] Advance the regional integration process by consolidating the bloc's prestige and image

[304] Contribute to deepening social democracy in the region

[305] Promote economic and financial measures that counteract the potential negative effects of extra-regional conflicts

[306] Continue market diversification to reduce the country's vulnerability to adverse global events

[307] Provide measures to protect the population from environmental and biological impacts

[308] Contribute to strengthening the United Nations as a forum for promoting peace and preventing conflict; in particular, promote the participation of Uruguay and the other UNASUR countries in the United Nations Security Council

[309] Actively participate in the United Nations collective security scheme through the mechanisms provided by that organization, insofar as it represents a sovereign decision determined by Uruguayan foreign policy, in accordance with the terms of the National Defense Framework Law

[310] To urge respect for and application of international law, especially the principles and mechanisms contained in the Charter of the United Nations, aimed at peacefully resolving disputes that arise between States

[311] Strengthen dispute resolution bodies at the MERCOSUR and UNASUR levels

[312] Consolidate the UNASUR Defense Council as the regional defense cooperation body par excellence

[313] Strengthen international cooperation among the South Atlantic coastal countries grouped in the South Atlantic Zone of Peace and Cooperation (ZOPACAS

[314] Consolidate relations of mutual trust between the countries of the region through intense engagement between the Foreign Ministries and the Ministries of Defense of these countries

[315] Strengthen regional bodies based on international law

[316] Contribute, through diplomacy, to the reduction of regional conflicts and the creation of zones of peace without nuclear weapons, respecting the principles of peaceful settlement of disputes, non-intervention in the internal affairs of other nations, and self-determination of peoples

[317] Seek the submission of conflicts to the mediation bodies provided for in the supranational organizations of which the States involved are part, with a view to achieving peace

[318] Strengthen ties between countries in the region, with special emphasis on academic, commercial, and tourism areas

[319] To disclose military defense expenditures and implement measures to build mutual trust, in accordance with the methodology approved by the South American Defense Council of UNASUR

[320] Promote regional and international cooperation in defense matters

[321] Participate actively and permanently in regional and international governmental forums dedicated to the exchange of information and discussion of security and defense policies, especially on issues related to organized crime and terrorism

[322] Maintain a balanced policy in multilateral forums that address or promote legislation on terrorism

[323] Expand regional cooperation in the development, adaptation, and use of open source software

[324] Promote the construction of a secure regional communications system

[325] Research, innovate and implement media free from external control for the strategic communications of the Uruguayan State in collaboration with other UNASUR States
