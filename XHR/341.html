[7] The Cabinet on August 24, 2020 approved the National Security Strategy which is now presented

[257] THE INTERNATIONAL TIRE RATING SYSTEM FOR THREATS

[258] The International Tier Rating System used is explained as follows: Tier 1 Threats: these require immediate attention

[259] They are clear and present dangers that must receive the priority indicated

[260] These are High Probability and High Impact Threats

[261] Tier 2 Threats: these threats have major potential for disaster

[262] They are imminent, might not be seen immediately to represent the adverse capacities they possess but they have catastrophic potential

[263] They are Low Probability, High Impact events, which can be upgraded to Tier 1 Threats in the “blink of an eye”

[264] c. Tier 3 Threats: these represent situations that the Federation is familiar with in everyday life, some of which are taken for granted, or, have become accepted over time as the norm

[265] They are an integral part of our social make-up

[266] They do however, have the potential to “spring into life” or take on an “adverse persona,” or, be upgraded to a higher threat level, as a result of being “sparked” by a single occurrence

[267] A riot in HMP, for example, could cause the renewal of gang violence amongst gangs in the Federation

[268] The perceived unfair dismissal of an employee, by the employer, could spark severe industrial actions in the Public/Private Sectors and peaceful political supporters becoming violent as tensions rise in a General Election scenario

[269] They are therefore of medium priority but require regular monitoring and reviews

[270] These are High Probability, Low Impact Threats

[271] Tier 4 Threats: although less probable than the other Threat Scenarios, they are potential risks to the security of the Federation

[272] Their potential to cause harm is not immediate, however, to negate their impact in the medium to long term they will need constant monitoring and the development of plans to negate their development

[273] The lack of basic utilities for some citizens/communities and the frustrations built up over time, fall in this category

[274] These are Low Probability, Low Impact Threats
