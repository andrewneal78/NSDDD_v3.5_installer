[2] Page | 2 Defense Directive 2014 Mr. Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces, Commanders of Forces, Commanders of units and departments of the Armed Forces, Civilian and military leaders of the Ministry of Defense, The mission of the Armed Forces to preserve independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity, support the population in times of peace, crisis and war, as well as to contribute to peace and security in the region and beyond remains a sacred mission

[3] This mission will continue to be realized by harmonizing all plans and efforts with the NATO Alliance and other state security institutions

[4] The new Government program in the field of defense contains ambitious objectives that require the continuation of reforms and the further transformation of our Armed Forces, the creation of the necessary military capacities to face the challenges of security and defense, as well as placing them at the height they deserve in Albanian society

[5] At the center of these reforms will be the elevation of the image and integrity of the military, through the development of the necessary policies in the field of personnel, legislation, career promotion, increasing transparency, accountability, and eliminating corruption in the Armed Forces

[6] Other important directions will be the restructuring of the Armed Forces, the development of operational and support capacities, the improvement of the education and training system, infrastructure, modernization and increasing the effectiveness of military diplomacy

[7] The year 2013 marked a series of achievements in terms of the full integration of the Armed Forces into NATO military structures, participation in NATO and EU-led operations, expansion of the range of training and exercises, and dismantling of surplus ammunition

[8] Despite the results achieved, I note shortcomings related to: drafting key strategic security and defense documents; developing full operational capacities; mismanagement of defense resources, which have resulted in a difficult financial situation; investments and maintenance of defense systems and equipment, as well as problems in the process of evaluation and promotion in career and rank of military personnel
