[530] The GAOs and GAORs, through violent actions, wage a continuous struggle for control of the territory and illegal economies that produce large dividends, representing a threat to the lives of Colombians

[531] Various analyses show a pattern of convergence between violence and illegal economies, resulting in an increase in homicides and other violent crimes in areas where these two phenomena converge

[532] At the same time, non-state actors capable of carrying out cyberattacks on public and private information and communication systems pursue economic or destabilization goals, posing a vital threat to national interests

[533] Other challenges that can threaten national interests include natural disasters and pandemics

[534] Due to its geographical location and meteorological conditions, Colombia is a country permanently exposed to natural disasters (Hernández-Vargas, 2021), with the poorest people being the most vulnerable to the aforementioned challenges, which can be real threats to the lives and property of Colombians

[535] Similarly, the COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated how vulnerable societies are to these types of challenges

[536] Drug trafficking, border disputes, and illegal oil extraction are the main trends threatening the nation's strategic assets

[537] Drug trafficking is a phenomenon that has plagued Colombia for several decades, although it has been combated by the Armed Forces

[538] It is constantly increasing; current figures demonstrate its exponential increase, facilitating the financing and growth of illegal armed groups. Therefore, it is currently emerging as one of the main trends affecting the environment, the lives of Colombians, and national security and defense

[539] Similarly, state actors with which Colombia has long-standing territorial disputes are also becoming a trend that in the future may pose a threat to the nation's territory and sovereignty, as they seek to gain land, river, and maritime portions of the national geography, disputes with a history of claims dating back to times as far back as independence from the Spanish Empire

[540] These claims materialize in violations of Colombian land, air, and maritime space

[541] For its part, illegal mining, which leads to deforestation and the contamination of water sources, poses a threat to the fauna, flora, biodiversity, rivers, and waterways where this scourge is present

[542] Similarly, attacks against the country's mining, energy, road, communications, and various economic production infrastructure by the GAO and GAOR constitute a threat to the country's critical infrastructure

[543] To make Colombia a powerhouse of life, this paper proposes a strategic approach comprised of desired ends, strategic objectives, and a theory of victory

[544] The desired end state pursued by the strategy is based on modern, equipped, trained, and organized armed forces, dismantled armed groups (GAOs) and armed groups (GAORs) welcomed and/or brought under the rule of law, and peaceful, prosperous, and developed regions where equity, human security, and respect for life prevail

[545] In addition, the strategy establishes five strategic objectives, which aim to defend and protect national interests

[546] The first objective is to guarantee the protection of the lives of all Colombians

[547] To achieve this goal, the concept of UA must be applied, complemented by the development of military operations against the various illegal armed groups that threaten the lives and integrity of Colombians throughout the country, emphasizing the protection of peasants, indigenous peoples, Afro-descendants, social leaders, women, Raizal people, and other minorities in the country

[548] Likewise, the Armed Forces will use all their capabilities to protect and mitigate the effects of pandemics and natural disasters

[549] CONCLUSIONS The second objective raises the defense of the territory, sovereignty and strategic assets of the nation

[550] Consequently, the Armed Forces, in compliance with the constitutional mandate described in Article 217 of the Constitution, will continue to conduct multi-domain operations throughout the national territory, employing conventional deterrent capabilities and defense diplomacy

[551] The third objective proposes modernizing and updating the equipment and capabilities of the Armed Forces. This requires modernizing military intelligence, close air support and aviation, special forces, and military engineers

[552] Regarding the defense of sovereignty and territory from external threats, it is vitally important to renew conventional minimum deterrent capabilities and strengthen defense diplomacy

[553] The fourth objective seeks to dismantle organized armed groups (GAO) and residual organized armed groups (GAOR) through increased use of force, combined with intelligence and judicial operations

[554] Finally, the fifth objective proposes that the Armed Forces contribute to the development and economic prosperity of the country's marginalized areas, supported by international economic cooperation mechanisms with friendly and allied countries, in addition to the use of the capabilities of the Army's military engineers for the construction or improvement of housing, road infrastructure, public services, and irrigation districts

[555] Finally, the theory of victory pursues the articulation of state and non-state institutions within the framework of a line of operations and lines of effort

[556] Line of Operations One (LOO1) uses the instrument of power (IOP) to protect the lives of Colombians, dismantle the GAOs and GAORs, and defend the nation's sovereignty, territory, and strategic assets

[557] Line of effort one (LOE1) articulates the instruments of political power (IPP) and military power (IPM) to strengthen and modernize the Armed Forces

[558] Line of effort two (LOE2) articulates the IPP, the instrument of economic power (IPE) and the IPM, to strengthen and modernize the Armed Forces, contributing to the development and economic prosperity of the nation and finally line of effort three (LOE3) articulates the IPM, IPE and the population, to promote the development of marginalized regions

[559] In short, for Colombia to become a global power, the national government must articulate its instruments of national power (IPP+IPE+IPM+IPD)² to establish the necessary measures to guarantee the defense and protection of national interests
