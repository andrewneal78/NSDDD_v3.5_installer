[747] ANNEXES Political guidelines for the update of the Defense Policy The Ministry of Defense will be the governing body of the process and generator of dialogue for the update of the National Defense Policy

[748] The agenda proposed for this purpose must promote a genuine national awareness of the need to actively participate in updating the National Defense Policy

[752] It will respond to a medium-term prospective analysis that will consider global, regional, and national aspects that will impact the defense sector

[753] It will adhere to a democratic culture and will emphasize strengthening the civil-military relationship, military subordination to the legally constituted political power, incorporation of civil society in the dialogue process to increase the power of knowledge on security and defense in Ecuadorian society, greater participation in the formulation of public policies, transparency and accountability in defense management and a wide dissemination of the importance of building a consensual Defense Policy, as State Policy

[754] It must contain the structure of the National Defense System, its organization, mission and role within this context

[755] The updating process should include the participation of national and international academic experts, as well as public and private institutions, representatives of civil society, non-governmental organizations, representatives of the media and the general population, in order to establish a forum for critical reflection through seminars and discussion workshops that allow for the obtaining of consensual conclusions
