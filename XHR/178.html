[500] Crimes of that type are motivated primarily by profit, so the only way to permanently reduce the level of crime is to take the profit out of crime

[518] Money laundering and tax evasion Some of the same tactics used in money laundering (where people try to conceal the proceeds of crime and ‘launder’ them into legitimate assets) are also used in tax evasion (where people try to avoid paying the taxes to which they are liable), so the measures needed to prevent money laundering can also help to reduce the level of tax evasion

[981] Tier 2 includes a wide range of diverse threats, with different combinations of assessed probability and likely impact
